andWHEREAS, A lifelong North Side resident, Ms. Burns was serving as a Pittsburgh Public School director when chosen as a candidate for a February 1994 special election for the 20th District; andWHEREAS, Sworn in March 7, 1994, Ms. Burns served in the General Assembly through the end of the 1993-1994 session on November 30, 1994; andWHEREAS, Ms. Burns previously contributed to the progress of the House of Representatives as an aide to Representative Ivan Itkin, a legislator from the 23rd District whose work was 123456789101112131415161718 similarly rooted in expanding protections and accountability; andWHEREAS, Ms. Burns faithfully directed her energies and skillful leadership to Pittsburgh City Council, the Pittsburgh Community Reinvestment Group and the University of Pittsburgh Institute of Politics; andWHEREAS, As a member of East Allegheny Community Council, Ms. Burns founded Pumpkinfest, a children's harvest festival held at East Park, Deutschtown; andWHEREAS, Families gathered for the 31st Pumpkinfest on October 11, 2025; andWHEREAS, The relationships Ms. Burns built as proprietor of Sweet Time General Store & Gathering Place exemplified her commitment to the East Ohio Street business district; andWHEREAS, Ms. Burns was educated at Allegheny High School and Chatham College; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives joins in expressing sorrow for the loss of Ms. Burns, who died November 5, 2025, at 76 years of age; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives offers condolences to the family of Ms. Burns, including her siblings, nieces, nephews, great-nieces and great-nephews; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives commends Ms. Burns's contributions as a legislator and legislative aide; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives celebrates the meaningful ways in which Ms. Burns cultivated civic life among the households, shops and institutions of the City of Pittsburgh's North Side community;
