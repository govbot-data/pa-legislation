A RESOLUTION
Honoring the memory of the late Honorable Barbara Ann Burns,
former member of the House of Representatives from the 20th
District, Allegheny County.
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives gratefully acknowledges
the policy goals and progress Barbara Ann Burns made for the
benefit of her neighbors and constituents as the first female
member of the House of Representatives from the City of
Pittsburgh; and
WHEREAS, A lifelong North Side resident, Ms. Burns was
serving as a Pittsburgh Public School director when chosen as a
candidate for a February 1994 special election for the 20th
District; and
WHEREAS, Sworn in March 7, 1994, Ms. Burns served in the
General Assembly through the end of the 1993-1994 session on
November 30, 1994; and
WHEREAS, Ms. Burns previously contributed to the progress of
the House of Representatives as an aide to Representative Ivan
Itkin, a legislator from the 23rd District whose work was
