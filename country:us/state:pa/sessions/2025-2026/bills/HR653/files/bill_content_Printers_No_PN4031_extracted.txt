2025-2026 Regular Session HR 653 PN 4031 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 4031 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.653 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BRENNAN, PIELLI, WAXMAN, KENYATTA, SANCHEZ AND HOWARD, OCTOBER 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LABOR AND INDUSTRY, OCTOBER 9, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDirecting the Joint State Government Commission to conduct a study and issue a report on the data that needs to be collected to evaluate the impact of artificial intelligence on the workforce.WHEREAS, The use of artificial intelligence (AI) is growing, with 54% of job seekers in the United States (US) having an interview led by AI; andWHEREAS, Eight in ten hiring managers are looking for candidates with AI skills; andWHEREAS, There are growing questions about the bias of AI in the hiring process, with one study showing that, across three AI platforms, there was evidence of gender and racial bias; andWHEREAS, According to employees, 41% of companies in the US have integrated AI into organizational practices; andWHEREAS, Half of employees in the US use AI at work, with 13% of employees using AI daily; andWHEREAS, It is estimated that 25% of work tasks in the US could be automated by AI; andWHEREAS, Yale University's Budget Lab argues that, as of May 12345678910111213141516171819 2026, there is no evidence of AI disrupting the US labor market; andWHEREAS, There is concern about the impact of AI on college graduates; andWHEREAS, While unemployment has risen for college graduates and in professional and business sectors, it is not currently believed that these jobs were lost due to AI; andWHEREAS, Some argue that AI will begin to be used by employers on a large scale within the next 10 years, with the knowledge and creative sectors potentially seeing the largest impact; andWHEREAS, The United States Bureau of Labor Statistics anticipates that AI will have the largest impact on occupations that rely on computer usage; andWHEREAS, Sixty-five percent of employees whose employers implemented AI in the workplace state that AI has extremely or somewhat positively impacted their productivity; andWHEREAS, The percentage of employees with concern over job displacement due to technology increased to 18% in the first quarter of 2026; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives direct the Joint State Government Commission to conduct a study and issue a report on the data that needs to be collected to evaluate the impact of artificial intelligence on the workforce;
