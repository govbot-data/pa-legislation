A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the month of September 2026 as "National Recovery
Month" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, According to the Substance Abuse and Mental Health
Services Administration's 2025 National Survey on Drug Use and
Health, 30.6 million adult Americans have had a substance use
disorder and 22.3 million of these Americans are in recovery;
and
WHEREAS, In the past year, 7.6 million people received
substance use treatment; and
WHEREAS, According to preliminary drug overdose data issued
in June 2026 by the Department of Health, 2,760 Pennsylvanians
lost their lives due to a drug overdose in 2025; and
WHEREAS, Many Pennsylvanians are close to someone, whether
its their parents, children, siblings, neighbors, colleagues or
friends, working to overcome substance use and mental health
disorders; and
WHEREAS, Struggling individuals need support and resources

during their recovery process; and
WHEREAS, The recovery process can be difficult, and having
the necessary financial resources, support from family and
friends and a healthy environment is critical to success; and
WHEREAS, Since 1989, "National Recovery Month" has been a
national observance held every September; and
WHEREAS, This observance promotes and supports new evidence-
based treatment and recovery practices, supports the recovery
community and recognizes the dedication of service providers and
communities who make recovery in all its forms possible; and
WHEREAS, Recognizing "National Recovery Month" recommits our
support to the prevention, treatment and recovery of individuals
with substance use and mental health disorders; and
WHEREAS, All residents of this Commonwealth are invited to
participate in this observance by coming together and
celebrating individuals in recovery and offering support to
those still living with substance use and mental health
disorders; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the month of September
2026 as "National Recovery Month" in Pennsylvania.
