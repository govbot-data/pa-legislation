2025-2026 Regular Session SR 366 PN 1924 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1924 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.366 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KANE, TARTAGLIONE, COSTA, STREET, PICOZZI, SCHWANK, PISCIOTTANO, CAPPELLETTI, COMITTA, VOGEL, HUGHES, HAYWOOD, YAW, KEARNEY AND CULVER, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of September 2026 as "National Recovery Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, According to the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration's 2025 National Survey on Drug Use and Health, 30.6 million adult Americans have had a substance use disorder and 22.3 million of these Americans are in recovery; andWHEREAS, In the past year, 7.6 million people received substance use treatment; andWHEREAS, According to preliminary drug overdose data issued in June 2026 by the Department of Health, 2,760 Pennsylvanians lost their lives due to a drug overdose in 2025; andWHEREAS, Many Pennsylvanians are close to someone, whether its their parents, children, siblings, neighbors, colleagues or friends, working to overcome substance use and mental health disorders; andWHEREAS, Struggling individuals need support and resources 1234567891011121314151617 during their recovery process; andWHEREAS, The recovery process can be difficult, and having the necessary financial resources, support from family and friends and a healthy environment is critical to success; andWHEREAS, Since 1989, "National Recovery Month" has been a national observance held every September; andWHEREAS, This observance promotes and supports new evidence-based treatment and recovery practices, supports the recovery community and recognizes the dedication of service providers and communities who make recovery in all its forms possible; andWHEREAS, Recognizing "National Recovery Month" recommits our support to the prevention, treatment and recovery of individuals with substance use and mental health disorders; andWHEREAS, All residents of this Commonwealth are invited to participate in this observance by coming together and celebrating individuals in recovery and offering support to those still living with substance use and mental health disorders;
