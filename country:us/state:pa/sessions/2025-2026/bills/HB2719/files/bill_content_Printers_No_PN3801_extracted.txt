2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2719 PN 3801 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3801 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2719 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY HEFFLEY, STAATS AND WATRO, JULY 28, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON FINANCE, JULY 29, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An act relating to the public school system, including certain provisions applicable as well to private and parochial schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," in school finances, further providing for school districts lying in more than one county or in more than one municipality and limitation on total tax revenues.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 672.1(a) introductory paragraph of the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended and the section is amended by adding subsections to read:Section 672.1. School Districts Lying in More Than One County or in More Than One Municipality; Limitation on Total Tax Revenues.--(a) Whenever a school district shall lie in more than one county, the total taxes levied on real estate within the school district in each county shall be subject to subsection (a.1) and the following:* * *(a.1) (1) Notwithstanding the act of June 27, 2006 (1st 123456789101112131415161718192021 Sp.Sess., P.L.1873, No.1), known as the "Taxpayer Relief Act," and except as provided under paragraph (2), a board of school directors may not set or adjust the actual millage rate to a rate above:(i) the actual millage rate applied to that portion of the school district for the prior year; plus(ii) the allowable index adjustment for that portion of the school district.(2) Paragraph (1) does not apply to a change in millage rate after a revision of assessed values under section 327 of the "Taxpayer Relief Act" or a successful referendum under section 333 of the "Taxpayer Relief Act."* * *(d) As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Allowable index adjustment." The actual millage rate applied to that portion of the school district for the prior year multiplied by the index applicable to the school district for the school year."Index." As defined in section 302 of the "Taxpayer Relief Act."Section 2. The amendment or addition of section 672.1(a) introductory paragraph, (a.1) and (d) of the act shall apply to tax rates established by a school district for a school year that begins after June 30, 2026.Section 3.
