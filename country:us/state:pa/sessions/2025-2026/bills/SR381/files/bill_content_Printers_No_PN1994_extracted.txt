WARD AND STEFANO, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing September 15, 2026, as "Roberto Clemente Day" in Pennsylvania and encouraging the celebration of his life as a veteran, humanitarian and Hall of Fame baseball player.WHEREAS, On August 18, 1934, Roberto Enrique Clemente Walker was born in Barrio San Antón, Carolina, Puerto Rico, to Melchor Clemente and Luisa Walker; andWHEREAS, Mr. Clemente spent much time in his youth working alongside his father in the sugarcane fields, while also playing baseball; andWHEREAS, After spending one season in the minor leagues in Montreal in 1954, Mr. Clemente was drafted by the Pittsburgh Pirates on November 22, 1954; andWHEREAS, In September 1958, Mr. Clemente joined the United States Marine Corps Reserve and served his active duty commitment at Parris Island, South Carolina; Camp Lejeune, North Carolina; and Washington, DC; andWHEREAS, Mr. Clemente was set to be released from active duty in the United States Marine Corps Reserve on April 4, 1959; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, State Senator John M. Walker wrote to United States Senator Hugh Scott to request an early release on March 4, 1959, to allow Mr. Clemente to join the Pittsburgh Pirates for spring training; andWHEREAS, During his Major League Baseball career, Mr. Clemente won 12 Gold Glove Awards, accumulated 3,000 hits and 240 home runs and was the National League Most Valuable Player in 1966, a two-time World Series Champion and the World Series Most Valuable Player in 1971; andWHEREAS, Always a humanitarian, Mr. Clemente organized emergency relief aid flights from Puerto Rico to Managua, Nicaragua, after a devastating earthquake on December 23, 1972; andWHEREAS, After learning that the corrupt Nicaraguan government had absconded with the relief packages, Mr. Clemente decided to accompany the next relief flight on December 31, 1972; andWHEREAS, The flight crashed off the coast of Puerto Rico immediately after takeoff, killing Mr. Clemente and all other passengers on board; andWHEREAS, After his tragic and untimely death, the Baseball Writers' Association of America waived the waiting period and held a special election for the Baseball Hall of Fame; andWHEREAS, Mr. Clemente was posthumously elected for induction into the Baseball Hall of Fame with 92.7% of the vote; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize September 15, 2026, as "Roberto Clemente Day" in Pennsylvania and encourage the celebration of his life as a veteran, humanitarian and Hall of Fame baseball player.20260SR0381PN1994 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930
