A RESOLUTION
Recognizing September 15, 2026, as "Roberto Clemente Day" in
Pennsylvania and encouraging the celebration of his life as a
veteran, humanitarian and Hall of Fame baseball player.
WHEREAS, On August 18, 1934, Roberto Enrique Clemente Walker
was born in Barrio San Antón, Carolina, Puerto Rico, to Melchor
Clemente and Luisa Walker; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Clemente spent much time in his youth working
alongside his father in the sugarcane fields, while also playing
baseball; and
WHEREAS, After spending one season in the minor leagues in
Montreal in 1954, Mr. Clemente was drafted by the Pittsburgh
Pirates on November 22, 1954; and
WHEREAS, In September 1958, Mr. Clemente joined the United
States Marine Corps Reserve and served his active duty
commitment at Parris Island, South Carolina; Camp Lejeune, North
Carolina; and Washington, DC; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Clemente was set to be released from active duty
in the United States Marine Corps Reserve on April 4, 1959; and

WHEREAS, State Senator John M. Walker wrote to United States
Senator Hugh Scott to request an early release on March 4, 1959,
to allow Mr. Clemente to join the Pittsburgh Pirates for spring
training; and
WHEREAS, During his Major League Baseball career, Mr.
Clemente won 12 Gold Glove Awards, accumulated 3,000 hits and
240 home runs and was the National League Most Valuable Player
in 1966, a two-time World Series Champion and the World Series
Most Valuable Player in 1971; and
WHEREAS, Always a humanitarian, Mr. Clemente organized
emergency relief aid flights from Puerto Rico to Managua,
Nicaragua, after a devastating earthquake on December 23, 1972;
and
WHEREAS, After learning that the corrupt Nicaraguan
government had absconded with the relief packages, Mr. Clemente
decided to accompany the next relief flight on December 31,
1972; and
WHEREAS, The flight crashed off the coast of Puerto Rico
immediately after takeoff, killing Mr. Clemente and all other
passengers on board; and
WHEREAS, After his tragic and untimely death, the Baseball
Writers' Association of America waived the waiting period and
held a special election for the Baseball Hall of Fame; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Clemente was posthumously elected for induction
into the Baseball Hall of Fame with 92.7% of the vote; therefore
be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate recognize September 15, 2026, as
"Roberto Clemente Day" in Pennsylvania and encourage the
celebration of his life as a veteran, humanitarian and Hall of
Fame baseball player.
