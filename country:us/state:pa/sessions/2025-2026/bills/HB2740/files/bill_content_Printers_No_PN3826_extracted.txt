2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2740 PN 3826 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3826 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2740 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BURGOS, McNEILL, KAZEEM, GUZMAN, WEBSTER, PROKOPIAK, HILL-EVANS, DEASY, SANCHEZ, BOROWSKI, PARKER, RIVERA AND CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, AUGUST 17, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON CONSUMER PROTECTION, TECHNOLOGY AND UTILITIES, AUGUST 18, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in rates and distribution systems, further providing for voluntary changes in rates.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Sections 1308 of Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding subsections to read:§ 1308. Voluntary changes in rates.* * *(g) Mandatory hypothetical capital structure for ratemaking purposes.--The commission shall utilize a mandatory hypothetical capital structure consisting of 50% equity and 50% debt for the purpose of establishing just and reasonable rates, determining a reasonable rate of return and for revenue requirement calculations.(h) Exclusivity.--The commission shall be strictly prohibited from utilizing, considering or granting weight to a 1234567891011121314151617 public utility's actual capital structure, the capital structure of any parent or affiliate companies or any other alternative capital structure, regardless of whether the actual equity ratio is higher or lower than the hypothetical capital structure.(i) Cost of debt.--The commission shall utilize the public utility's actual cost of long-term debt for ratemaking purposes, provided that debt was prudently incurred.(j) Exclusivity.--The hypothetical capital structure required under subsection (g) shall be used exclusively for ratemaking purposes and shall not require a public utility to maintain any specific actual capital structure.(k) Applicability.--This subsection and subsections (g), (h), (i) and (j) shall apply to all base rate proceedings filed in accordance with subsection (d) after the effective date of this subsection.Section 2.
