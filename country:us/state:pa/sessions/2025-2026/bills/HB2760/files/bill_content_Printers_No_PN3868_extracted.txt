2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2760 PN 3868 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3868 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2760 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BENNINGHOFF, CIRESI, FLICK, GILLEN, HANBIDGE, KAUFFMAN, KRUPA, KUZMA, PICKETT, SMITH, STENDER, VEROBISH AND WALLEN, SEPTEMBER 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON ENVIRONMENTAL AND NATURAL RESOURCE PROTECTION, SEPTEMBER 9, 2026 AN ACTProviding for the protection of water supplies by requiring commercial data centers to restore or replace a polluted or diminutive water supply; and imposing duties on the Department of Environmental Protection.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Commercial Data Center Water Protection Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Commercial data center." A facility, campus of facilities or array of interconnected facilities located in this Commonwealth that meets all of the following:(1) Is used by a business entity or other enterprise to operate, manage or maintain a computer, group of computers or 123456789101112131415161718 other organized assembly of hardware and software for the primary purpose of processing, storing, retrieving or transmitting data.(2) Has a peak demand of 25 megawatts or greater."Department." The Department of Environmental Protection of the Commonwealth.Section 3. Protection of water supplies.(a) Requirement to restore or replace water supply.--(1) A commercial data center that affects a public or private water supply by pollution or diminution shall restore or replace the affected supply with an alternate source of water adequate in quantity or quality for the purposes served by the supply.(2) The department shall ensure that the quality of a restored or replaced water supply meets the standards established under the act of May 1, 1984 (P.L.206, No.43), known as the Pennsylvania Safe Drinking Water Act, or is comparable to the quality of the water supply before it was affected by the commercial data center if the water supply exceeded those standards.(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) shall be in addition to a requirement imposed under subsection (g).(4) The Environmental Quality Board shall promulgate regulations necessary to meet the requirements of this subsection.(b) Pollution or diminution of water supply.--(1) A landowner or water purveyor suffering pollution or diminution of a water supply as a result of the construction or operation of a commercial data center may notify the department and request that an investigation be conducted.20260HB2760PN3868 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (2) Within 10 days of notification, the department shall investigate the claim and make a determination within 45 days following notification. If the department finds that the pollution or diminution was caused by the construction or operation of the commercial data center or if the department presumes the commercial data center is responsible for pollution in accordance with subsection (f), the department shall issue orders to the operator of the commercial data center necessary to assure compliance with subsection (a), including orders requiring temporary replacement of a water supply where it is determined that pollution or diminution may be of limited duration.(c) Reporting water contamination.--(1) The department shall establish a single Statewide toll-free telephone number that individuals may use to report cases of water contamination which may be associated with the construction or operation of a commercial data center. The Statewide toll-free telephone number shall be provided in a conspicuous manner on the department's publicly accessible Internet website.(2) The department shall develop appropriate administrative responses to calls received on the Statewide toll-free telephone number for water contamination.(d) Publication of cases of contamination.--The department shall publish on the department's publicly accessible Internet website lists of confirmed cases of subterranean water supply contamination that result from the construction or operation of a commercial data center.(e) Facility operation qualifications.--The department shall ensure that a facility that seeks a National Pollutant Discharge 20260HB2760PN3868 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 Elimination System permit for the purposes of treating and discharging wastewater originating from a commercial data center into waters of this Commonwealth is operated by a competent and qualified individual.(f) Presumption.--Unless rebutted by a defense established under subsection (h), it shall be presumed that a commercial data center is responsible for pollution of a water supply if:(1) the water supply is within 1,000 feet of a commercial data center; and(2) the pollution occurred during the construction or operation of a commercial data center or alteration activities at a commercial data center.(g) Temporary water supply.--If the affected water supply is within the rebuttable presumption area as provided in subsection (f) and the rebuttable presumption applies, the operator of the commercial data center shall provide a temporary water supply if the water user is without a readily available alternative source of water. The temporary water supply provided under this subsection shall be adequate in quantity and quality for the purposes served by the supply.(h) Defenses.--To rebut the presumption established under subsection (f), the operator of a commercial data center must affirmatively prove at least one of the following:(1) the pollution existed prior to the construction of the commercial data center or alteration activity as determined by a preconstruction or prealteration survey;(2) the landowner or water purveyor refused to allow the operator of the commercial data center access to conduct a preconstruction or prealteration survey;(3) the water supply is not within 1,000 feet of the 20260HB2760PN3868 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 commercial data center; or(4) the pollution occurred as the result of a cause other than the construction or operation of a commercial data center or alteration activity.(i) Independent certified laboratory.--An operator of a commercial data center electing to preserve a defense under subsection (h) shall retain an independent certified laboratory to conduct a preconstruction or prealteration survey of the water supply. A copy of survey results shall be submitted to the department and the landowner or water purveyor in a manner prescribed by the department.(j) Notice.--An operator of a commercial data center must provide written notice to the landowner or water purveyor indicating that the presumption established under subsection (f) may be void if the landowner or water purveyor refused to allow the operator of the commercial data center access to conduct a preconstruction or prealteration survey. Proof of written notice to the landowner or water purveyor shall be provided to the department for the operator of the commercial data center to retain the protections under subsection (h)(2).(k) Other remedies preserved.--Nothing in this section shall prevent a landowner or water purveyor claiming pollution or diminution of a water supply from seeking any other remedy at law or in equity.Section 4.
