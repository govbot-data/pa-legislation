AN ACT
Providing for the protection of water supplies by requiring
commercial data centers to restore or replace a polluted or
diminutive water supply; and imposing duties on the
Department of Environmental Protection.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Commercial
Data Center Water Protection Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Commercial data center." A facility, campus of facilities
or array of interconnected facilities located in this
Commonwealth that meets all of the following:
(1) Is used by a business entity or other enterprise to
operate, manage or maintain a computer, group of computers or

(2) Has a peak demand of 25 megawatts or greater.
"Department." The Department of Environmental Protection of
the Commonwealth.
Section 3. Protection of water supplies.
(a) Requirement to restore or replace water supply.--
(1) A commercial data center that affects a public or
private water supply by pollution or diminution shall restore
or replace the affected supply with an alternate source of
water adequate in quantity or quality for the purposes served
by the supply.
(2) The department shall ensure that the quality of a
restored or replaced water supply meets the standards
established under the act of May 1, 1984 (P.L.206, No.43),
known as the Pennsylvania Safe Drinking Water Act, or is
comparable to the quality of the water supply before it was
affected by the commercial data center if the water supply
exceeded those standards.
(3) Paragraphs (1) and (2) shall be in addition to a
requirement imposed under subsection (g).
(4) The Environmental Quality Board shall promulgate
regulations necessary to meet the requirements of this
subsection.
(b) Pollution or diminution of water supply.--
(1) A landowner or water purveyor suffering pollution or
diminution of a water supply as a result of the construction
or operation of a commercial data center may notify the
department and request that an investigation be conducted.

(c) Reporting water contamination.--
(1) The department shall establish a single Statewide
toll-free telephone number that individuals may use to report
cases of water contamination which may be associated with the
construction or operation of a commercial data center. The
Statewide toll-free telephone number shall be provided in a
conspicuous manner on the department's publicly accessible
Internet website.
(2) The department shall develop appropriate
administrative responses to calls received on the Statewide
toll-free telephone number for water contamination.
(d) Publication of cases of contamination.--The department
shall publish on the department's publicly accessible Internet
website lists of confirmed cases of subterranean water supply
contamination that result from the construction or operation of
a commercial data center.

(f) Presumption.--Unless rebutted by a defense established
under subsection (h), it shall be presumed that a commercial
data center is responsible for pollution of a water supply if:
(1) the water supply is within 1,000 feet of a
commercial data center; and
(2) the pollution occurred during the construction or
operation of a commercial data center or alteration
activities at a commercial data center.
(g) Temporary water supply.--If the affected water supply is
within the rebuttable presumption area as provided in subsection
(f) and the rebuttable presumption applies, the operator of the
commercial data center shall provide a temporary water supply if
the water user is without a readily available alternative source
of water. The temporary water supply provided under this
subsection shall be adequate in quantity and quality for the
purposes served by the supply.
(h) Defenses.--To rebut the presumption established under
subsection (f), the operator of a commercial data center must
affirmatively prove at least one of the following:
(1) the pollution existed prior to the construction of
the commercial data center or alteration activity as
determined by a preconstruction or prealteration survey;
(2) the landowner or water purveyor refused to allow the
operator of the commercial data center access to conduct a
preconstruction or prealteration survey;
(3) the water supply is not within 1,000 feet of the

(i) Independent certified laboratory.--An operator of a
commercial data center electing to preserve a defense under
subsection (h) shall retain an independent certified laboratory
to conduct a preconstruction or prealteration survey of the
water supply. A copy of survey results shall be submitted to the
department and the landowner or water purveyor in a manner
prescribed by the department.
(j) Notice.--An operator of a commercial data center must
provide written notice to the landowner or water purveyor
indicating that the presumption established under subsection (f)
may be void if the landowner or water purveyor refused to allow
the operator of the commercial data center access to conduct a
preconstruction or prealteration survey. Proof of written notice
to the landowner or water purveyor shall be provided to the
department for the operator of the commercial data center to
retain the protections under subsection (h)(2).
(k) Other remedies preserved.--Nothing in this section shall
prevent a landowner or water purveyor claiming pollution or
diminution of a water supply from seeking any other remedy at
law or in equity.
Section 4. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 60 days.
