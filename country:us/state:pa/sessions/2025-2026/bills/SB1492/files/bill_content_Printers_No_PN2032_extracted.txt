WARD, STEFANO, KEEFER, FARRY AND VOGEL, OCTOBER 13, 2026 REFERRED TO STATE GOVERNMENT, OCTOBER 13, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of October 27, 1955 (P.L.744, No.222), entitled "An act prohibiting certain practices of discrimination because of race, color, religious creed, ancestry, age or national origin by employers, employment agencies, labor organizations and others as herein defined; creating the Pennsylvania Human Relations Commission in the Governor's Office; defining its functions, powers and duties; providing for procedure and enforcement; providing for formulation of an educational program to prevent prejudice; providing for judicial review and enforcement and imposing penalties," further providing for Pennsylvania Human Relations Commission.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 6 of the act of October 27, 1955 (P.L.744, No.222), known as the Pennsylvania Human Relations Act, is amended by adding a subsection to read:Section 6. Pennsylvania Human Relations Commission.--* * *(h) The administrative office of the Commission shall be headed by an executive director who must be appointed by a majority vote of the members of the Commission, with the advice and consent of two-thirds of the members elected to the Senate. In the performance of the functions as executive director and 1234567891011121314151617181920212223 the administration of the office, the executive director shall be directly responsible to the Commission. The functions of the executive director may not be delegated to any person not directly responsible, both with respect to program operation and administration, to the executive director. The executive director may not participate in the adjudication of any case before the Commission.Section 2. The individual serving as executive director of the Pennsylvania Human Relations Commission on the effective date of this section must receive advice and consent of two-thirds of the members elected to the Senate no later than six months after the effective date of this section. If the individual does not receive the advice and consent within that period, the individual may not continue to serve as executive director unless the advice and consent is obtained.Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260SB1492PN2032 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516
