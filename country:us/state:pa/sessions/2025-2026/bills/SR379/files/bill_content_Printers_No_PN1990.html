A RESOLUTION
Designating the week of September 21 through 25, 2026, as
"Safe2Say Something Week" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Act 44 of 2018 established the Safe2Say Something
Program in Pennsylvania; and
WHEREAS, The Safe2Say Something Program was created to be an
anonymous resource for students, school employees and community
members to report behavior perceived to be threatening to an
individual or school entity; and
WHEREAS, The Office of Attorney General partners with Sandy
Hook Promise to administer the Safe2Say Something Program; and
WHEREAS, The Office of Attorney General and Sandy Hook
Promise train individuals to do all of the following:
(1) recognize warning signs and signals from individuals
who may be a threat to themselves or others; and
(2) report concerns about individuals who may be a
threat to themselves or others through the Safe2Say Something
Program;

WHEREAS, On January 14, 2019, the Safe2Say Something Program
began accepting reports and continues to accept reports 24 hours
per day, seven days per week and 365 days per year; and
WHEREAS, In the 2025-2026 school year, the Office of Attorney
General and Sandy Hook Promise trained students on the Safe2Say
Something Program; and
WHEREAS, The Office of Attorney General's annual report on
the Safe2Say Something Program was released in August 2026; and
WHEREAS, The report stated that since the Safe2Say Something
Program began accepting tips in January 2019, 205,240 tips have
been received, with 26,240 received in the 2025-2026 school
year; and
WHEREAS, While initially intended to prevent threats against
schools in this Commonwealth, the Safe2Say Something Program has
highlighted a variety of behavior and mental health concerns in
students; and
WHEREAS, Bullying, cyberbullying, suicide or suicidal
ideation, cutting or self-harm and drug distribution and
possession were the top event types reported through the
Safe2Say Something Program in the 2025-2026 school year; and
WHEREAS, The Safe2Say Something Program is considered a
national model to protect students, teachers, school employees
and the community; and
WHEREAS, Data shows the continued success of the program as
established by Act 44 of 2018; and
WHEREAS, In the 2025-2026 school year, there was an 1%
increase in tips related to suicide or suicidal ideation and a
5% increase in tips related to cutting and self-harm,
highlighting the ongoing mental health struggles many students

WHEREAS, Safe2Say Something Program training was provided to
1,704 schools, reaching a total of 417,490 students; and
WHEREAS, Maintaining consistent student training and
awareness initiatives remains an important component to ensure
that students recognize warning signs and how to report concerns

WHEREAS, Continued emphasis should be placed on annual
student training and outreach to schools that have not recently
participated in training; and
WHEREAS, The Safe2Say Something Program has demonstrated in a
short period of time that the program serves a vital need in our
schools and our communities throughout this Commonwealth;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate designate the week of September 21
through 25, 2026, as "Safe2Say Something Week" in Pennsylvania.
