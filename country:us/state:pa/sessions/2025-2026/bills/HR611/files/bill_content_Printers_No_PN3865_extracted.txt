andWHEREAS, Each year firefighters respond to millions of emergency incidents across the United States and, in this Commonwealth, these dedicated men and women protect communities large and small through their response to structure fires, medical emergencies, vehicle accidents, rescue operations, hazardous materials situations, severe weather events and countless other threats that require skill, courage and unwavering public service, while routinely placing themselves in harm's way to safeguard their communities; andWHEREAS, September has become a meaningful period for 12345678910111213141516 acknowledging the contributions of the fire service, as fire departments and community organizations across the United States commonly hold public safety education events, remembrance ceremonies and volunteer recruitment efforts during this month; andWHEREAS, The choice of September reflects both the longstanding tradition of early autumn fire prevention initiatives and the importance of recognizing firefighters at the start of a season that historically presents increased fire risk and heightened emergency response demands; andWHEREAS, The brave men and women who serve as firefighters deserve to be formally honored for their continuous readiness to respond to danger, for their professionalism in confronting hazardous situations and for the sacrifices they make in order to protect the people and property of our nation and this Commonwealth;
