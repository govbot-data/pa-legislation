A RESOLUTION
Designating the month of September 2026 as "Firefighter
Appreciation Month" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Firefighters in this Commonwealth carry out
essential duties that protect the lives, property and well-being
of residents during fires, emergencies, natural disasters and
hazardous incidents; and
WHEREAS, Each year firefighters respond to millions of
emergency incidents across the United States and, in this
Commonwealth, these dedicated men and women protect communities
large and small through their response to structure fires,
medical emergencies, vehicle accidents, rescue operations,
hazardous materials situations, severe weather events and
countless other threats that require skill, courage and
unwavering public service, while routinely placing themselves in
harm's way to safeguard their communities; and
WHEREAS, September has become a meaningful period for

acknowledging the contributions of the fire service, as fire
departments and community organizations across the United States
commonly hold public safety education events, remembrance
ceremonies and volunteer recruitment efforts during this month;
and
WHEREAS, The choice of September reflects both the
longstanding tradition of early autumn fire prevention
initiatives and the importance of recognizing firefighters at
the start of a season that historically presents increased fire
risk and heightened emergency response demands; and
WHEREAS, The brave men and women who serve as firefighters
deserve to be formally honored for their continuous readiness to
respond to danger, for their professionalism in confronting
hazardous situations and for the sacrifices they make in order
to protect the people and property of our nation and this
Commonwealth; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the
month of September 2026 as "Firefighter Appreciation Month" in
Pennsylvania; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives encourage all
residents of this Commonwealth to recognize and thank
firefighters for their service and to participate in local
events that honor these individuals and promote community
safety.
