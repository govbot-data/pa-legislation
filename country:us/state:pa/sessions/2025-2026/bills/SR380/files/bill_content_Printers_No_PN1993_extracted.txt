WARD AND VOGEL, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the week of September 21 through 27, 2026, as "See Tracks? Think Train! Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Rail travel is an essential part of our transportation infrastructure, reducing congestion on the roads and in the skies, connecting cities and rural communities; andWHEREAS, Projections of the United States Department of Transportation show substantial increases in rail transport over the next three decades; andWHEREAS, As advanced technology helps build quieter and faster trains, the necessity to teach people how to be safe around them increases; andWHEREAS, In the United States, a person or vehicle is hit by a train approximately every three hours; andWHEREAS, While crossing collisions have been reduced over the past four decades, there were still 2,273 highway-rail grade crossing collisions in the United States, resulting in 286 fatalities and 766 injuries in 2025; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, Pennsylvania ranks 11th in highway-rail grade crossing collisions, with 56 collisions, 3 fatalities and 24 injuries in 2025; andWHEREAS, Railroads are private property, and accessing them outside of a designated crossing area is considered trespassing; andWHEREAS, Trespassing is a serious safety issue, resulting in 1,317 deaths and injuries in the United States in 2025; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania ranks sixth for total trespass casualties, with 34 deaths and 15 injuries in 2025; andWHEREAS, Collisions between trains and motor vehicles or pedestrians could have been prevented by increased public awareness of the necessity of making safe choices around railroad tracks and trains; andWHEREAS, Operation Lifesaver is a nonprofit public safety education and awareness organization dedicated to reducing collisions, fatalities and injuries at highway-rail crossings and trespassing on or near railroad tracks; andWHEREAS, Operation Lifesaver's nationwide campaign, called "See Tracks? Think Train! Week," runs from September 21 through 27, 2026; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate designate the week of September 21 through 27, 2026, as "See Tracks? Think Train!
