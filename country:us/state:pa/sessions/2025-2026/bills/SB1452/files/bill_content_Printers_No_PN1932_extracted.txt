2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1452 PN 1932 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1932 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1452 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY COLLETT, KIM, KEARNEY, CAPPELLETTI, COSTA, SCHWANK, KANE AND STREET, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO JUDICIARY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in abuse of office, providing for the offense of interference with a criminal investigation by a government official.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 5304. Interference with a criminal investigation by a government official.(a) Offense defined.--A government official commits an offense if the government official, during a criminal investigation, does any of the following:(1) intentionally obstructs, impairs, prevents or attempts to prevent by force, violence, physical interference, deceit, breach of official duty or any other unlawful act, a law enforcement officer from accessing, locating, securing, reviewing, copying, testing or preserving evidence;12345678910111213141516171819 (2) provides, causes to be provided or directs another person to provide a materially false, altered or intentionally misleading record, document, recording, data file, electronically stored information or other thing with the intent to obstruct, impair or prevent a law enforcement officer's criminal investigation; or(3) orders, directs or knowingly allows another government official to commit an act in paragraph (1) or (2).(b) Grading.--An offense under this section constitutes a felony of the third degree.(c) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Government official." A person employed by the Federal Government, the Commonwealth or a local government.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260SB1452PN1932 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516
