A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the cultural, historical and religious significance
of Armenia and the numerous contributions of Armenian-
Americans residing in this Commonwealth.
WHEREAS, Armenia is among the world's oldest continuously
inhabited civilizations, with a recorded history extending for
thousands of years and a legacy of language, art and religion;
and
WHEREAS, The Armenian people have made vital contributions to
human achievement, including the creation of a distinct alphabet
in the fifth century, literary, musical, artistic, scientific
and civic accomplishments and the preservation of their unique
culture throughout a global Armenian diaspora; and
WHEREAS, Armenia was the first nation to adopt Christianity
as its state religion, and the Armenian Apostolic Church created
the basis for spiritual, cultural and communal contributions as
an institution for Armenians throughout the world; and
WHEREAS, The establishment of Christianity in Armenia is
regarded as one of the most significant events in their history,
ultimately shaping the people's religious identity and helping

WHEREAS, Armenian religious heritage includes ancient

face of extreme adversity and vast cultural achievements; and
WHEREAS, The Armenian people have demonstrated extraordinary
perseverance in preserving their culture, faith, language and
identity despite hundreds of years of foreign domination,
violence and oppressive persecution; and
WHEREAS, The Armenian Genocide of 1915-1923 resulted in the
deaths of 1.5 million Armenians in the Ottoman Empire,
devastating Armenian communities and contributing to the
formation and growth of the Armenian emigration throughout the
world; and
WHEREAS, The Armenian Genocide was the first instance in
history of the term genocide being used, to describe the actions
taken by the Ottoman Empire to attempt to intentionally wipe out
one of the world's oldest cultures and its people from the
earth; and
WHEREAS, Armenian-American communities have enriched the
civic, economic, educational, cultural, charitable, scientific,
military, artistic and religious life of communities throughout
the United States and this Commonwealth; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the cultural, historical
and religious significance of Armenia and the numerous
contributions of Armenian-Americans residing in this
Commonwealth; and be it further
