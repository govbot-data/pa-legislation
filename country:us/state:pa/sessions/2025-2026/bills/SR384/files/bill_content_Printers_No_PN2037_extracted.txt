andWHEREAS, Armenia was the first nation to adopt Christianity as its state religion, and the Armenian Apostolic Church created the basis for spiritual, cultural and communal contributions as an institution for Armenians throughout the world; andWHEREAS, The Armenian Genocide of 1915-1923 resulted in the deaths of 1.5 million Armenians in the Ottoman Empire, devastating Armenian communities and contributing to the formation and growth of the Armenian emigration throughout the world;
