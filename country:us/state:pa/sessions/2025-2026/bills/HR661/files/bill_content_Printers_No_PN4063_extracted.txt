2025-2026 Regular Session HR 661 PN 4063 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 4063 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.661 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY WEBSTER, GUENST, WAXMAN, STEELE, MAYES, RIVERA AND SCOTT, OCTOBER 19, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, OCTOBER 19, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDirecting the Joint State Government Commission to conduct a study on the feasibility of implementing automated transit systems in this Commonwealth and issue a report.WHEREAS, Public transportation plays a vital role in the lives of many Pennsylvanians; andWHEREAS, The importance of public transportation warrants further research into methods of expanding, modernizing and improving public transportation systems; andWHEREAS, An automated transit system (ATS) is an organized network of coordinated passenger vehicles that can operate without an onboard driver, in part through the use of dedicated infrastructure that separates the vehicles from other modes of transportation; andWHEREAS, The concept of ATS has existed since the 1960s and has been implemented at varying scales throughout the world; andWHEREAS, ATS has been used to accommodate passenger demand that exceeds the capacity of other modes of transportation and provide service according to a consistent, automated schedule; 123456789101112131415161718 andWHEREAS, An ATS may offer a cost advantage over a subway system, particularly in a smaller city or suburban community, because an ATS may require less extensive infrastructure; andWHEREAS, The International Electrotechnical Commission classifies levels of urban rail automation from GoA0 through GoA4 based on the extent to which automated systems perform functions relating to starting and stopping a train, closing doors, detecting obstacles and collisions, operating during a disruption and evacuating passengers; andWHEREAS, An ATS operating at GoA4 is fully automated, does not require permanent staff on board during normal operation and allows personnel to monitor operations and manage emergencies remotely; andWHEREAS, Well-known examples of ATS operating at GoA4 include the Vancouver SkyTrain, Honolulu Skyline, Dubai Metro, Kobe New Transit Port Island Line, Copenhagen Metro and Line 4 of the São Paulo Metro; andWHEREAS, Pittsburgh International Airport operated an automated underground people mover until 2025; andWHEREAS, Implementing an ATS could expand access to public transportation within a metropolitan area and generate economic benefits for surrounding suburban communities; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth could benefit from examining the feasibility of expanding its public transportation infrastructure to include an ATS; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives direct the Joint State Government Commission to conduct a study on the feasibility of implementing automated transit systems in this Commonwealth and issue a report;
