Col. Palmer Brown Memorial Bridge.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Lt. Col. Palmer Brown Memorial Bridge.(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) Palmer Brown was born June 24, 1937, in Duncansville, Pennsylvania.(2) Lt. Col. Brown was a 1955 graduate of Hollidaysburg High School.(3) Outside of work, Lt. Col. Brown enjoyed spending time with family and was an ordained Southern Baptist minister who was instrumental in starting and building churches in Pearl City, Hawaii, and the Pumpkin Hill section of Laurel, Maryland.(4) Lt. Col. Brown also served as a Blair Township 123456789101112131415161718 Supervisor, was active in his church and an advisor to the Cross Team Ministries in Williamsburg, Pennsylvania.(5) Lt. Col. Brown served a 35-year career with the United States Marine Corps, rising from the rank of private to lieutenant colonel when he retired in 1990.(6) Lt. Col. Brown received various honors for his service, including the Meritorious Service Medal, Joint Service Commendation Medal, Navy Unit Commendation, Presidential Unit Citation (2nd Award), Navy Commendation Medal, Meritorious Unit Commendation (3rd Award), Good Conduct Medal (2nd Awards), National Defense Service Medal, Republic of Vietnam Meritorious Unit Citation (Gallantry Cross Color With Palm), Vietnam Service Medal (6th Award), Sea Service Deployment Ribbon, Republic of Vietnam Meritorious Unit Citation (Civil Action Medal First Class Color With Palm), Naval Security Group Meritorious Service Award, Letter of Appreciation, Republic of Vietnam Campaign Medal, Rifle Marksman badge (2nd Award) and Pistol Export badge (7th Award).(7) Lt. Col. Brown passed away on December 14, 2025, in Altoona, Pennsylvania.(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key 70551, carrying State Route 3013 over Blair Gap Run in Allegheny Township, Blair County, is designated as the Lt. Col. Palmer Brown Memorial Bridge.(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to traffic in both directions on the bridge.Section 2.
