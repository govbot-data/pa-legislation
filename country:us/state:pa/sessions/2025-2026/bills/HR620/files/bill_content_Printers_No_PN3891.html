A RESOLUTION
Urging the Congress of the United States to adopt legislation
providing screening to firefighters for sarcoidosis and other
occupational diseases linked to chronic exposure to smoke,
soot, PFAS, combustion byproducts, particulate matter and
other fireground toxins and ensuring that our nation's
firefighters receive comprehensive early detection for
conditions that carry significant risks of long-term lung
damage and other ailments, which require timely diagnoses for
effective intervention.
WHEREAS, Thousands of firefighters across the United States,
including many across Pennsylvania, are routinely exposed to
smoke, soot, PFAS, combustion byproducts, particulates and other
hazardous fireground toxins that are strongly linked to
sarcoidosis and other related inflammatory conditions; and
WHEREAS, Firefighters who develop sarcoidosis and other
related occupational diseases face significantly elevated risks
of chronic lung inflammation, permanent organ damage,
misdiagnosis due to symptom overlap with cancer and delayed
treatment, demonstrating a critical gap in early detection and
comprehensive health screening; and

WHEREAS, Sarcoidosis and related inflammatory conditions

byproducts and toxic particulates, which often presents with
symptoms that mimic cancer, and is frequently detected

WHEREAS, Multiple stakeholders have emphasized the urgent
need for legislation, highlighting the growing prevalence of
sarcoidosis and other related occupational diseases among
firefighters; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania is home to a large community of career
and volunteer firefighters who respond to thousands of
emergencies each year across Pennsylvania, exemplifying the
longstanding history of public service in Pennsylvania and
underscoring the Statewide need for comprehensive health
protections, including enhanced screening for sarcoidosis and
other related inflammatory conditions; and
WHEREAS, Screening for sarcoidosis and other related
occupational diseases helps firefighters by enabling earlier
