Change of operator license.An entering operator shall obtain a change of operator license from the department prior to taking over the operations of a long-term care nursing facility when a change of ownership or control of the long-term care nursing facility occurs. Each of the following requirements must be satisfied before the department issues a change of operator license from an exiting operator to an entering operator:(1) The entering operator shall complete a change of operator license application on a form prescribed by the department and pay the applicable fee as determined by the department.
