AN ACT
Providing for change of operator licenses for long-term care
nursing facilities; and imposing penalties.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Long-Term
Care Nursing Facility Transfer of Operations Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Department." The Department of Health of the Commonwealth.
"Entering operator." The person that takes over the daily
operations and management of a long-term care nursing facility
when change of ownership or control occurs.

license from the department prior to taking over the operations
of a long-term care nursing facility when a change of ownership
or control of the long-term care nursing facility occurs. Each

department issues a change of operator license from an exiting
operator to an entering operator:
(1) The entering operator shall complete a change of
operator license application on a form prescribed by the
department and pay the applicable fee as determined by the
department. The completed application shall be submitted no
later than 45 days before the proposed effective date of the
change of operator if the change of operator does not entail

(e) Appeal.--An entering operator may appeal the denial of a
change of operator license application in accordance with 2
Pa.C.S. (relating to administrative law and procedure).
(f) Duties of entering operator.--
(1) An entering operator shall:
(i) Notify the department immediately upon discovery
of any error, omission or change of information in a
change of operator license application.
(ii) Notify the department within 30 days of any
change in the information or documentation required by
this act that occurs after the effective date of the
change of operator.
(iii) Truthfully supply any additional information
or documentation requested by the department.
(iv) Not complete the change of operator until the
department issues to the entering operator notice of
intent to grant a change of operator license in
accordance with subsection (a). The entering operator
shall submit a final document indicating completion of a
change of operator transaction within five days of the
transaction.
(2) An entering operator that fails to notify the
department or supply additional information or documentation
in accordance with the requirements of this section shall be
subject to a civil penalty of $2,000. The penalty shall be
collected by the department and the proceeds shall be
deposited into the General Fund.

This act shall take effect in 60 days.
