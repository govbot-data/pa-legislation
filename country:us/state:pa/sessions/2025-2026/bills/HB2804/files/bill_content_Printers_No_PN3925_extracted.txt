Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 5532.1. Animal in unattended motor vehicle.(a) Offense defined.--A person commits an offense if the person intentionally, recklessly or negligently leaves or confines an animal in an unattended motor vehicle under conditions that:(1) endanger the health or well-being of the animal due to heat, cold, lack of adequate ventilation or lack of food or water; or(2) could reasonably be expected to cause suffering, disability or death to the animal.123456789101112131415161718 (b) Penalties.--(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), a person convicted of violating subsection (a) shall be sentenced as follows:(i) For a first violation, the person shall be sentenced to pay a fine of not more than $100 per animal.(ii) For a second or subsequent violation, the person shall be sentenced to pay a fine of not more than $500 per animal or imprisonment for not more than six months, or both.(2) If a person is convicted of violating subsection (a) and the animal has suffered serious bodily injury as a result of the violation, the person shall be sentenced, for a first conviction, to pay a fine of not more than $500 per animal or imprisonment for not more than six months, or both.(3) If a person was sentenced in accordance with paragraph (2) and subsequently is convicted of violating subsection (a), the person shall be sentenced, for a second or subsequent conviction, to pay a fine of not more than $500 per animal or imprisonment for not more than six months, or both, regardless of injury to the animal caused by the second or subsequent violation.(c) Construction.--Nothing in this section shall be construed to prevent:(1) A person from taking reasonable and necessary steps to remove an animal from a motor vehicle if the person reasonably believes that the animal's safety is in immediate danger from heat, cold, lack of adequate ventilation, lack of food or water or other conditions that could reasonably be expected to cause suffering, disability or death to the 20260HB2804PN3925 -
