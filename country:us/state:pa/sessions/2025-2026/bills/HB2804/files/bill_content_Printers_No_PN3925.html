AN ACT
Amending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in riot, disorderly conduct and
related offenses, providing for the offense of animal in
unattended motor vehicle; and imposing penalties.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 5532.1. Animal in unattended motor vehicle.
(a) Offense defined.--A person commits an offense if the
person intentionally, recklessly or negligently leaves or
confines an animal in an unattended motor vehicle under
conditions that:
(1) endanger the health or well-being of the animal due
to heat, cold, lack of adequate ventilation or lack of food
or water; or
(2) could reasonably be expected to cause suffering,
disability or death to the animal.

(b) Penalties.--
(1) Subject to paragraphs (2) and (3), a person
convicted of violating subsection (a) shall be sentenced as
follows:
(i) For a first violation, the person shall be
sentenced to pay a fine of not more than $100 per animal.
(ii) For a second or subsequent violation, the
person shall be sentenced to pay a fine of not more than
$500 per animal or imprisonment for not more than six
months, or both.
(2) If a person is convicted of violating subsection (a)
and the animal has suffered serious bodily injury as a result
of the violation, the person shall be sentenced, for a first
conviction, to pay a fine of not more than $500 per animal or
imprisonment for not more than six months, or both.
(3) If a person was sentenced in accordance with
paragraph (2) and subsequently is convicted of violating
subsection (a), the person shall be sentenced, for a second
or subsequent conviction, to pay a fine of not more than $500
per animal or imprisonment for not more than six months, or
both, regardless of injury to the animal caused by the second
or subsequent violation.
(c) Construction.--Nothing in this section shall be
construed to prevent:
(1) A person from taking reasonable and necessary steps
to remove an animal from a motor vehicle if the person
reasonably believes that the animal's safety is in immediate
danger from heat, cold, lack of adequate ventilation, lack of
food or water or other conditions that could reasonably be
expected to cause suffering, disability or death to the

(2) A rescue official from removing an animal from a

(d) Authorization.--A rescue official is authorized to take
all steps that are reasonably necessary for the removal of an
animal from a motor vehicle, including breaking into the motor
vehicle, after a reasonable effort is made to locate the owner
of the motor vehicle or other person responsible for leaving or
confining the animal in the unattended motor vehicle.
(e) Liability.--A person who removes an animal from a motor
vehicle in accordance with subsection (c)(1) is not criminally
liable for actions taken reasonably and in good faith if all of
the following conditions are met:
(1) The person determines that the motor vehicle is
locked and another reasonable manner to remove the animal
from the locked motor vehicle does not exist.
(2) The person h as a good faith belief that forcible
entry into the motor vehicle is necessary because the animal
is in imminent danger of suffering harm if it is not
immediately removed from the motor vehicle, and, based upon
the circumstances known to the person at the time, the belief
is a reasonable one.
(3) The person has contacted a rescue official or the
911 emergency service prior to forcibly entering the motor
vehicle.
(4) After removal of the animal from the motor vehicle,

(f) Actions by rescue official.--A rescue official who
removes an animal from a motor vehicle in accordance with this

(1) T ake the animal to an animal shelter or other place

office of the rescue official and the address of the location
where the animal can be claimed.
(g) Costs.--The owner of the animal removed from the motor
vehicle under this section:
(1) May be required to pay the costs accrued for the

animal.
(2) May not claim the animal until after the payment of

(i) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
