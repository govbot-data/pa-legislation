andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth and its residents have a growing need and demand for reliable electricity generation and natural gas supply; andWHEREAS, As policymakers prepare for this new era of energy consumption, this Commonwealth's conventional oil and natural gas industry merits renewed examination as a strategic component of this Commonwealth's long-term energy future; andWHEREAS, While public focus has largely centered on large-scale unconventional shale development, this Commonwealth's conventional oil and natural gas industry continues to produce 12345678910111213141516171819 reliable domestic energy, support rural electric and gas infrastructure, sustain thousands of small businesses and working-class jobs and maintain legacy well systems and gathering networks; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth's conventional oil and natural gas industry supplies specialty Pennsylvania grade crude oil that is essential to refining and manufacturing, contributes to local economies across rural parts of this Commonwealth and maintains infrastructure and production systems in regions where energy resiliency and localized supply continue to matter; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth's conventional oil and natural gas production is strategically located near established energy markets and infrastructure, and the availability of in-State refining capacity for locally and regionally produced crude oil strengthens this Commonwealth's energy security by preserving a domestic supply chain from production through refining and end use; andWHEREAS, As electricity demand rises, the Commonwealth must carefully evaluate every available domestic energy asset and determine whether existing policies properly recognize the continuing role of the conventional industry in supporting energy diversity, resiliency, affordability and rural economic stability; andWHEREAS, This Commonwealth's conventional natural gas production contributes to local and regional energy reliability by supplying fuel for electricity generation, industrial manufacturing, residential heating and commercial energy needs, particularly in rural areas served by existing, gathering and distribution infrastructure;
