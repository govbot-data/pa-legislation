A RESOLUTION
Directing the Joint State Government Commission to study and
issue findings and recommendations on the strategic
importance of this Commonwealth's conventional oil and
natural gas industry in resolving the growing demand for
energy in this Commonwealth.
WHEREAS, This Commonwealth stands at the threshold of a major
transformation in energy demand driven by the rapid expansion of
electricity use, advanced manufacturing, cloud computing
infrastructure and domestic industrial reshoring; and
WHEREAS, This Commonwealth and its residents have a growing
need and demand for reliable electricity generation and natural
gas supply; and
WHEREAS, As policymakers prepare for this new era of energy
consumption, this Commonwealth's conventional oil and natural
gas industry merits renewed examination as a strategic component
of this Commonwealth's long-term energy future; and
WHEREAS, While public focus has largely centered on large-
scale unconventional shale development, this Commonwealth's
conventional oil and natural gas industry continues to produce

WHEREAS, This Commonwealth's conventional oil and natural gas

economies across rural parts of this Commonwealth and maintains

WHEREAS, This Commonwealth's conventional oil and natural gas
production is strategically located near established energy
markets and infrastructure, and the availability of in-State
refining capacity for locally and regionally produced crude oil
strengthens this Commonwealth's energy security by preserving a
domestic supply chain from production through refining and end
use; and
WHEREAS, As electricity demand rises, the Commonwealth must
carefully evaluate every available domestic energy asset and
determine whether existing policies properly recognize the
continuing role of the conventional industry in supporting
energy diversity, resiliency, affordability and rural economic
stability; and
WHEREAS, This Commonwealth's conventional natural gas
production contributes to local and regional energy reliability
by supplying fuel for electricity generation, industrial
manufacturing, residential heating and commercial energy needs,
particularly in rural areas served by existing, gathering and
distribution infrastructure; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That the study include:
(1) An examination of the current and projected economic
contribution of this Commonwealth's conventional oil and
natural gas industry.
(2) An examination of the oil and natural gas industry's
role in addressing future electricity generation demand.
(3) A review of the impact of statutory and regulatory
policy on small conventional operators.
(4) An examination of preserving domestic energy
production capacity in this Commonwealth.
(5) An examination of the energy security and supply
chain benefits associated with the proximity of conventional
oil and natural gas production done in this Commonwealth to
regional energy markets, transportation infrastructure,
manufacturing facilities and in-State refining capacity.
(6) A review of differences between conventional and
unconventional operations.
(7) An examination of rural workforce and economic
implications associated with oil and natural gas industry
decline.
(8) An examination of the energy security, reliability
and economic benefits associated with this Commonwealth's
conventional natural gas production, gathering systems,
storage facilities, distribution networks and contribution to
electricity generation and industrial demand.
