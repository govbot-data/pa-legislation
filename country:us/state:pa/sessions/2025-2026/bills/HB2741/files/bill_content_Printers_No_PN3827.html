AN ACT
Amending the act of November 21, 1990 (P.L.539, No.133),
entitled "An act prohibiting certain conduct with respect to
caves; and imposing penalties," further providing for
penalties.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 6 of the act of November 21, 1990
(P.L.539, No.133), known as the Cave Protection Act, is amended
by adding a subsection to read:
Section 6. Penalties.
* * *
(c) Community service.--In addition to other penalties
imposed under this section, a court of competent jurisdiction
may require a person convicted of a violation of this act to
pick up litter or illegally dumped trash.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
