AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in size, weight and load, further providing for
authority to issue permits; and abrogating regulations.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 4961 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a subsection to read:
§ 4961. Authority to issue permits.
* * *
(a.1) Carrying special permits in paper or electronic
form.--
(1) The driver shall possess the special permit and any
other documentation as prescribed in the department's
regulations in the permitted vehicle and shall present the
documents for inspection by a police officer or qualified
department employee. The driver may choose whether to present
the documents in paper or electronic form.
(2) If a driver chooses to present, on an interactive
mobile device, an electronic copy of the special permit and
