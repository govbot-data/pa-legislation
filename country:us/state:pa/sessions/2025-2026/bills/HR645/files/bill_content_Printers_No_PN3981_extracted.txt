2025-2026 Regular Session HR 645 PN 3981 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3981 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.645 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY DALEY, HILL-EVANS, McNEILL, HOHENSTEIN, GUZMAN, HANBIDGE, PROBST, DUKES AND MADDEN, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON STATE GOVERNMENT, OCTOBER 1, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating October 20, 2026, as "Domestic Violence Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania, in honor of the 50th anniversary of the Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence.WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence was founded in 1976 and is the oldest Statewide domestic violence coalition in the nation; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence annually serves 90,000 victims and survivors in all 67 counties, reaching more than 3 million since 1976; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence, known for grassroots advocacy and organizing, began at Susan Kelly-Dreiss' kitchen table in her home; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence has led efforts in establishing or expanding the Family Violence Prevention and Services Act, the National Resource Center on Domestic Violence, the Protection from Abuse Act and the Violence Against Women Act; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence 123456789101112131415161718 has launched national initiatives for data collection surrounding lethality prevention, while creating the Lethality Assessment Program to assist police officers in responding to intimate partner violence; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence has continued to advocate for victims and survivors for the last 50 years and plans to continue advocating on behalf of the same victims and survivors for many years to come; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence, with the support from the Commonwealth, has expanded to much more than grassroots advocacy, but continues work in housing, prevention, systems advocacy and civil legal representation; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence echoes the nationwide domestic violence movement, demonstrating that violence cannot be separated from the broader systems that make people vulnerable; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate October 20, 2026, as "Domestic Violence Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania, in honor of the 50th anniversary of the Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence;
