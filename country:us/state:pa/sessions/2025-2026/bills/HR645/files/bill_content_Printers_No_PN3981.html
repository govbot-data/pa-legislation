A RESOLUTION
Designating October 20, 2026, as "Domestic Violence Awareness
Day" in Pennsylvania, in honor of the 50th anniversary of the
Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence.
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence
was founded in 1976 and is the oldest Statewide domestic
violence coalition in the nation; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence
annually serves 90,000 victims and survivors in all 67 counties,
reaching more than 3 million since 1976; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic
Violence, known for grassroots advocacy and organizing, began at
Susan Kelly-Dreiss' kitchen table in her home; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence
has led efforts in establishing or expanding the Family Violence
Prevention and Services Act, the National Resource Center on
Domestic Violence, the Protection from Abuse Act and the
Violence Against Women Act; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence

has launched national initiatives for data collection
surrounding lethality prevention, while creating the Lethality

WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence
has continued to advocate for victims and survivors for the last
50 years and plans to continue advocating on behalf of the same
victims and survivors for many years to come; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic
Violence, with the support from the Commonwealth, has expanded
to much more than grassroots advocacy, but continues work in
housing, prevention, systems advocacy and civil legal
representation; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence
echoes the nationwide domestic violence movement, demonstrating
that violence cannot be separated from the broader systems that
make people vulnerable; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate October
20, 2026, as "Domestic Violence Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania,
in honor of the 50th anniversary of the Pennsylvania Coalition
Against Domestic Violence; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the
Pennsylvania Coalition Against Domestic Violence for its ongoing
support for victims and survivors of domestic violence in this
Commonwealth.
