Jadlowiec, of Englewood, Florida, who died August 13, 2024, at 73 years of age.WHEREAS, The House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania commends the distinguished service and lasting achievements of Kenneth M. Jadlowiec, an eight-term member of the General Assembly; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania notes that Mr. Jadlowiec consistently directed his insights and efforts for the benefit of others; andWHEREAS, Mr. Jadlowiec faithfully represented the 67th District from 1987 through 2002; andWHEREAS, The 67th District included Cameron, Clearfield and McKean Counties through 1992 and then included Cameron, McKean and Potter Counties; andWHEREAS, During the 1989-1990 Session, Mr. Jadlowiec was appointed to the select committee established to study the need and availability of funding for domestic violence and rape 123456789101112131415161718 crisis services in this Commonwealth pursuant to House Resolution 229, Printer's No. 2787 (1989); andWHEREAS, Born January 12, 1951, the son of Edward and Stella (Przygucki) Jadlowiec, Mr. Jadlowiec grew up in the North Hills area of Pittsburgh; andWHEREAS, Mr. Jadlowiec was a 1968 graduate of North Hills High School and 1971 graduate of Edinboro University, now PennWest Edinboro, where he studied political science; andWHEREAS, Before his November 1986 election to the General Assembly, Mr. Jadlowiec worked for Beneficial Finance and James R. Evans Clothing and served as a district justice; andWHEREAS, Mr. Jadlowiec provided leadership as a former District President for the Special Court Judges Association of Pennsylvania, District No. 2; andWHEREAS, Mr. Jadlowiec and his wife, Denise E. (Mackowski) Jadlowiec, raised their sons, Kenneth and Brian, in Bradford, McKean County; andWHEREAS, The Jadlowiecs attended St. Bernard Church in Bradford; andWHEREAS, Mr. Jadlowiec was an emeritus member of Pitt-Bradford Advisory Board at the University of Pittsburgh at Bradford, first elected in 1987; andWHEREAS, The Ken Jadlowiec Fitness Center at Pitt-Bradford was named in Mr. Jadlowiec's honor in 2003; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania joins in expressing sincere condolences to Mr. Jadlowiec's family; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania remember former House Member Kenneth M.
