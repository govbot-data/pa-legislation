A RESOLUTION
Remembering former House Member Kenneth M. Jadlowiec, of
Englewood, Florida, who died August 13, 2024, at 73 years of
age.
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of
Pennsylvania commends the distinguished service and lasting
achievements of Kenneth M. Jadlowiec, an eight-term member of
the General Assembly; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of
Pennsylvania notes that Mr. Jadlowiec consistently directed his
insights and efforts for the benefit of others; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Jadlowiec faithfully represented the 67th
District from 1987 through 2002; and
WHEREAS, The 67th District included Cameron, Clearfield and
McKean Counties through 1992 and then included Cameron, McKean
and Potter Counties; and
WHEREAS, During the 1989-1990 Session, Mr. Jadlowiec was
appointed to the select committee established to study the need
and availability of funding for domestic violence and rape

WHEREAS, Born January 12, 1951, the son of Edward and Stella
(Przygucki) Jadlowiec, Mr. Jadlowiec grew up in the North Hills
area of Pittsburgh; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Jadlowiec was a 1968 graduate of North Hills
High School and 1971 graduate of Edinboro University, now
PennWest Edinboro, where he studied political science; and
WHEREAS, Before his November 1986 election to the General
Assembly, Mr. Jadlowiec worked for Beneficial Finance and James
R. Evans Clothing and served as a district justice; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Jadlowiec provided leadership as a former
District President for the Special Court Judges Association of
Pennsylvania, District No. 2; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Jadlowiec and his wife, Denise E. (Mackowski)
Jadlowiec, raised their sons, Kenneth and Brian, in Bradford,
McKean County; and
WHEREAS, The Jadlowiecs attended St. Bernard Church in
Bradford; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Jadlowiec was an emeritus member of Pitt-
Bradford Advisory Board at the University of Pittsburgh at
Bradford, first elected in 1987; and
WHEREAS, The Ken Jadlowiec Fitness Center at Pitt-Bradford
was named in Mr. Jadlowiec's honor in 2003; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of
Pennsylvania joins in expressing sincere condolences to Mr.
Jadlowiec's family; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania remember former House Member
Kenneth M. Jadlowiec of Englewood, Florida, who died August 13,
