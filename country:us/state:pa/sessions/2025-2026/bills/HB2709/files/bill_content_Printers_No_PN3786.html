AN ACT
Amending Title 12 (Commerce and Trade) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in entertainment economic enhancement
program, further providing for limitations.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3205(a)(1) of Title 12 of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:
§ 3205. Limitations.
(a) Cap.--
(1) The aggregate amount of tax credits awarded in a
fiscal year under this chapter may not exceed [$24,000,000]
$26,000,000.
* * *
Section 2. The amendment of 12 Pa.C.S. § 3205(a)(1) shall
apply to fiscal years beginning on or after July 1, 2026.
Section 3. This act shall take effect immediately.
