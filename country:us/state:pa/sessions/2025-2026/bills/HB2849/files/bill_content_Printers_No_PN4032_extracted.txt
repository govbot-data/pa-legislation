2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2849 PN 4032 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 4032 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2849 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KHAN, ARMANINI, CONKLIN, PROBST, BRENNAN, OLSOMMER AND MERSKI, OCTOBER 9, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, OCTOBER 9, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in liquid fuels and fuels tax, further providing for definitions and for imposition of tax, exemptions and deductions.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The definition of "alternative fuels" in section 9002 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended and the section is amended by adding a definition to read:§ 9002. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this chapter shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Alternative fuels." Natural gas, compressed natural gas (CNG), liquified natural gas (LNG), liquid propane gas and liquified petroleum gas (LPG), alcohols, gasoline-alcohol mixtures containing at least 85% alcohol by volume, hydrogen, hythane, electricity, biomass product and any other fuel used to 12345678910111213141516171819 propel motor vehicles on the public highways which is not taxable as fuels or liquid fuels under this chapter.* * *"Biomass product." A fuel that:(1) is derived from renewable biomass sources, including plant materials and agricultural waste;(2) is created through a process that synthesizes methanol; and(3) is capable of being blended or mixed with liquid fuels to propel motor vehicles.* * *Section 2. Section 9004(d)(1) of Title 75 is amended to read:§ 9004. Imposition of tax, exemptions and deductions.* * *(d) Alternative fuels tax.--(1) A tax is hereby imposed upon alternative fuels used to propel vehicles of any kind or character on the public highways. The rate of tax applicable to each alternative fuel shall be computed by the department on a gallon equivalent basis and shall be published as necessary by notice in the Pennsylvania Bulletin[.], provided that the rate of tax applicable to biomass product shall be equal to the rate of tax applicable to methanol.* * *Section 3. This act shall apply to alternative fuels sold on or after the effective date of this section.Section 4.
