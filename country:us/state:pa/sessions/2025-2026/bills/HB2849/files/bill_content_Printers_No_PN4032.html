AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in liquid fuels and fuels tax, further providing
for definitions and for imposition of tax, exemptions and
deductions.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The definition of "alternative fuels" in section
9002 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is
amended and the section is amended by adding a definition to
read:
§ 9002. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Alternative fuels." Natural gas, compressed natural gas
(CNG), liquified natural gas (LNG), liquid propane gas and
liquified petroleum gas (LPG), alcohols, gasoline-alcohol
mixtures containing at least 85% alcohol by volume, hydrogen,
hythane, electricity, biomass product and any other fuel used to

propel motor vehicles on the public highways which is not

"Biomass product." A fuel that:
(1) is derived from renewable biomass sources, including
plant materials and agricultural waste;
(2) is created through a process that synthesizes
methanol; and
(3) is capable of being blended or mixed with liquid
fuels to propel motor vehicles.
* * *
Section 2. Section 9004(d)(1) of Title 75 is amended to
read:
§ 9004. Imposition of tax, exemptions and deductions.
* * *
(d) Alternative fuels tax.--
(1) A tax is hereby imposed upon alternative fuels used
to propel vehicles of any kind or character on the public
highways. The rate of tax applicable to each alternative fuel
shall be computed by the department on a gallon equivalent
basis and shall be published as necessary by notice in the
Pennsylvania Bulletin[.], provided that the rate of tax
applicable to biomass product shall be equal to the rate of
tax applicable to methanol.
* * *
Section 3. This act shall apply to alternative fuels sold on
or after the effective date of this section.
Section 4. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
