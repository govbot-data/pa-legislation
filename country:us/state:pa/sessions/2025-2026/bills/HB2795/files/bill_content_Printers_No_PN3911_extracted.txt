Legislative intent and purpose.The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:1234567891011121314151617 (1) On June 15, 2026, the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania held in In re Three Pennsylvania Skill Amusement Devices , 358 A.3d 385 (Pa. 2026), that skill game devices are subject to this part and 18 Pa.C.S. § 5513 (relating to gambling devices, gambling, etc.).(2) The Supreme Court of Pennsylvania stayed its order for 120 days in recognition of the reasonable reliance on prior case law regarding the lawfulness of skill games and the potential disruption to business owners and other good faith participants in the skill game industry.(3) Additional time is necessary for the General Assembly to consider legislation regarding the permanent authorization, regulation and taxation of skill games.(4) The intent of this chapter is to temporarily preserve the status quo regarding skill games through April 13, 2027, while the General Assembly considers legislation regarding the permanent authorization, regulation and taxation of skill games. This chapter is not intended to establish a permanent policy regarding skill games.§ 13D02. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this chapter shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Skill game." A skill slot machine or hybrid slot machine that is placed or operated at a location other than a licensed facility.§ 13D03.
