Amending Title 4 (Amusements) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, providing for temporary authorization of skill
games.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Chapter 13D of Title 4 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:

§ 13D01. Legislative intent and purpose.
The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:

(1) On June 15, 2026, the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania
held in In re Three Pennsylvania Skill Amusement Devices , 358
A.3d 385 (Pa. 2026), that skill game devices are subject to
this part and 18 Pa.C.S. § 5513 (relating to gambling
devices, gambling, etc.).
(2) The Supreme Court of Pennsylvania stayed its order
for 120 days in recognition of the reasonable reliance on
prior case law regarding the lawfulness of skill games and
the potential disruption to business owners and other good
faith participants in the skill game industry.
(3) Additional time is necessary for the General
Assembly to consider legislation regarding the permanent
authorization, regulation and taxation of skill games.
(4) The intent of this chapter is to temporarily
preserve the status quo regarding skill games through April
13, 2027, while the General Assembly considers legislation
regarding the permanent authorization, regulation and
taxation of skill games. This chapter is not intended to
establish a permanent policy regarding skill games.
§ 13D02. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Skill game." A skill slot machine or hybrid slot machine
that is placed or operated at a location other than a licensed
facility.

(b) Licensure.--A person shall not be required to obtain a
license, permit, certificate, registration, approval or other
authorization under this title to engage in an activity
authorized under subsection (a).
(c) Taxation.--A tax, fee or assessment imposed under this
title shall not apply solely by reason of an activity authorized
under subsection (a).
(d) Crimes Code.--An activity authorized under subsection
(a) shall constitute an activity lawfully conducted under this
title for purposes of 18 Pa.C.S. § 5513(e.1)(4) (relating to
gambling devices, gambling, etc.).
(e) Other prohibited acts.--Nothing in this chapter shall be
construed to authorize an act prohibited under section 1518
(relating to prohibited acts; penalties), other than an act
prohibited solely because a skill game, a location at which a
skill game is placed or operated or a person engaging in an
activity authorized under subsection (a) is not licensed,
permitted, registered, certified or approved under this title.
§ 13D04. Expiration.
This chapter shall expire April 13, 2027.
Section 2. This act shall take effect immediately.
