The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Universal
Child Care Task Force Act.
Section 2. Legislative findings and intent.
The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:
(1) It is within the purview of a responsible government
to provide for and maintain the conditions necessary to
public stability, out of which are born opportunity and
prosperity.
(2) Opportunity is fair and just access to one forum or
another. Opportunity enables the free exercise of choice and
facilitates economic competition.
(3) Prosperity is an abundance of resources and freedom
from want and requires:
(i) An exchange of energy, time and talent for fair
and just compensation.
(ii) Multiple systems working in coordination to
protect and preserve the economy of the home.
(4) A concentration of wealth that confines resources to
a small number of individuals does not strengthen society.
Resources create the most value when they circulate, support
innovation and contribute to shared prosperity. When
resources remain inactive, held only to accumulate more
value, they cannot fulfill their broader purpose of advancing
the public good.
(5) Opportunity and prosperity benefit this Commonwealth

(6) If this government fails to act in service to its

(7) For working families across this Commonwealth, child
care is a crucial service that allows those with children to

Section 3. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Child care."
(1) Supervision that is provided:
(i) to a child who is under 16 years of age;
(ii) away from the home of a child; and
(iii) in lieu of parental supervision for part of
the 24-hour day.
(2) The term does not include the supervision of a child
provided in a place of worship during religious services or
by a social service organization.
"Child care desert." A geographic area in which there are
three times the number of children than openings at child care
centers.
"Child care staff." An individual who is employed on a full-
time or part-time basis by a child care provider.
"Child care teacher." A child care staff member with
credentials, degrees or knowledge and expertise through
experience who is included in the staff:child ratio and is
responsible for child care activities.

Section 4. Establishment.
The Universal Child Care Task Force is established in the
office.
Section 5. Purpose.
The task force shall provide guidance for the establishment
of universal child care in this Commonwealth.

Section 7. Financial resources.

Section 8. Duties.
The task force shall:
(1) Determine and make publicly accessible through the
publicly accessible Internet website of the task force a
reasonable approximation of the total anticipated economic
impact of universal child care in this Commonwealth,
including the anticipated economic impact for every five
years over the 20-year period immediately following full and
complete implementation.
(2) Determine and make publicly accessible through the
public portal and Internet website of the task force the
determination and the means of determining a reasonable
approximation of the total economic value of child care staff
in this Commonwealth.
(3) Report a plan for complete implementation of
universal child care in this Commonwealth, with a target year
of 2032.
(4) Beginning November 1, 2026, and ending upon complete
implementation of universal child care in this Commonwealth,
report on and make annual recommendations for specific
appropriations for budget allocations that would facilitate
the development of a universal child care system, including:
(i) A permanent line of funding for the universal
child care system.

Section 10. Compensation and expenses.
(a) Payments.--Members of the task force:
(1) May not receive a salary or per diem allowance for
serving on the task force.
(2) Shall be reimbursed for actual and necessary
expenses, including the cost of travel, incurred in the
performance of their duties as members of the task force,
subject to the availability of money appropriated or
otherwise made available to the department for the
administration and operation of the task force.
(b) Prohibition.--The department may not require a member of
the task force enumerated in section 9(2) to bear unreimbursed
mileage or reasonable and necessary travel expenses as a
condition of service on the task force.

(3) The estimated cost of the activity.
(4) The personnel, technology, contracts, reimbursements
or other resources necessary to carry out the activity.
(5) The expected public benefit and measurable output of
the activity.

July 1, 2026, for the administration and operation of the task
