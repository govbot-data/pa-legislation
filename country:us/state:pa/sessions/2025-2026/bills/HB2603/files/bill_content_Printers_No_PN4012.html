§ 5765.1. DEFINITIONS.
THE FOLLOWING WORDS AND PHRASES WHEN USED IN THIS SUBCHAPTER
SHALL HAVE THE MEANINGS GIVEN TO THEM IN THIS SECTION UNLESS THE
CONTEXT CLEARLY INDICATES OTHERWISE:
"BIOMETRIC DATA." DATA GENERATED BY THE TECHNOLOGICAL
PROCESSING, MEASUREMENT OR ANALYSIS OF A PERSON'S BIOLOGICAL,
PHYSICAL OR BEHAVIORAL CHARACTERISTICS THAT CAN BE USED TO
IDENTIFY AN INDIVIDUAL.
"BIOMETRIC IDENTIFICATION." THE USE OF A SPECIFIC
INDIVIDUAL'S BIOMETRIC DATA TO IDENTIFY THE INDIVIDUAL BY
MATCHING THE INDIVIDUAL'S BIOMETRIC DATA TO THAT OF A KNOWN
INDIVIDUAL.

"CAPTURE." TO COLLECT AND STORE SOUND OR VIDEO OF ANOTHER
PERSON OR TO COLLECT AND TRANSMIT SOUND OR VIDEO OF ANOTHER
PERSON TO ANY OTHER PERSON FOR THE PURPOSE OF MAKING SUCH SOUND
OR VIDEO ACCESSIBLE TO THEM. THE TERM DOES NOT INCLUDE:
(1) THE TEMPORARY PROCESSING OF SOUND OR VIDEO THAT IS
NOT STORED OR TRANSMITTED TO ANOTHER INDIVIDUAL;
(2) INCIDENTAL, UNINTENDED OR BACKGROUND CAPTURE OF
SOUND OR VIDEO OF INDIVIDUALS IN PUBLIC PLACES WHERE CAPTURE
IS NOT SPECIFICALLY DIRECTED AT THE INDIVIDUALS; OR
(3) THE TRANSMISSION OF SOUND OR VIDEO EXCLUSIVELY FOR
THE PURPOSE OF PROCESSING THAT DOES NOT STORE THE SOUND OR
VIDEO.
"RECORDING INDICATOR." A LIGHT, SOUND OR OTHER INDICATOR ON
A WEARABLE RECORDING DEVICE THAT INDICATES THAT THE DEVICE IS
CAPTURING SOUND OR VIDEO.
"WEARABLE RECORDING DEVICE." A DEVICE OR APPARATUS THAT IS
DESIGNED TO BE WORN ON THE BODY THAT HAS THE CAPACITY TO
INTERCEPT AND MAKE SOUND OR VIDEO RECORDINGS AND STORE THOSE
RECORDINGS, TRANSMIT THEM TO ANOTHER DEVICE, THE INTERNET OR ANY
OTHER CLOUD-BASED SERVICE OR PROCESS THEM FOR THE PURPOSE OF
BIOMETRIC IDENTIFICATION.
"WEARER." A NATURAL PERSON WHO WEARS A WEARABLE RECORDING
DEVICE AND USES IT FOR THE PURPOSE OF CAPTURING SOUND OR VIDEO
OF ANOTHER PERSON.
§ 5765.2. WEARABLE RECORDING DEVICE REQUIREMENTS.
(a) Prohibitions.--
(1) A person shall not manufacture, sell, deliver, hold
or offer for sale in commerce in this Commonwealth:
(i) A wearable recording device without a visual
indicator.

(ii) A device, computer code or other form of

(I) A WEARABLE RECORDING DEVICE WITHOUT A RECORDING
INDICATOR THAT IS SUFFICIENTLY PROMINENT AND CONTINUOUS

(2) A person shall not purchase, trade for or otherwise
acquire the device, computer code or other form of technology
described under paragraph (1).
(3) An individual in this Commonwealth shall not use any
technology or other means to permanently or temporarily
disable or obstruct a visual RECORDING indicator.
(4) A PERSON SHALL NOT COLLECT OR USE FOR BIOMETRIC
IDENTIFICATION THE BIOMETRIC DATA OF AN INDIVIDUAL OTHER THAN
THE WEARER GENERATED BY A WEARABLE RECORDING DEVICE.
(5) A PERSON SHALL NOT MAKE AVAILABLE TO ANY INDIVIDUAL
IN THIS COMMONWEALTH A PRODUCT, DEVICE OR SERVICE THAT IS
DESIGNED TO BE USED IN CONJUNCTION WITH A WEARABLE RECORDING
DEVICE TO VIOLATE PARAGRAPH (4).
(b) Reporting system.--A manufacturer of a wearable
recording device shall establish a system to receive reports
about visual RECORDING indicator malfunctions from members of
the public.

§ 5765.3. 5765.4. Operation of wearable recording devices.
(a) Recording of others.-- An individual A WEARER shall not
operate a wearable recording device to capture sound or video of
any other individual in any area where the other individual has
a reasonable expectation of privacy unless the individual
operating the device has the explicit consent of that other
individual.
(b) Visual RECORDING indicator requirement.-- An individual A
WEARER shall not operate a wearable recording device to capture
sound or video of any other individual in any place without the
other individual's actual knowledge if:
(1) the wearable recording device does not have a visual
RECORDING indicator; or
(2) the visual RECORDING indicator of the recording
device has been permanently or temporarily disabled.
