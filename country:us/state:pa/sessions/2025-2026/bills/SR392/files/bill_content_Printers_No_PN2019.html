INTRODUCED AND ADOPTED, OCTOBER 7, 2026
A RESOLUTION
Commemorating the anniversary of the cruel and barbaric attack
by Hamas against the State of Israel that took place on
October 7, 2023, during Shabbat, mourning the loss of life
and human suffering that took place on that day and in the
years since the attack and expressing deep condolences to the
families of those who have lost their lives as a result of
this senseless act of violence.
WHEREAS, On October 7, 2023, during Shabbat, and nearly 50
years to the day following the commencement of the Yom Kippur
War, a war deliberately initiated on Judaism's holiest day,
Hamas initiated an unprovoked terrorist attack against the State
of Israel; and
WHEREAS, Hostilities were initiated by a rocket barrage of
more than 5,000 missiles, which killed more than 1,000 Israeli
civilians and soldiers and resulted in the invasion of more than
20 communities in Israel; and
WHEREAS, The attacks and invasions orchestrated by Hamas
resulted in the death of more than 1,200 people, injuries to
more than 4,000 people and the kidnapping of more than 250
hostages; and

WHEREAS, October 7, 2023, was one of the deadliest terrorist
attacks in modern history, and the deadliest day for the Jewish
people on any single day since the Holocaust; and
WHEREAS, It is the moral and ethical duty of the
international community to stand firmly against acts of
terrorism and violence targeting innocent civilians, regardless
of their nationality or religious affiliation; and
WHEREAS, Antisemitic incidents rose by nearly 400% in the
three months following October 7, 2023, and by an estimated 344%
over the past five years; and
WHEREAS, The Jewish and Israeli people in this Commonwealth,
nationwide and throughout the world should be able to live their
lives without fear of being harassed or attacked; and
WHEREAS, Thousands of innocent Israeli and Palestinian
citizens have been wounded and killed as a result; and
WHEREAS, The death and suffering which has occurred as a
result of the October 7, 2023, terrorist attack launched by
Hamas is horrific and devastating; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate commemorate the anniversary of the
cruel and barbaric attack by Hamas against the State of Israel
that took place on October 7, 2023, during Shabbat, and mourn
the loss of life and human suffering that took place on that day
and in the years since the attack; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the Senate express deep condolences to the
families of those who have lost their lives as a result of this
senseless act of violence; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the Senate stand in solidarity with the
innocent Israeli and Palestinian people who suffered and
continue to suffer the consequences of the violence and
hostilities inflicted as a result of the Hamas terrorist attack
