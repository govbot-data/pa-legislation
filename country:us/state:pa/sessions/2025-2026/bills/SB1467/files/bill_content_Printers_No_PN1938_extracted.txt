2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1467 PN 1938 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1938 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1467 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KEARNEY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO TRANSPORTATION, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 AN ACTDesignating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 15287, carrying State Route 2031 over Amtrak in Ridley Park Borough, Delaware County, as the Mayor Hank Eberle Memorial Bridge.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Mayor Hank Eberle Memorial Bridge.(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) Henry "Hank" Eberle, Jr., was born August 30, 1933, in Darby and was raised in Sharon Hill.(2) Mr. Eberle graduated from Sharon Hill High School in 1951 and Franklin and Marshall College in 1955 with a degree in sociology.(3) After graduating from college, Mr. Eberle was drafted into the United States Army and served for two years at Fort Knox as a battery clerk and personnel clerk.(4) Mr. Eberle worked for more than three decades as a human resources executive for Sun Company and, following his retirement, provided consulting services to nonprofit 12345678910111213141516171819 organizations, including Elwyn and Project HOME.(5) Mr. Eberle served as Mayor of Ridley Park for 28 years and was actively involved in community events and activities throughout the borough.(6) In addition to his public service as mayor, Mr. Eberle served his community through Immanuel Lutheran Church of Norwood, Taylor Hospital, The Community Foundation, the Ridley Park Athletic Club and numerous local committees and boards.(7) As Mayor of Ridley Park, Mr. Eberle championed local causes, including the revitalization of downtown Ridley Park, and tirelessly advocated for the Sellers Avenue Bridge.(8) Mr. Eberle was named Mayor of the Year in 2011 and was honored as Taylor Community Foundation's Philanthropist of the Year.(9) Mr. Eberle passed away on August 2, 2025, at 91 years of age.(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key 15287, carrying State Route 2031 over Amtrak in Ridley Park Borough, Delaware County, is designated the Mayor Hank Eberle Memorial Bridge.(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to traffic in both directions on the bridge.Section 2.
