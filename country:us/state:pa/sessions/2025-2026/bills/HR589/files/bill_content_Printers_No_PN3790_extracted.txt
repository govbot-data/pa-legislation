andWHEREAS, Following the passage of the Stamp Act in 1765, a series of unpopular British laws met with stiff opposition in the colonies, fueling a bitter struggle over whether Parliament had the right to tax colonists without the consent of the representative colonial governments; andWHEREAS, This struggle erupted into violence in 1770 when British troops killed five colonists and injured six others in the Boston Massacre; andWHEREAS, Three years later, outrage over the Tea Act of 1773 prompted colonists to board an East India Company ship in Boston Harbor and dump its cargo into the sea in the now-infamous Boston Tea Party;
