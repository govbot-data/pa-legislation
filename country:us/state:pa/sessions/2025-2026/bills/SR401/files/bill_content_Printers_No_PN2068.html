A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the week of October 19 through 23, 2026, as "School
Bus Safety Week" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The Department of Transportation (PennDOT)
participates in "National School Bus Safety Week" in conjunction
with the National Association for Pupil Transportation; and
WHEREAS, Motorists must stop at least 10 feet away from
school buses that have their red lights flashing and stop arms
extended; and
WHEREAS, Motorists must stop when they are behind a school
bus, meeting the bus or approaching an intersection where a bus
is stopped; and
WHEREAS, Motorists following or traveling alongside a school
bus must also stop until the red lights have stopped flashing,
the stop arm is withdrawn and all children have reached safety;
and
WHEREAS, If physical barriers such as grassy medians, guide
rails or concrete median barriers separate oncoming traffic from

WHEREAS, PennDOT provides helpful tips to motorists, parents
and students to help ensure school bus safety; and
WHEREAS, According to PennDOT, during the 2023-2024 school
year, school buses throughout this Commonwealth traveled
approximately 435 million miles transporting roughly 1.4 million
students to and from school and school-related activities; and
WHEREAS, The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration
(NHTSA) reported that, from 2015 through 2024, there were 962
fatal school-transportation-related traffic crashes, resulting
in 1,069 fatalities, an average of 107 fatalities per year; and
WHEREAS, These figures reflect a slight decrease compared
with the period from 2014 through 2023, when NHTSA recorded 971
fatal school-transportation-related traffic crashes; and
WHEREAS, These figures also reflect a decrease compared with
the period from 2011 through 2020, when NHTSA recorded 1,009
such crashes among 326,149 fatal motor vehicle traffic crashes
nationwide; and
WHEREAS, The annual average of fatalities in school-
transportation-related crashes decreased to 107 from 2015
through 2024, compared with 113 from 2011 through 2020; and
WHEREAS, Of the fatalities from 2015 through 2024, 11% were
occupants of school transportation vehicles, 19% were
nonoccupants, including pedestrians and bicyclists, and 70% were
occupants of other vehicles; and
WHEREAS, NHTSA reported that 45% of school-transportation-
related fatal crashes from 2015 through 2024 occurred in rural
areas, with the remainder occurring in urban areas; and
WHEREAS, Studies affirm that school buses represent the

WHEREAS, The outstanding safety record of school buses is due
to several factors, including the enhanced vehicle safety
standards that school buses must meet, the recognizable color of

bus lighting and signage to control traffic when children are
getting on or off a school bus; and
WHEREAS, School bus drivers are trained to provide directions
to children when crossing the road in front of a school bus,
which also contributes to the safety of the children; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania School Bus Association provides
additional resources to help ensure the safety of school buses,
school bus drivers and student passengers; and
WHEREAS, School bus drivers are directly responsible for the
safety of Pennsylvania's children as they work to bring them to
their destinations safely and on time; and
WHEREAS, In recent years, the General Assembly has advanced
measures to boost school bus driver recruitment and enhance
student safety, underscoring its commitment to protecting
school-age children; and
