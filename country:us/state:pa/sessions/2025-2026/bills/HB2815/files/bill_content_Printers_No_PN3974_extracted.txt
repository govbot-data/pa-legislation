Victim protection.The owner of a stolen vehicle or an authorized representative of the owner shall present a police report verifying the vehicle as reported stolen to claim protection under this subchapter. The owner of a stolen vehicle shall not be required to present information other than required under this section.§ 7143. Fines and fees prohibited.(a) Prohibition.--No victim of any of the following crimes may be charged a fee, fine or otherwise be held liable for the costs of vehicle recovery or storage of the vehicle by a private or public entity that recovers the victim's vehicle:(1) 18 Pa.C.S. § 3702 (relating to robbery of motor vehicle).(2) 18 Pa.C.S. § 3921 (relating to theft by unlawful taking or disposition), when the property taken is a vehicle.(3) 18 Pa.C.S. § 3928 (relating to unauthorized use of automobiles and other vehicles).(b) Release of vehicle.--Any recovering entity shall immediately release the vehicle to the lawful owner or an authorized representative of the owner when presented with a police report that verifies the vehicle as being reported stolen. The recovering entity shall only be required to release 20260HB2815PN3974 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 the vehicle during normal business hours on normal business days.(c) Additional fees prohibited.--No additional charges or fees shall be charged to the victim of a crime under this section if law enforcement or another investigating authority requests that the recovering entity remain in possession of the vehicle for investigatory purposes.§ 7144. Transfer of ownership prohibited.(a) Sale or transfer limitation.--Stolen vehicles in the custody of a recovering entity may not be auctioned, sold or otherwise have ownership transferred to another entity or individual, except in accordance with subsection (c).(b) Abandoned vehicle.--A stolen vehicle shall be considered abandoned if:(1) the lawful owner of the vehicle has been notified of the recovery and location of the stolen vehicle, either by law enforcement or the recovering entity, through certified mail and successful or attempted telephone contact; and(2) more than 30 days have passed from the date of recovery and the stolen vehicle remains unclaimed by the lawful owner or 60 days have passed from the date the lawful owner has been notified of the recovery and location of the vehicle and the vehicle has not been retrieved. If the entity has a duty to return the vehicle under section 7145 (relating to duty to return), the vehicle may only be considered not retrieved if the lawful owner fails to provide a proper address or location where the vehicle may be lawfully returned or stored.(c) Notification.--If a stolen vehicle becomes abandoned under subsection (b), the recovering entity shall notify the 20260HB2815PN3974 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 department of the vehicle's abandonment under this section and produce receipts of certified mail and successful or attempted telephone contact. Further action shall be subject to the provisions of Chapter 73 (relating to abandoned vehicles and cargos).(d) Regulations.--The department shall promulgate regulations as needed to provide for the recovery of abandoned vehicles under this subchapter and Chapter 73.§ 7145. Duty to return.A public entity that maintains possession of a stolen vehicle has a duty to return or arrange for the return of the vehicle to the lawful owner's place of residence or storage at no cost to the owner if the owner's documented place of residence is farther than 30 miles from the actual location of the vehicle. Nothing in this section shall be construed to prevent the continued possession of a vehicle by a public entity for investigatory purposes or to prevent the lawful owner from retrieving the vehicle.§ 7146. Penalties.(a) Imposition.--A private entity that violates:(1) Section 7143(a) or (c) (relating to fines and fees prohibited) shall be subject to a fine levied by the department of not less than $1,000 and not more than $2,000 for a first offense. Each subsequent offense within two years of the first offense shall be punishable by a fine of not less than $2,500 and not more than $10,000.(2) Section 7143(b) shall be subject to a fine levied by the department of not less than $250 and not more than $750. Each day the vehicle is unlawfully retained by the private entity after the first offense shall result in an additional 20260HB2815PN3974 - 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 $100 fine.(3) Section 7144(a) (relating to transfer of ownership prohibited) shall be subject to a fine levied by the department of not less than $10,000 and not more than $20,000. Each subsequent offense within five years of the first offense shall be punishable by a fine of not less than $20,000 and not more than $30,000.(b) Complaint.--A victim whose protections under this subchapter are violated by a public entity may file a complaint in the court of common pleas in the county in which the individual resides. Any restitution ordered by the court shall include, but not be limited to:(1) Court costs and attorney fees.(2) For a violation of section 7143, an amount equal to the fine or fee paid by the victim.(3) For a violation of section 7143(b), an amount equal to $50 for each day in which the vehicle was unlawfully retained by the public entity.(4) For a violation of section 7144(a), an amount equal to the fair market value of the victim's vehicle.(5) In the case of a violation of section 7145 (relating to duty to return), an amount equal to the victim's expenses for towing or transporting the vehicle.(c) Revocation.--The department shall revoke any special registration plates issued under section 1337 (relating to use of "Miscellaneous Motor Vehicle Business" registration plates) to a private entity that violates:(1) section 7143 not less than three times in two years; or(2) section 7144(a) not less than two times in five 20260HB2815PN3974 - 6 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 years.§ 7147. Victims of Vehicle Theft Protection Fund.(a) Establishment.--The Victims of Vehicle Theft Protection Fund is established in the State Treasury. All fines collected by the department under section 7146 (relating to penalties) shall be deposited into the fund. Money in the fund is appropriated to the department on a continuing basis for the purposes of this subchapter.(b) Remittance.--A public entity ordered to pay restitution to a victim under section 7146 shall remit to the department for deposit into the fund an amount equal to 50% of the restitution ordered to be paid to the victim. Nothing in this section shall be construed to mean that 50% of the restitution amount shall be diverted from the victim to the fund.(c) Application.--A victim whose protections under this subchapter have been violated may apply to the department for compensation from the fund. Disbursements from the fund shall be for reimbursement of:(1) fees assessed on and paid by the victim in violation of section 7143 (relating to fines and fees prohibited); and(2) the cost of replacing a vehicle if the ownership of the victim's vehicle was transferred in violation of section 7144(a) (relating to transfer of ownership prohibited).(d) Limitation.--Reimbursement amounts shall not exceed the actual amount paid by the victim if applying for compensation under subsection (c)(1) or 50% of the fair market value of the vehicle if applying for compensation under subsection (c)(2). Compensation shall be awarded in order of application as money in the fund is available.(e) Regulations.--The department shall promulgate 20260HB2815PN3974 - 7 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 regulations establishing the process through which victims may apply for and receive compensation, as well as outlining what documents the victim may need to provide for a claim to be approved. The department shall make determinations of eligibility.§ 7148. Contracts with private entities.No public entity may contract with or continue to contract with a private entity that has violated the provisions of this subchapter more than five times in three years. This section shall:(1) Serve as legal justification for the termination of contracts violated after the effective date of this paragraph.(2) Override relevant termination provisions that may be present within a contract entered into or renewed after the effective date of the contract.(3) Absolve the public entity of all further obligations to the private entity that may have been provided for in the terminated contract.§ 7149. Authority and duties of department.(a) Complaints.--The department shall establish a mechanism to receive and investigate complaints of violations of this subchapter.(b) Notification.--The department shall, to the greatest extent possible, annually inform all public and private entities of this subchapter.Section 2.
