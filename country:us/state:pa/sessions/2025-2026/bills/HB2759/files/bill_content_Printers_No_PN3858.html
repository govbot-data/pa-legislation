AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in financial responsibility, providing for
dealership plate frame or logo.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1799.8. Dealership plate frame or logo.
(a) Consent required.--A vehicle dealership shall obtain the
verbal or written permission of a customer who has agreed to
purchase a vehicle from the vehicle dealership before attaching
a plate frame or logo of the vehicle dealership on the front
bumper of the vehicle.
(b) Removal.--A vehicle dealership that violates subsection
(a) shall, upon the request of the customer and without charge
to the customer:
(1) remove the plate frame or logo; and
(2) return any plugs to the front bumper or otherwise
restore the front bumper to the condition that existed before
