2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2759 PN 3858 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3858 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2759 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY McNEILL, PIELLI AND HILL-EVANS, SEPTEMBER 3, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, SEPTEMBER 8, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in financial responsibility, providing for dealership plate frame or logo.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1799.8. Dealership plate frame or logo.(a) Consent required.--A vehicle dealership shall obtain the verbal or written permission of a customer who has agreed to purchase a vehicle from the vehicle dealership before attaching a plate frame or logo of the vehicle dealership on the front bumper of the vehicle.(b) Removal.--A vehicle dealership that violates subsection (a) shall, upon the request of the customer and without charge to the customer:(1) remove the plate frame or logo; and(2) return any plugs to the front bumper or otherwise restore the front bumper to the condition that existed before 12345678910111213141516171819 the attachment of the plate frame or logo.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2759PN3858 - 2 - 12
