AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," in preliminary provisions, providing
for education support professional employment information to
be posted.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known
as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a
section to read:
Section 135. Education Support Professional Employment
Information to be Posted.--(a) By October 1, 2027, and by
October 1 of each year thereafter, the department shall post all
of the following information for each school entity on the
department's publicly accessible Internet website:
(1) The name of the school entity.
(2) The school identification number assigned by the
department to the school entity.

(3) The Administrative Unit Number assigned by the
department to the school entity.
(4) The number of education support professionals in each of
the following job classifications:
(i) Instructional paraprofessional or education assistant.
(ii) Non-instructional paraprofessional.
(iii) Secretary.
(iv) Bus driver, bus aide or mechanic.
(v) Maintenance.
(vi) Technology.
(vii) Custodian.
(viii) Nurse assistant.
(ix) Crossing guard or other safety worker.
(x) Personal care assistant.
(5) For each job classification under paragraph (4), all of
the following:
(i) Wage rate.
(ii) Total working hours per week, excluding overtime.
(iii) Work year, as follows:
(A) Full year, full time.
(B) School year, full time.
(C) Full year, part time.
(D) School year, part time.
(iv) The number of education support professionals in the
job classification, disaggregated by all of the following:
(A) Gender.
(B) Race or ethnicity.
(C) Total years worked in the school entity.
(D) Original date of hire.
(v) The employment status of education support professionals

(b) In posting employment information under subsection (a),
the department shall take all of the following actions:
(1) Post the information in a location and manner that is
easily accessible to the public.
(2) Include definitions and other explanatory references
necessary to assist the public in understanding the information.
(3) Use existing databases and electronic reporting systems
to the extent possible.
(4) Provide an electronic feature that allows the public to
compare employment information for at least four (4) school
entities.
(5) Report the employment information in aggregate form,
except to the extent that disaggregation is required under
subsection (a).
(c) In a form and manner and by a deadline determined by the
department, the following shall apply:
(1) Each school entity shall report to the department the
information under subsection (a).
(2) Each third party that has contracted to provide non-
instructional services to a school entity shall report to the
department the information under subsection (a)(4) and (5),
disaggregated by school entity.

"Department." The Department of Education of the

"Education support professional." A person who is not a
professional employe, temporary professional employe or

"Furlough." A suspension or discharge from work or a
reduction in work hours due to economic conditions or shortage

"School entity." A school district, cyber charter school,
