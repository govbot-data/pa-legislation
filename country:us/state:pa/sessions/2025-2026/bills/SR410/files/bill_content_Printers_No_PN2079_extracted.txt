WARD, OCTOBER 27, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, OCTOBER 27, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing September 7, 2026, as "Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, "World Duchenne Awareness Day" is observed globally on September 7 to raise awareness about Duchenne muscular dystrophy, a rare progressive disorder caused by a mutation in the gene producing dystrophin, a protein essential for muscle protection; andWHEREAS, According to the Muscular Dystrophy Association, Duchenne muscular dystrophy affects 1 out of every 5,000 children, approximately 15,000 children in the United States; andWHEREAS, Decker Murphy was diagnosed with Duchenne muscular dystrophy in January 2024 and celebrated his ninth birthday on August 30, 2026; andWHEREAS, Through "Decker's Blueprint," a nonprofit organization that was created to honor Decker and his passion for building and designing new homes just like his father, the 1234567891011121314151617 Murphy family has done much to advocate for early diagnosis and long-term treatment or cure to Duchenne muscular dystrophy; andWHEREAS, Despite the challenges posed by his condition, Decker embodies the spirit of a fighter, never giving up and always pushing forward; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize September 7, 2026, as "Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate honor Decker Murphy and all of the courageous children who have been diagnosed with Duchenne muscular dystrophy and highlight the incredible work of advocacy groups and nonprofit organizations like Decker's Blueprint, who help play a critical role in advancing research, supporting families and advocating for improved treatment options; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate encourage residents in communities throughout this Commonwealth to commit to raising awareness of this rare progressive disorder.20260SR0410PN2079 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718
