A RESOLUTION
Recognizing September 7, 2026, as "Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy
Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, "World Duchenne Awareness Day" is observed globally
on September 7 to raise awareness about Duchenne muscular
dystrophy, a rare progressive disorder caused by a mutation in
the gene producing dystrophin, a protein essential for muscle
protection; and
WHEREAS, According to the Muscular Dystrophy Association,
Duchenne muscular dystrophy affects 1 out of every 5,000
children, approximately 15,000 children in the United States;
and
WHEREAS, Decker Murphy was diagnosed with Duchenne muscular
dystrophy in January 2024 and celebrated his ninth birthday on
August 30, 2026; and
WHEREAS, Through "Decker's Blueprint," a nonprofit
organization that was created to honor Decker and his passion
for building and designing new homes just like his father, the

Murphy family has done much to advocate for early diagnosis and

WHEREAS, Despite the challenges posed by his condition,
Decker embodies the spirit of a fighter, never giving up and
always pushing forward; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate recognize September 7, 2026, as
"Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy Awareness Day" in Pennsylvania; and
be it further
RESOLVED, That the Senate honor Decker Murphy and all of the
courageous children who have been diagnosed with Duchenne
muscular dystrophy and highlight the incredible work of advocacy
groups and nonprofit organizations like Decker's Blueprint, who
help play a critical role in advancing research, supporting
families and advocating for improved treatment options; and be
it further
RESOLVED, That the Senate encourage residents in communities
throughout this Commonwealth to commit to raising awareness of
this rare progressive disorder.
