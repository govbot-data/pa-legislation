AN ACT
Amending Title 42 (Judiciary and Judicial Procedure) of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in bonds and
recognizances, further providing for right to bail.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 5701 of Title 42 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:
§ 5701. Right to bail.
(a) Bail.--All prisoners shall be bailable by sufficient
sureties, unless:
(1) for capital offenses or for offenses for which the
maximum sentence is life imprisonment; or
(2) no condition or combination of conditions other than
imprisonment will reasonably assure the safety of any person
and the community when the proof is evident or presumption
great.
(b) Public safety factors.--In determining whether
subsection (a)(2) applies to a defendant charged with a felony,

(1) Has previously been convicted of a crime of violence
as defined in section 9714(g) (relating to sentences for

(2) Has previously been convicted of an offense under

crimes and offenses):
(i) Section 2701(a)(2) (relating to simple assault).
(ii) Section 4952 (relating to intimidation of
witnesses or victims).
(iii) Section 4953 (relating to retaliation against
witness, victim or party).
(iv) Section 4953.1 (relating to retaliation against
prosecutor or judicial official).
(v) Section 4958 (relating to intimidation,
retaliation or obstruction in child abuse cases).
(3) Has previously been convicted of any other offense
that resulted in death or serious bodily injury as defined in
18 Pa.C.S. § 2301 (relating to definitions).
(4) Has one or more criminal charges pending, including,
but not limited to, charges involving violence, threats of
violence, possession of a weapon or violations of court
orders.
(5) Is currently subject to or has previously been
subject to an order issued under 23 Pa.C.S. Ch. 61 (relating
to protection from abuse).

defendant meets two of the criteria specified in subsection (b)

(d) Written findings.--If the court denies bail under
subsection (a)(2), the court shall make written findings on the
record that include all of the following:
(1) A statement of the evidence supporting the
determination that the proof is evident or presumption great.
(2) A statement of the evidence supporting the
determination that no condition or combination of conditions
other than imprisonment will reasonably assure the safety of
any person and the community.
(e) Construction.--Nothing in this section shall be
construed to modify the presumption of innocence or prevent a
defendant from petitioning for review of a bail determination
under applicable law.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
