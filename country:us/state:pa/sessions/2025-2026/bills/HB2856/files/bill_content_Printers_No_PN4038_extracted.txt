Section 5701 of Title 42 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:§ 5701. Right to bail.(a) Bail.--All prisoners shall be bailable by sufficient sureties, unless:(1) for capital offenses or for offenses for which the maximum sentence is life imprisonment; or(2) no condition or combination of conditions other than imprisonment will reasonably assure the safety of any person and the community when the proof is evident or presumption great.(b) Public safety factors.--In determining whether subsection (a)(2) applies to a defendant charged with a felony, 123456789101112131415161718 the court shall consider, in addition to any other relevant factor, whether the defendant:(1) Has previously been convicted of a crime of violence as defined in section 9714(g) (relating to sentences for second and subsequent offenses) or an offense under 18 Pa.C.S. § 3502 (relating to burglary).(2) Has previously been convicted of an offense under any of the following provisions of 18 Pa.C.S. (relating to crimes and offenses):(i) Section 2701(a)(2) (relating to simple assault).(ii) Section 4952 (relating to intimidation of witnesses or victims).(iii) Section 4953 (relating to retaliation against witness, victim or party).(iv) Section 4953.1 (relating to retaliation against prosecutor or judicial official).(v) Section 4958 (relating to intimidation, retaliation or obstruction in child abuse cases).(3) Has previously been convicted of any other offense that resulted in death or serious bodily injury as defined in 18 Pa.C.S. § 2301 (relating to definitions).(4) Has one or more criminal charges pending, including, but not limited to, charges involving violence, threats of violence, possession of a weapon or violations of court orders.(5) Is currently subject to or has previously been subject to an order issued under 23 Pa.C.S.
