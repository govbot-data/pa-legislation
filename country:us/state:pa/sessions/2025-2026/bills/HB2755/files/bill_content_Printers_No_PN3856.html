AN ACT
Amending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in restructuring of electric utility
industry, providing for membership in regional transmission
organization.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 2816. Membership in regional transmission organization.
(a) Requirement.--An electric distribution company,
organization or person owning or operating a facility for the
jurisdictional transmission of electricity located in this
Commonwealth shall participate as a member of a regional
transmission organization.
(b) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Regional transmission organization." An entity that
