imposing duties upon the Department of Revenue, certain
employers, fiduciaries, individuals, persons, corporations
and other entities; prescribing crimes, offenses and
penalties," in tax credit and tax benefit administration,
further providing for definitions; and providing for green
infrastructure tax credit.

hereby enacts as follows:

Section 2. The act is amended by adding an article to read:
ARTICLE XIX-L
GREEN INFRASTRUCTURE TAX CREDIT
Section 1901-L. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this article
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Department." The Department of Environmental Protection of
the Commonwealth.
"Green infrastructure." A storm water management practice or
facility that reduces, captures, infiltrates, evapotranspirates
or reuses storm water runoff, including permeable pavement, a
green roof, a rain garden, a bioswale, a rainwater harvesting
system, tree canopy planting or another practice approved by the
department under section 1904-L(a).
"Pass-through entity." A partnership as defined in section
301(n.0) or a Pennsylvania S corporation as defined in section
301(n.1).
"Qualified costs." Costs paid or incurred by a taxpayer for
the design, engineering, materials, installation and inspection
of green infrastructure as part of a qualified project,
including reasonable costs of obtaining a permit or other
governmental approval required for the project. The term does
not include costs of routine operation, maintenance or repair,

"Qualified project." The installation of green

"Tax credit." The green infrastructure tax credit

"Taxpayer." A person, corporation, pass-through entity or
other entity subject to tax under Article III, IV or VI. The
term includes the shareholder, owner or member of a pass-through
entity that receives a tax credit under section 1906-L.
Section 1902-L. Eligibility.
To be eligible to receive a tax credit, a taxpayer shall
demonstrate to the Department of Revenue all of the following:
(1) Completion of a qualified project within this
Commonwealth during the taxable year for which the tax credit
is claimed or the immediately preceding taxable year.
(2) Certification of the qualified project by the
department under section 1904-L or possession of a form
described under section 1904-L(c)(2).
(3) Documentation of qualified costs in the form and
manner required by the Department of Revenue.
Section 1903-L. Application and approval.
(a) Application.--A taxpayer may apply to the Department of
Revenue for a tax credit. The application shall be submitted on
a form prescribed by the Department of Revenue and shall include
the certification or form described under section 1902-L(2) and
documentation of qualified costs.
(b) Approval.--The Department of Revenue shall approve or

Section 1904-L. Certification.
(a) Standards.--Not later than 180 days after the effective
date of this subsection, the department shall publish technical
guidelines establishing the green infrastructure practices,
design standards and performance criteria that qualify a project
for certification under this article. The department shall
review and may revise the guidelines no less frequently than
every five years.
(b) Submission.--A taxpayer seeking certification of a
project must submit to the department, on a form published by
the department, documentation of the project, including the
location, design specifications and, upon completion, evidence
of installation consistent with the guidelines published under
subsection (a).

Section 1905-L. Green infrastructure tax credit.
(a) Amount.--A taxpayer that receives approval under section
1903-L may claim a tax credit equal to 50% of the qualified
costs of the qualified project, not to exceed $500,000 for any
taxpayer in any taxable year.
(b) Applicable taxes.--A taxpayer may apply the tax credit
against up to 100% of the taxpayer's liability under Article III
or IV, or any combination thereof.
(c) Carryforward.--If the taxpayer cannot use the entire
amount of the tax credit in the taxable year in which the tax
credit is first approved, the excess may be carried forward for
no more than five consecutive taxable years and applied against
the taxpayer's tax liability in those years.
(d) Nonrefundable.--A tax credit is not refundable and may
not be carried back to a prior taxable year.

Section 1907-L. Recapture.
If, within five years of certification, a taxpayer removes,
materially alters or fails to maintain green infrastructure for
which a tax credit was granted under this article, or if the
department determines that a project deemed certified under
section 1904-L(c)(2) did not meet the requirements of this
article, the Department of Revenue may recapture from the
taxpayer all or a portion of the tax credit granted, in the
manner provided for the assessment and collection of tax under
Article IV.
Section 1908-L. Guidelines and regulations.
The department and the Department of Revenue shall jointly
develop written guidelines for the implementation of this
article. The guidelines shall be in effect until the department
and the Department of Revenue promulgate regulations for the
implementation of this article.
