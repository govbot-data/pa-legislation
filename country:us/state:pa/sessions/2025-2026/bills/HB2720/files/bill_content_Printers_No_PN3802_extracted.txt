The act is amended by adding an article to read:ARTICLE XIX-LGREEN INFRASTRUCTURE TAX CREDITSection 1901-L. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this article shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Department." The Department of Environmental Protection of the Commonwealth."Green infrastructure." A storm water management practice or facility that reduces, captures, infiltrates, evapotranspirates or reuses storm water runoff, including permeable pavement, a green roof, a rain garden, a bioswale, a rainwater harvesting system, tree canopy planting or another practice approved by the department under section 1904-L(a)."Pass-through entity." A partnership as defined in section 301(n.0) or a Pennsylvania S corporation as defined in section 301(n.1)."Qualified costs." Costs paid or incurred by a taxpayer for the design, engineering, materials, installation and inspection of green infrastructure as part of a qualified project, including reasonable costs of obtaining a permit or other governmental approval required for the project. The term does not include costs of routine operation, maintenance or repair, 20260HB2720PN3802 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 costs financed by a grant or forgivable loan from a Federal, State or local government source, or costs for which a tax credit has been claimed under any other article of this act."Qualified project." The installation of green infrastructure on real property located within this Commonwealth that has been certified by the department under section 1904-L."Tax credit." The green infrastructure tax credit established under this article."Taxpayer." A person, corporation, pass-through entity or other entity subject to tax under Article III, IV or VI. The term includes the shareholder, owner or member of a pass-through entity that receives a tax credit under section 1906-L.Section 1902-L. Eligibility.To be eligible to receive a tax credit, a taxpayer shall demonstrate to the Department of Revenue all of the following:(1) Completion of a qualified project within this Commonwealth during the taxable year for which the tax credit is claimed or the immediately preceding taxable year.(2) Certification of the qualified project by the department under section 1904-L or possession of a form described under section 1904-L(c)(2).(3) Documentation of qualified costs in the form and manner required by the Department of Revenue.Section 1903-L. Application and approval.(a) Application.--A taxpayer may apply to the Department of Revenue for a tax credit. The application shall be submitted on a form prescribed by the Department of Revenue and shall include the certification or form described under section 1902-L(2) and documentation of qualified costs.(b) Approval.--The Department of Revenue shall approve or 20260HB2720PN3802 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 disapprove a completed application not later than 60 days after receipt of the application. Applications shall be approved in the order in which completed applications are received.(c) Limitation.--The Department of Revenue may not approve more than $50,000,000 in tax credits in any fiscal year.(d) Deferred approval.--If the total amount of tax credits applied for in a fiscal year exceeds the limitation under subsection (c), the Department of Revenue shall place each otherwise eligible application on a list in chronological order based on the date the completed application was received. An application that cannot be approved solely because of the limitation under subsection (c) shall receive priority in the next fiscal year and shall be approved before an application submitted in that fiscal year.Section 1904-L. Certification.(a) Standards.--Not later than 180 days after the effective date of this subsection, the department shall publish technical guidelines establishing the green infrastructure practices, design standards and performance criteria that qualify a project for certification under this article. The department shall review and may revise the guidelines no less frequently than every five years.(b) Submission.--A taxpayer seeking certification of a project must submit to the department, on a form published by the department, documentation of the project, including the location, design specifications and, upon completion, evidence of installation consistent with the guidelines published under subsection (a).(c) Review.--(1) The department shall determine, not later than 90 20260HB2720PN3802 - 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 days after receipt of submission of completed documentation under subsection (b), whether the project meets the requirements of this article and, if the project meets the requirements, shall issue a certification to the taxpayer.(2) If the department does not issue a determination within the period under paragraph (1), the project shall be deemed certified, and the department shall, upon request of the taxpayer, issue a form acknowledging deemed certification for submission with the taxpayer's application under section 1903-L. Deemed certification does not relieve the taxpayer of the requirements of section 1902-L(1) and (3) and is subject to recapture under section 1907-L.Section 1905-L. Green infrastructure tax credit.(a) Amount.--A taxpayer that receives approval under section 1903-L may claim a tax credit equal to 50% of the qualified costs of the qualified project, not to exceed $500,000 for any taxpayer in any taxable year.(b) Applicable taxes.--A taxpayer may apply the tax credit against up to 100% of the taxpayer's liability under Article III or IV, or any combination thereof.(c) Carryforward.--If the taxpayer cannot use the entire amount of the tax credit in the taxable year in which the tax credit is first approved, the excess may be carried forward for no more than five consecutive taxable years and applied against the taxpayer's tax liability in those years.(d) Nonrefundable.--A tax credit is not refundable and may not be carried back to a prior taxable year.Section 1906-L. Pass-through entities.(a) Allocation.--If a pass-through entity receives approval of a tax credit and does not have an eligible tax liability 20260HB2720PN3802 - 6 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 against which the tax credit may be applied, the pass-through entity may allocate all or a portion of the tax credit to its shareholders, owners or members in proportion to their ownership interests or as otherwise agreed in writing.(b) Application.--A shareholder, owner or member to whom a tax credit is allocated under subsection (a) may apply the tax credit in the same manner and subject to the same limitations as the pass-through entity, except that the tax credit may not be reallocated.Section 1907-L. Recapture.If, within five years of certification, a taxpayer removes, materially alters or fails to maintain green infrastructure for which a tax credit was granted under this article, or if the department determines that a project deemed certified under section 1904-L(c)(2) did not meet the requirements of this article, the Department of Revenue may recapture from the taxpayer all or a portion of the tax credit granted, in the manner provided for the assessment and collection of tax under Article IV.Section 1908-L. Guidelines and regulations.The department and the Department of Revenue shall jointly develop written guidelines for the implementation of this article. The guidelines shall be in effect until the department and the Department of Revenue promulgate regulations for the implementation of this article.Section 1909-L. The report shall include:(1) the number of applications received and approved;(2) the total amount of tax credits approved;(3) the types of green infrastructure installed; and(4) the counties in which qualified projects were completed during the preceding fiscal year.Section 3.
