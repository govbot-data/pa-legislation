AN ACT
Amending the act of July 7, 1947 (P.L.1368, No.542), entitled
"An act amending, revising and consolidating the laws
relating to delinquent county, city, except of the first and
second class and second class A, borough, town, township,
school district, except of the first class and school
districts within cities of the second class A, and
institution district taxes, providing when, how and upon what
property, and to what extent liens shall be allowed for such
taxes, the return and entering of claims therefor; the
collection and adjudication of such claims, sales of real
property, including seated and unseated lands, subject to the
lien of such tax claims; the disposition of the proceeds
thereof, including State taxes and municipal claims recovered
and the redemption of property; providing for the discharge
and divestiture by certain tax sales of all estates in
property and of mortgages and liens on such property, and the
proceedings therefor; creating a Tax Claim Bureau in each
county, except counties of the first and second class, to act
as agent for taxing districts; defining its powers and
duties, including sales of property, the management of
property taken in sequestration, and the management, sale and
disposition of property heretofore sold to the county
commissioners, taxing districts and trustees at tax sales;
providing a method for the service of process and notices;
imposing duties on taxing districts and their officers and on
tax collectors, and certain expenses on counties and for
their reimbursement by taxing districts; providing for a
county demolition and rehabilitation fund; and repealing
existing laws," in sale of property, providing for protection
of senior homeowners experiencing financial hardship.

hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of July 7, 1947 (P.L.1368, No.542), known
as the Real Estate Tax Sale Law, is amended by adding a section
to read:
Section 620. Protection Of Senior Homeowners Experiencing
Financial Hardship.-- (a) An eligible property owner may apply
for protection under this section at any time prior to the
court's confirmation of a sale under this article . Upon receipt
of a completed application in a form and manner specified by the
bureau, the bureau shall stay all sale proceedings under this
article pending a determination. The bureau may deny an
application that is materially incomplete or that contains
information known by the bureau to be false. The bureau shall
provide written notice of the deficiency and provide a
reasonable opportunity to cure the deficiency.
(b) The bureau may not conduct an upset sale, judicial sale
or other sale under this article of property owned by an
eligible property owner who has applied for and been approved
for protection under this section.
(c) The bureau shall develop and implement, with the
eligible property owner, a repayment plan for the taxes due on
the property. The repayment plan shall be specified in a written
agreement with the bureau under section 603 and administered in
accordance with section 603. If the eligible property owner dies
while protection under this section applies, the eligible
property owner's personal representative may, during the period
that protection continues under subsection (f)(3), perform the
repayment plan on behalf of the eligible property owner's
estate.
(d) Nothing in this section shall be construed to affect the

(e) The bureau shall provide written notice of the

(f) Protection under this section shall terminate only if

(3) The eligible property owner dies, except that protection

(g) The bureau shall review continued eligibility under this

demonstrate continued eligibility. Review by the bureau shall be
limited to verification that the eligible property owner
continues to meet the age, residency and financial hardship
requirements of this section. If the bureau determines that the
eligible property owner no longer meets the eligibility
requirements, the bureau shall provide written notice and an
opportunity to respond before the bureau terminates protection
under this section.

(i) As used in this section, the following words and phrases
shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless
the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Department," shall mean the Department of Community and
Economic Development.
"Eligible property owner," shall mean an individual who:
(1) is sixty-five (65) years of age or older at the time the
bureau initiates proceedings under this act;
(2) owns and occupies the property as the person's principal
residence; and
(3) demonstrates financial hardship.
"Financial hardship," shall mean a household income at or
below one hundred per centum (100%) of the Federal poverty
guidelines or other documented circumstances as determined by
the department, including medical expenses, disability or
caregiving obligations, which materially impair the ability of
an eligible property owner to pay real estate taxes.
"Household income," shall mean household income as defined in
section 1303 of the act of June 27, 2006 (1st Sp.Sess.,
P.L.1873, No.1), known as the "Taxpayer Relief Act."
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
