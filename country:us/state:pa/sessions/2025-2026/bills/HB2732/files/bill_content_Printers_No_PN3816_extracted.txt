The act of July 7, 1947 (P.L.1368, No.542), known as the Real Estate Tax Sale Law, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 620. Protection Of Senior Homeowners Experiencing Financial Hardship.-- (a) An eligible property owner may apply for protection under this section at any time prior to the court's confirmation of a sale under this article . Upon receipt of a completed application in a form and manner specified by the bureau, the bureau shall stay all sale proceedings under this article pending a determination. The bureau may deny an application that is materially incomplete or that contains information known by the bureau to be false. The bureau shall provide written notice of the deficiency and provide a reasonable opportunity to cure the deficiency.(b) The bureau may not conduct an upset sale, judicial sale or other sale under this article of property owned by an eligible property owner who has applied for and been approved for protection under this section.(c) The bureau shall develop and implement, with the eligible property owner, a repayment plan for the taxes due on the property. The repayment plan shall be specified in a written agreement with the bureau under section 603 and administered in accordance with section 603. If the eligible property owner dies while protection under this section applies, the eligible property owner's personal representative may, during the period that protection continues under subsection (f)(3), perform the repayment plan on behalf of the eligible property owner's estate.(d) Nothing in this section shall be construed to affect the 20260HB2732PN3816 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 attachment, priority or enforcement of a tax lien or the accrual of interest, penalties or costs, except that the bureau may not proceed to sale while protection under this section applies.(e) The bureau shall provide written notice of the protection available under this section with each notice issued under section 602.(f) Protection under this section shall terminate only if any of the following apply:(1) The property is conveyed or transferred to another owner.(2) The property ceases to be the eligible property owner's principal residence.(3) The eligible property owner dies, except that protection under this section shall continue for a period of up to twelve (12) months after the eligible property owner's death to permit administration of the eligible property owner's estate.(g) The bureau shall review continued eligibility under this section at least once every two (2) years. The eligible property owner shall provide documentation requested by the bureau to demonstrate continued eligibility. Review by the bureau shall be limited to verification that the eligible property owner continues to meet the age, residency and financial hardship requirements of this section. If the bureau determines that the eligible property owner no longer meets the eligibility requirements, the bureau shall provide written notice and an opportunity to respond before the bureau terminates protection under this section.(h) The department, in consultation with each bureau, shall develop uniform application forms and documentation requirements, establish standards and procedures for verifying 20260HB2732PN3816 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 age, residency and financial hardship and post the application form and other information deemed necessary by the department on the department's publicly accessible Internet website. The Department of Revenue shall assist the bureau, upon request, with verification of financial hardship.(i) As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Department," shall mean the Department of Community and Economic Development."Eligible property owner," shall mean an individual who:(1) is sixty-five (65) years of age or older at the time the bureau initiates proceedings under this act;(2) owns and occupies the property as the person's principal residence; and(3) demonstrates financial hardship."Financial hardship," shall mean a household income at or below one hundred per centum (100%) of the Federal poverty guidelines or other documented circumstances as determined by the department, including medical expenses, disability or caregiving obligations, which materially impair the ability of an eligible property owner to pay real estate taxes."Household income," shall mean household income as defined in section 1303 of the act of June 27, 2006 (1st Sp.Sess., P.L.1873, No.1), known as the "Taxpayer Relief Act."Section 2.
