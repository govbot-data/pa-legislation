A RESOLUTION
Designating September 28, 2026, as "Green Star Veterans and
Families Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Veterans of the United States Armed Forces served
our country with honor, courage and unwavering commitment to
protecting our freedoms and securing our national defense; and
WHEREAS, Many veterans who have bravely served continue to
face significant mental health challenges upon returning home,
including invisible wounds that can profoundly impact their
daily lives; and
WHEREAS, Mental health conditions, including posttraumatic
stress disorder, depression and traumatic brain injury often
intensify over time and, without adequate support, can increase
the risk of suicide; and
WHEREAS, An estimated 22 veterans are tragically lost to
suicide every day, 6,407 veterans took their lives in 2022 and
over 140,000 veterans have died by suicide since September 11,
2001; and

WHEREAS, Despite these alarming numbers, Green Star Veterans,
veterans who have lost their lives to suicide in connection with
their military service, are not formally recognized in our
Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, Every veteran whose life ends as a result of their
service deserves to be honored with the same dignity; and
WHEREAS, The families of veterans lost to suicide deserve the
assurance that their loved ones' service, sacrifice and memory
will be respectfully preserved and recognized; and
WHEREAS, Green Star Families serve an essential role in
sharing their loved ones' stories, spreading public awareness of
the toll service-related mental health challenges have on a
veteran and advocating for stronger prevention and support
initiatives to protect future veterans and their families; and
WHEREAS, The Green Star Service Banner serves as a symbol to
honor our veterans who have lost their lives to suicide in
connection with their military service and should be recognized
in our Commonwealth; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate designate September 28, 2026, as
"Green Star Veterans and Families Day" in Pennsylvania; and be
it further
RESOLVED, That the Senate officially recognize the Green Star
Service Banner; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the Senate honor veterans whose lives were
lost to suicide, to recognize the resilience and dedication of
the families they leave behind and to raise awareness of the
ongoing mental health and suicide crisis affecting those who
have served.
