2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2854 PN 4036 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 4036 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2854 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY DAVIDSON, CUTLER, GILLEN, PARKER, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ AND INGLIS, OCTOBER 13, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON INSURANCE, OCTOBER 14, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of May 17, 1921 (P.L.682, No.284), entitled "An act relating to insurance; amending, revising, and consolidating the law providing for the incorporation of insurance companies, and the regulation, supervision, and protection of home and foreign insurance companies, Lloyds associations, reciprocal and inter-insurance exchanges, and fire insurance rating bureaus, and the regulation and supervision of insurance carried by such companies, associations, and exchanges, including insurance carried by the State Workmen's Insurance Fund; providing penalties; and repealing existing laws," in fire and marine insurance, further providing for municipal certificate required prior to payment of fire loss claims.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 508 of the act of May 17, 1921 (P.L.682, No.284), known as The Insurance Company Law of 1921, is amended by adding a subsection to read:Section 508. Municipal Certificate Required Prior to Payment of Fire Loss Claims.--* * *(d.1) (1) Except as otherwise provided in this subsection, if, with respect to a loss of buildings or other structure to which subsection (c) of this section applies, the designated 1234567891011121314151617181920212223 officer of the municipality fails to receive reasonable proof, no later than one hundred twenty (120) days after the proceeds are received by the municipality, that repairs, removal or securing of the buildings or other structure have been completed, the municipality shall use the proceeds to repair, remove or secure the buildings or other structure in order for the buildings or other structure to comply with municipal code requirements and applicable ordinances of the municipality.(2) The municipality may extend the one hundred twenty-day time period under paragraph (1) of this subsection for up to two hundred forty-five (245) days.(3) If the municipality received the proceeds on or before the effective date of this paragraph, the time period for receipt of reasonable proof under paragraph (1) shall be one year after the municipality received the proceeds.(4) The municipality shall return to the named insured any portion of the proceeds not used in accordance with this subsection.(5) If the municipality fails to return the proceeds as required under this section, the named insured may file a civil action against the municipality for the return of the proceeds.(6) An action under this subsection must be filed no later than three years after the municipality received the proceeds or one year after the effective date of this paragraph, whichever is later.* * *Section 2.
