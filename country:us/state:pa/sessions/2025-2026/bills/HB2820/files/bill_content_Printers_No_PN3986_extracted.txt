2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2820 PN 3986 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3986 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2820 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY RIGBY, SMITH, JAMES, GUENST, ANDERSON, METZGAR, WALLEN, VEROBISH, KAUFFMAN, DEASY, ROWE AND GILLEN, OCTOBER 1, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, OCTOBER 2, 2026 AN ACTDesignating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 46807, on that portion of State Route 2007 over the South Fork Little Conemaugh River in Summerhill Township, Cambria County, as Army Specialist Four Anthony Michael Notich Memorial Bridge.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Army Specialist Four Anthony Michael Notich Memorial Bridge.(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) Anthony Michael Notich was born October 28, 1948, in Lloydell.(2) Army Specialist Four (Spec. Four) Notich was a class of 1967 graduate from Forest Hills High School and worked at U.S. Steel in Moxham, Johnstown, prior to being drafted.(3) Outside of work, Spec. Four Notich enjoyed hunting, fishing and softball.(4) Spec. Four Notich began his tour in Vietnam on April 123456789101112131415161718 7, 1969. He served as a light weapons infantrymen with A Company, 3rd Battalion, 47th Infantry, 9th Infantry Division.(5) Spec. Four Notich was killed in action on June 16, 1969, at 20 years of age in Kien Hoa Province, South Vietnam. Spec. Four Notich died from wounds sustained from the detonation of a hostile explosive device.(6) Spec. Four Notich is honored on Panel 22W, Line 59 of the Vietnam Veterans Memorial in Washington, DC.(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key 46807, located on that portion of State Route 2007 over the South Fork Little Conemaugh River in Summerhill Township, Cambria County, is designated the Army Specialist Four Anthony Michael Notich Memorial Bridge.(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to traffic in both directions on the bridge.Section 2. Effective date.This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2820PN3986 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718
