AN ACT
Designating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 46807, on that
portion of State Route 2007 over the South Fork Little
Conemaugh River in Summerhill Township, Cambria County, as
Army Specialist Four Anthony Michael Notich Memorial Bridge.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Army Specialist Four Anthony Michael Notich Memorial
Bridge.
(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as
follows:
(1) Anthony Michael Notich was born October 28, 1948, in
Lloydell.
(2) Army Specialist Four (Spec. Four) Notich was a class
of 1967 graduate from Forest Hills High School and worked at
U.S. Steel in Moxham, Johnstown, prior to being drafted.
(3) Outside of work, Spec. Four Notich enjoyed hunting,
fishing and softball.
(4) Spec. Four Notich began his tour in Vietnam on April

7, 1969. He served as a light weapons infantrymen with A
Company, 3rd Battalion, 47th Infantry, 9th Infantry Division.
(5) Spec. Four Notich was killed in action on June 16,
1969, at 20 years of age in Kien Hoa Province, South Vietnam.
Spec. Four Notich died from wounds sustained from the
detonation of a hostile explosive device.
(6) Spec. Four Notich is honored on Panel 22W, Line 59
of the Vietnam Veterans Memorial in Washington, DC.
(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key
46807, located on that portion of State Route 2007 over the
South Fork Little Conemaugh River in Summerhill Township,
Cambria County, is designated the Army Specialist Four Anthony
Michael Notich Memorial Bridge.
(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and
maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to
traffic in both directions on the bridge.
Section 2. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 60 days.
