MACKENZIE, HILL-EVANS, HADDOCK, SAMUELSON, BRENNAN, MENTZER, HOHENSTEIN, DEASY, NEILSON AND MALAGARI, OCTOBER 15, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, OCTOBER 15, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating December 13, 2026, as the "390th Birthday of the National Guard" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The National Guard was assembled as the first organized militia of the United States in Boston, Massachusetts, through an act legislated by the Massachusetts General Court on December 13, 1636; andWHEREAS, The Massachusetts General Court declared that the colony's existing militia companies be organized into three regiments: North, South and East; andWHEREAS, Four current Army National Guard units descended from those original three regiments and remain the oldest units in the United States military; andWHEREAS, Citizens first assembled for military training in Salem, Massachusetts, during the spring of 1637, which became known as the "First Muster" of a militia regiment; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania National Guard was assembled as the 12345678910111213141516 first militia of this Commonwealth in Philadelphia when Ben Franklin established the Associators in 1747; andWHEREAS, During the 19th century, the term "National Guard" began to replace "militia" in honor of Marquis de Lafayette and the contributions of France's "Guarde Nacional de Paris" during the Revolutionary War; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania's militia was redesignated as the "National Guard of Pennsylvania" on April 7, 1870; andWHEREAS, Throughout its history, the National Guard has expanded and organized into units in each of the 50 states, the District of Columbia, the Virgin Islands, Puerto Rico and Guam; andWHEREAS, The National Guard has a dual duty to both state and nation, consisting of "Citizen Soldiers" and airmen who provide protection from natural disasters and other threats, foreign and domestic; andWHEREAS, National Guard members train regularly to sharpen their military readiness skills in the name of protecting life and property, maintaining peace and order and defending our interests at home and abroad; andWHEREAS, The National Guard has served our communities, protected our Commonwealth and defended our country for more than three centuries; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate December 13, 2026, as the "390th Birthday of the National Guard" in Pennsylvania;
