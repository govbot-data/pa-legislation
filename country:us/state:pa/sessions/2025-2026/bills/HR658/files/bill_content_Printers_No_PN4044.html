A RESOLUTION
Designating December 13, 2026, as the "390th Birthday of the
National Guard" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The National Guard was assembled as the first
organized militia of the United States in Boston, Massachusetts,
through an act legislated by the Massachusetts General Court on
December 13, 1636; and
WHEREAS, The Massachusetts General Court declared that the
colony's existing militia companies be organized into three
regiments: North, South and East; and
WHEREAS, Four current Army National Guard units descended
from those original three regiments and remain the oldest units
in the United States military; and
WHEREAS, Citizens first assembled for military training in
Salem, Massachusetts, during the spring of 1637, which became
known as the "First Muster" of a militia regiment; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania National Guard was assembled as the

first militia of this Commonwealth in Philadelphia when Ben
Franklin established the Associators in 1747; and
WHEREAS, During the 19th century, the term "National Guard"
began to replace "militia" in honor of Marquis de Lafayette and
the contributions of France's "Guarde Nacional de Paris" during
the Revolutionary War; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania's militia was redesignated as the
"National Guard of Pennsylvania" on April 7, 1870; and
WHEREAS, Throughout its history, the National Guard has
expanded and organized into units in each of the 50 states, the
District of Columbia, the Virgin Islands, Puerto Rico and Guam;
and
WHEREAS, The National Guard has a dual duty to both state and
nation, consisting of "Citizen Soldiers" and airmen who provide
protection from natural disasters and other threats, foreign and
domestic; and
WHEREAS, National Guard members train regularly to sharpen
their military readiness skills in the name of protecting life
and property, maintaining peace and order and defending our
interests at home and abroad; and
WHEREAS, The National Guard has served our communities,
protected our Commonwealth and defended our country for more
than three centuries; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate
December 13, 2026, as the "390th Birthday of the National Guard"
in Pennsylvania; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives celebrate and
congratulate the United States National Guard on its 390th
birthday; and be it further
