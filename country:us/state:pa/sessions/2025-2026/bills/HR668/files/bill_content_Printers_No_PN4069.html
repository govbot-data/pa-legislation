A RESOLUTION
Noting with sadness the death of the Honorable Frank J.
Pistella, III, a distinguished former member of the House of
Representatives from Allegheny County.
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives honors the memory of
Frank J. Pistella, who died June 20, 2025, at 74 years of age;
and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives commends Mr. Pistella's
leadership and service to the House and the General Assembly
from the Session of 1979-1980 through the Session of 2005-2006;
and
WHEREAS, A resident of the East End neighborhoods of Garfield
and Bloomfield, Mr. Pistella faithfully represented the 21st
District, comprising the City of Pittsburgh and neighboring
suburbs; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Pistella was a member of the Irish Caucus, the
Italian-American Caucus and the History Caucus; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Pistella was appointed to the Pennsylvania
Intra-Governmental Council on Long-Term Care, the Local

Government Commission and the Legislative Budget and Finance
Committee; and
WHEREAS, Born April 22, 1951, a son of Rita B. Kineavy
Pistella and Frank J. "Sonny" Pistella, Jr., Mr. Pistella
attended Central District Catholic High School in Pittsburgh's
Oakland neighborhood; and
WHEREAS, Faith, scholarship and service were central to Mr.
Pistella's student experience there; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Pistella was a history major at John Carroll
University; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Pistella's education continued at the John F.
Kennedy School of Government at Harvard University and Widener
University School of Law; and
WHEREAS, After 14 consecutive terms as a legislator, Mr.
Pistella worked as a Neighborhood Legal Services public benefits
attorney; and
WHEREAS, In this way, Mr. Pistella continued his important
work: resolving problems, effectuating goals and safeguarding
individuals and families in western Pennsylvania; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Pistella was a member of St. Lawrence O'Toole
Parish, Garfield; and
WHEREAS, A Mass of Christian Burial for Mr. Pistella is being
conducted at Saint Aidan Parish, St. Alexis Church, Wexford;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives note with sadness
the death of the Honorable Frank J. Pistella, III, a
distinguished former member of the House of Representatives from
Allegheny County; and be it further
RESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to
Mr. Pistella's daughter, Lauren Pistella Ley.
