Kineavy Pistella and Frank J. "Sonny" Pistella, Jr., Mr. Pistella attended Central District Catholic High School in Pittsburgh's Oakland neighborhood; andWHEREAS, Faith, scholarship and service were central to Mr. Pistella's student experience there; andWHEREAS, Mr. Pistella was a history major at John Carroll University; andWHEREAS, Mr. Pistella's education continued at the John F. Kennedy School of Government at Harvard University and Widener University School of Law; andWHEREAS, After 14 consecutive terms as a legislator, Mr. Pistella worked as a Neighborhood Legal Services public benefits attorney; andWHEREAS, In this way, Mr. Pistella continued his important work: resolving problems, effectuating goals and safeguarding individuals and families in western Pennsylvania; andWHEREAS, Mr. Pistella was a member of St. Lawrence O'Toole Parish, Garfield; andWHEREAS, A Mass of Christian Burial for Mr. Pistella is being conducted at Saint Aidan Parish, St. Alexis Church, Wexford; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives note with sadness the death of the Honorable Frank J.
