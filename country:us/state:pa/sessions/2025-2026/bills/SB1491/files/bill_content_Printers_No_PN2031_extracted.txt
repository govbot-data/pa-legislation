Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1513. High-voltage transmission lines.(a) Consideration of corridors.--(1) An entity responsible for siting a new electric transmission facility, including a high-voltage transmission line, shall consider corridors in the following order of priority:(i) E xisting utility corridors. (ii) Highway corridors.(iii) New corridors.(2) When reviewing a siting application filed under 52 Pa. Code Ch. 57 Subch.
