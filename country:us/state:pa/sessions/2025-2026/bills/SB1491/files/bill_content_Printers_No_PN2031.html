AN ACT
Amending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in service and facilities, providing
for high-voltage transmission lines.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1513. High-voltage transmission lines.
(a) Consideration of corridors.--
(1) An entity responsible for siting a new electric
transmission facility, including a high-voltage transmission
line, shall consider corridors in the following order of
priority:
(i) E xisting utility corridors.
(ii) Highway corridors.
(iii) New corridors.
(2) When reviewing a siting application filed under 52
Pa. Code Ch. 57 Subch. G (relating to commission review of

considerations, reliability of the electric system,
protection of the environment and the rights of private
property owners.
(b) Permit.--An electric distribution company or developer
may apply for a permit from the department to construct, place
or maintain a high-voltage transmission line within a State-
owned public right-of-way or along any State-owned highway,
federally aided State highway, controlled access highway,
interstate highway or roadway.
(c) Approval.--The department shall approve a permit
application under subsection (b) if the application meets the
requirements of this section unless the department denies the
application under subsection (d).
(d) Denial.--The department may deny a permit application if
the department determines that the denial is necessary to
protect public safety or ensure the proper functioning of the
highway.
(e) Reasons for denial.--If the department denies an
application, the department shall provide the reasons for the
denial to the electric distribution company or developer and
post the reasons on the department's publicly accessible
Internet website . Within 90 days of the denial, the department
shall submit the reasons for the denial to all of the following
for review:

(f) Process.--If the department denies a permit application ,
the electric distribution company or developer may modify and
resubmit the application for consideration by the department
under this section.
(g) Coordinated activities.--Upon written request, the
department shall coordinate with an electric distribution
company or developer to review requested highway corridors for
possible locations at which high-voltage transmission lines may
be permitted .
(h) Information on future projects.-- In connection with
coordination under subsection (g), the department shall share
with the requesting electric distribution company or developer
all known plans for potential future projects that could affect
the placement of a high-voltage transmission line.
(i) Constructability.--If the department and an electric
distribution company or developer identify a route for which a
permit may be issued, the electric distribution company or
developer shall prepare a constructability report in
consultation with the department. The report shall include the

(j) Commission authority.--Nothing in this section shall be
construed to limit or otherwise affect the authority of the
commission under this title.
(k) Agricultural security areas and agricultural
conservation easements.--When siting high-voltage transmission
lines, the department, commission, public utilities and
developers shall avoid agricultural security areas and
agricultural conservation easements as defined under section 3
of the act of June 30, 1981 (P.L.128, No.43), known as the
Agricultural Area Security Law.
(l) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Department." The Department of Transportation of the
Commonwealth.
"Developer." A person that develops a high-voltage
transmission line project.
"Electric distribution company." As defined in section 2803
(relating to definitions).
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
