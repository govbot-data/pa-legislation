(d) Sources and deposits.--Money available to the account shall include appropriations and transfers from the General Fund, special funds, Federal funds and other sources of revenue made available to the account.Section 1303-F. Homeless Education Assisting with Academic Resources for Displaced Students Grant Program.(a) Establishment and purpose.--The Homeless Education Assisting with Academic Resources for Displaced Students Grant Program is established within the commission to award grants to school entities and community-based organizations. Grants awarded under the program shall be used to positively impact school attendance and graduation rates, improve academic performance and reduce chronic absenteeism among students experiencing homelessness . (b) Application for grants.--The commission shall establish the form and manner for submitting an application for a grant under the program. A school entity or community-based organization seeking a grant under the program must submit a completed application to the commission. The commission shall accept applications annually, subject to the availability of money in the account.Section 1304-F. Award of grants.(a) Awards.--The commission shall award grants under the 20260HB2796PN3921 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 program on a competitive basis for activities that are consistent with one or more of the eligible purposes under subsection (c).(b) Criteria.--The commission shall:(1) Develop criteria for grantees under the program.(2) Award grants in amounts of not less than $20,000 and not more than $200,000 based on community needs.(3) Give priority to applications that demonstrate any of the following:(i) The proposed project will serve a high-need community, including an area with a greater proportion of students experiencing homelessness.(ii) The proposed project will serve students experiencing homelessness who live in a rural school district.(iii) The applicant has demonstrated funding gaps in the identification or support of students experiencing homelessness.(4) Ensure that grants awarded under the program are geographically dispersed throughout this Commonwealth.(c) Eligible purposes.--A school entity or community-based organization may use a grant awarded under the program for any of the following : (1) Providing short-term housing supports for students experiencing homelessness , including hotel or motel stays.
