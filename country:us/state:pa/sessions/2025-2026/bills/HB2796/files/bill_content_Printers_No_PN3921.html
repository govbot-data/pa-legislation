AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," establishing the Homeless Education
Assisting with Academic Resources for Displaced Students
Grant Program and the Homeless Education Assisting with
Academic Resources for Displaced Students Account; and
imposing duties on the Pennsylvania Commission on Crime and
Delinquency.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known
as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding an
article to read:
ARTICLE XIII-F
HOMELESS EDUCATION ASSISTING WITH
ACADEMIC RESOURCES FOR DISPLACED STUDENTS

Section 1303-F. Homeless Education Assisting with Academic
Resources for Displaced Students Grant Program.
(a) Establishment and purpose.--The Homeless Education
Assisting with Academic Resources for Displaced Students Grant
Program is established within the commission to award grants to
school entities and community-based organizations. Grants
awarded under the program shall be used to positively impact
school attendance and graduation rates, improve academic
performance and reduce chronic absenteeism among students
experiencing homelessness .
(b) Application for grants.--The commission shall establish
the form and manner for submitting an application for a grant
under the program. A school entity or community-based
organization seeking a grant under the program must submit a
completed application to the commission. The commission shall
accept applications annually, subject to the availability of
money in the account.

(b) Criteria.--The commission shall:
(1) Develop criteria for grantees under the program.
(2) Award grants in amounts of not less than $20,000 and
not more than $200,000 based on community needs.
(3) Give priority to applications that demonstrate any
of the following:
(i) The proposed project will serve a high-need
community, including an area with a greater proportion of
students experiencing homelessness.
(ii) The proposed project will serve students
experiencing homelessness who live in a rural school
district.
(iii) The applicant has demonstrated funding gaps in
the identification or support of students experiencing
homelessness.
(4) Ensure that grants awarded under the program are
geographically dispersed throughout this Commonwealth.
(c) Eligible purposes.--A school entity or community-based
organization may use a grant awarded under the program for any
of the following :
(1) Providing short-term housing supports for students
experiencing homelessness , including hotel or motel stays.
(2) Providing emergency funds to prevent student
displacement.
(3) Providing flexible transportation solutions for
students experiencing homelessness to and from school and
extracurricular programs.
