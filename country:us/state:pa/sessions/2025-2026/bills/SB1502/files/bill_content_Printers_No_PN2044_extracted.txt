Section 603 of the act of May 1, 1933 (P.L.103, No.69), known as The Second Class Township Code, is amended to read:Section 603. Monthly Meetings; Quorum; and Voting.--(a) The board of supervisors shall meet for the transaction of business at least once each month at a time and place determined by the board of supervisors.
