2025-2026 Regular Session HR 606 PN 3851 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3851 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.606 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY GUZMAN, HILL-EVANS, SANCHEZ, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, PASHINSKI AND McANDREW, AUGUST 31, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, AUGUST 31, 2026 A RESOLUTIONUrging the Department of Transportation to conduct a planning and environmental linkages study of U.S. Route 222 corridor from the U.S. Route 422 interchange in Berks County to the Interstate 78 interchange in Lehigh County.WHEREAS, U.S. Route 222 is a critical transportation corridor serving Berks County, Lehigh County and surrounding communities by connecting the Reading metropolitan area, the Lehigh Valley and the Interstate Highway System; andWHEREAS, The corridor provides an essential connection for commuters, freight movement, commercial traffic and regional economic activity, including access to major employment centers, manufacturing facilities and distribution operations throughout the eastern part of this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, Since the completion of the Commonwealth's previous comprehensive evaluation of the U.S. Route 222 corridor in 2003, the region has experienced substantial changes in population, land use, economic development and transportation demand, including significant growth in freight activity; andWHEREAS, Reading and the Lehigh Valley represent significant 12345678910111213141516171819 population and employment centers in the eastern part of this Commonwealth, and improving transportation connectivity between these regions is important to regional mobility, safety and economic competitiveness; andWHEREAS, Increased traffic volumes and heavy commercial vehicle activity have contributed to recurring congestion, reduced travel-time reliability and transportation challenges along portions of the corridor, demonstrating the need for updated long-range transportation planning; andWHEREAS, A comprehensive planning and environmental linkages study would provide current information necessary to evaluate transportation needs, identify reasonable alternatives, estimate costs, assess environmental and community impacts and determine potential funding strategies for future improvements; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives urge the Department of Transportation to conduct a comprehensive planning and environmental linkages study of the U.S. Route 222 corridor from the U.S. Route 422 interchange in Berks County to the Interstate 78 interchange in Lehigh County; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the study evaluate existing and projected transportation conditions, including traffic demand, freight movement, highway safety, congestion, travel-time reliability, emergency access and regional economic impacts;
