A RESOLUTION
Urging the Department of Transportation to conduct a planning
and environmental linkages study of U.S. Route 222 corridor
from the U.S. Route 422 interchange in Berks County to the
Interstate 78 interchange in Lehigh County.
WHEREAS, U.S. Route 222 is a critical transportation corridor
serving Berks County, Lehigh County and surrounding communities
by connecting the Reading metropolitan area, the Lehigh Valley
and the Interstate Highway System; and
WHEREAS, The corridor provides an essential connection for
commuters, freight movement, commercial traffic and regional
economic activity, including access to major employment centers,
manufacturing facilities and distribution operations throughout
the eastern part of this Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, Since the completion of the Commonwealth's previous
comprehensive evaluation of the U.S. Route 222 corridor in 2003,
the region has experienced substantial changes in population,
land use, economic development and transportation demand,
including significant growth in freight activity; and
WHEREAS, Reading and the Lehigh Valley represent significant

WHEREAS, Increased traffic volumes and heavy commercial

WHEREAS, A comprehensive planning and environmental linkages

transportation needs, identify reasonable alternatives, estimate
costs, assess environmental and community impacts and determine
potential funding strategies for future improvements; therefore
be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives urge the
Department of Transportation to conduct a comprehensive planning
and environmental linkages study of the U.S. Route 222 corridor

Interstate 78 interchange in Lehigh County; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the study evaluate existing and projected
transportation conditions, including traffic demand, freight
movement, highway safety, congestion, travel-time reliability,
emergency access and regional economic impacts; and be it
further

reasonable transportation alternatives to address current and
future corridor needs, including operational improvements,
