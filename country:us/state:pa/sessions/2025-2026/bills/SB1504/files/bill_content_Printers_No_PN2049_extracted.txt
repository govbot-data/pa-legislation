Purpose and intent.3512. Informal dispute resolution process.3513. Data collection.3514. Liability and responsibility.§ 3511. Purpose and intent.The purpose of this subchapter is to establish an informal review process for long-term care nursing facilities to dispute Department of Health survey findings using an independent agent. This informal review process is intended to ensure that a 20260SB1504PN2049 - 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 statement of deficiencies, as maintained in Federal and State data systems, accurately identifies a long-term care nursing facility's actual state of compliance with applicable regulations by providing nursing facilities with the opportunity to redress grievances arising during the survey process prior to the entry of the survey results in the Federal data system and without need to engage in formal litigation.§ 3512. Informal dispute resolution process. (a) Establishment of an independent IDR process.--(1) The department shall establish an IDR process to determine whether a cited deficiency contained in a statement of deficiencies against a long-term care nursing facility should be upheld.(2) (i) The department shall designate Pennsylvania's QIO as the independent IDR agent.(ii) If Pennsylvania's QIO is unable to serve as the independent IDR agent, the department, in consultation with the Health Policy Board, shall designate the QIO of another state that has experience in conducting informal dispute resolutions for a state survey agency as the independent IDR agent.(iii) If no other QIO is available to serve as the independent IDR agent, the department, in consultation with the Health Policy Board, shall designate as the independent IDR agent an independent review organization that is accredited by the Utilization Review Accreditation Commission.(iv) The department shall enter into a sole source contract with the independent IDR agent as necessary to implement the provisions of this subchapter.20260SB1504PN2049 - 6 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (3) The department shall obtain all necessary approvals from the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services to contract with the independent IDR process.(4) The independent IDR agent shall offer long-term care nursing facilities that request the independent IDR process a choice of the following levels of review:(i) desk review;(ii) telephone review; or(iii) in-person review, including the utilization of video conferencing.(5) The independent IDR process may not replace or be a substitute for the existing informal dispute resolution process conducted by the department, but shall be an optional process that can be selected by long-term care nursing facilities on a fee-for-service basis.(6) The fee for the independent IDR process shall be established by the independent IDR agent, provided that the fees are approved by the department and consistent with law.(7) Independent IDR recommendations made by the independent IDR agent shall be subject to final review and approval by the department.(b) Minimum requirements of the independent IDR process.--(1) The department shall establish written policies and procedures governing the independent IDR process that comply with the provisions of 42 CFR 488.331 (relating to informal dispute resolution) and the State Operations Manual regarding the informal dispute resolution process.(2) The independent IDR procedures must include the following:(i) Within 10 business days of the end of the 20260SB1504PN2049 - 7 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 survey, the department shall, by certified mail or by the intranet of the department, if the long-term care nursing facility is connected to the intranet, transmit to the long-term care nursing facility a statement of alleged deficiencies.(ii) A long-term care nursing facility may request that an informal dispute resolution review be conducted by:(A) department staff not associated with the survey, at no charge; or(B) an independent IDR by the independent IDR agent designated by the department on a fee-for- service basis.(iii) A long-term care nursing facility may select only one method of review under subparagraph (ii).(iv) A long-term care nursing facility must submit its request for an independent IDR review and indicate its selection of the type of review and level of review in writing within the same 10-day period applicable to the long-term care nursing facility for submission of the plan of correction.(3) (i) Within 45 days of receipt of the request for an independent IDR by a long-term care nursing facility, the independent IDR agent shall issue a written decision to the long-term care nursing facility based upon its review of the facts, survey findings, State Operations Manual and applicable law.(ii) If the independent IDR agent sustains the deficiency, the independent IDR agent's written determination shall include the rationale for its 20260SB1504PN2049 - 8 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 decision and provide recommended action that the long-term care nursing facility can implement to achieve compliance.(iii) If the department disagrees with an independent IDR agent's determination that reverses a deficiency, the department shall provide a written explanation for its decision to nullify the independent IDR agent's report to the independent IDR agent and to the long-term care nursing facility.§ 3513. Right to pursue.A hospital may pursue formal dispute resolution regarding substantial and bona fide disputes of deficiencies that are cited during a survey and that:(1) involve immediate jeopardy findings for open hospitals;(2) prevent a closed hospital from reopening;(3) prevent a hospital that has not yet opened from beginning operations; or(4) involve State-assessed penalties amounting to $10,000 or more in the aggregate.§ 3532. Notice of intent.A hospital that wishes to pursue formal dispute resolution under this subchapter shall, not later than 10 days after receiving formal communication of deficiencies, provide to the department written notice of intent to pursue formal dispute resolution.§ 3533. Consolidation.All disputes of deficiencies contested under this subchapter by a hospital must be consolidated into a single dispute resolution proceeding such that the hospital may not appeal each finding as to deficiencies separately but must address all contested matters in a single dispute resolution process.§ 3534. Arbitrators.(a) Selection.--(1) Under the formal dispute resolution process:(i) a hospital shall select a single arbitrator with 20260SB1504PN2049 - 14 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 the mutual consent and agreement of the department; or(ii) each party shall select one arbitrator, who shall then jointly appoint a neutral third-party arbitrator.(2) Arbitrators shall be selected from an arbitration organization.(b) Disclosure.--Before accepting an appointment, an arbitrator must disclose to all parties any known facts that could affect the arbitrator's impartiality, including financial or personal interests in the outcome, and any prior relationships with the parties, counsel or witnesses.§ 3535. Hearing.(a) Scheduling and notice.--As soon as practicable, but not later than 10 days after receiving a request for a hearing, the arbitrators shall:(1) Fix a date for the hearing.(2) Notify both parties of the time and place of the hearing.(b) Purpose.--The purpose of the hearing is to:(1) facilitate a negotiated resolution or compromise; or(2) provide final resolution through the issuance of a binding ruling.(c) Conduct.--(1) The hearing shall be conducted by all the arbitrators but a majority may determine any issue and render a final decision.(2) If, during the course of the hearing, an arbitrator for any reason ceases to act, the remaining arbitrator or arbitrators appointed to act as neutrals may continue with the hearing and determine the controversy.20260SB1504PN2049 - 15 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 § 3536. Final decision.(a) Authorization.--If a compromise cannot be reached, the arbitrators shall be empowered to issue a decision that shall be binding on both parties.(b) Written and signed.--The decision of the arbitrators shall be in writing and shall be signed by the arbitrators.(c) Delivery.--The arbitrators shall deliver a copy of the decision to each party personally or by registered or certified mail, or as otherwise agreed to by the parties.(d) Deficiencies.-- If a hospital is successful during the formal dispute resolution process at demonstrating that deficiencies should not have been cited, the deficiencies shall be removed from the statement of deficiencies and any enforcement actions imposed solely as a result of those cited deficiencies shall be rescinded.§ 3537. Costs shall include the expenses and fees of the arbitrators and other administrative expenses, except counsel fees.(b) Exception.--Notwithstanding subsection (a), the arbitrators may award the costs under subsection (a) to the hospital as the prevailing party or may allocate the costs if the department has engaged in willful or malicious misconduct against the hospital.§ 3538.
