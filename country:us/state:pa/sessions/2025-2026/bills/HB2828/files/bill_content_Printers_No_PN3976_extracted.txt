Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Large Computational Load Customer and Consumer Protection Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Bring your own new capacity." The standard for a large computational load customer to secure new capacity resources to meet its electric demand. The term shall have the same meaning as defined in PJM's Interim Resource Adequacy Service tariff filing with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission at Docket 123456789101112131415161718 No. ER26-3515, including modifications to the definition upon Federal Energy Regulatory Commission approval of the filing or any successor filings that are substantially consistent with the purpose of PJM's Interim Resource Adequacy tariff filing."Commission." The Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission."Contract capacity." The mutually agreed peak demand of a large computational load customer for which an electric distribution company provides service, as specified in a service agreement."Delivery year." The 12-month period beginning on June 1 of one calendar year and ending on May 31 of the next calendar year, as used by PJM."Electric distribution company." As defined in 66 Pa.C.S. § 2803 (relating to definitions)."Exit fee." A dollar amount equal to the minimum monthly bill due from a large computational load customer to an electric distribution company under a service agreement multiplied by the number of months remaining in the service agreement term at the time of termination or reduction in contract capacity."Incremental cost." The total costs caused in whole or in part by a large computational load customer that would not have been incurred but for the electricity demand of the large computational load customer and that are directly attributable to the provision of electric service to the large computational load customer. The term includes the cost of infrastructure upgrades or improvements and service associated with capacity, energy, ancillary services, transmission, distribution and dedicated facilities."Interim Resource Adequacy Service" or "IRAS." Tariff for large computational load customers.(a) Prohibition of cost-shifting.--The incremental cost attributable to a large computational load customer may not be recovered, in whole or in part, from other customer classes.(b) Tariff filing and approval.--Within 90 days of the effective date of this subsection, each electric distribution company operating in this Commonwealth shall file a tariff with the commission addressing rates and terms for large computational load customers. The commission shall approve or modify the electric distribution company's large computational load customer tariff submission within 120 days after the public process as determined by the commission.(c) Tariff requirements.--An electric distribution company's large computational load customer tariff approved by the commission shall comply with subsection (a) and, at a minimum, with the following:(1) Require a service agreement to have a term of at least 15 years, inclusive of a load ramp period.(2) Enable a large computational load customer to 20260HB2828PN3976 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 designate a load ramp schedule over a period of up to five years.(3) Establish a minimum monthly billing demand, in megawatts, of at least 80% of a large computational load customer's contract capacity.(4) Establish a demand charge, in dollars per megawatt-month, which, when multiplied by the minimum monthly billing demand, is sufficient to recover the component of the incremental cost of serving a large computational load customer that does not vary based on its energy consumption and is not recovered through other methods.(5) Establish a minimum monthly bill, in dollars per month, which, when applied over the duration of the service agreement term, is estimated to be sufficient to recover the incremental cost incurred to serve the customer that is not otherwise recovered through direct payments. The minimum monthly bill shall include, at a minimum, the customer charge, the product of the minimum monthly billing demand and the demand charge and a charge based on the customer's volumetric energy consumption.(6) Assign the full costs of all distribution and transmission upgrades or improvements to a large computational load customer of which the customer primarily receives the benefits and would not have been required but for the interconnection of the customer, regardless of whether other customers will benefit from the upgrades or improvements. A large computational load customer shall pay for the full costs through a contribution in aid of construction provided prior to the start of construction of the infrastructure improvements or upgrades. A contribution 20260HB2828PN3976 - 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 in aid of construction may not be included in the electric distribution company's rate base.(7) Prior to entering into a service agreement with an electric distribution company, require a large computational load customer to demonstrate site control and any additional project development milestones necessary to demonstrate a reasonable degree of project readiness, as determined by the commission.(8) Allow a large computational load customer, without the payment of a fee or penalty, to reduce its contract capacity beginning five years after the service agreement commencement date, with at least 48 months written notice, by the following amounts:(i) No more than 10% if the reduced capacity is not reassigned to another customer.(ii) No more than 25% if the full amount of contract capacity being reduced is reassigned to another customer.(9) Require a large computational load customer that terminates its service agreement or reduces its contract capacity beyond the amounts specified in subsection (c)(8) to pay at least the amount of the exit fee if the customer provides at least 48 months written notice, or to pay at least two times the exit fee if the customer fails to provide at least 48 months written notice.(10) Require a large computational load customer to provide collateral in an amount equal to 50% of the minimum monthly bill multiplied by the number of months remaining in the service agreement term.
