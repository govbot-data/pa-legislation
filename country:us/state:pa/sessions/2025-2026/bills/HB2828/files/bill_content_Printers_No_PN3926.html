AN ACT
Providing for tariffs for large computational load customers and
for emergency load control procedures during regional
resource adequacy shortfalls; and imposing duties on the
Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Large
Computational Load Customer and Consumer Protection Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:

computational load customer to secure new capacity resources to
meet its electric demand. The term shall have the same meaning
as defined in PJM's Interim Resource Adequacy Service tariff

"Commission." The Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission.
"Contract capacity." The mutually agreed peak demand of a
large computational load customer for which an electric
distribution company provides service, as specified in a service
agreement.
"Delivery year." The 12-month period beginning on June 1 of
one calendar year and ending on May 31 of the next calendar
year, as used by PJM.
"Electric distribution company." As defined in 66 Pa.C.S. §
2803 (relating to definitions).
"Exit fee." A dollar amount equal to the minimum monthly
bill due from a large computational load customer to an electric
distribution company under a service agreement multiplied by the
number of months remaining in the service agreement term at the
time of termination or reduction in contract capacity.
"Incremental cost." The total costs caused in whole or in
part by a large computational load customer that would not have
been incurred but for the electricity demand of the large
computational load customer and that are directly attributable
to the provision of electric service to the large computational
load customer. The term includes the cost of infrastructure
upgrades or improvements and service associated with capacity,
energy, ancillary services, transmission, distribution and
dedicated facilities.

reduction program implemented jointly between PJM and electric

"Resource adequacy shortfall." A circumstance, as determined

Section 3. Tariff for large computational load customers.
(a) Prohibition of cost-shifting.--The incremental cost
attributable to a large computational load customer may not be
recovered, in whole or in part, from other customer classes.
(b) Tariff filing and approval.--Within 90 days of the
effective date of this subsection, each electric distribution
company operating in this Commonwealth shall file a tariff with
the commission addressing rates and terms for large
computational load customers. The commission shall approve or
modify the electric distribution company's large computational
load customer tariff submission within 120 days after the public
process as determined by the commission.
(c) Tariff requirements.--An electric distribution company's
large computational load customer tariff approved by the
commission shall comply with subsection (a) and, at a minimum,
with the following:
(1) Require a service agreement to have a term of at
least 15 years, inclusive of a load ramp period.
(2) Enable a large computational load customer to

jurisdiction of the commission shall update its emergency load
control procedures under 52 Pa. Code § 57.52 (relating to
emergency load control and energy conservation by electric
utilities) in a manner consistent with the following:

(3) Revisions to emergency load control procedures made

(b) Commission duties.--The commission shall:
(1) Approve or modify an electric distribution company's
update to its emergency load control procedures under this

(2) Ensure that updates to each electric distribution
company's emergency load control procedures under this
section are in place prior to June 1, 2027, and will remain
effective in all subsequent delivery years in which PJM
projects there will be a resource adequacy shortfall expected
to occur in the planning horizon that is caused primarily by
the addition of large computational load customers.
(3) Establish penalties for noncompliance with this
section.

including the authority to interpret this act and adopt any
order or regulation necessary, to implement this act.
