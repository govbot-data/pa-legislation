A RESOLUTION
Designating October 13, 2026, as the "Birthday of the United
States Navy" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The Continental Navy was founded in this
Commonwealth on October 13, 1775, when the Second Continental
Congress at Independence Hall in Philadelphia authorized the
first ships of the fleet; and
WHEREAS, The founders of this nation recognized that a navy
is essential to the strength and longevity of a nation by
providing authority to the Congress of the United States "To
provide and maintain a Navy" in Article I of the Constitution of
the United States; and
WHEREAS, The Continental Navy began a proud tradition,
carried out for 251 years by the United States Navy, to protect
this nation and pursue the causes of freedom held so dear by all
Americans; and
WHEREAS, Whether in peace or at war, United States citizens

WHEREAS, The core values of "Honor, Courage and Commitment"

and
WHEREAS, When the United States Navy was founded, it was
composed of a small fleet of just 27 ships that succeeded in
defending our independence; and
WHEREAS, Today, the United States Navy is made up of hundreds
of ships, thousands of aircraft and is the most capable,
respected and effective sea combat force in the world; and
WHEREAS, The United States Navy's inaugural "Harrisburg Navy
Week" was held from May 20 through 26, 2026, where more than 90
sailors gathered for a weeklong series of performances,
educational events and community service in celebration of the
250th anniversary of the United States; and
WHEREAS, On October 13, 2026, the United States Navy
celebrates its birthday; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate October
13, 2026, as the "Birthday of the United States Navy" in
Pennsylvania; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the
service and sacrifice of the service members in the United
States Navy.
