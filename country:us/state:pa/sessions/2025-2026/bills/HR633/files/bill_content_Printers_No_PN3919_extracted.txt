andWHEREAS, The founders of this nation recognized that a navy is essential to the strength and longevity of a nation by providing authority to the Congress of the United States "To provide and maintain a Navy" in Article I of the Constitution of the United States; andWHEREAS, The Continental Navy began a proud tradition, carried out for 251 years by the United States Navy, to protect this nation and pursue the causes of freedom held so dear by all Americans; andWHEREAS, Whether in peace or at war, United States citizens 12345678910111213141516 around the world can rest assured that the United States Navy is on watch, ever vigilant and ready to respond; andWHEREAS, The core values of "Honor, Courage and Commitment" are the guides by which United States sailors live and serve; andWHEREAS, When the United States Navy was founded, it was composed of a small fleet of just 27 ships that succeeded in defending our independence; andWHEREAS, Today, the United States Navy is made up of hundreds of ships, thousands of aircraft and is the most capable, respected and effective sea combat force in the world; andWHEREAS, The United States Navy's inaugural "Harrisburg Navy Week" was held from May 20 through 26, 2026, where more than 90 sailors gathered for a weeklong series of performances, educational events and community service in celebration of the 250th anniversary of the United States; andWHEREAS, On October 13, 2026, the United States Navy celebrates its birthday; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate October 13, 2026, as the "Birthday of the United States Navy" in Pennsylvania;
