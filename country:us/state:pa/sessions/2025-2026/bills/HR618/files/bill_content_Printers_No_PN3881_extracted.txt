WILLIAMS, KINKEAD, HOHENSTEIN, HOWARD, STEELE, KHAN, MADDEN, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, NEILSON, MALAGARI, GALLAGHER, SANCHEZ AND CIRESI, SEPTEMBER 14, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, SEPTEMBER 15, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDirecting the Pennsylvania Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to study treatment and support for individuals with mental illnesses, behavioral health disorders, intellectual and developmental disabilities, and Autism in Pennsylvania's county jails.WHEREAS, Many Pennsylvanians incarcerated in county jails who suffer from mental illness, behavioral health disorders, substance abuse disorders and intellectual and developmental disabilities find themselves without crucial supports and medically prescribed healthcare, medications and therapies; andWHEREAS, The number of individuals with mental illness in Pennsylvania has increased; andWHEREAS, Treatment options in county jails are often limited or nonexistent; andWHEREAS, County jails often do not have the resources to provide mental health services; andWHEREAS, County jails are often used as a de facto social worker when social work services are unavailable in the 123456789101112131415161718 community; andWHEREAS, Incarcerating individuals without the provision of needed and appropriate modes of care may result in incarcerated individuals experiencing deteriorating mental health; andWHEREAS, Upon release from incarceration with worse or untreated mental health issues, formerly incarcerated individuals may pose a larger threat to themselves and to the community than when the individuals were first incarcerated; and WHEREAS, In order to fulfill the Commonwealth's duty to care for these individuals, it is necessary to know the magnitude of the problem, what services are being offered in which facilities and which services are effective; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives direct the Pennsylvania Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to research and report by county how many inmates suffer from documented mental illnesses, behavioral health disorders, intellectual and developmental disabilities and autism; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the study provide a comprehensive list of services currently available and rendered to incarcerated individuals with mental illnesses, behavioral health disorders, intellectual and developmental disabilities and autism by county jail and a list of needed or beneficial services that are not currently provided;
