A RESOLUTION
Directing the Pennsylvania Legislative Budget and Finance
Committee to study treatment and support for individuals with
mental illnesses, behavioral health disorders, intellectual
and developmental disabilities, and Autism in Pennsylvania's
county jails.
WHEREAS, Many Pennsylvanians incarcerated in county jails who
suffer from mental illness, behavioral health disorders,
substance abuse disorders and intellectual and developmental
disabilities find themselves without crucial supports and
medically prescribed healthcare, medications and therapies; and
WHEREAS, The number of individuals with mental illness in
Pennsylvania has increased; and
WHEREAS, Treatment options in county jails are often limited
or nonexistent; and
WHEREAS, County jails often do not have the resources to
provide mental health services; and
WHEREAS, County jails are often used as a de facto social
worker when social work services are unavailable in the

community; and
WHEREAS, Incarcerating individuals without the provision of
needed and appropriate modes of care may result in incarcerated
individuals experiencing deteriorating mental health; and
WHEREAS, Upon release from incarceration with worse or
untreated mental health issues, formerly incarcerated
individuals may pose a larger threat to themselves and to the
community than when the individuals were first incarcerated; and
WHEREAS, In order to fulfill the Commonwealth's duty to care
for these individuals, it is necessary to know the magnitude of
the problem, what services are being offered in which facilities
and which services are effective; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives direct the
Pennsylvania Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to
research and report by county how many inmates suffer from
documented mental illnesses, behavioral health disorders,
intellectual and developmental disabilities and autism; and be
it further
RESOLVED, That the study provide a comprehensive list of
services currently available and rendered to incarcerated
individuals with mental illnesses, behavioral health disorders,
intellectual and developmental disabilities and autism by county
jail and a list of needed or beneficial services that are not
currently provided; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the Pennsylvania Legislative Budget and
Finance Committee issue a report with their findings and
recommendations within 12 months of the adoption of this
resolution to the following:
