2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2783 PN 3896 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3896 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2783 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY RASEL, CAUSER, BRENNAN, JAMES, GUENST AND WALLEN, SEPTEMBER 21, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, SEPTEMBER 22, 2026 AN ACTDesignating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 48271, on that portion carrying Pennsylvania Route 130 over Turtle Creek in Trafford Borough, Westmoreland County, as the Sergeant First Class Robert J. Fike Memorial Bridge.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Sergeant First Class Robert J. Fike Memorial Bridge.(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) Robert James Fike was born July 23, 1971, in Jeannette.(2) Sergeant First Class Fike graduated from Penn-Trafford High School, Class of 1989, and attended Edinboro University.(3) Sergeant First Class Fike was a Pennsylvania State employee with thirteen and one-half years of service. He served as a corrections officer at SCI Albion beginning in 2002 and held the rank of Sergeant.(4) Outside of work, Sergeant First Class Fike was an 12345678910111213141516171819 avid outdoorsman who enjoyed hunting, fishing and spending time with his daughter, MacKenzie, whom he considered the love and joy of his life.(5) Sergeant First Class Fike served with the Pennsylvania Army National Guard, 28th Military Police Company, for 16 years. He was a highly decorated soldier whose honors and awards included the Purple Heart, Bronze Star, Army Achievement Medal (third award), Combat Action Badge, NATO Medal, Meritorious Service Medal, Keystone Freedom Medal, Afghan Service Ribbon and Overseas Service Ribbon.(6) Sergeant First Class Fike passed away on June 11, 2010, in Zabul, Afghanistan, while serving with the Pennsylvania Army National Guard.(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key 48271, carrying Pennsylvania Route 130 over Turtle Creek in Trafford Borough, Westmoreland County, is designated as the Sergeant First Class Robert J. Fike Memorial Bridge.(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to traffic in both directions on the bridge.Section 2. Repeal.Repeals are as follows:(1) The General Assembly declares that the repeal under paragraph (2) is necessary to effectuate section 1.(2) Section 4 of the act of October 19, 2010 (P.L.543, No.79), is repealed.Section 3.
