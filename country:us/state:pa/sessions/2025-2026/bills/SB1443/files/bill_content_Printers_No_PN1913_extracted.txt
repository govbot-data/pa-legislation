2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1443 PN 1913 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1913 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1443 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KANE, FONTANA, COSTA AND FLYNN, AUGUST 26, 2026 REFERRED TO JUDICIARY, AUGUST 26, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in wiretapping and electronic surveillance, further providing for exceptions to prohibition of interception and disclosure of communications.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 5704 of Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a paragraph to read:§ 5704. Exceptions to prohibition of interception and disclosure of communications.It shall not be unlawful and no prior court approval shall be required under this chapter for:* * *(20) A person to intercept an electronic or oral conversation where the conversation was intercepted via an electronic home security system installed on the premises of the person's home. The following shall apply:(i) A person who intercepts an electronic or oral conversation under this paragraph may not disseminate the 12345678910111213141516171819 conversation unless the dissemination is to a law enforcement agency for the purpose of an investigation.(ii) An electronic home security system under this paragraph shall contain reasonable parameters that take into account the density of the neighborhood and the structure of housing, including whether the neighboring homes share walls, porches, roofs or other structural features that make privacy difficult to address and secure, to ensure the privacy of individuals not on the premises of the person's home.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260SB1443PN1913 - 2 - 1234567891011
