AN ACT
Amending Title 42 (Judiciary and Judicial Procedure) of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in sentencing, providing
for animal abuse registry.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 42 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 9778. Animal abuse registry.
(a) Maintenance and posting.--Beginning January 1, 2027, the
Pennsylvania State Police shall maintain and post a registry on
a publicly accessible Internet website that includes individuals
convicted on or after the effective date of this subsection of
an animal abuse offense.
(b) Regulations.--The Pennsylvania State Police may issue
rules and promulgate regulations necessary to implement this
section.
(c) Identifying registry information.--
(1) The registry shall include a photograph taken of the

(2) The registry shall not include the individual's

judgment and date of birth of an individual convicted of an
animal abuse offense to the Pennsylvania State Police within 60

which time the Pennsylvania State Police shall remove the

subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Animal abuse offense." An offense described under 18
Pa.C.S. § 5534 (relating to aggravated cruelty to animal).
"Conviction." A judgment entered by a court upon a plea of

jury or the court, notwithstanding any pending appeal or habeas
corpus proceeding arising from the judgment.
"Registry." The registry maintained and posted by the
Pennsylvania State Police under subsection (a).
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.

(A) MAINTENANCE AND POSTING.--BEGINNING JANUARY 1, 2027, THE
PENNSYLVANIA STATE POLICE SHALL MAINTAIN AND PUBLISH A REGISTRY
ON A PUBLICLY ACCESSIBLE INTERNET WEBSITE THAT INCLUDES
INDIVIDUALS CONVICTED ON OR AFTER THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS
SUBSECTION OF AN ANIMAL ABUSE OFFENSE.
(B) REGULATIONS.--THE PENNSYLVANIA STATE POLICE MAY ISSUE
RULES AND PROMULGATE REGULATIONS NECESSARY TO IMPLEMENT THIS
SECTION.
(C) IDENTIFYING REGISTRY INFORMATION.--
(1) WITHIN 20 CALENDAR DAYS OF THE INDIVIDUAL ' S RELEASE
FROM INCARCERATION OR, IF NOT INCARCERATED, FROM THE DATE OF
JUDGMENT, AN INDIVIDUAL CONVICTED OF AN ANIMAL ABUSE OFFENSE
SHALL APPEAR IN PERSON BEFORE THE PENNSYLVANIA STATE POLICE
IN ORDER FOR REGISTRY INFORMATION REQUIRED UNDER PARAGRAPH
(2) TO BE OBTAINED AND VERIFIED.
(2) NOTWITHSTANDING 18 PA.C.S. CH. 91 (RELATING TO
CRIMINAL HISTORY RECORD INFORMATION), THE REGISTRY SHALL
INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING INFORMATION:
(I) A PHOTOGRAPH TAKEN OF THE INDIVIDUAL CONVICTED
OF AN ANIMAL ABUSE OFFENSE AS PART OF THE BOOKING
PROCESS;
(II) THE INDIVIDUAL'S FULL LEGAL NAME AND ANY
ALIASES;
(III) IF THE INDIVIDUAL IS SUBJECT TO A COURT-
ORDERED PROHIBITION ON ANIMAL OWNERSHIP, THE DATE ON
WHICH THE ORDER EXPIRES AND A NOTATION THAT THE ORDER IS
IN EFFECT; AND
(IV) OTHER IDENTIFYING INFORMATION THAT THE
PENNSYLVANIA STATE POLICE DETERMINES NECESSARY TO
PROPERLY IDENTIFY THE INDIVIDUAL AND TO EXCLUDE INNOCENT

INDIVIDUAL ' S SECOND OR SUBSEQUENT ANIMAL ABUSE OFFENSE,
FIVE YEARS OR THE LENGTH OF ANY COURT-ORDERED PROHIBITION

(IV) FOR A FELONY OFFENSE THAT IS THE INDIVIDUAL ' S

(F) EXPUNGEMENT.--THE PENNSYLVANIA STATE POLICE SHALL REMOVE
AN INDIVIDUAL'S NAME AND IDENTIFYING INFORMATION FROM THE
REGISTRY IF THE OFFENSE FOR WHICH THE INDIVIDUAL IS INCLUDED ON
THE REGISTRY IS EXPUNGED.
(G) MANDATORY REGISTRY VERIFICATION PRIOR TO SALE, TRANSFER
OR ADOPTION.--
(1) PRIOR TO THE SALE, TRANSFER OR ADOPTION IN THIS
COMMONWEALTH OF ANY ANIMAL, THE PERSON SELLING OR OFFERING AN
ANIMAL FOR SALE, TRANSFER OR ADOPTION SHALL VERIFY THAT THE
PROSPECTIVE OWNER IS NOT LISTED ON THE REGISTRY AND, IF THE
PROSPECTIVE OWNER IS LISTED ON THE REGISTRY, REFUSE SALE,
TRANSFER OR ADOPTION.
(2) A PERSON WHO INTENTIONALLY OR KNOWINGLY SELLS,
TRANSFERS OR ADOPTS ANY ANIMAL TO AN INDIVIDUAL APPEARING ON
THE REGISTRY COMMITS A SUMMARY OFFENSE AND, UPON CONVICTION,
SHALL BE SENTENCED AS FOLLOWS:
(I) FOR A FIRST OFFENSE, A FINE OF NOT LESS THAN
$500 OR IMPRISONMENT FOR NOT MORE THAN 90 DAYS, OR BOTH.
(II) FOR A SECOND OFFENSE, A FINE OF NOT LESS THAN
$1,000 OR IMPRISONMENT FOR NOT MORE THAN 90 DAYS, OR
BOTH.
(III) FOR A THIRD OR SUBSEQUENT OFFENSE, A PERMANENT
REVOCATION OF A KENNEL LICENSE, DEALER LICENSE OR OUT-OF-
STATE DEALER LICENSE ISSUED UNDER THE ACT OF DECEMBER 7,
1982 (P.L.784, NO.225), KNOWN AS THE DOG LAW, AND A FINE
OF NOT LESS THAN $1,000 OR IMPRISONMENT FOR NOT MORE THAN
90 DAYS, OR BOTH.
(3) FOR PURPOSES OF THIS SUBSECTION, THE TRANSFER OR
ADOPTION OF AN ANIMAL INCLUDES WHETHER OR NOT A FEE IS
CHARGED FOR THE TRANSFER OR ADOPTION.

(H) SENTENCING COURT NOTICE.--THE SENTENCING COURT SHALL
INFORM INDIVIDUALS CONVICTED OF AN ANIMAL ABUSE OFFENSE ON OR
AFTER THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS SUBSECTION, AT THE TIME OF
SENTENCING OF THE PROVISIONS OF THIS SECTION. THE COURT SHALL:
(1) SPECIFICALLY INFORM THE INDIVIDUAL OF THE LENGTH OF
TIME THE INDIVIDUAL WILL BE ON THE REGISTRY, THE INDIVIDUAL ' S
DUTIES UNDER THIS SECTION AND THE OBLIGATION TO PROVIDE THE
INFORMATION REQUIRED.
(2) REQUIRE THE INDIVIDUAL TO READ AND SIGN A FORM
STATING THAT DUTIES UNDER THIS SECTION HAVE BEEN EXPLAINED.
IF THE INDIVIDUAL IS INCAPABLE OF READING, THE COURT SHALL
CERTIFY THAT THE DUTIES WERE EXPLAINED TO THE INDIVIDUAL AND
THE INDIVIDUAL INDICATED AN UNDERSTANDING OF THEIR DUTIES.
(I) IMMUNITY.--THE FOLLOWING ENTITIES SHALL BE IMMUNE FROM
LIABILITY FOR GOOD FAITH CONDUCT UNDER THIS SECTION:
(1) THE PENNSYLVANIA STATE POLICE.
(2) A CLERK OF COURT WHO TRANSMITS INFORMATION AS
DIRECTED UNDER THIS SECTION.
(3) THE DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS AND ITS AGENTS AND
EMPLOYEES.
(4) COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITIES AND THEIR AGENTS AND
EMPLOYEES.
(5) A PERSON WHO REFUSES SALE, TRANSFER OR ADOPTION TO
AN INDIVIDUAL ON THE REGISTRY.
(J) DEFINITIONS.--AS USED IN THIS SECTION, THE FOLLOWING
WORDS AND PHRASES SHALL HAVE THE MEANINGS GIVEN TO THEM IN THIS
SUBSECTION UNLESS THE CONTEXT CLEARLY INDICATES OTHERWISE:
"ANIMAL ABUSE OFFENSE." AN OFFENSE DESCRIBED UNDER THE
FOLLOWING PROVISIONS OF 18 PA.C.S. (RELATING TO CRIMES AND
OFFENSES):
