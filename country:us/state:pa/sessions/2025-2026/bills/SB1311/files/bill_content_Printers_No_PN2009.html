AN ACT
Amending Titles 18 (Crimes and Offenses) and 42 (Judiciary and
Judicial Procedure) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in riot, disorderly conduct and related offenses,
further providing for prohibition of ownership; and, in
sentencing, providing for animal abuse registry.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 5555 of Title 18 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:
§ 5555. Prohibition of ownership.
Notwithstanding any provision of law and in addition to any
other penalty provided by law, the authority imposing sentence
upon a conviction for a violation of this subchapter [may] shall
order the prohibition or limitation of the defendant's
ownership, possession, control or custody of animals or
employment with the care of animals for a period of time not to
exceed the statutory maximum term of imprisonment applicable to
the offense for which sentence is being imposed. A humane

Section 1.1. Title 42 is amended by adding a section to
read:
§ 9778. Animal abuse registry.
(a) Maintenance and posting.--Beginning not later than 60
days after the effective date of this subsection , the
Pennsylvania State Police shall maintain and publish a registry
on a publicly accessible Internet website that includes
individuals convicted on or after the effective date of this
subsection of an animal abuse offense.
(b) Regulations.--
(1) The Pennsylvania State Police may issue rules and
promulgate regulations necessary to implement this section.
(2) The Pennsylvania State Police may promulgate
temporary regulations that shall expire upon final
regulations being published in the Pennsylvania Bulletin. The
temporary regulations are not subject to:
(i) Sections 201, 202, 203, 204 and 205 of the act
of July 31, 1968 (P.L.769, No.240), referred to as the
Commonwealth Documents Law.
(ii) Sections 204(b) and 301(10) of the act of
October 15, 1980 (P.L.950, No.164), known as the
Commonwealth Attorneys Act.
(iii) The act of June 25, 1982 (P.L.633, No.181),
known as the Regulatory Review Act.
(iv) Section 612 of the act of April 9, 1929

(c) Identifying registry information.--
(1) Within 20 calendar days of the individual ' s release
from incarceration or, if not incarcerated, from the date of
conviction , an individual convicted of an animal abuse
offense shall appear in person before the Pennsylvania State
Police and provide registry information required under
paragraph (2).
(2) Notwithstanding 18 Pa.C.S. Ch. 91 (relating to
criminal history record information), the registry shall
include the following information:
(i) a photograph of the individual convicted of an
animal abuse offense;
(ii) the individual's full legal name and any
aliases;
(iii) if the individual is subject to a court-
ordered prohibition on animal ownership, the date on
which the order expires and a notation that the order is
in effect; and
(iv) other identifying information that the
Pennsylvania State Police determines necessary to
properly identify the individual and to exclude innocent
individuals.
(3) The registry published on the publicly accessible
Internet website shall not include the individual's Social
Security number, driver's license number or any other Federal
or State identification number.

the Pennsylvania State Police within three calendar days of

(d) Judgment.--A clerk of court shall forward a copy of the
court order reflecting a conviction for an animal abuse offense
and prohibition on animal ownership, if applicable, including
expiration date of the prohibition, to the Pennsylvania State
Police within 10 calendar days of the date of the conviction .
(e) Conviction and removal .--
(1) Following an individual's conviction of an animal
abuse offense, an individual shall register for the length of
any court-ordered prohibition on animal ownership. The
Pennsylvania State Police shall maintain the individual's
name and other identifying information, as described under
subsection (c) .
(2) After the period of time determined by paragraph (1)
has elapsed and if the individual was not convicted of
another animal abuse offense during that period, the
Pennsylvania State Police shall remove the individual's name
and identifying information from the registry.
(f) Removal .--The Pennsylvania State Police shall remove an
individual's name and identifying information from the registry
if the offense for which the individual is included on the
registry is removed . (RESERVED).
(g) Mandatory registry verification prior to sale, transfer
or adoption.--
(1) Prior to the sale, transfer or adoption in this
Commonwealth of any animal, the person selling or offering an
animal for sale, transfer or adoption shall verify that the
prospective owner is not listed on the registry and, if the
prospective owner is listed on the registry, refuse sale,

(2) A person who intentionally or knowingly sells,
transfers or adopts any animal to an individual appearing on
the registry commits a summary offense and, upon conviction,
shall be sentenced as follows:
(i) For a first offense, a fine of not less than
$500 or imprisonment for not more than 90 days, or both.
(ii) For a second offense, a fine of not less than
$1,000 or imprisonment for not more than 90 days, or
both.
(iii) For a third or subsequent offense, a permanent
revocation of a kennel license, dealer license or out-of-
State dealer license issued under the act of December 7,
1982 (P.L.784, No.225), known as the Dog Law, and a fine
of not less than $1,000 or imprisonment for not more than
90 days, or both.
(3) For purposes of this subsection, the transfer or
adoption of an animal includes whether or not a fee is
charged for the transfer or adoption.
(h) Sentencing court notice.--The sentencing court shall
inform individuals convicted of an animal abuse offense on or
after the effective date of this subsection, at the time of
sentencing of the provisions of this section. The court shall:
(1) Specifically inform the individual of the length of
time the individual will be on the registry, the individual ' s
duties under this section and the obligation to provide the
information required.
(2) Require the individual to read and sign a form
stating that duties under this section have been explained.

(i) Failure to register.--A person commits a felony of the
third degree if the individual fails to:
(1) register with the Pennsylvania State Police in
accordance with this section;
(2) be photographed as provided in this section; or
(3) provide accurate information when registering under
this section.
(j) Immunity.--The following entities shall be immune from
liability for good faith conduct under this section:
(1) The Pennsylvania State Police.
(2) A clerk of court who transmits information as
directed under this section.
(3) The Department of Corrections and its agents and
employees.
(4) County correctional facilities and their agents and
employees.
(5) A person who refuses sale, transfer or adoption to
an individual on the registry.
(k) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Animal." A dog, cat or equine animal.
"Animal abuse offense." An offense described under the
following provisions of 18 Pa.C.S. (relating to crimes and
offenses):
(1) section 3129 (relating to sexual intercourse with
animal);
(2) section 5104.3 (relating to harming a police animal
