Animal abuse registry.(a) Maintenance and posting.--Beginning January 1, 2027, the Pennsylvania State Police shall maintain and post a registry on a publicly accessible Internet website that includes court shall forward a copy of the judgment and date of birth of an individual convicted of an animal abuse offense to the Pennsylvania State Police within 60 calendar days of the date of the identifying information, as described under subsection (c), on the registry for two years following the date of conviction, after which time the Pennsylvania State Police shall remove the individual's name and identifying information from the registry if the individual is not convicted of another animal abuse offense during that two-year period.(2) Except if ordered for longer under paragraph (3), years.(ii) A lifetime ban on the ownership of an animal.(f) Expungement.--The Pennsylvania State Police shall remove an individual's name and identifying information from the registry if the offense for which the individual is included on the registry is expunged.(g) Mandatory registry verification prior to sale, transfer or adoption.--(1) Prior to the sale, transfer or adoption in this
