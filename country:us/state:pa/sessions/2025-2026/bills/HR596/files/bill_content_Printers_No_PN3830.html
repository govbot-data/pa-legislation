A RESOLUTION
Recognizing September 1, 2026, as "National Hotel Employee Day"
in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Hotel employees are the heart of the hospitality
industry, providing exceptional service, welcoming guests and
strengthening communities across this Commonwealth and
throughout the nation; and
WHEREAS, Hotels in this Commonwealth employ approximately
62,435 individuals, contributing to an industry that supports
more than 9.1 million jobs nationwide; and
WHEREAS, The contributions of hotel employees throughout this
Commonwealth showcase the best of hospitality every day; and
WHEREAS, The hospitality industry is committed to creating a
welcoming workplace where individuals can begin their careers,
develop new skills and achieve upward mobility; and
WHEREAS, Today we celebrate the dedication, professionalism
and service of hotel employees, whose work helps make this
Commonwealth a welcoming destination for visitors and a stronger
