2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2803 PN 3924 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3924 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2803 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PASHINSKI, MERSKI, GALLAGHER AND RIVERA, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in special vehicles and pedestrians, further providing for penalty for violation of subchapter.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 3502 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:§ 3502. Penalty for violation of subchapter.[Any] (a) General violations.--Except as otherwise provided in this subchapter, any person violating any provision of this subchapter is guilty of a summary offense and shall, upon conviction, be sentenced to pay a fine of $10.(b) Lamps and other equipment.--Any person violating section 3507 (relating to lamps and other equipment on pedalcycles) is guilty of a summary offense and shall, upon conviction, be sentenced to pay a fine of $100.Section 2. This act shall take effect July 1, 2027, or immediately, whichever is later.123456789101112131415161718
