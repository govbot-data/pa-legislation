AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in special vehicles and pedestrians, further
providing for penalty for violation of subchapter.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3502 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:
§ 3502. Penalty for violation of subchapter.
[Any] (a) General violations.--Except as otherwise provided
in this subchapter, any person violating any provision of this
subchapter is guilty of a summary offense and shall, upon
conviction, be sentenced to pay a fine of $10.
(b) Lamps and other equipment.--Any person violating section
3507 (relating to lamps and other equipment on pedalcycles) is
guilty of a summary offense and shall, upon conviction, be
sentenced to pay a fine of $100.
Section 2. This act shall take effect July 1, 2027, or
immediately, whichever is later.
