The definition of "aggregate contribution" in section 1902-K of the act of March 4, 1971 (P.L.6, No.2), known as the Tax Reform Code of 1971, added July 11, 2024 (P.L.674, No.56), is amended to read:Section 1902-K. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this article shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:12345678910111213141516171819202122 "Aggregate contribution."
