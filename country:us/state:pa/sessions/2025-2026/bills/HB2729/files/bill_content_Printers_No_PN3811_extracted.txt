WILLIAMS, McANDREW, MADSEN, STEELE, HANBIDGE, MERSKI AND MALAGARI, AUGUST 5, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON AGRICULTURE AND RURAL AFFAIRS, AUGUST 7, 2026 AN ACTAmending Titles 18 (Crimes and Offenses) and 42 (Judiciary and Judicial Procedure) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in riot, disorderly conduct and related offenses, providing for the offense of animal hoarding; and, in sentencing, providing for sentencing for animal hoarding.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 5532.1. Animal hoarding.(a) Offense defined.--A person commits a misdemeanor of the third degree if the person maintains an animal on real property which results in unsanitary, unhealthful, malodorous or dangerous conditions for an individual, the animal or real property.(b) Separate offense.--Each animal maintained in violation of subsection (a) constitutes a separate offense.(c) Applicability.--This section shall not apply to animals 123456789101112131415161718 that meet the definition of "agricultural commodity" under 3 Pa.C.S. § 4502 (relating to definitions).Section 2. Title 42 is amended by adding a section to read:§ 9720.9. Sentencing for animal hoarding.The following apply to sentencing for an offense under 18 Pa.C.S. § 5532.1 (relating to animal hoarding):(1) Prior to sentencing the defendant, the court shall order an evaluation to be performed by a mental health professional to determine if the defendant, at the time of the commission of the offense, suffered from or acted in accordance with a psychological or psychiatric disability.(2) Sentencing shall not be scheduled until at least 10 days after the court receives the evaluation under paragraph (1).(3) At sentencing, if the court finds by a preponderance of the evidence that the defendant, at the time of the commission of the offense, suffered from or acted in accordance with a psychological or psychiatric disability, the court shall:(i) order the defendant to:(A) undergo treatment appropriate to the diagnosis; and(B) submit a report of treatment; and(ii) stay the action against the defendant pending submission of the report under subparagraph (i)(B).(4) Upon review of the report under paragraph (3)(i)(B), if the court determines that the treatment under paragraph (3)(i)(A) is successful, the court shall do all of the following:(i) Order forfeiture under 18 Pa.C.S. § 5554 20260HB2729PN3811 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (relating to forfeiture).(ii) Order prohibition of ownership under 18 Pa.C.S. § 5555 (relating to prohibition of ownership).(iii) Reverse the conviction.(iv) Order expungement under 234 Pa. Code Rule 790 (relating to procedure for obtaining expungement in court cases; expungement order) of:(A) the complaint against the defendant under 234 Pa.
