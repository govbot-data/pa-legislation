AN ACT
Amending Titles 18 (Crimes and Offenses) and 42 (Judiciary and
Judicial Procedure) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in riot, disorderly conduct and related offenses,
providing for the offense of animal hoarding; and, in
sentencing, providing for sentencing for animal hoarding.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 5532.1. Animal hoarding.
(a) Offense defined.--A person commits a misdemeanor of the
third degree if the person maintains an animal on real property
which results in unsanitary, unhealthful, malodorous or
dangerous conditions for an individual, the animal or real
property.
(b) Separate offense.--Each animal maintained in violation
of subsection (a) constitutes a separate offense.
(c) Applicability.--This section shall not apply to animals

Section 2. Title 42 is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 9720.9. Sentencing for animal hoarding.
The following apply to sentencing for an offense under 18
Pa.C.S. § 5532.1 (relating to animal hoarding):
(1) Prior to sentencing the defendant, the court shall
order an evaluation to be performed by a mental health
professional to determine if the defendant, at the time of
the commission of the offense, suffered from or acted in
accordance with a psychological or psychiatric disability.
(2) Sentencing shall not be scheduled until at least 10
days after the court receives the evaluation under paragraph
(1).
(3) At sentencing, if the court finds by a preponderance
of the evidence that the defendant, at the time of the
commission of the offense, suffered from or acted in
accordance with a psychological or psychiatric disability,
the court shall:
(i) order the defendant to:
(A) undergo treatment appropriate to the
diagnosis; and
(B) submit a report of treatment; and
(ii) stay the action against the defendant pending
submission of the report under subparagraph (i)(B).
(4) Upon review of the report under paragraph (3)(i)(B),
if the court determines that the treatment under paragraph
(3)(i)(A) is successful, the court shall do all of the
following:
(i) Order forfeiture under 18 Pa.C.S. § 5554
