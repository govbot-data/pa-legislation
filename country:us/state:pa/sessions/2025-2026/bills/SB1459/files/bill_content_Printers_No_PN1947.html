AN ACT
Requiring proposed data centers to conduct water usage studies.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Data Center
Water Usage Study Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Data center." A facility or group of facilities that is
used to house and operate equipment that:
(1) receives, stores, aggregates, manages, processes,
transforms, retrieves, researches or transmits data; or
(2) is necessary for the proper operation of equipment
that receives, stores, aggregates, manages, processes,
transforms, retrieves, researches or transmits data.

"Data center developer." An entity that plans, entitles,
sites, finances and equips data centers.
"Department." The Department of Environmental Protection of
the Commonwealth.
Section 3. Study required.
(a) Water usage study.--An entity, business or land owner
proposing a data center must:
(1) Submit a comprehensive water analysis and study to
the host municipality no later than 30 days prior to a formal
development application.
(2) Use an independent, State-recognized third party,
which may be an engineering firm, to prepare the water
analysis and study.
(3) Provide data on the following for the proposed data
center, adjoining properties and the surrounding region
within a 20-mile radius of the development:
(i) Anticipated water usage, water quality and
short-term and long-term sustainability of water supply.
(ii) Other factors deemed necessary by the
department.
(4) Ensure that the water analysis and study calculates
the proposed data center's total water footprint to capture
the combined direct or onsite water use and indirect or
offsite water use embedded in a site's demand for
electricity.
(5) Ensure that the water analysis and study is publicly
available upon request.
(b) Costs of study.--All costs for the water analysis and
study shall be incurred by the data center developer.
