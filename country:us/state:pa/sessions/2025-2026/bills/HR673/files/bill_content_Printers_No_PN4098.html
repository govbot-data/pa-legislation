A RESOLUTION
Directing the Joint State Government Commission to conduct a
study on the feasibility, community impact and process for
converting immigrant detention centers operating in this
Commonwealth into community-centered projects, with fair,
transparent and rigorous local stakeholder participation as a
condition for eligibility for State transition grant funds.
WHEREAS, Several counties and municipalities within this
Commonwealth contract with Federal agencies, including the
United States Department of Homeland Security, to detain
immigrants in local or privately operated detention centers; and
WHEREAS, The detention facilities have faced growing public
scrutiny due to human rights concerns and increased deaths in
immigration detention and cause net economic harm and negatively
impact community cohesion and trust in local government; and
WHEREAS, There is increasing national and local interest in
reimagining public use of carceral infrastructure, particularly
toward community-centered projects such as workforce training
hubs, affordable housing, green manufacturing, small business
incubators and restorative justice initiatives; and

WHEREAS, Counties and municipalities that wish to transition
away from immigration detention contracts may require technical
assistance, stakeholder engagement frameworks and access to
state transition grant funds to support equitable redevelopment;
and
WHEREAS, Ensuring that any conversion of detention sites
proceeds through a fair, transparent and rigorous process that
meaningfully involves residents, community-based organizations,
faith leaders, labor unions and local businesses is essential to
building public trust and maximizing community benefit;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives direct the Joint
State Government Commission to conduct a study on the
feasibility, community impact and process for converting
immigrant detention centers operating in this Commonwealth into
community-centered projects, with fair, transparent and rigorous
local stakeholder participation as a condition for eligibility
for State transition grant funds; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the study:
(1) identify all detention facilities in this
Commonwealth operated by or under contract with Federal
immigration authorities;
(2) assess the economic and environmental impacts of
these facilities on all communities in Pennsylvania and
examine best practices from other states or jurisdictions
that have successfully repurposed or transitioned immigrant
detention centers or correctional facilities to provide human
services;
(3) develop a framework for community-centered
conversion of detention facilities that:
