Subsequent Consolidation Grant and the Sustainability
Consolidation Grant; and imposing duties on the State Fire
Commissioner.

hereby enacts as follows:

§ 7818. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this subchapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Active responding member." As follows:
(1) An individual authorized by a fire company to
respond to a fire, rescue or other emergency incident and
who responded to at least 10 emergency incidents during the
preceding calendar year, as documented by incident response
records.
(2) The term does not include a social, honorary,
auxiliary or other nonresponding member of a fire company who
does not meet the requirements of paragraph (1).
§ 7819. Small Fire Company Safety Grant.
(a) Award.--The commissioner may award a Small Fire Company
Safety Grant under this subchapter. A Small Fire Company Safety
Grant may not exceed $25,000 and may not be awarded more than
once every three fiscal years.
(b) Eligibility.--A fire company that meets all of the
following may apply, in the manner provided for by the
commissioner, for a Small Fire Company Safety Grant:
(1) Has 25 or fewer active responding members.
(2) Has actively responded to at least 30 fire or rescue
emergencies during the preceding calendar year.
(3) Satisfies Commonwealth incident-reporting
requirements.

"Consolidated company." A consolidated fire company or a
consolidated EMS company that received an Initial Consolidation

independent EMS companies.

§ 7863. Subsequent Consolidation Grant.
(a) Eligibility.--Subject to subsection (b), if one or more
additional independent companies consolidate into a consolidated
company, the consolidated company may immediately apply to the
commissioner for a Subsequent Consolidation Grant.
(b) Amount.--
(1) Subject to paragraph (2), upon application under
subsection (a), the commissioner may award the consolidated
company an additional amount not to exceed the difference
between the total consolidation grant amount awarded under
section 7862 (relating to Initial Consolidation Grant) and
the maximum tier applicable to the total number of fire
companies or EMS companies, as provided for under section
7862(b), participating in the qualifying consolidation at the
time of application.
(2) Before an additional amount awarded under this
subsection is distributed, the commissioner shall verify that
the additional fire company or EMS company was, prior to the
consolidation, an independent fire company or independent EMS
company and that a bona fide consolidation occurred.

section may not be considered a new application under section

§ 7864. Sustainability Consolidation Grant.
(a) Application.--A consolidated company may apply to the
commissioner for a Sustainability Consolidation Grant three
fiscal years after the award of an Initial Consolidation Grant
under section 7862 (relating to Initial Consolidation Grant) and
once every three fiscal years thereafter.
(b) Eligibility.--Upon receipt of an application under
subsection (a), the commissioner may award a Sustainability
Consolidation Grant if the consolidated company:
(1) Remains consolidated.
(2) Is operational and in good standing with the office.
(3) Satisfies incident-reporting requirements.
(4) Actively responds to at least 60 fire or rescue
emergencies in the preceding year.
(c) Amount.--The commissioner may award a Sustainability
Consolidation Grant not to exceed:
(1) For consolidated fire companies, $50,000.
(2) For consolidated EMS companies, $35,000.
(d) Eligible uses.--Grants awarded under this section may be
used for:
(1) The purchase, repair, maintenance or modification
of:
(i) Apparatus or equipment.
(ii) Personal protective equipment.
(iii) Self-contained breathing apparatus.
(iv) Communication equipment.
(v) Facilities.
(2) Training, recruitment and retention approved by the
commissioner.

§ 7865. Additional eligible entities.
The following shall apply:
(1) The commissioner may provide grants under sections
7862 (relating to Initial Consolidation Grant), 7863
(relating to Subsequent Consolidation Grant) and 7864
(relating to Sustainability Consolidation Grant), to a fire
company or EMS company that has initiated a consolidation
process but has not completed a qualifying consolidation
before the effective date of this paragraph if:
(i) the commissioner verifies that the qualifying
consolidation was a bona fide consolidation; and
(ii) the applicant provides contemporaneous
documentation demonstrating that the consolidation
process was formally initiated prior to the effective
date of this subparagraph.
(2) (i) Acceptable documentation under paragraph (1)
(ii) includes:
(A) Dated resolutions.
(B) Approved meeting minutes.
(C) Dated letters of intent.
(D) Memoranda of understanding.
(E) Consolidation agreements.
(F) Other contemporaneous written records from
participating fire companies or EMS companies and the
municipality or municipalities served that
demonstrate that a bona fide consolidation process
began before the effective date of this subparagraph.
(ii) The documentation must identify or reasonably
establish the date on which the consolidation process

§ 7866. Funding.
The following apply to funding under this subchapter:
(1) If eligible applications exceed money appropriated
by the General Assembly, the commissioner shall establish a
fair and equitable distribution method and may prorate grants
based upon available appropriations, demonstrated need and
the scope of a qualifying consolidation.
(2) Receipt of a Small Fire Company Safety Grant under
section 7819 (relating to Small Fire Company Safety Grant)
shall not reduce the amount of an Initial Consolidation Grant
awarded under section 7862 (relating to Initial Consolidation
Grant).
§ 7867. Documentation.
A consolidated company shall maintain invoices, receipts,
proof of payment and other records reasonably required by the
commissioner. Records shall be retained by the consolidated
company for the period established by the commissioner and made
available for audit or inspection.
