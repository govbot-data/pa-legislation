WILLIAMS AND KANE, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO EDUCATION, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An act relating to the public school system, including certain provisions applicable as well to private and parochial schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," in miscellaneous provisions relating to institutions of higher education, providing for welcoming campuses at public institutions of higher education.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 2007-I. Welcoming campuses at public institutions of higher education.(a) Purpose.--The purpose of this section is to ensure that public institutions of higher education provide continued access of educational opportunities, enhancement activities, social and cultural opportunities, resources, services, spaces, buildings and facilities provided to each student, parent, guardian and institution personnel, regardless of immigration status.1234567891011121314151617181920 (b) Welcoming campuses.--Within 90 days of the effective date of this subsection, each institution shall provide assurance of continued access of the institution's campus to each student, parent, guardian and institution personnel, regardless of immigration status, by establishing and implementing policies to protect students, parents, guardians and institution personnel from Federal civil immigration enforcement and to prevent the facilitation of Federal civil immigration enforcement.(c) Prohibitions on discrimination.--Except as provided under subsection (c.1), a public institution of higher education shall not:(1) Compel, require, induce or solicit a student enrolled at the institution, institution personnel or an applicant for admission to or employment or contracting at the institution to provide a statement of the person's immigration status.(2) Restrict academic research, coursework, resources, services, educational opportunities, institution-sponsored activities, events, participation in or benefits of activities, sports, services, privileges or other enrichment activities or opportunities of learning, programs or campus life based on immigration status.(3) Formally or informally provide or discuss information related to the immigration status of a student, a student's parent or guardian, a member of the student's family or an individual who is deemed institution personnel.(c.1) Existing guidelines.--If a Federal or State guideline exists that provides a mandate of campus or situated outside the boundaries of an institution's campus, an individual who is deemed institution personnel may not:(1) inquire into the immigration status of a student, a student's or an individual who is deemed institution personnel.(e) Prevention of facilitation of Federal civil immigration enforcement.--(1) Institution personnel must refuse an enforcement official's entry to an institution's campus for the purpose of a wellness check when the designated institution personnel, at the personnel's discretion, determines the wellness check requires a lawful warrant.(2) In the event circumstances permit, a request made by an immigration enforcement agency or a law enforcement agency for access to an institution's campus must be directed to the institution's legal by an institution.
