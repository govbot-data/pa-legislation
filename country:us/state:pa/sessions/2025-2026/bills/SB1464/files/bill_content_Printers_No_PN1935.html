AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," in miscellaneous provisions relating
to institutions of higher education, providing for welcoming
campuses at public institutions of higher education.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known
as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a
section to read:
Section 2007-I. Welcoming campuses at public institutions of
higher education.
(a) Purpose.--The purpose of this section is to ensure that
public institutions of higher education provide continued access
of educational opportunities, enhancement activities, social and
cultural opportunities, resources, services, spaces, buildings
and facilities provided to each student, parent, guardian and
institution personnel, regardless of immigration status.

(b) Welcoming campuses.--Within 90 days of the effective
date of this subsection, each institution shall provide
assurance of continued access of the institution's campus to
each student, parent, guardian and institution personnel,
regardless of immigration status, by establishing and
implementing policies to protect students, parents, guardians
and institution personnel from Federal civil immigration
enforcement and to prevent the facilitation of Federal civil
immigration enforcement.
(c) Prohibitions on discrimination.--Except as provided
under subsection (c.1), a public institution of higher education
shall not:
(1) Compel, require, induce or solicit a student
enrolled at the institution, institution personnel or an
applicant for admission to or employment or contracting at
the institution to provide a statement of the person's
immigration status.
(2) Restrict academic research, coursework, resources,
services, educational opportunities, institution-sponsored
activities, events, participation in or benefits of
activities, sports, services, privileges or other enrichment
activities or opportunities of learning, programs or campus
life based on immigration status.
(3) Formally or informally provide or discuss
information related to the immigration status of a student, a
student's parent or guardian, a member of the student's
family or an individual who is deemed institution personnel.
(c.1) Existing guidelines.--If a Federal or State guideline
exists that provides a mandate of disclosure in areas, including
F, J or I Visa purposes, funding, tuition, admission or other

(d) Prohibition on institution personnel.--Regardless of
whether within an institution's campus or situated outside the
boundaries of an institution's campus, an individual who is
deemed institution personnel may not:
(1) inquire into the immigration status of a student, a
student's family, parent or guardian; or
(2) formally or informally provide or discuss
information related to the immigration status of a student, a
student's parent or guardian, a member of the student's
family or an individual who is deemed institution personnel.
(e) Prevention of facilitation of Federal civil immigration
enforcement.--
(1) Institution personnel must refuse an enforcement
official's entry to an institution's campus for the purpose
of a wellness check when the designated institution
personnel, at the personnel's discretion, determines the
wellness check requires a lawful warrant.
(2) In the event circumstances permit, a request made by
an immigration enforcement agency or a law enforcement agency
for access to an institution's campus must be directed to the
institution's legal representative 24 hours before the
requested time of entry for determination of whether to grant
the request.
(3) All requests made by an immigration enforcement
agency or law enforcement agency for access to an institution
area zone must include presentation to institution personnel
of:

(h) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Campus." Institution property, land, parking lots, parking
garages, facilities, services and resources that meet the
following criteria:
(1) Exist to serve a public institution of higher
education's purposes, educational opportunities or
enhancement activities provided to the institution's
students, parents, guardians and institution personnel, owned
by, leased by or under the control of a public institution of
higher education.
(2) Have been designated by the institution as a
nonpublic area, service or resource with entry prohibited to
the public.
"Enforcement agency." An immigration enforcement agency or a
law enforcement agency.
"Enforcement official." An official of an enforcement
agency.
"Enhancement activities." Institution activities and
leadership opportunities designed by the institution to increase
students' meaningful involvement and engagement in activities,
events or extracurricular activities, including athletic,
theatrical, entertainment-valued, civil, social or culturally
focused opportunities.
"Immigration enforcement agency." The Department of Homeland
Security of the United States and its subagencies, including:
(1) United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement,
