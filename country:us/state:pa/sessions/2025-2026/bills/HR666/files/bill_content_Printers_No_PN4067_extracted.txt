2025-2026 Regular Session HR 666 PN 4067 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 4067 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.666 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY FRANKEL, OCTOBER 19, 2026 INTRODUCED AS NONCONTROVERSIAL RESOLUTION UNDER RULE 35, OCTOBER 19, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRemembering and giving thanks for the late Honorable Gerald Kaufman, former three-term member of the House of Representatives from southwestern Pennsylvania, who died in Philadelphia County May 10, 2025, at 92 years of age.WHEREAS, A longtime resident of the Mount Airy neighborhood of the City of Philadelphia, Philadelphia County, Mr. Kaufman was born June 14, 1932, in the Squirrel Hill neighborhood of the City of Pittsburgh, Allegheny County; andWHEREAS, Mr. Kaufman practiced law in Pittsburgh after receiving a bachelor's degree from Yale University and doctor of laws degree from Columbia Law School; andWHEREAS, As a lawmaker in the General Assembly, Mr. Kaufman built an extensive public record and accomplished significant legislative goals; andWHEREAS, Representing Allegheny County during the 1967-1968 session and the newly created 23rd District from 1969 through 1972, Mr. Kaufman advanced civil rights, environmental rights, industrial development, mental health office reorganization, public school integration, no-fault automobile insurance and 12345678910111213141516171819 public assistance; andWHEREAS, The constitutional amendment prohibiting discrimination based on gender, approved in 1971, was introduced by Mr. Kaufman in 1969; andWHEREAS, Mr. Kaufman went on to commit his energies and influence to the Pennsylvania Legal Services Center, the Center for Effective Public Policy and other nonprofit and organizational development initiatives; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Prison Society has recognized Mr. Kaufman's legacy as a former member of the board of directors; andWHEREAS, Awbury Arboretum has recognized Mr. Kaufman's legacy as a former executive director; andWHEREAS, The Tookany/Tacony-Frankford Watershed Partnership has recognized Mr. Kaufman's legacy as a founder and first president; andWHEREAS, In 1972, Mr. Kaufman gifted the Gerald Kaufman Papers, 1969-1972, to the University of Pittsburgh; andWHEREAS, Welcoming Mr. Kaufman to the House on April 6, 1976, the Speaker pro tempore remarked: "The Chair is very happy to recognize ... a former and, indeed, a very distinguished member of this House. He is still so much a part of the family that we do not accept him as a guest"; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives remember and give thanks for the late Honorable Gerald Kaufman, former three-term member of the House of Representatives from southwestern Pennsylvania, who died in Philadelphia County May 10, 2025, at 92 years of age; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to Mrs. Shelly D.
