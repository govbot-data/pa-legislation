A RESOLUTION
Remembering and giving thanks for the late Honorable Gerald
Kaufman, former three-term member of the House of
Representatives from southwestern Pennsylvania, who died in
Philadelphia County May 10, 2025, at 92 years of age.
WHEREAS, A longtime resident of the Mount Airy neighborhood
of the City of Philadelphia, Philadelphia County, Mr. Kaufman
was born June 14, 1932, in the Squirrel Hill neighborhood of the
City of Pittsburgh, Allegheny County; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Kaufman practiced law in Pittsburgh after
receiving a bachelor's degree from Yale University and doctor of
laws degree from Columbia Law School; and
WHEREAS, As a lawmaker in the General Assembly, Mr. Kaufman
built an extensive public record and accomplished significant
legislative goals; and
WHEREAS, Representing Allegheny County during the 1967-1968
session and the newly created 23rd District from 1969 through
1972, Mr. Kaufman advanced civil rights, environmental rights,
industrial development, mental health office reorganization,
public school integration, no-fault automobile insurance and

WHEREAS, The constitutional amendment prohibiting
discrimination based on gender, approved in 1971, was introduced
by Mr. Kaufman in 1969; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Kaufman went on to commit his energies and
influence to the Pennsylvania Legal Services Center, the Center
for Effective Public Policy and other nonprofit and
organizational development initiatives; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Prison Society has recognized Mr.
Kaufman's legacy as a former member of the board of directors;
and
WHEREAS, Awbury Arboretum has recognized Mr. Kaufman's legacy
as a former executive director; and
WHEREAS, The Tookany/Tacony-Frankford Watershed Partnership
has recognized Mr. Kaufman's legacy as a founder and first
president; and
WHEREAS, In 1972, Mr. Kaufman gifted the Gerald Kaufman
Papers, 1969-1972, to the University of Pittsburgh; and
WHEREAS, Welcoming Mr. Kaufman to the House on April 6, 1976,
the Speaker pro tempore remarked: "The Chair is very happy to
recognize ... a former and, indeed, a very distinguished member
of this House. He is still so much a part of the family that we
do not accept him as a guest"; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives remember and give
thanks for the late Honorable Gerald Kaufman, former three-term
member of the House of Representatives from southwestern
Pennsylvania, who died in Philadelphia County May 10, 2025, at
92 years of age; and be it further
RESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to
Mrs. Shelly D. Yanoff.
