The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 1303.3-A. Artificial Intelligence Policy.--(a) No later than the beginning of the 2027-2028 school year, the governing body of each school entity and nonpublic school shall adopt a policy related to the use of artificial intelligence technology, programs and platforms. The governing body of each school entity and nonpublic school shall seek input from instructional staff during the development of the policy.
