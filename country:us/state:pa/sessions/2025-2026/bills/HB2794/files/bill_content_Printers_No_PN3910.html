AN ACT
Amending Title 74 (Transportation) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in turnpike, providing for signage.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 74 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 8124. Signage.
The commission shall install and maintain standardized
signage at each toll road entrance and such intervals of the
turnpike as determined appropriate by the commission that:
(1) is visible and understandable to operators of
vehicles; and
(2) displays the rates of video tolls and flat video
tolls for each vehicle classification established by the
commission and any electronic toll collection device approved
by the commission for payment of video tolls.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
