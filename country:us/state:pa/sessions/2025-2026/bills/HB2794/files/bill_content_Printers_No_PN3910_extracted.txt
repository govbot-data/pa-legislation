2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2794 PN 3910 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3910 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2794 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CIRESI, PROBST, GUZMAN, HILL-EVANS, BOROWSKI, RIVERA, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ AND PASHINSKI, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 74 (Transportation) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in turnpike, providing for signage.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 74 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 8124. Signage.The commission shall install and maintain standardized signage at each toll road entrance and such intervals of the turnpike as determined appropriate by the commission that:(1) is visible and understandable to operators of vehicles; and(2) displays the rates of video tolls and flat video tolls for each vehicle classification established by the commission and any electronic toll collection device approved by the commission for payment of video tolls.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.1234567891011121314151617
