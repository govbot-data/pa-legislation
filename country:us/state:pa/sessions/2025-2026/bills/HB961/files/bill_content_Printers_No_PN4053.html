AN ACT
Amending Title 30 (Fish) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in dams, bar racks and migration devices, further
providing for marking of dams.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3510(b), (c)(2), (h)(1), (2) and (3) and
the definitions of "owner," "permittee" and "run-of-the-river
dam" in subsection (i) of Title 30 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes are amended, subsection (h) is amended by
adding a paragraph PARAGRAPHS and the section is amended by
adding a subsection to read:
§ 3510. Marking of dams.
* * *
(b) Existing dams.--
[(1) The department shall compile and maintain a current
list of existing dams on the waters of this Commonwealth that
the department determines to be run-of-the-river type dams.

Within three months of the effective date of this section,
the department shall notify the permittees and owners of
those run-of-the-river dams of the requirements of this
section by certified letter. Within six months of receiving
notification of the requirements of this section from the
department, the permittees and owners of run-of-the-river
dams shall comply with the requirements of this section to
mark the areas above and below their dams and on the banks
immediately adjacent to their dams during the time periods of
the year required by the commission with signs and buoys of a

swimming, fishing and boating public of the hazards posed by
the dam.]

current list of existing dams on the waters of this
Commonwealth that are run-of-the-river dams. The department
shall provide the commission with identifying information
about the permittee or owner, location, size and
specifications of the run-of-the-river dams annually, unless
otherwise requested by the commission. The following shall
apply:
(i) Within three months of the discovery of a run-
of-the river dam not already inventoried by the
department, the department shall notify the permittees
and owners of the dam of the requirements of this section
in writing.

the requirements of this section from the department, the
permittees and owners of run-of-the-river dams shall
comply with the requirements of this section to mark the

(2) When a permittee or owner learns that signs or buoys

(h) Penalties.--
(1) Any person who fails to comply with subsection (a)
or (b) commits a summary offense of the first degree for the
first offense regardless of adjudication and a misdemeanor of
the third degree for a subsequent offense. Each [month] 15-
day period of noncompliance shall constitute a separate
violation.
(2) Any person who fails to comply with subsection (c)
or (d) commits a summary offense of the second degree for the
first offense regardless of adjudication and a summary
offense of the first degree for a subsequent offense. Each
[month] 15-day period of noncompliance shall constitute a
separate violation.
(2.1) Any person who fails to comply with subsection (g)
commits a summary offense of the third degree.

Encroachments Act.

"Run-of-the-river dam." A manmade structure which:
(1) is a dam regulated by the Department of
Environmental Protection pursuant to the act of November 26,

Encroachments Act;
(2) is built across or partially across a watercourse
and certain flows pass over the [entire] dam structure,
excluding abutments, to a natural channel downstream; and
(3) the department, in consultation with the commission,
determines to have hydraulic characteristics such that at
certain flows persons may be caught in the hydraulic roller
or boil immediately below the dam.
