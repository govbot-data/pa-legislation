A RESOLUTION
Honoring the life and expressing condolences on the passing of
former State Senator Franklin Kury.
WHEREAS, Franklin Kury was born in Sunbury, Northumberland
County, on October 15, 1936, to Barney and Helen C. Witkowski
Kury; and
WHEREAS, He graduated from the University of Pennsylvania Law
School in 1961; and
WHEREAS, Senator Kury represented the 27th Senatorial
District from 1973 to 1980 and served three terms in the
Pennsylvania House of Representatives from 1967 to 1972; and
WHEREAS, Besides serving in both the Senate and the House of
Representatives, Senator Kury had a distinguished career marked
by numerous other professional accomplishments, including staff
assistant to Governor George M. Leader, state co-chair of
Students for Kennedy-Johnson, 1960, Deputy Attorney General,
Pennsylvania Office of the Attorney General, 1962, and

congressional aide to United States Representative Honorable

WHEREAS, Senator Kury was a leader in the enactment of
environmental laws, including amendments to the Clean Streams
Act, and most notably, he was the author and lead advocate for
the environmental rights amendment found in Article I, Section
27, of the Constitution of Pennsylvania; and
WHEREAS, Senator Kury passed away on August 16, 2026, at 89
years of age, having been predeceased by his wife Elizabeth
(Heazlett) Kury; and
WHEREAS, Senator Kury leaves behind a legacy of preservation
of the rights of the people of this Commonwealth and the
natural, scenic, historic and esthetic values of our natural
resources; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate honor the life and express
condolences on the passing of former State Senator Franklin
Kury; and be it further
RESOLVED, That a copy of this condolence resolution be sent
to his three surviving sons Steven, David and James Kury.
