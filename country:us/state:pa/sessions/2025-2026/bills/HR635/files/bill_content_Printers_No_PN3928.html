A RESOLUTION
Designating the month of October 2026 as "Pennsylvania Chamber
of Business and Industry - Chamber of the Year Month" in
Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Chamber of Business and Industry
(PA Chamber) was founded December 16, 1916, by a group of more
than 100 business leaders from across this Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, The mission of the PA Chamber is to advocate for
policies that create economic opportunities and to lead
Pennsylvania and its residents to greater prosperity; and
WHEREAS, Headquartered in Harrisburg, the PA Chamber is the
largest broad-based business association in Pennsylvania; and
WHEREAS, The membership of the PA Chamber comprises more than
13,000 businesses in each of this Commonwealth's 67 counties,
representing all industry sectors and all sizes of companies;
and
WHEREAS, The PA Chamber represents nearly 50% of the private
workforce in this Commonwealth and helps its members understand

WHEREAS, The United States Chamber of Commerce awarded the PA
Chamber with a rating of "Accredited with Distinction," one of
only six state chambers in the country to reach this
achievement; and
WHEREAS, The National Association of State Chambers recently
selected the PA Chamber as the "2026 State Chamber of the Year";
and
WHEREAS, This award is the highest national honor for a state
chamber, reflecting the PA Chamber's hard work and effectiveness
in advocating for business interests in this Commonwealth and
helping create family-sustaining employment opportunities; and
WHEREAS, The PA Chamber has served as an influential voice
for Pennsylvania's business community for 110 years,
contributing greatly to this Commonwealth's economic growth and
vitality; therefore be it
