AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," in pupils and attendance, providing
for education of children and youth experiencing
homelessness.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known
as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a
section to read:
Section 1331.2. Education of Children and Youth Experiencing
Homelessness.--(a) (1) The department and each school entity
shall ensure that a child or youth experiencing homelessness has
equal access to public education, including public preschool
education.
(2) The school entity shall ensure that a child or youth
experiencing homelessness has access to academic,

education, special education, English learner services, school
nutrition and other educational programs and services for which
the child or youth is eligible.
(b) (1) A school entity shall r eview and revise a policy or
practice that creates a difficulty in identifying, enrolling,
attending, retaining or impacting the educational success of a
child or youth experiencing homelessness.
(2) A school entity may not:
(i) Create a difficulty on the enrollment of a child or
youth based on the residency status, documentation,
guardianship, immunization or other health record of the child
or youth.
(ii) Burden the child's or youth's ability to enroll in a
school entity, including the setting of fines, fees, limited
number of permitted absences, application or enrollment
deadlines.
(iii) Segregate or stigmatize a child or youth based on the
housing status of the child or youth.
(c) A school entity shall designate an appropriate staff
person as homeless education liaison. The homeless education
liaison shall ensure that:
(1) Children and youth experiencing homelessness are
identified.
(2) Children and youth experiencing homelessness are
immediately enrolled and have a full and equal opportunity to
succeed in school.
(3) Parents, guardians and children or youth experiencing
homelessness receive information concerning their educational
rights under this section.

other children and youth, who are living in the attendance area
in which the child or youth is actually living, are eligible to
attend.

(i) Presume that the child or youth remaining in the school

(g) The department shall:
(1) Designate a State Coordinator for the Education of

(2) Provide technical assistance and professional

(h) As used in this section, the following words and phrases
shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless
the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Child or youth experiencing homelessness." A child or youth
who lacks a fixed, regular and adequate nighttime residence,
including a child or youth who:
(1) is sharing the housing of other individuals due to loss
of housing, economic hardship or a similar reason;
(2) is living in a motel, hotel, trailer park or camping
ground due to the lack of alternative adequate accommodations;
(3) is living in an emergency or transitional shelter;

(5) has a primary nighttime residence that is a public or

(6) is living in a car, park, public space, abandoned

"Enroll" or "enrollment." Attending classes and
participating fully in school activities.
"Homeless education liaison." The staff person designated by
a school entity under subsection (c) to carry out the duties
described in this section.
"School entity." A school district, intermediate unit, area
career and technical school, charter school, regional charter
school or cyber charter school.
"School of origin." The school that a child or youth
attended when permanently housed or the school in which the
child or youth was last enrolled, including a preschool. If a
child or youth completes the final grade offered by the school
of origin, the term includes the designated receiving school at
the next grade level.
"Unaccompanied youth." A child or youth experiencing
homelessness who is not in the physical custody of a parent or
guardian.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
