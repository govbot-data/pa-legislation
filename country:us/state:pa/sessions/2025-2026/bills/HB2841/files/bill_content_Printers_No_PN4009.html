AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in liquid fuels and fuels tax, further providing
for definitions; and providing for consumer fuel price
relief.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The definition of "average wholesale price" in
section 9002 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended to read:

The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:

Section 2. Title 75 is amended by adding a chapter to read:

§ 90A01. Scope of chapter.
This chapter provides for consumer fuel price relief.
§ 90A02. Consumer fuel price relief.
(a) Reduction.--After December 31, 2026, the average
wholesale price shall be determined annually by the Department
of Revenue and shall be a price per gallon that establishes a
total tax rate for the oil company franchise tax imposed under
Chapters 90 (relating to liquid fuels and fuels tax) and 95
(relating to taxes for highway maintenance and construction) as

§ 90A03. Revenue loss estimate.
No later than February 1, 2028, and every February 1
thereafter, the Department of Revenue shall, in consultation
with the Department of Transportation, provide the Office of the
Budget and the General Assembly with the estimated loss in
revenue due to the reduction in the average wholesale price
under section 90A02 (relating to consumer fuel price relief).
The estimated loss in revenue shall be determined by the
difference between the total amount of the oil company franchise
tax revenue collected in the previous calendar year and the
estimated amount that would have been collected in the previous
calendar year if the oil company franchise tax was applied to
the average wholesale price calculation method used by the
Department of Revenue after December 31, 2016, and before
January 1, 2027 .
