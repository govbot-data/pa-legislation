AN ACT
Amending Title 23 (Domestic Relations) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in child custody, further providing
for standing for partial physical custody and supervised
physical custody and for factors to consider when awarding
custody.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Sections 5325 and 5328(c)(1) introductory
paragraph and (2) introductory paragraph of Title 23 of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes are amended to read:
§ 5325. Standing for partial physical custody and supervised
physical custody.
(a) Standing.--In addition to situations set forth in
section 5324 (relating to standing for any form of physical
custody or legal custody), grandparents and great-grandparents
may file an action under this chapter for partial physical
custody or supervised physical custody in the following
situations:
(1) where the parent of the child is deceased, a parent
or grandparent of the deceased parent may file an action

under this section[;] if all of the following conditions are

(i) The parent or grandparent of the deceased parent
establishes by clear and convincing evidence that the
surviving parent or guardian has denied the parent or
grandparent all access to the child.
(ii) The child has, for a period of at least six
consecutive months, resided with the parent or
grandparent of the deceased parent, excluding brief
temporary absences of the child from the home, and is
removed from the home by a parent or guardian, in which
case the action must be filed within six months after the
removal of the child from the home.
(iii) The parent or grandparent of the deceased
parent has not previously placed the child at risk due to
that individual's abuse, neglect, drug or alcohol abuse
or incapacity;
(2) where the relationship with the child began either
with the consent of a parent of the child or under a court
order and where the parents of the child:
(i) have commenced a proceeding for custody; and
(ii) do not agree as to whether the grandparents or
great-grandparents should have custody under this
section; or
(3) when the child has, for a period of at least 12
consecutive months, resided with the grandparent or great-
grandparent, excluding brief temporary absences of the child
from the home, and is removed from the home by the parents,
an action must be filed within six months after the removal
of the child from the home.

(b) Parental rights.--Nothing in this section shall be
construed to restrict a parent's right to limit a nonparent's
access to the child. A limitation on access to the child shall
not, by itself, confer standing under this section on a parent
or grandparent of a deceased parent to file an action for
partial physical custody or supervised physical custody.
(c) Modification or termination.--A parent of a child
subject to a custody order entered in an action for partial
physical custody or supervised physical custody in which
standing was based on the death of a parent of the child under
subsection (a)(1) may petition the court to modify or terminate
the order. Upon the filing of a petition under this subsection,
the court shall determine whether the individual awarded custody
would have satisfied the requirements for standing under
subsection (a)(1) at the time the action was filed.
