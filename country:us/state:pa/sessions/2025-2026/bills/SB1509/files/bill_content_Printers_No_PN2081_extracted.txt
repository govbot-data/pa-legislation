Standing for partial physical custody and supervised physical custody.(a) Standing.--In addition to situations set forth in section 5324 (relating to standing for any form of physical custody or legal custody), grandparents and great-grandparents may file an action under this chapter for partial physical custody or supervised physical custody in the following situations:(1) where the parent of the child is deceased, a parent or grandparent of the deceased parent may file an action 1234567891011121314151617181920 under this section[;] if all of the following conditions are met:(i) The parent or grandparent of the deceased parent establishes by clear and convincing evidence that the surviving parent or guardian has denied the parent or grandparent all access to the child.(ii) The child has, for a period of at least six consecutive months, resided with the parent or grandparent of the deceased parent, excluding brief temporary absences of the child from the home, and is removed from the home by a parent or guardian, in which case the action must be filed within six months after the removal of the child from the home.(iii) The parent or grandparent of the deceased parent has not previously placed the child at risk due to that individual's abuse, neglect, drug or alcohol abuse or incapacity;(2) where the relationship with the child began either with the consent of a parent of the child or under a court order and where the parents of the child:(i) have commenced a proceeding for custody;
