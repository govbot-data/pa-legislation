2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1463 PN 1950 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1950 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1463 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CAPPELLETTI, COLLETT, TARTAGLIONE, HAYWOOD AND KANE, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of April 23, 1956 (1955 P.L.1510, No.500), entitled "An act providing for the prevention and control of communicable and non-communicable diseases including venereal diseases, fixing responsibility for disease prevention and control, requiring reports of diseases, and authorizing treatment of venereal diseases, and providing for premarital and prenatal blood tests; amending, revising and consolidating the laws relating thereto; and repealing certain acts," providing for cyclosporiasis.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The act of April 23, 1956 (1955 P.L.1510, No.500), known as the Disease Prevention and Control Law of 1955, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 14.2. Cyclosporiasis.--(a) A health care provider shall report each positive test of cyclosporiasis to the department as provided by regulation. The positive test shall be reportable within twenty-four hours in accordance with 28 Pa. Code § 27.21a(b)(1) (relating to reporting of cases by health care practitioners and health care facilities).123456789101112131415161718192021 (b) The department shall promulgate rules and regulations necessary to implement and enforce this section.(c) As used in this section, the term "cyclosporiasis" shall mean a gastrointestinal illness caused by the microscopic parasite Cyclospora cayetanensis.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260SB1463PN1950 - 2 - 123456
