A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the month of September 2026 as "Living Independence
for the Elderly (LIFE) Month" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, September is recognized as National Program of All-
Inclusive Care for the Elderly (PACE) Month; and
WHEREAS, In Pennsylvania, PACE is known as the Living
Independence for the Elderly (LIFE) program; and
WHEREAS, LIFE is a fully integrated model that provides
comprehensive, highly coordinated services that allow older
adults who qualify for a nursing home level of care to continue
living in the community; and
WHEREAS, LIFE provides a model for integrating social
supports and medical care while allowing the person to remain at
home and avoid a hospital admission or nursing home placement;
and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania was one of the first states to
implement this comprehensive, all-inclusive managed care model
in 1998 as an innovative and cost-effective way to provide more

WHEREAS, Today, Pennsylvania LIFE providers operate more than
50 LIFE centers across 54 of this Commonwealth's 67 counties,
serving more than 8,000 participants; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania LIFE Provider Alliance and
LeadingAge PA are committed to supporting, safeguarding and
expanding Pennsylvania's LIFE program through education and
advocacy in order to further enhance the lives of LIFE
participants and others who could benefit from this program; and
WHEREAS, LIFE is an integral part of the aging services
ecosystem by delivering much needed medical, social and
supportive services for older Pennsylvanians with chronic care
needs through community-focused program centers and
interdisciplinary teams; and
WHEREAS, LIFE assumes full financial risk for all medical
care and related services, including transportation,
hospitalization and long-term care, in return for capitated
payments from Medicare and Medicaid, which helps control costs
for the Commonwealth while providing positive health and social
outcomes; and
WHEREAS, Greater awareness of the LIFE program is needed so
that more older Pennsylvanians and their loved ones can make
fully informed decisions on their options for care and support,
thereby helping to alleviate strain on other parts of the aging
services ecosystem; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the
month of September 2026 as "Living Independence for the Elderly
(LIFE) Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it further
