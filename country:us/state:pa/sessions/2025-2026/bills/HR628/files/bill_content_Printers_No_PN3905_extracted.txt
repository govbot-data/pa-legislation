WILLIAMS, SEPTEMBER 23, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON AGING AND OLDER ADULT SERVICES, SEPTEMBER 23, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of September 2026 as "Living Independence for the Elderly (LIFE) Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, September is recognized as National Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly (PACE) Month; andWHEREAS, In Pennsylvania, PACE is known as the Living Independence for the Elderly (LIFE) program; andWHEREAS, LIFE is a fully integrated model that provides comprehensive, highly coordinated services that allow older adults who qualify for a nursing home level of care to continue living in the community; andWHEREAS, LIFE provides a model for integrating social supports and medical care while allowing the person to remain at home and avoid a hospital admission or nursing home placement; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania was one of the first states to implement this comprehensive, all-inclusive managed care model in 1998 as an innovative and cost-effective way to provide more 1234567891011121314151617 nursing home Medicaid-eligible individuals the opportunity to age in their homes and live independently in their communities as long as possible; andWHEREAS, Today, Pennsylvania LIFE providers operate more than 50 LIFE centers across 54 of this Commonwealth's 67 counties, serving more than 8,000 participants; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania LIFE Provider Alliance and LeadingAge PA are committed to supporting, safeguarding and expanding Pennsylvania's LIFE program through education and advocacy in order to further enhance the lives of LIFE participants and others who could benefit from this program; andWHEREAS, LIFE is an integral part of the aging services ecosystem by delivering much needed medical, social and supportive services for older Pennsylvanians with chronic care needs through community-focused program centers and interdisciplinary teams; andWHEREAS, LIFE assumes full financial risk for all medical care and related services, including transportation, hospitalization and long-term care, in return for capitated payments from Medicare and Medicaid, which helps control costs for the Commonwealth while providing positive health and social outcomes; andWHEREAS, Greater awareness of the LIFE program is needed so that more older Pennsylvanians and their loved ones can make fully informed decisions on their options for care and support, thereby helping to alleviate strain on other parts of the aging services ecosystem;
