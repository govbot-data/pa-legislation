A RESOLUTION
Recognizing November 11, 2026, as "Veterans Day" in
Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, From the founding of this Commonwealth to modern day
operations and missions, Pennsylvanians have served our great
nation during war and peace; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania is home to one of the largest veteran
populations in the United States, with more than 600,000
veterans residing in this Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, Veterans have demonstrated courage, dedication and
selfless service in defense of our nation and the freedoms we
cherish; and
WHEREAS, The spouses, friends and family members of service
members provide critical support as service members return to
civilian life with the physical and emotional tolls of their
service; and

WHEREAS, Veterans thrive as members of their communities,
supporting and inspiring the next generation to lead; and
WHEREAS, Veterans make significant contributions to this
Commonwealth's workforce and economy; and
WHEREAS, Individuals in public service have a duty to honor
those who have served our country; and
WHEREAS, "Veterans Day" reminds us of our shared
responsibility to recognize the service and sacrifice of living
and past members of the United States Armed Forces and extend
our continued support; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize
November 11, 2026, as "Veterans Day" in Pennsylvania.
