A RESOLUTION
Recognizing October 20, 2026, as "Medium-chain Acyl-CoA
Dehydrogenase Deficiency Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Medium-chain Acyl-CoA Dehydrogenase (MCAD)
deficiency is a rare inherited genetic disorder that prevents
the body from breaking down medium-chain fatty acids, which are
a type of saturated fats, and converting them into energy; and
WHEREAS, MCAD deficiency can cause problems with an
individual's metabolism and energy production; and
WHEREAS, This deficiency affects approximately 1 in 15,000
babies born in the United States; and
WHEREAS, MCAD deficiency is tested as part of required
newborn screenings in all 50 states in the United States and is
present from birth and throughout one's life; and
WHEREAS, Vital organs that depend on energy signaling, like
the brain, heart and muscles, may stop working properly if MCAD
deficiency goes untreated, leading to a severe lack of energy;
and
WHEREAS, Symptoms of MCAD deficiency can range from mild to

WHEREAS, While there is no cure for MCAD deficiency, there
are treatment options that focus on helping developing babies
maintain normal blood sugar levels; and
WHEREAS, Treatment includes eating more often throughout the
day, eating a diet high in carbohydrates and low in fat and
avoiding overexertion when sick or in recovery; and
WHEREAS, There are currently medical programs within this
Commonwealth focused on metabolic diseases in children at the
Children's Hospital of Philadelphia and the University of
Pittsburgh Medical Center; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize October
20, 2026, as "Medium-chain Acyl-CoA Dehydrogenase Deficiency
Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the
achievements and important work of Pennsylvania-based hospitals
in treating rare metabolic disorders and deficiencies and
acknowledge the importance of raising awareness around MCAD
deficiency in order to continue treatment research.
