JONES, STEHR, FLICK AND ROWE, SEPTEMBER 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HUMAN SERVICES, SEPTEMBER 8, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing October 20, 2026, as "Medium-chain Acyl-CoA Dehydrogenase Deficiency Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Medium-chain Acyl-CoA Dehydrogenase (MCAD) deficiency is a rare inherited genetic disorder that prevents the body from breaking down medium-chain fatty acids, which are a type of saturated fats, and converting them into energy; andWHEREAS, MCAD deficiency can cause problems with an individual's metabolism and energy production; andWHEREAS, This deficiency affects approximately 1 in 15,000 babies born in the United States; andWHEREAS, MCAD deficiency is tested as part of required newborn screenings in all 50 states in the United States and is present from birth and throughout one's life; andWHEREAS, Vital organs that depend on energy signaling, like the brain, heart and muscles, may stop working properly if MCAD deficiency goes untreated, leading to a severe lack of energy; andWHEREAS, Symptoms of MCAD deficiency can range from mild to 123456789101112131415161718 severe, including low or no energy, low blood sugar, muscle weakness, reduced tolerance for exercise and vomiting; andWHEREAS, While there is no cure for MCAD deficiency, there are treatment options that focus on helping developing babies maintain normal blood sugar levels; andWHEREAS, Treatment includes eating more often throughout the day, eating a diet high in carbohydrates and low in fat and avoiding overexertion when sick or in recovery; andWHEREAS, There are currently medical programs within this Commonwealth focused on metabolic diseases in children at the Children's Hospital of Philadelphia and the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize October 20, 2026, as "Medium-chain Acyl-CoA Dehydrogenase Deficiency Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the achievements and important work of Pennsylvania-based hospitals in treating rare metabolic disorders and deficiencies and acknowledge the importance of raising awareness around MCAD deficiency in order to continue treatment research.20260HR0613PN3867 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920
