AN ACT
Amending the act of March 4, 1971 (P.L.6, No.2), entitled "An
act relating to tax reform and State taxation by codifying
and enumerating certain subjects of taxation and imposing
taxes thereon; providing procedures for the payment,
collection, administration and enforcement thereof; providing
for tax credits in certain cases; conferring powers and
imposing duties upon the Department of Revenue, certain
employers, fiduciaries, individuals, persons, corporations
and other entities; prescribing crimes, offenses and
penalties," providing for work opportunity tax credit.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:

the Tax Reform Code of 1971, is amended by adding an article to

Section 1902-L. Amount of credit.
(a) Determination of amount.--The amount of the tax credit
under this section for the taxable year shall be up to $2,400
for the 12 months after the effective date of this subsection.
(b) Tax liability.--A tax credit shall be applied against
the taxpayer's qualified tax liability for the current taxable
year.

Section 1905-L. Remuneration must be for trade or business
employment.
Remuneration paid by an employer to an employee during any
taxable year shall be taken into account only if more than 50%
of the remuneration paid is for services performed in a trade or
business of the employer.
Section 1906-L. Department to notify employers of availability
of credit.
The department shall take steps as necessary or appropriate
to keep employers apprised of the availability of the tax credit
determined under this article.

Section 1908-L. Election to have tax credit not apply.
(a) Election of taxpayer.--A taxpayer may elect to have this
article not apply for any taxable year.
(b) Time for election.--An election under subsection (a) for
any taxable year may be made or revoked at any time before the
expiration of the three-year period beginning on the last date
prescribed by law for filing the return for the taxable year,
without regard to extensions.
(c) Regulations.--An election or revocation under subsection
(a) shall be made in a manner as the secretary determines by
regulation.

Section 1909-L. Treatment of successor employers and treatment
of employees performing services for other persons.
(a) Treatment of successor employers.--Under regulations
promulgated by the secretary, in the case of a successor
employer, the determination of the amount of the tax credit
under this section with respect to wages paid by the successor
employer shall be made in the same manner as if the wages were
paid by the predecessor employer.
(b) Treatment of employees performing services for other
persons.--A tax credit may not be determined under this section
with respect to remuneration paid by an employer to an employee
for services performed by the employee for another person unless
the amount reasonably expected to be received by the employer
for services from the other person exceeds the remuneration paid
by the employer to the employee for the services.
