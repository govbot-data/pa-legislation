AN ACT
Requiring health care providers to provide parents with
information about cortical visual impairment and encourage
communication with ophthalmologists or neurologists.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Provider
Education of Cortical Visual Impairment Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Cortical visual impairment" or "CVI." A condition that
causes vision loss due to brain damage or other injury to the
brain.
"Health care provider." A person, corporation, facility,
institution or other entity licensed, certified or approved by
the Commonwealth to provide health care or professional medical

Section 3. Duties of health care providers.
A health care provider that provides a medical service to a
child shall provide information about CVI along with a
recommendation that an ophthalmologist or neurologist test the
child for CVI if the child has any of the following conditions:
(1) Asphyxia/hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy.
(2) Chromosomal disorders.
(3) Stroke.
(4) Intraventricular hemorrhage.
(5) Seizures.
(6) Congenital abnormalities.
(7) Periventricular leukomalacia.
(8) Cooling protocol.
(9) Congenital or acquired infection.
(10) Metabolic disorders.
(11) Abnormal neurological exam.
(12) Physical trauma.
Section 4. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 90 days.
