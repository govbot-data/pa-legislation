A RESOLUTION
Recognizing October 7, 2026, as "National Propane Day" in
Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Propane has been helping Americans meet their energy
needs for more than 100 years; and
WHEREAS, Propane is a clean-burning and low-carbon fuel that
provides essential energy to residential, commercial, industrial
and agricultural customers across Pennsylvania ensuring a
variety of market sectors can thrive; and
WHEREAS, Propane is a nontoxic and federally designated
clean, alternative fuel that reduces emissions and improves air
quality; and
WHEREAS, America is the world's leading propane producer and
supplier, and propane supports energy security, reliability and
resilience and helps consumers meet their unique heating and
energy needs; and
WHEREAS, America has been a net-exporter of propane for 15
years and propane helps reduce trade deficits with other
countries, increases national security and aids in statecraft;

WHEREAS, Pennsylvania produces more than 245 million gallons
of propane yearly, 2.84% of the United States total; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania's propane industry supports good-paying
jobs and employs nearly 3,000 residents working for more than
175 companies covering every county of this Commonwealth and
contributes $2.7 billion in State economic activity annually;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize October
7, 2026, as "National Propane Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it
further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives highlight propane
as a fuel source, as well as the industry's important role in
assisting the residents of this Commonwealth to meet their
unique energy needs; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize and
celebrate "National Propane Day" and the important contributions
made by this Commonwealth's propane industry.
