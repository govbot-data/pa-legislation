AN ACT
Amending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in assault, further providing for the
offense of strangulation.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 2718(d)(1), (2) and (3)(iii) of Title 18
of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended and the
section is amended by adding a subsection to read:
§ 2718. Strangulation.
* * *
(d) Grading.--
(1) Except as provided in paragraph [(2) or] (3), a
[violation of this section shall constitute a misdemeanor of
the second degree.
(2) A] violation of this section shall constitute a
felony of the second degree.[ if committed:
(i) against a family or household member as defined
in 23 Pa.C.S. § 6102 (relating to definitions);

(ii) by a caretaker against a care-dependent person;
or
(iii) in conjunction with sexual violence as defined
in 42 Pa.C.S. § 62A03 (relating to definitions) or

(relating to stalking) or Subchapter B of Chapter 30

(3) A violation of this section shall constitute a
felony of the first degree if:
* * *
(iii) the defendant has previously been convicted of
an offense under paragraph [(2)] (1) or a substantially
similar offense in another jurisdiction.
(d.1) Bail considerations.--Before setting bail for an
individual arrested for a violation of this section, the issuing
authority shall give due consideration to the fact that
strangulation is a significant predictor of future violent
behavior and is associated with a heightened risk of lethality.
The court shall weigh this factor when determining the
conditions of release, including the potential danger to the
victim and the public.
* * *
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
