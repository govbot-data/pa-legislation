2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2518 PN 4102 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 4102 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2518 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SCHEUREN, PICKETT, KUZMA, LABS AND GILLEN, OCTOBER 20, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, OCTOBER 28, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in assault, further providing for the offense of strangulation.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 2718(d)(1), (2) and (3)(iii) of Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended and the section is amended by adding a subsection to read:§ 2718. Strangulation.* * *(d) Grading.--(1) Except as provided in paragraph [(2) or] (3), a [violation of this section shall constitute a misdemeanor of the second degree.(2) A] violation of this section shall constitute a felony of the second degree.[ if committed:(i) against a family or household member as defined in 23 Pa.C.S. § 6102 (relating to definitions);123456789101112131415161718 (ii) by a caretaker against a care-dependent person; or(iii) in conjunction with sexual violence as defined in 42 Pa.C.S. § 62A03 (relating to definitions) or conduct constituting a crime under section 2709.1 (relating to stalking) or Subchapter B of Chapter 30 (relating to prosecution of human trafficking).](3) A violation of this section shall constitute a felony of the first degree if:* * *(iii) the defendant has previously been convicted of an offense under paragraph [(2)] (1) or a substantially similar offense in another jurisdiction.(d.1) Bail considerations.--Before setting bail for an individual arrested for a violation of this section, the issuing authority shall give due consideration to the fact that strangulation is a significant predictor of future violent behavior and is associated with a heightened risk of lethality. The court shall weigh this factor when determining the conditions of release, including the potential danger to the victim and the public.* * *Section 2.
