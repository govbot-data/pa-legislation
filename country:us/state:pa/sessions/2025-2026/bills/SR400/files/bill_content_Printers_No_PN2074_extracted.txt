Federal Election Commission (2010), the Supreme Court has issued a 12345678910111213141516171819 series of decisions that reshaped the law governing campaign finance, voting rights, redistricting, reproductive freedom, firearms regulation and presidential power, including Shelby County v. andWHEREAS, Public confidence in the Supreme Court has fallen to historically low levels, and the legitimacy of the Court depends on the trust of the public in the integrity and fairness of the institution; andWHEREAS, Terms of 18 years of regular active service, staggered so that a vacancy arises every two years, would give each President two appointments during a four-year term, would reduce the incentive to time retirements for political advantage, would bring new perspectives to the Supreme Court at regular intervals and would lower the stakes of individual confirmation proceedings; andWHEREAS, Under the leading proposals, a justice who completes 18 years of regular active service would assume senior status rather than leave judicial service entirely; andWHEREAS, More than 50 constitutional scholars and retired Federal and state judges have concluded that Congress may establish term limits for justices by statute, legislation to do so has been introduced in the 119th Congress, including S. 5076, H.R. 1074 and H.R. 3544, and members of the Pennsylvania congressional delegation have cosponsored such legislation; andWHEREAS, No other major democracy grants life tenure to the judges of its highest constitutional court, the highest court of every state but one does not provide life tenure, and the Constitution of Pennsylvania provides for ten-year terms, subject to retention by the voters, and retirement at age 75 for the justices of the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania;
