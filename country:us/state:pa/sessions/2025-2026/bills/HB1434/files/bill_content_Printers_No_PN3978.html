AN ACT
Amending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in minors, further providing for the
offense of sale of tobacco products; and imposing penalties.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 6305(b)(2) introductory paragraph of
Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended,
subsection (b) is amended by adding a paragraph and subsection
(h) is amended by adding an introductory paragraph to read:
SECTION 1. SECTION 6305(B)(2) INTRODUCTORY PARAGRAPH AND (C)
(2) OF TITLE 18 OF THE PENNSYLVANIA CONSOLIDATED STATUTES ARE
AMENDED, SUBSECTIONS (B) AND (H) ARE AMENDED BY ADDING
PARAGRAPHS AND SUBSECTION (K) IS AMENDED BY ADDING A DEFINITION
TO READ:
§ 6305. Sale of tobacco products.
