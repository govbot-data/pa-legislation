AN ACT
Amending the act of June 13, 1967 (P.L.31, No.21), entitled "An
act to consolidate, editorially revise, and codify the public
welfare laws of the Commonwealth," in public assistance,
providing for drug cost transparency and pharmacy purchasing
protections.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of June 13, 1967 (P.L.31, No.21), known
as the Human Services Code, is amended by adding a section to
read:
Section 449.3. Drug Cost Transparency and Pharmacy
Purchasing Protections.--(a) Each pharmacy enrolled in the
medical assistance program and each distributor that supplies
prescription drugs to a pharmacy enrolled in the medical
assistance program shall submit to the department, in accordance
with subsection (b), drug cost transparency reports related to
drugs dispensed and distributed under the medical assistance
program for the following purposes:

(1) Identifying factors that impact pharmacy drug
acquisition cost.
(2) Improving transparency in the drug supply chain.
(3) Understanding geographic differences in drug acquisition
costs across this Commonwealth.
(4) Enabling the department to analyze and make informed
policy decisions.
(5) Protecting pharmacies from distributor practices that
disadvantage pharmacies.
(b) A drug cost transparency report under subsection (a)
shall be submitted in a manner, format and at a frequency as
determined by the department, but no less than quarterly. The
department shall determine the scope of drugs to be included in
a report, the required drug and pricing data, including rebates
and other discounts, and the method by which the report shall be
submitted.
(c) The following prohibitions shall apply to drugs
distributed under the medical assistance program:
(1) A distributor that supplies prescription drugs to a
pharmacy enrolled in the medical assistance program may not:
(i) Tie the purchase price of a brand name drug to the
purchase volume of generic drugs.
(ii) Offer, grant or apply any off-invoice rebate or
allowance to an enrolled pharmacy for purchased drugs.
(2) A pharmacy enrolled in the medical assistance program
may not accept or receive from a distributor any off-invoice
rebate or allowance for purchased drugs.
(d) As used in this section, the following words and phrases
shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless
the context clearly indicates otherwise:

"Discounts" means price reductions applied at the time of
purchase, including volume purchasing, prompt pay and group
purchasing.
"Distributor" shall have the same meaning as defined in
section 2 of the act of April 14, 1972 (P.L.233, No.64), known
as The Controlled Substance, Drug, Device and Cosmetic Act.
"Off-invoice rebate or allowance" means any reduction,
deduction, subtraction or discount applied by a distributor
directly to the gross billing amount on the face of the actual
purchase invoice.
"Rebate" means the percentage of payment returned after the
purchase.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
