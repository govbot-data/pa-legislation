2025-2026 Regular Session HR 652 PN 4030 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 4030 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.652 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KULIK, VENKAT, DUKES AND HOHENSTEIN, OCTOBER 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON GAME AND FISHERIES, OCTOBER 9, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDirecting the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to conduct a study and issue a report on the compensation, benefits and classification of Pennsylvania Game Commission game wardens and Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission waterways conservation officers.WHEREAS, In 2025, the Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission and the Pennsylvania Game Commission jointly retained the Wildlife Management Institute (WMI) to evaluate opportunities for cooperation, shared services and potential consolidation of their law enforcement bureaus; andWHEREAS, WMI concluded that unifying the law enforcement functions of the Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission and the Pennsylvania Game Commission would streamline communication, improve efficiency and provide greater officer coverage during peak hunting, fishing and boating seasons; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee conduct a study and issue a report on the compensation, benefits and classification of Pennsylvania Game Commission game wardens and Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission waterways conservation 12345678910111213141516171819 officers; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the study analyze and make recommendations regarding the following:(1) the compensation and benefits packages currently given to Pennsylvania Game Commission game wardens and Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission waterways conservation officers compared with those provided to law enforcement officers performing similar duties in other states; and(2) the appropriate compensation, benefits and classification of officers if a unified conservation law enforcement structure were implemented by the Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission and the Pennsylvania Game Commission, including how the compensation compares with law enforcement officers performing similar duties in other states;and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee submit a report of its findings and recommendations to the Game and Fisheries Committee of the Senate and the Game and Fisheries Committee of the House of Representatives no later than 180 days after the adoption of this resolution;
