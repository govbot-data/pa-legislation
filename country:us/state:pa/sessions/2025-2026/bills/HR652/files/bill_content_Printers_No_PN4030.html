A RESOLUTION
Directing the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to
conduct a study and issue a report on the compensation,
benefits and classification of Pennsylvania Game Commission
game wardens and Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission
waterways conservation officers.
WHEREAS, In 2025, the Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission
and the Pennsylvania Game Commission jointly retained the
Wildlife Management Institute (WMI) to evaluate opportunities
for cooperation, shared services and potential consolidation of
their law enforcement bureaus; and
WHEREAS, WMI concluded that unifying the law enforcement
functions of the Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission and the
Pennsylvania Game Commission would streamline communication,
improve efficiency and provide greater officer coverage during
peak hunting, fishing and boating seasons; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee
conduct a study and issue a report on the compensation, benefits
and classification of Pennsylvania Game Commission game wardens
and Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission waterways conservation

RESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to
