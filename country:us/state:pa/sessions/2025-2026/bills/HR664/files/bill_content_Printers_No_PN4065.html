A RESOLUTION
Honoring the memory of the late Honorable Franklin L. Kury,
Esq., former member of the House of Representatives from the
108th Legislative District and the Senate of Pennsylvania
from the 27th Senatorial District.
WHEREAS, Mr. Kury was born October 15, 1936, in Sunbury, to
Barney and Helen Witkowski Kury; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Kury graduated from Sunbury High School in 1954
and Trinity College in 1958, and he earned a Bachelor of Laws
degree from the University of Pennsylvania Law School in 1961;
and
WHEREAS, Mr. Kury served this country with honor and
distinction as a member of the United States Army and Army
Reserve; and
WHEREAS, Early in his career, Mr. Kury was employed as an
attorney and served as a Deputy Attorney General with the
Pennsylvania Office of Attorney General; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Kury also served as a staff assistant to
Governor George M. Leader and as an aide to United States
Representative George M. Rhodes; and

WHEREAS, Mr. Kury was elected to the House of Representatives
for the 1967 term and served the 108th Legislative District
through 1972; and
WHEREAS, During his tenure, Mr. Kury was responsible for
authoring the 1971 amendment to the Pennsylvania Constitution
that outlined the rights provided to citizens of this
Commonwealth regarding the State's natural resources and its
responsibility for conserving and maintaining them; and
WHEREAS, As a member of the Senate from 1973 through 1980,
Mr. Kury represented the 27th Senatorial District, served as
Chair of the Consumer Affairs Committee and was instrumental in
reforming the Confirmation Process and the Pennsylvania Public
Utility Commission and in passing the Flood Plain Management and
Storm Water Management Acts of 1978; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Kury later served as a partner with Kury and
Kury and Reed Smith, LLP, and was involved with Tive Hetrick &
Pierce and Malady & Wooten; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Kury was the author of several books, including
"Clean Politics, Clean Streams: A Legislative Autobiography and
Reflections," "Why Are You Here? A Primer for Legislators and
Citizens" and "The Constitutional Question to Save the Planet:
The Peoples' Right to a Healthy Environment"; and
WHEREAS, A member of the Northumberland County and American
Bar associations, Mr. Kury was active with the Polish National
Alliance, the Hawk Mountain Sanctuary, the Pennsylvania
Environmental Council and the Pennsylvania Heritage Foundation;
and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives joins in expressing
sorrow for the loss of Mr. Kury, who died August 16, 2026, at 89
years of age; and

WHEREAS, The House of Representatives offers condolences to
Mr. Kury's family, including his sons, grandchildren and
siblings, and his extended family and friends; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives commends Mr. Kury's
contributions as a legislator and attorney; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives celebrates the
meaningful ways in which Mr. Kury contributed to the residents,
businesses and organizations of Montour, Northumberland,
Juniata, Snyder and Union Counties and this Commonwealth;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives honor the memory
of the late Honorable Franklin L. Kury, Esq., former member of
the House of Representatives from the 108th Legislative District
and the Senate of Pennsylvania from the 27th Senatorial
District; and be it further
RESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to
Mr. Kury's family.
