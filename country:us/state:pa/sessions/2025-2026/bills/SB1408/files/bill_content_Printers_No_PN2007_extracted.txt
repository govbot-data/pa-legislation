PROVIDING FOR DATA CENTER TRANSPARENCY; PROHIBITING CERTAIN NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENTS BETWEEN PUBLIC AGENCIES AND DATA CENTERS; PROVIDING FOR DATA CENTERS AND PUBLIC INPUT; PROHIBITING DATA CENTERS FROM BEING DEVELOPED OR OPERATED BY CERTAIN FOREIGN ENTITIES; AND PROVIDING FOR ENFORCEMENT BY ATTORNEY GENERAL AND FOR ENERGY AND WATER REPORTING.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Data Center Nondisclosure Agreement Prohibition Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Data center." A facility or group of facilities, including an ancillary use or associated structure located on the same tract or parcel of land, that:(1) is predominantly used to house working servers or <--<--<--123456789101112131415161718192021 similar data storage systems; and(2) has an uninterruptible energy supply or generator backup power, or both, cooling systems, towers and other temperature control infrastructure."Public agency." A Commonwealth agency or local agency as defined in section 102 of the act of February 14, 2008 (P.L.6, No.3), known as the Right-to-Know Law.Section 3. Prohibition.A public agency may not enter into an agreement with the owner or operator of a data center or a person acting on behalf of a data center concerning the construction, development or location of the data center, if the agreement prohibits or otherwise limits the public agency from disclosing information about the data center, the construction, development or location of the data center or the agreement to the public.Section 4. Legal effect.An agreement made in violation of section 3 is void and unenforceable.Section 5. Effective date.This act shall take effect in 60 days.CHAPTER 1PRELIMINARY PROVISIONSSECTION 101. DATA CENTERS AND PUBLIC INPUT.(A) PUBLIC HEARING.--IN ADDITION TO ANY OTHER APPLICABLE PROVISION OF LAW, THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE MUNICIPALITY THAT HAS THE AUTHORITY TO APPROVE A DATA CENTER APPLICATION SHALL HOLD AT LEAST TWO PUBLIC MEETINGS FOR THE PURPOSE OF SOLICITING PUBLIC COMMENTS BEFORE APPROVAL OF A DATA CENTER APPLICATION. THE PUBLIC MEETINGS SHALL BE ADVERTISED BY PUBLIC NOTICE, WHICH SHALL INCLUDE:(1) THE DATA CENTER OWNER OR OPERATOR'S NAME, ADDRESS AND TELEPHONE NUMBER.(2) THE ADDRESS OF THE SITE WHERE THE PROPOSED DATA CENTER WILL BE LOCATED.(B) ADDITIONAL INFORMATION.--PRIOR TO SCHEDULING THE HEARINGS REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (A), THE GOVERNING BODY MUST BE IN RECEIPT OF DOCUMENTATION EVIDENCING ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:(1) THE APPLICANT HAS FILED AN APPLICATION FOR ELECTRIC SERVICE FOR THE DATA CENTER WITH THE PUBLIC UTILITY OR OTHER PROVIDER FROM WHICH THE APPLICANT PROPOSES TO OBTAIN ELECTRIC SERVICE.(2) THE APPLICANT HAS PAID ALL FEES AND COSTS IMPOSED BY THE PUBLIC UTILITY OR OTHER PROVIDER IN CONNECTION WITH THE APPLICATION UNDER PARAGRAPH (1).CHAPTER 7SECURE DATA CENTER FACILITIESSECTION 701.
