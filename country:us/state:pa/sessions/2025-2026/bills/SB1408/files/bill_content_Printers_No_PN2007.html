AN ACT
Prohibiting certain nondisclosure agreements between public
agencies and data centers. PROVIDING FOR DATA CENTER
TRANSPARENCY; PROHIBITING CERTAIN NONDISCLOSURE AGREEMENTS
BETWEEN PUBLIC AGENCIES AND DATA CENTERS; PROVIDING FOR DATA
CENTERS AND PUBLIC INPUT; PROHIBITING DATA CENTERS FROM BEING
DEVELOPED OR OPERATED BY CERTAIN FOREIGN ENTITIES; AND
PROVIDING FOR ENFORCEMENT BY ATTORNEY GENERAL AND FOR ENERGY
AND WATER REPORTING.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Data Center
Nondisclosure Agreement Prohibition Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Data center." A facility or group of facilities, including
an ancillary use or associated structure located on the same
tract or parcel of land, that:
(1) is predominantly used to house working servers or

(2) has an uninterruptible energy supply or generator

temperature control infrastructure.
"Public agency." A Commonwealth agency or local agency as
defined in section 102 of the act of February 14, 2008 (P.L.6,
No.3), known as the Right-to-Know Law.
Section 3. Prohibition.
A public agency may not enter into an agreement with the
owner or operator of a data center or a person acting on behalf
of a data center concerning the construction, development or
location of the data center, if the agreement prohibits or
otherwise limits the public agency from disclosing information
about the data center, the construction, development or location
of the data center or the agreement to the public.
Section 4. Legal effect.
An agreement made in violation of section 3 is void and
unenforceable.
Section 5. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 60 days.

CHAPTER 3
DATA CENTER NONDISCLOSURE
AGREEMENT PROHIBITION
SECTION 301. PROHIBITION.
A PUBLIC AGENCY MAY NOT ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH THE
OWNER OR OPERATOR OF A DATA CENTER OR A PERSON ACTING ON BEHALF
OF A DATA CENTER CONCERNING THE CONSTRUCTION, DEVELOPMENT OR
LOCATION OF THE DATA CENTER, IF THE AGREEMENT PROHIBITS OR
OTHERWISE LIMITS THE PUBLIC AGENCY FROM DISCLOSING INFORMATION
ABOUT THE DATA CENTER, THE CONSTRUCTION, DEVELOPMENT OR LOCATION
OF THE DATA CENTER OR THE AGREEMENT TO THE PUBLIC.

CHAPTER 5
PUBLIC INPUT
SECTION 501. DATA CENTERS AND PUBLIC INPUT.
(A) PUBLIC HEARING.--IN ADDITION TO ANY OTHER APPLICABLE
PROVISION OF LAW, THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE MUNICIPALITY THAT
HAS THE AUTHORITY TO APPROVE A DATA CENTER APPLICATION SHALL
HOLD AT LEAST TWO PUBLIC MEETINGS FOR THE PURPOSE OF SOLICITING
PUBLIC COMMENTS BEFORE APPROVAL OF A DATA CENTER APPLICATION.
THE PUBLIC MEETINGS SHALL BE ADVERTISED BY PUBLIC NOTICE, WHICH
SHALL INCLUDE:
(1) THE DATA CENTER OWNER OR OPERATOR'S NAME, ADDRESS
AND TELEPHONE NUMBER.
(2) THE ADDRESS OF THE SITE WHERE THE PROPOSED DATA
CENTER WILL BE LOCATED.
(B) ADDITIONAL INFORMATION.--PRIOR TO SCHEDULING THE
HEARINGS REQUIRED UNDER SUBSECTION (A), THE GOVERNING BODY MUST
BE IN RECEIPT OF DOCUMENTATION EVIDENCING ALL OF THE FOLLOWING:
(1) THE APPLICANT HAS FILED AN APPLICATION FOR ELECTRIC
SERVICE FOR THE DATA CENTER WITH THE PUBLIC UTILITY OR OTHER
PROVIDER FROM WHICH THE APPLICANT PROPOSES TO OBTAIN ELECTRIC
SERVICE.
(2) THE APPLICANT HAS PAID ALL FEES AND COSTS IMPOSED BY
THE PUBLIC UTILITY OR OTHER PROVIDER IN CONNECTION WITH THE
APPLICATION UNDER PARAGRAPH (1).

THE FOLLOWING WORDS AND PHRASES WHEN USED IN THIS CHAPTER
SHALL HAVE THE MEANINGS GIVEN TO THEM IN THIS SECTION UNLESS THE
CONTEXT CLEARLY INDICATES OTHERWISE:
"CONTROL." HAVING THE POWER TO DIRECT OR CAUSE THE DIRECTION
OF THE MANAGEMENT OR POLICIES OF A BUSINESS ENTERPRISE, WHETHER
THROUGH OWNERSHIP OF SECURITIES, BY CONTRACT, OR OTHERWISE.
"FOREIGN COUNTRY OF CONCERN." A COUNTRY DESIGNATED ON THE
COUNTRIES OF PARTICULAR CONCERN LIST OR THE SPECIAL WATCH LIST
COUNTRIES BY THE UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF STATE.
"FOREIGN ENTITY." A BUSINESS ENTERPRISE THAT IS:
(1) OWNED OR CONTROLLED BY THE GOVERNMENT OF A FOREIGN
COUNTRY OF CONCERN; OR
(2) A PARTNERSHIP, AN ASSOCIATION, A CORPORATION, AN
ORGANIZATION OR OTHER COMBINATION OF PERSONS ORGANIZED UNDER
THE LAWS OF OR HAVING ITS PRINCIPAL PLACE OF BUSINESS IN A
FOREIGN COUNTRY OF CONCERN, OR A SUBSIDIARY OF SUCH ENTITY.
SECTION 702. DATA CENTER CONTROLLED BY FOREIGN ENTITY.
(A) PROHIBITION.--A DATA CENTER CONTROLLED BY A FOREIGN
ENTITY MAY NOT BE OPERATED OR DEVELOPED IN THIS COMMONWEALTH.
(B) EXISTING DATA CENTERS.--A DATA CENTER OPERATED OR BEING
DEVELOPED IN THIS COMMONWEALTH BY A FOREIGN ENTITY ON THE
EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS SUBSECTION SHALL IMMEDIATELY CEASE
OPERATIONS OR DEVELOPMENT OF THE DATA CENTER.
SECTION 703. ENFORCEMENT.
THE ATTORNEY GENERAL MAY, ON BEHALF OF THE COMMONWEALTH,
BRING AN ACTION IN A COURT OF COMPETENT JURISDICTION FOR
INJUNCTIVE RELIEF TO PREVENT A VIOLATION OF THIS CHAPTER.

SECTION 902. ENERGY AND WATER REPORT.
IN ADDITION TO THE CONTENTS OF THE REPORT UNDER SECTION 1813-
B OF THE FISCAL CODE, THE REPORT SHALL INCLUDE A CERTIFICATION
THAT THE DATA CENTER HAS MITIGATED OR ELIMINATED ANY COST-
SHIFTING ONTO RATEPAYERS FROM OTHER CLASSES OF ELECTRIC SERVICE
AS DELINEATED BY THE TARIFF OF THE ELECTRIC DISTRIBUTION COMPANY
SERVING THE DATA CENTER. THE MITIGATION AND ELIMINATION SHALL BE
CARRIED OUT TO AN EXTENT NO LESS STRINGENT THAN THOSE DETAILED
IN THE COMMISSION'S FINAL LARGE LOAD CUSTOMER MODEL TARIFF
(DOCKET M-2025-3054271) AS OF THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS SECTION
AND SHALL BE CERTIFIED ACCORDINGLY.
