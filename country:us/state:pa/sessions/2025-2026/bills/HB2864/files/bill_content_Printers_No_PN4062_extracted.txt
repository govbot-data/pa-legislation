WILLIAMS AND BERNSTINE, OCTOBER 19, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON INTERGOVERNMENTAL AFFAIRS AND OPERATIONS, OCTOBER 19, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An act relating to the public school system, including certain provisions applicable as well to private and parochial schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," in interscholastic athletics accountability, further providing for council recommendations and standards.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 1604-A(b) of the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a paragraph to read:Section 1604-A. Council Recommendations and Standards.--* * *(b) The association shall take all steps necessary to comply with the following standards:* * *(15) By August 1, 2027, establish a sports medicine policy that creates a role for doctors of chiropractic licensed under the act of December 16, 1986 (P.L.1646, No.188), known as the 1234567891011121314151617181920 Chiropractic Practice Act. The policy shall:(i) Consider the criteria required for a doctor of chiropractic to qualify as an authorized medical examiner to complete preparticipation examinations.(ii) Consider the criteria required for a doctor of chiropractic to perform return to play examinations following either an injury or a concussion.(iii) Assess the role of advanced professional certifications in qualifying a doctor of chiropractic to work in school sports settings. The assessment shall include, but not be limited to, certifications offered by the American Chiropractic Board of Sports Physicians.Section 2. Nothing in this act shall be construed to authorize a licensed doctor of chiropractic to practice beyond the provisions of the act of December 16, 1986 (P.L.1646, No.188), known as the Chiropractic Practice Act.Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260HB2864PN4062 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617
