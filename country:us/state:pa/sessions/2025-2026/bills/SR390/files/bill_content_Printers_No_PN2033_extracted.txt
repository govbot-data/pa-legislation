andWHEREAS, Medical assistance funding comprised 30% of General Fund spending in the 2024-2025 fiscal year, while total Federal and State payments topped $49 billion; andWHEREAS, Approximately a third of Medicaid-funded personal care services are provided through participant-directed programs; andWHEREAS, The Federal Government announced in March 2026 an executive order establishing the "Task Force to Eliminate Fraud" and has moved aggressively to identify fraud in benefit programs; and12345678910111213141516171819 WHEREAS, Minnesota and California have been subject to Medicaid deferrals over the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services' concerns related to the states' respective oversight of fraud, waste and abuse and all states have been notified of additional obligations and Federal oversight; andWHEREAS, The United States Department of Health and Human Services Office of Inspector General reported that in 2025 the Medicaid Fraud Control Section of the Commonwealth Attorney General's Office ranked first nationally in criminal convictions and third in charges filed against violators, demonstrating the significance of fraud in the Commonwealth's programs; andWHEREAS, Based upon known instances of fraud within the Commonwealth's programs, reports of fraud, waste and abuse in other states and the Federal Government's stance on addressing fraud, the General Assembly, exercising our oversight power, should determine if our participant-directed medical assistance programs are operating safe from fraud, waste and abuse and within the purposes and restrictions provided; andWHEREAS, Preventing fraud, waste and abuse will work to maximize the resources available to qualified recipients and avoid adverse consequences from Federal oversight authorities; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate direct the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to conduct a performance audit of the participant-directed medical assistance programs of the Commonwealth and provide a report to the General Assembly;
