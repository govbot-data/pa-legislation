A RESOLUTION
Directing the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to
conduct a performance audit of the participant-directed
medical assistance programs of the Commonwealth and provide a
report to the General Assembly.
WHEREAS, Medical assistance, or Medicaid, is a joint Federal
and State program administered by the Department of Human
Services that provides comprehensive physical and behavioral
health care services to qualifying low-income and disabled
individuals; and
WHEREAS, Medical assistance funding comprised 30% of General
Fund spending in the 2024-2025 fiscal year, while total Federal
and State payments topped $49 billion; and
WHEREAS, Approximately a third of Medicaid-funded personal
care services are provided through participant-directed
programs; and
WHEREAS, The Federal Government announced in March 2026 an
executive order establishing the "Task Force to Eliminate Fraud"
and has moved aggressively to identify fraud in benefit
programs; and

WHEREAS, Minnesota and California have been subject to
Medicaid deferrals over the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid
Services' concerns related to the states' respective oversight
of fraud, waste and abuse and all states have been notified of
additional obligations and Federal oversight; and
WHEREAS, The United States Department of Health and Human
Services Office of Inspector General reported that in 2025 the
Medicaid Fraud Control Section of the Commonwealth Attorney
General's Office ranked first nationally in criminal convictions
and third in charges filed against violators, demonstrating the
significance of fraud in the Commonwealth's programs; and
WHEREAS, Based upon known instances of fraud within the
Commonwealth's programs, reports of fraud, waste and abuse in
other states and the Federal Government's stance on addressing
fraud, the General Assembly, exercising our oversight power,
should determine if our participant-directed medical assistance
programs are operating safe from fraud, waste and abuse and
within the purposes and restrictions provided; and
WHEREAS, Preventing fraud, waste and abuse will work to
maximize the resources available to qualified recipients and
avoid adverse consequences from Federal oversight authorities;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate direct the Legislative Budget and
Finance Committee to conduct a performance audit of the
participant-directed medical assistance programs of the
Commonwealth and provide a report to the General Assembly; and
be it further
RESOLVED, That the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee
be authorized to request any data or information that is
necessary to conduct the performance audit from the agencies and
