AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in certificate of title and security interests,
providing for digital titling system.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1120. Digital titling system.
The department shall create a digital titling system that
allows for the electronic submission of registration and title
documents from dealers for initial registration and titling and
the transfer of ownership of a vehicle. The system shall be
implemented by qualified and currently contracted electronic
registration vendors already approved by the department. The
following shall apply:
(1) The system shall include the following:
(i) A digitization of all forms required for title
and registration, which shall:
(A) be performed by the qualified vendor in

(B) allow for the forms to be submitted without

(ii) The use of electronic signatures on all
required title and registration forms that are required
to be submitted for titling and registering a vehicle in
this Commonwealth. The following shall apply:
(A) The department:
(I) may require a document containing an
electronic signature to include an indication
that the signature is electronic;
(II) shall not require an electronic
signature process or an electronic signature
vendor to be certified by the department before
accepting a document that is executed with an
electronic signature;
(III) shall not require a document that has
been executed with an electronic signature to be
converted to physical format by printing for any
purpose, except when submission by physical means
is necessary and required;
(IV) shall not impose any additional
requirement upon an electronic signature; and
(V) may require a certification of
authenticity, audit trail or similar statement or
proof of assurance.
(B) If a signature on a document is required by
law and the document is submitted electronically, the
signature requirement is satisfied by an
authenticated, electronically submitted signature.
