The act of June 13, 1967 (P.L.31, No.21), known as the Human Services Code, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 443.16 443.17 . Medical Assistance Coverage for Menopause Treatments.-- (a) Subject to Federal approval, the department shall provide medical assistance coverage for hormonal and non- hormonal treatments for the management of menopausal symptoms for eligible and enrolled medical assistance recipients . Coverage under this section shall include all of the following:<--<--1234567891011121314151617 (1) Coverage for all drugs, devices and combination products approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration for the treatment of menopausal symptoms.(2) Coverage for behavioral therapy that assists individuals in coping with menopausal symptoms.(3) Coverage for therapy to treat menopause that has been induced by a hysterectomy. MEDICALLY NECESSARY TREATMENTS FOR MENOPAUSE SHALL BE COMPENSABLE UNDER THE MEDICAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAM FOR ELIGIBLE AND ENROLLED MEDICAL ASSISTANCE RECIPIENTS.(b) The department shall seek a State plan amendment or Federal waiver from the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services, if needed, for the provision of hormonal and non- hormonal MEDICALLY NECESSARY treatments for the management of menopausal symptoms MENOPAUSE under the medical assistance program.(c) As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Hysterectomy." A surgical procedure to remove a woman's uterus, which may include removal of the cervix, ovaries or fallopian tubes."Menopausal symptoms." Any of the following:(1) Irregular menstrual periods.(2) Hot flashes.(3) Vaginal or bladder changes.(4) Decreased fertility.(5) Loss of bone density, including osteoporosis.(6) Elevated low-density lipoprotein cholesterol levels.(7) Sleep disturbances, including night sweats."Menopause."
