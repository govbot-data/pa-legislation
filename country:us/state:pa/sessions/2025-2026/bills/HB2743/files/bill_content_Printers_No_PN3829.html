AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," in terms and courses of study,
further providing for dates and times of school terms and
sessions and commencement.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 1504(a) of the act of March 10, 1949
(P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is
amended and the section is amended by adding a subsection to
read:
Section 1504. Dates and Times of School Terms and Sessions;
Commencement.--(a) (1) The board of school directors of each
school [district] entity shall fix the date of the beginning of
the school term[. Unless], subject to the following:
(i) Except as provided in subparagraph (ii), in a school
entity that is not on a year-round education calendar, a school
term for students may not begin:

(A) earlier than the day after Labor Day; and
(B) on the same day as an agricultural fair in the county in
which the school entity is located.
(ii) Upon approval of the Secretary of Education, a board of
school directors of a school entity may begin a school term
prior to the day after Labor Day because of a situation beyond
the control of the school entity, including a major construction
or renovation project involving a school building of the school
entity or a natural disaster.
(2) Unless otherwise determined by the board, the daily
session of school shall open at nine ante-meridian and close at
four post-meridian, with an intermission of one hour at noon,
and an intermission of fifteen minutes in the forenoon and in
the afternoon. Upon request of a board of school directors for
an exception to the aforesaid daily schedule, the Secretary of
Education may, when in his opinion a meritorious educational
program warrants, approve a school week containing a minimum of
twenty seven and one-half hours of instruction as the equivalent
of five (5) school days, or a school year containing a minimum
of nine hundred ninety hours of instruction at the secondary
level or nine hundred (900) hours of instruction at the
elementary level as the equivalent of one hundred eighty (180)
school days.
(3) Professional and temporary professional employes shall
be allowed a lunch period free of supervisory or other duties of
at least thirty minutes.

to:

Section 2. If a conflict exists between this act and a
contractual obligation, which is between a school entity and its
professional and nonprofessional employees, which adopts a
school calendar or provides a process that determines a school
calendar and which is in effect on the effective date of this
section, the contractual obligation shall not be affected by the
conflict and shall remain in effect for the duration of the
contract or until modified in accordance with the contract.
Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
