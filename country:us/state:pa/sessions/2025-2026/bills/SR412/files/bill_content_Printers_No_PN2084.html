A RESOLUTION
Directing the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to
conduct a performance audit of pharmacy benefit arrangements
under the Commonwealth's Medical Assistance managed care
program, including the use of public resources, payment
transparency, applicable requirements, Department of Human
Services oversight and beneficiary pharmacy access and report
its findings and recommendations.
WHEREAS, Pharmacists and other stakeholders have expressed
concerns about pharmacy reimbursement, fees, network
participation and beneficiary access under Medical Assistance
managed care and the extent and contributing factors of those
concerns warrant independent examination; and
WHEREAS, Medical Assistance managed care pharmacy
arrangements involve the Department of Human Services, managed
care organizations (MCOs), pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs), and
pharmacies and transparent payment information and effective
oversight are important to accountability for public resources
and access to covered services; and
WHEREAS, Act 77 of 2024 addressed pharmacy benefit practices
and required the Insurance Department to study specified issues
in the fully insured commercial insurance market; and

WHEREAS, The Insurance Department's May 2026 study excluded
Medicaid and does not establish the payment practices, access
conditions or policy effects in Medical Assistance managed care;
and
WHEREAS, An independent examination can inform legislative
decisions regarding pharmacy reimbursement, network protections,
purchasing arrangements, reporting requirements and oversight;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate direct the Legislative Budget and
Finance Committee to conduct a performance audit of pharmacy
benefit arrangements under the Commonwealth's Medical Assistance
managed care program, including the use of public resources,
payment transparency, applicable requirements, Department of
Human Services oversight and beneficiary pharmacy access and
report its findings and recommendations; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the principal payment analysis cover the most
recent complete 12-month service period ending before adoption
of this resolution for which sufficient claims run out and
reconciled payment information can be obtained, with the period
and covered managed care arrangements documented by the
committee within 30 calendar days after adoption of this
resolution and that limited records outside that period may be
examined as necessary to understand contract history, network
changes and implementation of oversight actions; and be it
further
RESOLVED, That the audit:
(1) describe Medicaid managed care pharmacy utilization
and payment patterns and compare selected, sufficiently
comparable measures with the Insurance Department's fully
insured commercial study, identifying differences in
