The act is amended by adding a section to read:Section 7.1. Employer Policies and Procedures.--(a) An employer shall adopt written policies and procedures for internal investigation of harassment, discrimination or retaliation against employes. The policies shall include, at minimum, a written and signed or a recorded, transcribed and signed statement from the reporting party, accused party and all relevant parties and a collection of any other relevant information, such as videos, audio recordings, photos or police reports.(b) During the process of the investigation, employes shall be informed of confidentiality rules regarding statements made during the investigation and reporting process.(c) Each individual who makes a signed statement shall receive a copy of the statement at the time of intake.(d) An internal investigation shall be conducted even if the current or former employe has initiated a lawsuit against the employer for discrimination.(e) All statements and evidence collected shall be kept for a minimum of one hundred eighty-one days from the date of the initial report.(f) All statements made during the investigation and personal records created may not be used in retaliation against a current or former employe for reporting discrimination to a government agency.(g) An individual may not aid and abet in violating any acts prohibited under this act. (h) An employer shall make the policies and procedures under 20260HB2843PN4019 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 this section available and easily accessible to employes. An employer may comply with this requirement by posting the policies and procedures in common work spaces or break rooms.(i) Employers shall keep a record of all prior policies and procedures regarding discrimination implemented for a period of two years after the effective date of the current policies and procedures.(j) All policies for investigation of discrimination required to be retained under this section shall be provided to the Commission upon request. (k) An employer shall annually report any adverse judgments or administrative rulings made against the employer relating to discrimination to the Commission within a time frame as determined by the Commission by regulation which shall include a sixty-day grace period from the annual reporting date.(l) Within forty-five days of any changes to Federal or State harassment, discrimination or retaliation laws that would impact an employer's standard policies and procedures under this section, the Commission shall develop proposed regulations regarding the change and shall promulgate final regulations regarding the same within six months. Employers shall adopt the required change to the standard policies and procedures not later than forty-five days after publication of final regulations.(m) An individual claiming a violation of this section may file a complaint with the Commission. The complaint must state the name and address of the employer-alleged violation.
