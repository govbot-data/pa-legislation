A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the week of October 25 through 31, 2026, as
"Respiratory Care Week" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists are highly educated and
skilled health care professionals who provide essential care to
people with conditions that affect their ability to breathe; and
WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists care for patients of all ages
and at all points of life, from premature infants requiring
life-sustaining support to critically ill patients requiring
advanced airway management and mechanical ventilation; and
WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists are integral members of the
health care team, providing lifesaving interventions and
expertise in emergency and critical care, neonatal and pediatric
care, medical and surgical services, pulmonary diagnostics,
sleep medicine, pulmonary rehabilitation and home care; and
WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists use their expertise in airway
management, mechanical ventilation, oxygen therapy, pulmonary
function testing, medication delivery, rehabilitation and
