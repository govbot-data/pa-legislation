WARD, BROWN AND CULVER, OCTOBER 14, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, OCTOBER 14, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the week of October 25 through 31, 2026, as "Respiratory Care Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists are highly educated and skilled health care professionals who provide essential care to people with conditions that affect their ability to breathe; andWHEREAS, Respiratory therapists care for patients of all ages and at all points of life, from premature infants requiring life-sustaining support to critically ill patients requiring advanced airway management and mechanical ventilation; andWHEREAS, Respiratory therapists are integral members of the health care team, providing lifesaving interventions and expertise in emergency and critical care, neonatal and pediatric care, medical and surgical services, pulmonary diagnostics, sleep medicine, pulmonary rehabilitation and home care; andWHEREAS, Respiratory therapists use their expertise in airway management, mechanical ventilation, oxygen therapy, pulmonary function testing, medication delivery, rehabilitation and 1234567891011121314151617 patient education to improve outcomes and quality of life; andWHEREAS, Respiratory therapists bring clinical expertise, compassion and calm during some of the most critical moments in a patient's life, when every breath matters; andWHEREAS, "Respiratory Care Week" recognizes the dedication and extraordinary contributions of respiratory therapists in Pennsylvania; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the week of October 25 through 31, 2026, as "Respiratory Care Week" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate encourage residents of this Commonwealth to recognize respiratory therapists for their service and commitment to helping people breathe and live better.20260SR0396PN2041 - 2 - 1234567891011121314
