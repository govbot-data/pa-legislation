AN ACT
Designating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 68471, carrying
Pennsylvania Route 403 over Pine Run in Marion Center
Borough, Indiana County, as the Sergeant Larry McManus
Memorial Bridge.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Sergeant Larry McManus Memorial Bridge.
(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as
follows:
(1) Larry Richard McManus was born August 15, 1952.
(2) He joined the United States Marine Corps on June 28,
1971, became a Sergeant E-5 and was honorably discharged on
June 27, 1975.
(3) For his service, Sergeant McManus received a Good
Conduct Medal, National Defense Service Medal, Vietnam
Service Medal and Presidential Unit Citation.
(4) Sergeant McManus died on January 13, 2023, in
Indiana County.
(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key
