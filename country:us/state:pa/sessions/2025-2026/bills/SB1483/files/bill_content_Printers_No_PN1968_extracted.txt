2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1483 PN 1968 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1968 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1483 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PITTMAN, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 REFERRED TO TRANSPORTATION, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 AN ACTDesignating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 68471, carrying Pennsylvania Route 403 over Pine Run in Marion Center Borough, Indiana County, as the Sergeant Larry McManus Memorial Bridge.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Sergeant Larry McManus Memorial Bridge.(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) Larry Richard McManus was born August 15, 1952.(2) He joined the United States Marine Corps on June 28, 1971, became a Sergeant E-5 and was honorably discharged on June 27, 1975.(3) For his service, Sergeant McManus received a Good Conduct Medal, National Defense Service Medal, Vietnam Service Medal and Presidential Unit Citation.(4) Sergeant McManus died on January 13, 2023, in Indiana County.(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key 12345678910111213141516171819 68471, carrying Pennsylvania Route 403 over Pine Run in Marion Center Borough, Indiana County, is designated as the Sergeant Larry McManus Memorial Bridge.(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to traffic in both directions on the bridge.Section 2. Effective date.This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260SB1483PN1968 - 2 - 12345678
