2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1447 PN 1916 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1916 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1447 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MUTH, COMITTA, ARGALL, COSTA, KEARNEY, HUGHES AND CAPPELLETTI, SEPTEMBER 10, 2026 REFERRED TO TRANSPORTATION, SEPTEMBER 10, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in registration of vehicles, providing for Pennsylvania Environmental Rights Amendment registration plate; and, in fees, further providing for payments to special funds.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1352.3. Pennsylvania Environmental Rights Amendment registration plate.(a) Design.--The department, in consultation with the Department of Environmental Protection and the Department of Conservation and Natural Resources, shall design a special Pennsylvania Environmental Rights Amendment registration plate. The plate shall highlight the rights guaranteed under section 27 of Article I of the Constitution of Pennsylvania, including the right of the people of this Commonwealth to clean air, pure water and the preservation of the natural, scenic, historic and 12345678910111213141516171819 esthetic values of the environment.(b) Application and fee.--Upon application of a person, accompanied by a fee of $27 which shall be in addition to the annual registration fee, the department shall issue the registration plate under subsection (a) for a passenger car or truck with a registered gross weight of not more than 14,000 pounds.(c) Proceeds.--The Keystone Recreation, Park and Conservation Fund shall receive $15 of the fee paid by an applicant under subsection (b).Section 2. Section 1905 of Title 75 is amended by adding a subsection to read:§ 1905. Payments to special funds.* * *(e) Keystone Recreation, Park and Conservation Fund.--Fifteen dollars of each fee received under section 1352.3 (relating to Pennsylvania Environmental Rights Amendment registration plate) shall be credited to the Keystone Recreation, Park and Conservation Fund.Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260SB1447PN1916 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920
