AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in registration of vehicles, providing for
Pennsylvania Environmental Rights Amendment registration
plate; and, in fees, further providing for payments to
special funds.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1352.3. Pennsylvania Environmental Rights Amendment
registration plate.
(a) Design.--The department, in consultation with the
Department of Environmental Protection and the Department of
Conservation and Natural Resources, shall design a special
Pennsylvania Environmental Rights Amendment registration plate.
The plate shall highlight the rights guaranteed under section 27
of Article I of the Constitution of Pennsylvania, including the
right of the people of this Commonwealth to clean air, pure
water and the preservation of the natural, scenic, historic and

(b) Application and fee.--Upon application of a person,
accompanied by a fee of $27 which shall be in addition to the
annual registration fee, the department shall issue the
registration plate under subsection (a) for a passenger car or
truck with a registered gross weight of not more than 14,000
pounds.
(c) Proceeds.--The Keystone Recreation, Park and
Conservation Fund shall receive $15 of the fee paid by an
applicant under subsection (b).
Section 2. Section 1905 of Title 75 is amended by adding a
subsection to read:
§ 1905. Payments to special funds.
* * *
(e) Keystone Recreation, Park and Conservation Fund.--
Fifteen dollars of each fee received under section 1352.3
(relating to Pennsylvania Environmental Rights Amendment
registration plate) shall be credited to the Keystone
Recreation, Park and Conservation Fund.
Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
