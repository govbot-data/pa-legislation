AN ACT
Amending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in assault, further providing for the
offense of strangulation.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 2718 of Title 18 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a subsection to read:
§ 2718. Strangulation.
* * *
(d.1) Duty to warn.--
(1) A law enforcement officer who, in responding to or
investigating an incident, has reasonable grounds to believe
that an individual has been subjected to conduct described in
subsection (a) shall provide the individual with a verbal or
written warning concerning the potential health consequences
of strangulation.
(2) The warning required under paragraph (1) shall
inform the individual of all of the following:
(i) Strangulation may cause serious or life-

threatening injuries even when there are no visible
injuries or immediate symptoms.
(ii) Health consequences may develop or become
apparent after the incident and may include internal
bleeding, brain injury, thyroid damage, miscarriage,
stroke or death.
(iii) The individual should seek immediate medical
evaluation, even if the individual feels well or has no
visible injuries.
(iv) The individual should call 911 or seek
emergency medical assistance immediately if the
individual experiences difficulty breathing or
swallowing, changes in voice, neck or throat swelling, a
severe headache, confusion, weakness, loss of
consciousness or other new or worsening symptoms.
(3) The warning shall be provided as soon as practicable
after the officer identifies the individual as a potential
victim of strangulation. If the individual's medical
condition prevents the individual from receiving or
understanding the warning, the officer shall first obtain
necessary emergency medical assistance and provide the
warning as soon as practicable thereafter. Providing the
warning may not delay emergency medical care.
(4) The duty under this subsection applies regardless of
whether:
(i) The individual has a visible injury.
(ii) An arrest is made or a criminal charge is
filed.
(iii) The individual agrees to cooperate with an
investigation or prosecution.

(5) The law enforcement officer shall document in the

in writing. If the warning could not be provided, the officer

(6) The Pennsylvania State Police, in consultation with
the Department of Health, shall develop and make publicly

(2). A law enforcement officer may satisfy paragraph (1) by
reading or providing the model warning to the individual.

Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
