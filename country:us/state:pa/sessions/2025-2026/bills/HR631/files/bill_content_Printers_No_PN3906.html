A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the week of October 25 through 31, 2026, as
"Respiratory Care Week" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists are highly educated and
skilled health care professionals who provide essential care to
patients with conditions that affect their ability to breathe,
including some critical conditions and cardiac or pulmonary
diseases; and
WHEREAS, To become licensed as a respiratory therapist in
this Commonwealth, an individual must complete an approved
respiratory care program, an examination from the National Board
for Respiratory Care and, every two years, a minimum of 30 hours
of continuing education for licensure renewal; and
WHEREAS, Demand for respiratory therapists is increasing as
the United States population continues to age; and
WHEREAS, Further, estimates from the American Lung
Association show that more than 35 million individuals in the
United States live with a chronic lung disease and almost 44

WHEREAS, The United States Bureau of Labor Statistics

WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists care for patients of all
ages, from premature infants requiring life-sustaining support

and mechanical ventilation; and
WHEREAS, Common conditions that a respiratory therapist may
treat during their work include acute respiratory failure,
asthma, bronchitis, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and
sleep apnea, among many other acute and chronic conditions; and
WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists are integral members of the
health care team, providing lifesaving interventions and
expertise in emergency and critical care, neonatal and pediatric
care, medical and surgical services, pulmonary diagnostics,
sleep medicine, pulmonary rehabilitation and home care; and
WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists use their expertise in airway
management, mechanical ventilation, oxygen therapy, pulmonary
function testing, medication delivery, rehabilitation and
patient education to improve outcomes and quality of life; and
WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists bring clinical expertise,
compassion and calm during some of the most critical moments in
a patient's life, when every breath matters; and
WHEREAS, Respiratory therapists deserve recognition for their
service and commitment to helping Pennsylvanians breathe and
live better; and
