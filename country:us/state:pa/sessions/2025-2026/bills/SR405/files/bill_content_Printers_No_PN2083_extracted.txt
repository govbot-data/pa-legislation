andWHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel is synonymous with Knoebels Amusement Resort, but his life and legacy extend far beyond the beloved family-owned-and-operated park and campground in Elysburg; andWHEREAS, While Mr. Knoebel grew up in the family business and spent much of his career at the helm, he also graduated from Lehigh University with a bachelor of science in engineering geophysics and served our country as an aircraft maintenance officer in the United States Marine Corps and later became a captain in the Marine Corps Reserve; andWHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel was an Eagle Scout with a Bronze Palm and a recipient of the God and Country Award; andWHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel was also a loyal Freemason, attaining the distinction of 33rd Degree Scottish Rite Mason and recently celebrating 60 years of membership;
