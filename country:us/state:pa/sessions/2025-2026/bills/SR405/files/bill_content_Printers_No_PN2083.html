A RESOLUTION
Honoring the life and expressing condolences to the family of
Richard "Dick" Knoebel.
WHEREAS, Richard "Dick" Knoebel was born April 28, 1939; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel is synonymous with Knoebels Amusement
Resort, but his life and legacy extend far beyond the beloved
family-owned-and-operated park and campground in Elysburg; and
WHEREAS, While Mr. Knoebel grew up in the family business and
spent much of his career at the helm, he also graduated from
Lehigh University with a bachelor of science in engineering
geophysics and served our country as an aircraft maintenance
officer in the United States Marine Corps and later became a
captain in the Marine Corps Reserve; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel was an Eagle Scout with a Bronze Palm
and a recipient of the God and Country Award; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel was also a loyal Freemason, attaining
the distinction of 33rd Degree Scottish Rite Mason and recently
celebrating 60 years of membership; and

WHEREAS, With an engineer's mind and a Marine's
determination, Mr. Knoebel led Knoebels Amusement Resort as
president beginning in 1988; and
WHEREAS, During his nearly four decades of leadership, Mr.
Knoebel guided one of the most transformative periods in the
resort's history while carefully preserving the traditions and
values that made Knoebels a place where generations of families
could spend time together making memories; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel took immense pride in the Knoebels team
and believed the park's success was built on the dedication,
craftsmanship and the heart of the people who cared for it every
day; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel served in various leadership roles with
the Pennsylvania Amusement Parks and Attractions and the
International Association of Amusement Parks and Attractions;
and
WHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel was inducted into the International
Association of Amusement Parks and Attractions in 2014; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel's dedication to service and his
community reached well beyond the amusement park industry and
the military; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Knoebel was much more than his accolades and
titles, he was a devoted family man who touched countless lives
through his steady leadership, his determination and his life's
work at Knoebels Amusement Resort; and
WHEREAS, On July 16, 2026, Richard "Dick" Knoebel passed away
at the age of 87, leaving behind his two sons and their wives,
four grandchildren, his brother and sister-in-law, his sister
and her husband, along with many nieces, nephews, cousins and
extended family members; therefore be it
