andWHEREAS, Mr. Columbus sought to find a westward sea route from Europe to Asia and embarked on the first of what would become four voyages across the Atlantic Ocean; andWHEREAS, On October 12, 1492, during that initial voyage, Columbus made landfall on an island in the Bahamas, which he named San Salvador, marking the beginning of centuries of European exploration and settlement in the Americas; andWHEREAS, Columbus's voyages helped initiate the Columbian Exchange, a widespread exchange of people, plants, animals, diseases, goods, ideas and cultural practices between Europe, Africa and the Americas that profoundly influenced the course of human history; andWHEREAS, While individuals of Italian heritage have 1234567891011121314151617 contributed to the history of the Americas for centuries, significant Italian immigration to the United States did not begin until the 19th century; andWHEREAS, Challenging social and economic conditions in Italy, including high unemployment, oppressive taxation, poverty, natural disasters, disease and famine, prompted many Italians to seek greater opportunity and quality of life abroad; andWHEREAS, While educated Italian professionals also emigrated, many Italian immigrants arrived with limited education or specialized training but went on to fuel America's industrial growth by providing labor for factories, mines and the construction of roads, dams, tunnels and bridges; andWHEREAS, As Italian Americans established communities in the nation's major cities, they often faced religious and ethnic discrimination, including acts of violence; andWHEREAS, During this period, Italian Americans embraced Christopher Columbus as a symbol of Italian heritage, honoring his life and accomplishments as an expression of cultural pride and a means of affirming their place in their new homeland; andWHEREAS, In 1892, President Benjamin Harrison issued Proclamation 335, commemorating the 400th anniversary of Columbus's arrival in the Americas and recognizing him as a "pioneer of progress and enlightenment"; andWHEREAS, In 1905, the Order Sons of Italy in America was formed, with the Grand Lodge of Pennsylvania to follow in 1913, laying the foundation for organizations dedicated to preserving Italian-American heritage and culture; andWHEREAS, Beginning in 1937, President Franklin D.
