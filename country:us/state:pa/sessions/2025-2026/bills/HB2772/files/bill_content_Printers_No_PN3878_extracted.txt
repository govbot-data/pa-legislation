Price Memorial Bridge.(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) Job Wilson Price was born July 29, 1970, in Pottstown, Montgomery County.(2) Commander Price was the son of Nancy and Harry Price and brother of Bronwyn Price and attended Pottstown Area School District (PASD), where he was subsequently inducted into the PASD Alumni Honor Roll in 2011.(3) While attending high school from 1985 to 1989, Commander Price served as captain of the football team, receiving first-team All-Conference honors as both an offensive and defensive lineman while concurrently reigning subsequently graduated from the United States Air Force Academy on June 2, 1993, with a degree in behavioral sciences.(5) Commander Price was commissioned as an officer in the United States Navy and subsequently graduated from Basic Underwater Demolition/Seal training with Class 193 in June 1994, receiving the Christopher Tilghman Memorial Fire-in-the-Gut Award.(6) From 1994 to 1997, Commander Price served with SEAL Team Four, from which he received a Navy Achievement Medal for his leadership during two high-profile deployments to Ecuador and Venezuela.(7) Commander Price graduated from Ranger School Class 10-97 and subsequently served with SEAL Team Two from 1997 to 2001, during which time he received the Navy and Marine Corps Commendation and Achievement Medals.(8) From November to December 2000, Commander Price served on a peacekeeping mission in Kosovo, where he was honored with the NATO Medal of Service.(9) Following his initial SEAL Team assignments, Commander Price joined Naval Special Warfare Unit Ten, and he received a Joint Service Commendation Medal and a second Navy and Marine Corps Commendation Medal for his actions as a Lead Planner for Naval Special Warfare
