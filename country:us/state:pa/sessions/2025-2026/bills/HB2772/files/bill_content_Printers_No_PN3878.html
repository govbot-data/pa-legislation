AN ACT
Designating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 27860, carrying
Hanover Street over the Schuylkill River in the City of
Pottstown, Montgomery County, as the Commander Job W. Price
Memorial Bridge.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Commander Job W. Price Memorial Bridge.
(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as
follows:
(1) Job Wilson Price was born July 29, 1970, in
Pottstown, Montgomery County.
(2) Commander Price was the son of Nancy and Harry Price
and brother of Bronwyn Price and attended Pottstown Area
School District (PASD), where he was subsequently inducted
into the PASD Alumni Honor Roll in 2011.
(3) While attending high school from 1985 to 1989,
Commander Price served as captain of the football team,
receiving first-team All-Conference honors as both an
offensive and defensive lineman while concurrently reigning

(4) Commander Price completed Midshipman/Cadet Naval
Special Warfare Orientation on July 17, 1992, and
subsequently graduated from the United States Air Force
Academy on June 2, 1993, with a degree in behavioral
sciences.
(5) Commander Price was commissioned as an officer in
the United States Navy and subsequently graduated from Basic
Underwater Demolition/Seal training with Class 193 in June
1994, receiving the Christopher Tilghman Memorial Fire-in-
the-Gut Award.
(6) From 1994 to 1997, Commander Price served with SEAL
Team Four, from which he received a Navy Achievement Medal
for his leadership during two high-profile deployments to
Ecuador and Venezuela.
(7) Commander Price graduated from Ranger School Class
10-97 and subsequently served with SEAL Team Two from 1997 to
2001, during which time he received the Navy and Marine Corps
Commendation and Achievement Medals.
(8) From November to December 2000, Commander Price
served on a peacekeeping mission in Kosovo, where he was
honored with the NATO Medal of Service.
(9) Following his initial SEAL Team assignments,
Commander Price joined Naval Special Warfare Unit Ten, and he
received a Joint Service Commendation Medal and a second Navy
and Marine Corps Commendation Medal for his actions as a Lead
Planner for Naval Special Warfare actions in support of
Operation Enduring Freedom.
(10) From 2002 to 2004, Commander Price joined United

(11) Commander Price rotated back to SEAL Team Four from
2004 to 2007, during which time he received two Bronze Stars
and a third Navy and Marine Corps Commendation Medal for his
leadership in joint forces operations in Iraq.
(12) Commander Price served with United States Naval
Forces Central Command from 2007 to 2008, and received a
Meritorious Service Medal for his coordination of maritime
operations in the Gulf of Oman.
(13) From 2008 to 2010, Commander Price served with
Joint Special Operations Command, from which he received a
Defense Meritorious Service Medal for his planning in support
of Joint Forces tactical combat operations.
(14) In 2010, Commander Price joined Combined Forces
Special Operations Component Command, serving as Director of
the Special Operations Planning Liaison Element to Regional
Command South, and he received his third Bronze Star, a Joint
Meritorious Unit Award and a third Defense Meritorious
Service Medal for his coordination of more than 500 Special
Operations Forces operations in four Afghan provinces.
(15) Commander Price rotated back to SEAL Team Four in
2011, taking command of the team before his deployment to
Afghanistan, where he assisted in training the Afghan Police
against insurgent elements.
(16) On December 22, 2012, Commander Price succumbed to
a non-combat-related injury in the Uruzgan Province of
Afghanistan while engaging in village stability operations.

(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key
27860, carrying Hanover Street over the Schuylkill River in the
City of Pottstown, Montgomery County, is designated as the

(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and
maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to
traffic in both directions on the bridge.
Section 2. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 60 days.
