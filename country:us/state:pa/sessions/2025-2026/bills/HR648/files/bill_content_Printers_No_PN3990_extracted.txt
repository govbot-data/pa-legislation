2025-2026 Regular Session HR 648 PN 3990 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3990 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.648 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY VENKAT, WAXMAN, HANBIDGE, GUZMAN, RIVERA, DEASY, McNEILL, DUKES AND SANCHEZ, OCTOBER 1, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, OCTOBER 2, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of January 2027 as "National Blood Donor Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, In the United States, approximately 29,000 units of blood are needed every day for blood transfusions; andWHEREAS, These transfusions are lifesaving for millions of people, like individuals who have been in an accident or those with diseases that require frequent transfusions such as certain cancers and sickle cell disease; andWHEREAS, Every two seconds, someone in the United States needs blood, but only approximately 3% of age-eligible people donate yearly; andWHEREAS, Donations also have a limited lifespan, with red blood cells lasting, at most, 42 days and platelets lasting just five days; andWHEREAS, Donations typically decline during the winter months due to inclement weather, holiday celebrations and illness, resulting in less availability for donors, increased odds of canceled blood drives and, ultimately, less access to the blood 123456789101112131415161718 that hospitals critically need; andWHEREAS, To highlight the crucial need for continued donations, January has been recognized as "National Blood Donor Month" since 1970; andWHEREAS, Individuals can honor and observe "National Blood Donor Month" not just by donating blood, but also by learning and remembering their blood type or even organizing or volunteering at a blood drive or screening; andWHEREAS, Most individuals 16 years of age and older, and who are in good health, can donate blood every 56 days, up to six times a year; andWHEREAS, Anyone can partner with the American Red Cross to start the process of hosting their own blood drive; andWHEREAS, It is important to recognize the need for continued blood donations and new blood donors across the Commonwealth; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize January 2027 as "National Blood Donor Month" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0648PN3990 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718
