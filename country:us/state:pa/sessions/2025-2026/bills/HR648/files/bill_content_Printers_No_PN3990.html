A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the month of January 2027 as "National Blood Donor
Month" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, In the United States, approximately 29,000 units of
blood are needed every day for blood transfusions; and
WHEREAS, These transfusions are lifesaving for millions of
people, like individuals who have been in an accident or those
with diseases that require frequent transfusions such as certain
cancers and sickle cell disease; and
WHEREAS, Every two seconds, someone in the United States
needs blood, but only approximately 3% of age-eligible people
donate yearly; and
WHEREAS, Donations also have a limited lifespan, with red
blood cells lasting, at most, 42 days and platelets lasting just
five days; and
WHEREAS, Donations typically decline during the winter months
due to inclement weather, holiday celebrations and illness,
resulting in less availability for donors, increased odds of
canceled blood drives and, ultimately, less access to the blood

WHEREAS, To highlight the crucial need for continued

Month" since 1970; and
WHEREAS, Individuals can honor and observe "National Blood
Donor Month" not just by donating blood, but also by learning
and remembering their blood type or even organizing or
volunteering at a blood drive or screening; and
WHEREAS, Most individuals 16 years of age and older, and who
are in good health, can donate blood every 56 days, up to six
times a year; and
WHEREAS, Anyone can partner with the American Red Cross to
start the process of hosting their own blood drive; and
WHEREAS, It is important to recognize the need for continued
blood donations and new blood donors across the Commonwealth;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize January
2027 as "National Blood Donor Month" in Pennsylvania.
