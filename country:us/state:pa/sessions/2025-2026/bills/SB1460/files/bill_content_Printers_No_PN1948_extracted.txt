The act of July 31, 1968 (P.L.805, No.247), known as the Pennsylvania Municipalities Planning Code, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 622. Prohibiting the Location of Data Centers.--(a) 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627 A data center may not be established or operated within a residential zoning area.(b) A hyperscale and major data center shall only be located in an industrial and mining zone.(c) As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Data center."
