AN ACT
Amending Title 42 (Judiciary and Judicial Procedure) of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in sentencing, providing
for lifetime no-contact orders for victims of covered
offenses.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 42 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 9766. Lifetime no-contact orders for victims of covered
offenses.
(a) Covered offenses.--At the time of sentencing, on the
request of the victim or the prosecutor, the court shall issue
an injunction that prohibits the defendant from contacting the
victim if the defendant is convicted of an offense under the
following:
(1) 18 Pa.C.S. Ch. 25 (relating to criminal homicide).
(2) 18 Pa.C.S. § 2702 (relating to aggravated assault).
(3) 18 Pa.C.S. § 2706 (relating to terroristic threats).
(4) 18 Pa.C.S. § 2709.1 (relating to stalking).

(5) 18 Pa.C.S. § 2901 (relating to kidnapping).
(6) 18 Pa.C.S. Ch. 30 (relating to human trafficking).
(7) 18 Pa.C.S. Ch. 31 (relating to sexual offenses).
(8) 18 Pa.C.S. § 4302 (relating to incest).
(9) 18 Pa.C.S. § 5903 (relating to obscene and other
sexual materials and performances), when the victim is a
minor.
(10) 18 Pa.C.S. § 6312 (relating to sexual abuse of
children).
(11) 18 Pa.C.S. § 6320 (relating to sexual exploitation
of children).
(b) Minor victims.--If the victim is a minor, the motion may
be filed by a parent, legal guardian or other lawful
representative.
(c) Injunction.--If the victim did not request an injunction
at the time of sentencing under subsection (a), the victim may
submit a petition to the court requesting an injunction against
a defendant who was sentenced for an offense listed in
subsection (a). The court may not charge a fee for filing the
petition. A law enforcement agency shall serve an injunction
issued pursuant to this subsection at no charge to the victim.
(d) Lifetime.--An injunction that is issued under this
section does not expire and is valid for the defendant's natural
lifetime unless any of the following occurs:
(1) The defendant makes a showing to the court that
either:
(i) The victim has died.
(ii) The conviction has been dismissed, expunged or
overturned or the defendant has been pardoned.
(2) The victim submits a written request to the court

(e) Violation.--A person who knowingly violates an
injunction issued under this section commits a misdemeanor of
the second degree. A second or subsequent offense under this
subsection constitutes a misdemeanor of the first degree.
(f) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Contact." Any direct or indirect communication,
transmission or physical interaction, whether written, oral,
electronic, digital or physical, initiated personally, through
another individual or through an automated or technological
means.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 30 days.
