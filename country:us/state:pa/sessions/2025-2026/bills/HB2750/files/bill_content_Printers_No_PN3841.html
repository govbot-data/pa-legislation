AN ACT
Amending Title 3 (Agriculture) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in food protection, further providing for
definitions, for license required and for applicability,
providing for microenterprise home kitchen operation and
further providing for definitions.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The definition of "retail food establishment" in
section 5702 of Title 3 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended and the section is amended by adding
definitions:
§ 5702. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this subchapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
* * *
"Home." The primary residence occupied by the residence's
owner, which:
(1) contains only one stove or oven used for cooking,

(2) the stove or oven is operated in an ordinary kitchen

"Homemade food item." Food made at a home. The term does not
include any of the following:
(1) Low-acid canned foods.
(2) Alcoholic beverages.
(3) Foods prepared through smoking or curing as a
preservation method.
(4) Juices unless produced by a microenterprise home
kitchen operation in accordance with 21 CFR Pt. 120 (relating
to hazard analysis and critical control point (HACCP)
systems).
(5) Custom processed meats or poultry.
(6) Shellfish from the operation of a molluscan
shellfish life-support system display tank used to store or
display shellfish that are offered for human consumption.
(7) Potentially hazardous foods prepared using reduced
oxygen methods.
(8) Food containing any drug.
* * *
"Microenterprise home kitchen operation." A home that is
registered with a licensor to sell and deliver directly to a
consumer a homemade food item.
* * *

prepares, packages, vends, offers for sale or otherwise provides
food for human consumption and which relinquishes possession of

Section 3. Section 5712(2) of Title 3 is amended to read:
§ 5712. Applicability.
This subchapter shall not apply to food that meets all of the

Section 4. Title 3 is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 5713.1. Microenterprise home kitchen operation.
(a) Requirements.--A microenterprise home kitchen operation
shall:
(1) Produce homemade food items only in a home kitchen.
(2) Comply with Chapter 65 (relating to food employee
certification).
(3) Operate in accordance with this chapter.
(4) Sell and deliver homemade food items in accordance
with this section.
(b) Prohibitions.--A licensor may not require a
microenterprise home kitchen operation to do any of the
following:
(1) Use commercial kitchen equipment or other
specialized kitchen equipment such as a three-compartment
sink.
(2) Alter the structure or design of the home kitchen.
(3) Install or modify venting in the home kitchen.
(4) Require a certain countertop be used in a home
kitchen.
(5) Prevent the presence of pets from parts of the home

(c) Sales and delivery.--A homemade food item may:
(1) Not be offered or sold to a consumer from another

(2) Be sold by a microenterprise home kitchen operation

delivered by the microenterprise home kitchen operation to
the consumer in person.
(d) Meats.--A microenterprise home kitchen operation may not
slaughter or process meats.
