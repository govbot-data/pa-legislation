Firefighter cancer screening program.(a) Establishment.--To the extent money is available in the fund, the department shall establish and administer a program to make available cancer screenings to eligible firefighters as provided under this part.(b) Eligibility.--The department shall determine a firefighter's eligibility for cancer screenings as provided by the program. A firefighter shall be eligible if:(1) The firefighter meets the following requirements:(i) the firefighter has served 12 or more months in continuous firefighting duties; and(ii) the firefighter has actively responded to at least 15 fire or rescue emergencies during the previous calendar year, as determined and verified by the firefighter's fire company.(2) The firefighter is covered by section 301(f) of the act of June 2, 1915 (P.L.736, No.338), known as the Workers' Compensation Act.(c) Screening locations.--Subject to money being available in the fund, cancer screenings shall be provided at least twice per year in each community health district.(d) Screening availability.--Subject to money being available in the fund, once every three years, an eligible firefighter may obtain cancer screenings from a program screening entity.20260HB2826PN3995 - 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (e) Option.--An eligible firefighter may decline to obtain a cancer screening offered under the program.(f) Screening types.--Subject to available funds, an eligible firefighter may receive any combination of offered cancer screenings, not to exceed a cost of $2,000 per individual, adjusted each January 1 by the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) for the Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware and Maryland area, for the most recent 12-month period for which figures have been officially reported by the United States Department of Labor, Bureau of Labor Statistics immediately prior to the date the adjustment is set to take effect. Cancer screenings available shall include at least the following:(1) A blood test.(2) An ultrasound of vital organs.(3) A lung CT scan, if indicated.(4) A skin cancer screening.(g) Screening conditions.--Cancer screenings shall be offered for at least the following types of cancer:(1) colon;(2) lung;(3) bladder;(4) oral;(5) thyroid;(6) skin;(7) blood;(8) breast;(9) cervical;(10) testicular;(11) prostate;20260HB2826PN3995 - 6 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (12) liver; and(13) kidney.(h) Additional cancer screenings.--(1) Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit a firefighter from paying out-of-pocket for additional cancer screenings provided by the program screening entity.(2) The program screening entity shall inform the firefighter that the purchase of additional cancer screenings is not a condition of receiving cancer screenings under this part.(i) Data.--The program shall collect the following data, which may be shared with the Department of Health and the Office of the Fire Commissioner:(1) Demographic information, if voluntarily provided by the firefighter, including the number of years the individual has been serving as a firefighter.(2) The number of positive cancer screenings, delineated by cancer diagnosis and community service area.(j) Reports.--By June 1 of the second year of the program and each June 1 thereafter, in which the program operates, the department shall prepare and submit a report to the General Assembly that provides:(1) The number of cancer screenings provided in each year of program operation and cumulatively since the inception of the program for each community health district.(2) An accounting of the fund balance, including any amounts generated by investment income.(3) Recommendations, if any, as to the types and frequency of cancer screenings to be provided under the program.20260HB2826PN3995 - 7 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (4) A description of the program's efforts to conduct outreach to eligible firefighters regarding the availability and benefits of cancer screenings.§ 10303. Contracting and procurement.(a) Procurement.--(1) The department may contract with persons and entities as deemed necessary in order to administer the program, including an entity to determine eligibility and one or more program screening entities.(2) The department shall be exempt from the provisions of 62 Pa.C.S. Pts. I (relating to Commonwealth Procurement Code) and II (relating to general procurement provisions) for the purposes of this subsection.(b) Preference.--An entity that can screen for the following conditions shall be preferred:(1) A multicancer blood test that detects signals from as many cancer types as possible, including those that are commonly unscreened.(2) An ultrasound diagnostic that screens, at minimum:(i) Abdominal aorta.(ii) Thyroid.(iii) Liver.(iv) Gallbladder.(v) Spleen.(vi) Bladder.(vii) Kidney.(viii) Testicles.(ix) Exterior pelvis for females.(3) An echocardiogram and a carotid doppler.(c) Multiple entities.--The department may contract with 20260HB2826PN3995 - 8 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 more than one program screening entity as needed to ensure that cancer screenings are available in each community health district.§ 10304. Outreach.To the extent funds are made available and in consultation with the Office of Fire Commissioner and the Department of Health, the department shall conduct educational outreach and create materials regarding the availability and benefits of cancer screenings under the program to the following:(1) The fire chief and president of each volunteer fire company.(2) The chief executive of each municipality in which a municipal fire company is located.(3) The head of each combined volunteer and municipal fire company and each airport fire company.Section 2.
