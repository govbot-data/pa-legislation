andWHEREAS, Luzerne County was formed from a portion of Northumberland County and was named in honor of the Chevalier de la Luzerne, Anne-César de La Luzerne, who served as French minister to the United States during the American Revolution and was a steadfast supporter of the cause of American independence; andWHEREAS, Situated along the Susquehanna River in northeastern Pennsylvania, Luzerne County has played an important role in the history and development of this Commonwealth and our nation; andWHEREAS, The discovery and development of the region's vast anthracite coal resources transformed Luzerne County into a center of industry, attracting generations of hardworking 123456789101112131415161718 families whose labor helped fuel the growth of Pennsylvania and the United States; andWHEREAS, Generations of immigrants from across Europe and around the world made Luzerne County their home, creating a diverse cultural heritage that continues to be reflected in the county's neighborhoods, churches, festivals, traditions and cuisine; andWHEREAS, Luzerne County is home to the cities of Wilkes-Barre, Hazleton, Nanticoke and Pittston, along with numerous boroughs and townships, each contributing to the distinctive character and proud identity of the county; andWHEREAS, From its historic downtowns and close-knit neighborhoods to its mountains, forests, waterways and farmlands, Luzerne County represents the natural beauty, resilience and community spirit that define northeastern Pennsylvania; andWHEREAS, Luzerne County has continually adapted to changing times while honoring the people, industries and traditions that built its communities, demonstrating the strength and perseverance of its residents through periods of prosperity, hardship and renewal;
