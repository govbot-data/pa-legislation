A RESOLUTION
Designating September 25, 2026, as "Luzerne County Day" in
Pennsylvania in recognition of the 240th anniversary of the
establishment of Luzerne County.
WHEREAS, Luzerne County was established on September 25,
1786, making 2026 the 240th anniversary of the county's
founding; and
WHEREAS, Luzerne County was formed from a portion of
Northumberland County and was named in honor of the Chevalier de
la Luzerne, Anne-César de La Luzerne, who served as French
minister to the United States during the American Revolution and
was a steadfast supporter of the cause of American independence;
and
WHEREAS, Situated along the Susquehanna River in northeastern
Pennsylvania, Luzerne County has played an important role in the
history and development of this Commonwealth and our nation; and
WHEREAS, The discovery and development of the region's vast
anthracite coal resources transformed Luzerne County into a
center of industry, attracting generations of hardworking

WHEREAS, The county's rich industrial heritage was built by

WHEREAS, Generations of immigrants from across Europe and

WHEREAS, Luzerne County is home to the cities of Wilkes-
Barre, Hazleton, Nanticoke and Pittston, along with numerous
boroughs and townships, each contributing to the distinctive
character and proud identity of the county; and
WHEREAS, From its historic downtowns and close-knit
neighborhoods to its mountains, forests, waterways and
farmlands, Luzerne County represents the natural beauty,
resilience and community spirit that define northeastern
Pennsylvania; and
WHEREAS, Luzerne County has continually adapted to changing
times while honoring the people, industries and traditions that
built its communities, demonstrating the strength and
perseverance of its residents through periods of prosperity,
hardship and renewal; and
WHEREAS, Today, more than 300,000 residents call Luzerne
County home and continue its proud tradition of service,
entrepreneurship, education, industry and civic engagement; and
