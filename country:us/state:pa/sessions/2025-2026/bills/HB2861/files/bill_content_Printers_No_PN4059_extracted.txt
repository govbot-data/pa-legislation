Section 7304(b) of Title 68 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a paragraph to read:§ 7304. Disclosure form.* * *(b) Contents of property disclosure statement.--The form of property disclosure statement promulgated by the State Real Estate Commission shall call for disclosures with respect to all of the following subjects:* * *(18) Chimney, as provided under section 7316 (relating to chimney inspection).* * *123456789101112131415161718 Section 2. Section 7308 of Title 68 is amended to read:§ 7308. Affirmative duty of seller.[The] Except as provided under section 7316 (relating to chimney inspection), the seller is not obligated by this chapter to make any specific investigation or inquiry in an effort to complete the property disclosure statement. In completing the property disclosure statement, the seller shall not make any representations that the seller or the agent for the seller knows or has reason to know are false, deceptive or misleading and shall not fail to disclose a known material defect.Section 3. Title 68 is amended by adding a section to read:§ 7316. Chimney inspection.(a) Inspection requirement.--Except as provided under subsection (c), prior to the settlement of a residential real estate transfer involving property containing a chimney, not including a chimney that has been permanently rendered unusable, the seller shall obtain a Level II chimney inspection performed in accordance with the National Fire Protection Association 211 standard, in effect on the effective date of this subsection, by a qualified chimney inspector at the seller's expense.(b) Inspection report.--The seller shall provide a copy of the inspection report required under subsection (a) to the buyer prior to the signing of an agreement of transfer by the seller and buyer with respect to the property.(c) Exceptions.--The inspection requirement under subsection (a) shall not apply to a transfer exempt from the requirements under section 7302 (relating to application of chapter).(d) Regulations.--The State Real Estate Commission may promulgate regulations necessary to implement this section.(e) Definition.--As used in this section, the term 20260HB2861PN4059 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 "qualified chimney inspector" shall mean an individual who:(1) holds a current certified chimney sweep credential issued by the Chimney Safety Institute of America; or(2) holds a current certification issued by a nationally recognized chimney or fireplace inspection organization that requires demonstrated knowledge of National Fire Protection Association 211 standard and the inspection of chimney and venting systems.Section 4.
