AN ACT
Amending Title 68 (Real and Personal Property) of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in seller disclosures,
further providing for disclosure form and for affirmative
duty of seller and providing for chimney inspection.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 7304(b) of Title 68 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a paragraph to read:
§ 7304. Disclosure form.
* * *
(b) Contents of property disclosure statement.--The form of
property disclosure statement promulgated by the State Real
Estate Commission shall call for disclosures with respect to all
of the following subjects:
* * *
(18) Chimney, as provided under section 7316 (relating
to chimney inspection).

Section 2. Section 7308 of Title 68 is amended to read:
§ 7308. Affirmative duty of seller.
[The] Except as provided under section 7316 (relating to
chimney inspection), the seller is not obligated by this chapter
to make any specific investigation or inquiry in an effort to
complete the property disclosure statement. In completing the
property disclosure statement, the seller shall not make any
representations that the seller or the agent for the seller
knows or has reason to know are false, deceptive or misleading
and shall not fail to disclose a known material defect.
Section 3. Title 68 is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 7316. Chimney inspection.
(a) Inspection requirement.--Except as provided under
subsection (c), prior to the settlement of a residential real
estate transfer involving property containing a chimney, not
including a chimney that has been permanently rendered unusable,
the seller shall obtain a Level II chimney inspection performed
in accordance with the National Fire Protection Association 211
standard, in effect on the effective date of this subsection, by
a qualified chimney inspector at the seller's expense.
(b) Inspection report.--The seller shall provide a copy of
the inspection report required under subsection (a) to the buyer
prior to the signing of an agreement of transfer by the seller
and buyer with respect to the property.
(c) Exceptions.--The inspection requirement under subsection
(a) shall not apply to a transfer exempt from the requirements
under section 7302 (relating to application of chapter).
(d) Regulations.--The State Real Estate Commission may
promulgate regulations necessary to implement this section.
