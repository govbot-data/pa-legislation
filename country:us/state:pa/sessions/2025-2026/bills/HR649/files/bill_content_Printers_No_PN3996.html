A RESOLUTION
Designating November 10, 2026, as "Chesapeake Bay Foundation
Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The Chesapeake Bay Foundation, a private, nonprofit,
multistate organization opened its Pennsylvania office in 1986
in recognition of the significance of the Susquehanna River as
the Chesapeake Bay's largest tributary and to further the
organization's ambitious mission statement to "Save the Bay and
its waterways by educating, uniting and inspiring people to take
action"; and
WHEREAS, The Chesapeake Bay Foundation has, from its
beginning, offered private citizens opportunities to support and
participate directly in the protection and restoration of its
namesake estuary; and
WHEREAS, The Chesapeake Bay Foundation, which has its
headquarters in Annapolis, Maryland, has also grown its presence
to include offices in Richmond and Hampton Roads, Virginia and
Washington, DC; and

WHEREAS, The Chesapeake Bay Foundation's Pennsylvania
membership is 25,000, and the organization, collectively, has
surpassed 202,000; and
WHEREAS, As it marks its 40th anniversary in Pennsylvania,
the Chesapeake Bay Foundation continues to advance environmental
education with the support of public and private funding,
engaging more than 1,500 participant days each year through
hands-on watershed experiences, educator training, curriculum
support, restoration programs and opportunities to explore
Pennsylvania's water challenges firsthand, inspiring generations
to protect and preserve the Chesapeake Bay for years to come;
and
WHEREAS, Through dedicated advocacy, the Chesapeake Bay
Foundation's members and staff have advanced clean water
policies and programs at the Federal, State and local levels,
contributing significantly to the restoration of the Susquehanna
River watershed and supporting the Chesapeake Bay's continued
recovery; and
WHEREAS, As part of that recovery effort, the Chesapeake Bay
Foundation's members and staff have effectively worked with
legislative leaders to advocate in Pennsylvania for the
designation of the Eastern Hellbender as Pennsylvania's official
amphibian, raising awareness of the species' reliance on cold,
clean water, for the creation of the Keystone Tree Fund, which
enables Pennsylvanians to support stream-side and urban tree
plantings, and for the establishment of the Clean Streams Fund,
which promotes healthier waterways and communities by helping
farmers implement conservation projects through the Agricultural
Conservation Assistance Program; and
WHEREAS, The Chesapeake Bay Foundation has partnered with

WHEREAS, Since 1997, the Chesapeake Bay Foundation has helped
secure and direct more than $40 million in conservation funding
in Pennsylvania, working with more than 5,000 landowners to
implement on-farm and stream-side conservation practices that
improve water quality, enhance wildlife habitat and support
sustainable land stewardship; and
WHEREAS, Since 2009, Chesapeake Bay Foundation Pennsylvania
staff, students, partner organizations, families and individual
volunteers collectively installed more than 1,200 conservation
projects across Pennsylvania, including the establishment of
more than 970 acres of forested riparian buffers that protect
and restore Pennsylvania's waterways; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania office of the Chesapeake Bay
Foundation has demonstrated leadership in watershed restoration
through the Keystone 10 Million Trees Partnership, mobilizing
more than 300 partners and countless volunteers to successfully
plant 10 million new native trees in eight years, advancing the
restoration of Pennsylvania's waterways through the expansion of
riparian forest buffers and public engagement in conservation
efforts; and
WHEREAS, The Chesapeake Bay Foundation has worked
collaboratively with the public and private sectors and
residents to develop innovative and effective solutions to
address the complex challenges posed by storm water runoff and
its impacts on water quality and community health; and

WHEREAS, The Chesapeake Bay Foundation has won two of the
nation's highest environmental honors, the President's
Environmental and Conservation Challenge Award in 1992 and the
National Geographic Society's Chairman Award in 1993; and
WHEREAS, Since establishing the Harrisburg office in 1986,
the Chesapeake Bay Foundation's Pennsylvania office has advanced
clean water restoration through forested stream buffers, tree
planting, pollution reduction and community stewardship; and
WHEREAS, This work has earned Statewide and national
recognition, including the 2011 national PISCES Award for
completing 209 pollution-reduction projects on 43 farms within
the Susquehanna River Basin, the Pennsylvania Resources
Council's 2015 Leadership in Conservation Award, the Alliance
for the Chesapeake Bay's 2015 Forest Champion Award, the 2016
Pennsylvania Governor's Award for Environmental Excellence, the
Arbor Day Foundation's 2016 Good Steward Award and, most
recently, the Foxwynd Foundation's 2025 Visionary Leadership
Award, which was presented to the Chesapeake Bay Foundation's
Keystone 10 Million Trees Partnership; and
WHEREAS, For 40 years, the Pennsylvania office of the
Chesapeake Bay Foundation has been a leading force in the effort
to restore and protect the Chesapeake Bay, dedicating itself to
safeguarding the Susquehanna River watershed and preserving the
Chesapeake Bay as a national treasure and one of the Mid-
Atlantic region's most valuable natural resources; therefore be
it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania designate November 10, 2026, as
"Chesapeake Bay Foundation Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it
