§ 2803. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter

(2) This Commonwealth's retail choice program has

(3) The retail choice program has not worked equally

(4) Aggregation of small customer purchasing power can

(5) Democratically elected local governments should be

(b) Formation and powers.--
(1) A municipal corporation may establish a program for
residents and customers within the municipal corporation's
jurisdiction and act as a sponsor. The sponsor shall report
the formation of the program to the commission and provide to
the commission:
(i) The name of the jurisdiction being served.
(ii) The address of the municipal corporation's
principal office.
(iii) The address of the administrator, if different
than the sponsor.
(2) If municipal corporations within the same county
establish a program by agreement, any of the municipal
corporations may withdraw from the program in accordance with
the terms of the agreement. The program may also serve any
unincorporated areas in the county.
