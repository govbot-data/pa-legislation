AN ACT
Amending Title 53 (Municipalities Generally) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in general provisions relating to
government and administration, providing for pre-application
procedure for a proposed data center.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 53 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1144. Pre-application procedure for a proposed data center.
(a) Requirements.--After a data center development proposal
has been received by the municipality, the data center shall:
(1) Submit a request to meet with the host municipality
30 days prior to the submission of a data center development
application to the host municipal planning board. The meeting
shall include the following:
(i) Officials responsible for:
(A) zoning; and
(B) planning.
(ii) Elected officials.

(iii) Solicitors employed by the municipality.
(iv) Engineers employed by the municipality.
(2) Share all will-serve letters from water, sewer,
electric and fiber infrastructure organizations showing
available resources dedicated to this project. Each letter
shall show:
(i) available infrastructure of the data center
facility; and
(ii) any impacts on water, sewer, electric and fiber
infrastructure of local residents.
(b) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Data center." A facility or group of facilities that is
used to house and operate equipment that:
(1) receives, stores, aggregates, manages, processes,
transforms, retrieves, researches or transmits data; or
(2) is necessary for the proper operation of equipment
that receives, stores, aggregates, manages, processes,
transforms, retrieves, researches or transmits data.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
