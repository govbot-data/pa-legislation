2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1455 PN 1926 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1926 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1455 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BROWN, ARGALL, VOGEL, CULVER AND FLYNN, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO LOCAL GOVERNMENT, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 53 (Municipalities Generally) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in general provisions relating to government and administration, providing for pre-application procedure for a proposed data center.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 53 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1144. Pre-application procedure for a proposed data center.(a) Requirements.--After a data center development proposal has been received by the municipality, the data center shall:(1) Submit a request to meet with the host municipality 30 days prior to the submission of a data center development application to the host municipal planning board. The meeting shall include the following:(i) Officials responsible for:(A) zoning; and(B) planning.(ii) Elected officials.12345678910111213141516171819 (iii) Solicitors employed by the municipality.(iv) Engineers employed by the municipality.(2) Share all will-serve letters from water, sewer, electric and fiber infrastructure organizations showing available resources dedicated to this project. Each letter shall show:(i) available infrastructure of the data center facility; and(ii) any impacts on water, sewer, electric and fiber infrastructure of local residents.(b) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Data center." A facility or group of facilities that is used to house and operate equipment that:(1) receives, stores, aggregates, manages, processes, transforms, retrieves, researches or transmits data; or(2) is necessary for the proper operation of equipment that receives, stores, aggregates, manages, processes, transforms, retrieves, researches or transmits data.Section 2.
