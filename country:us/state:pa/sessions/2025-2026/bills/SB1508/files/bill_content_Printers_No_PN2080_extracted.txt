Title 75 is amended by adding a section to read: § 6123.2. Emergency LED warning devices.(a) Use authorized.--(1) Subject to the requirements under subsections (b)(1) and (d) and this chapter, emergency LED warning devices may be used by authorized personnel performing work on highways as supplemental warning lights to warn drivers of upcoming work upon the highway or utility construction or maintenance areas.(2) Subject to the requirements under subsections (b)(2) and (d) and this chapter, emergency LED warning devices may be used by emergency service responders performing incident management upon highways in emergency response areas as defined under section 3327(f) (relating to duty of driver in emergency response areas and in relation to disabled vehicles).(b) Compliance with other traffic laws.--Emergency LED warning devices shall comply, as applicable, with the requirements of:(1) Section 3326 (relating to the duty of driver in construction and maintenance areas or on highway safety corridors).20260SB1508PN2080 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (2) Section 3327.(c) Approaching emergency LED warning device.--When approaching authorized personnel or emergency service responders using an emergency LED warning device in accordance with this section, a driver of a vehicle shall yield the right-of-way subject to the requirements under section 3326(a) or 3327(a), as applicable, unless otherwise directed by the authorized personnel or emergency service responders.(d) Lighting requirements.--Emergency LED warning devices shall:(1) Have a rechargeable battery.(2) Have a 360-degree revolving light burn.(3) Be placed at a minimum height of 30 inches above the surface of the roadway , except that the light may be less than 30 inches above the surface of the roadway if used for incident management by an emergency service responder as provided under subsection (a)(2) and section 3327.
