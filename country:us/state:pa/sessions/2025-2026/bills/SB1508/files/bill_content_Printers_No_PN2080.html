AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in preliminary provisions, further providing for
definitions; in rules of the road in general, further
providing for duty of driver in construction and maintenance
areas or on highway safety corridors and for duty of driver
in emergency response areas and in relation to disabled
vehicles; and, in powers of department and local authorities,
providing for emergency LED warning devices.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The definition of "official traffic-control
device" in section 102 of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended and the section is amended by
adding a definition to read:

used in this title shall have, unless the context clearly

"Emergency LED warning device." A warning light or light
stick used by authorized personnel and emergency service
responders in accordance with section 6123.2 (relating to
emergency LED warning devices) for construction, maintenance,
utility or incident management.
* * *
"Official traffic-control [devices." Signs, signals,
markings and devices] device." A sign, signal, marking or
device not inconsistent with this title placed or erected by
authority of a public body or official having jurisdiction, for
the purpose of regulating, warning or guiding traffic. The term
includes an emergency LED warning device.

Section 3. Title 75 is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 6123.2. Emergency LED warning devices.
(a) Use authorized.--
(1) Subject to the requirements under subsections (b)(1)
and (d) and this chapter, emergency LED warning devices may
be used by authorized personnel performing work on highways
as supplemental warning lights to warn drivers of upcoming
work upon the highway or utility construction or maintenance
areas.
(2) Subject to the requirements under subsections (b)(2)
and (d) and this chapter, emergency LED warning devices may
be used by emergency service responders performing incident
management upon highways in emergency response areas as
defined under section 3327(f) (relating to duty of driver in
emergency response areas and in relation to disabled
vehicles).
(b) Compliance with other traffic laws.--Emergency LED
warning devices shall comply, as applicable, with the
requirements of:
(1) Section 3326 (relating to the duty of driver in
construction and maintenance areas or on highway safety
corridors).

(2) Section 3327.
(c) Approaching emergency LED warning device.--When
approaching authorized personnel or emergency service responders
using an emergency LED warning device in accordance with this
section, a driver of a vehicle shall yield the right-of-way
subject to the requirements under section 3326(a) or 3327(a), as
applicable, unless otherwise directed by the authorized
personnel or emergency service responders.
(d) Lighting requirements.--Emergency LED warning devices
shall:
(1) Have a rechargeable battery.
(2) Have a 360-degree revolving light burn.
(3) Be placed at a minimum height of 30 inches above the
surface of the roadway , except that the light may be less
than 30 inches above the surface of the roadway if used for
incident management by an emergency service responder as
provided under subsection (a)(2) and section 3327. The light
under this paragraph may be attached to or detached from a
tripod stand.
(4) Be visible at least a distance of 2,000 feet during
a sunny day when the sun is not directly on or behind the
device and during a clear night.
(5) Be blue in color.
(e) Penalty.--
(1) A driver who violates subsection (c) commits a
summary offense and shall, upon conviction, be subject to a
fine as provided under section 3327(b).

(1), the driver shall pay the cost to replace any emergency
