AN ACT
Amending Title 20 (Decedents, Estates and Fiduciaries) of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in ownership of property
and legal title and equitable estate, further providing for
right to dispose of a decedent's remains; in dispositions
independent of letters and family exemption and probate of
wills and grant of letters, repealing provisions relating to
payments to family and funeral directors, providing for
collection of personal property by affidavit and for
collection of unclaimed property and further providing for
settlement of small estates on petition; in accounts and
distribution, further providing for income on distributive
shares; in health care, further providing for authority of
health care agent; in powers of attorney, providing for
judicial relief; in estates, further providing for
applicability of rule against perpetuities; and, in trusts,
further providing for income on distributive shares.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
