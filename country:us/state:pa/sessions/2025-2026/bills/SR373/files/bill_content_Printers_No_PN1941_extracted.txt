2025-2026 Regular Session SR 373 PN 1941 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1941 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.373 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY SANTARSIERO, HUGHES, PICOZZI, KANE, STREET, CAPPELLETTI AND COSTA, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the month of September 2026 as "Life Insurance Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Life insurance is an important component of financial planning that can provide individuals and families with financial security and stability following the death of a loved one; andWHEREAS, Life insurance benefits can help families replace lost income, pay household expenses and outstanding debts, support educational needs and protect long-term financial goals; andWHEREAS, Approximately 6 million Pennsylvania residents have life insurance coverage, with an average benefit of approximately $148,000; andWHEREAS, In 2024, approximately $11.7 billion in life insurance benefits were paid to beneficiaries in Pennsylvania, demonstrating the significant role life insurance plays in providing financial support to families throughout this Commonwealth; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, Despite the benefits of life insurance, many individuals and families remain uninsured or underinsured, potentially leaving their loved ones vulnerable to financial hardship following an unexpected loss; andWHEREAS, Approximately 102 million Americans have no life insurance or do not have enough life insurance to meet their financial needs; andWHEREAS, The financial impact of an unexpected loss can be particularly difficult for households with limited savings or other financial resources and for families responsible for children or other dependents; andWHEREAS, Reviewing life insurance coverage as financial circumstances, family responsibilities, debts and long-term goals change can help individuals and families determine whether they have adequate financial protection; andWHEREAS, September provides an opportunity to raise awareness of the importance of life insurance and encourage Pennsylvanians to consider the financial protection needs of themselves and their families; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate designate the month of September 2026 as "Life Insurance Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate encourage residents of this Commonwealth to learn more about life insurance, review their existing financial protection and take appropriate steps to promote the long-term financial security of themselves and their families.20260SR0373PN1941 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627
