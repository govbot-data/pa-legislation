A RESOLUTION
Designating the month of September 2026 as "Life Insurance
Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Life insurance is an important component of
financial planning that can provide individuals and families
with financial security and stability following the death of a
loved one; and
WHEREAS, Life insurance benefits can help families replace
lost income, pay household expenses and outstanding debts,
support educational needs and protect long-term financial goals;
and
WHEREAS, Approximately 6 million Pennsylvania residents have
life insurance coverage, with an average benefit of
approximately $148,000; and
WHEREAS, In 2024, approximately $11.7 billion in life
insurance benefits were paid to beneficiaries in Pennsylvania,
demonstrating the significant role life insurance plays in
providing financial support to families throughout this
Commonwealth; and

WHEREAS, Despite the benefits of life insurance, many
individuals and families remain uninsured or underinsured,
potentially leaving their loved ones vulnerable to financial
hardship following an unexpected loss; and
WHEREAS, Approximately 102 million Americans have no life
insurance or do not have enough life insurance to meet their
financial needs; and
WHEREAS, The financial impact of an unexpected loss can be
particularly difficult for households with limited savings or
other financial resources and for families responsible for
children or other dependents; and
WHEREAS, Reviewing life insurance coverage as financial
circumstances, family responsibilities, debts and long-term
goals change can help individuals and families determine whether
they have adequate financial protection; and
WHEREAS, September provides an opportunity to raise awareness
of the importance of life insurance and encourage Pennsylvanians
to consider the financial protection needs of themselves and
their families; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate designate the month of September
2026 as "Life Insurance Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania; and be
it further
RESOLVED, That the Senate encourage residents of this
Commonwealth to learn more about life insurance, review their
existing financial protection and take appropriate steps to
promote the long-term financial security of themselves and their
families.
