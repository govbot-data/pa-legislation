AN ACT
Amending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in transportation network service,
further providing for transportation network company drivers.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 2605(b) of Title 66 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a paragraph to read:
§ 2605. Transportation network company drivers.
* * *
(b) Requirements for transportation network company
drivers.--A transportation network company driver must:
* * *
(10) If a transportation network company driver conducts
audio or visual recording during a ride, display a sign
notifying any passengers of the audio or visual recording.
The sign must be:
(i) displayed on the center console of the vehicle
or on the back of the front passenger seat and be
