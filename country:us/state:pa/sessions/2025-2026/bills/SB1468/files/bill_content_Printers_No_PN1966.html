AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," providing for growing, retaining and
elevating accomplished teachers; imposing duties on the
Department of Education; and making a transfer.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known
as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding an
article to read:
ARTICLE XV-O
GROWING, RETAINING AND ELEVATING ACCOMPLISHED TEACHERS
Section 1501-O. Short title of article.
This article shall be known and may be cited as the Growing,
Retaining and Elevating Accomplished Teachers Law.

Section 1503-O. Program establishment and department duties.
(a) Program.--The National Board Certified Teacher High-Need
District Pilot Grant Program is established within the
department to provide grants in accordance with this article.
(b) Goal.--The goal of the program is to increase the number
of NBCT teachers employed in high-need schools.
(c) Duties of department.--The department shall:
(1) Identify high-need schools in this Commonwealth.
(2) Identify the number of NBCT teachers currently
teaching in this Commonwealth disaggregated by school entity.
(3) Identify the retention rate of teachers in high-need
schools.
(4) Award grants in accordance with this article and in
a manner to ensure geographic diversity across this
Commonwealth.
(d) Priority.--The department shall give priority to an
application from an eligible applicant that:
(1) Demonstrates existing local supports for NBCT
teachers.
(2) Provides matching funds to supplement a grant from
the program.
(3) Provides a plan to maintain candidate support beyond
the grant period.
(4) Represents a consortium of small or rural districts.

(a) Application.--The department shall develop an
application for an eligible applicant to apply for the program.

Section 1505-O. Eligibility.
(a) Eligibility.--
(1) To be eligible for a grant under the program, the
school entity must be identified as a high-need school.
(2) To maintain eligibility in the program, the school
entity must increase the number of NBCT teachers in each year
of the program.
(b) Use of consortia.--Two or more eligible applicants may
apply jointly and shall designate a fiscal agent for purposes of
the grant.
Section 1506-O. Allowable uses of funds.
(a) Term.--The term of a grant shall be for five years.
(b) Required uses.--A grant shall be used for:
(1) Payment of fees associated with obtaining initial
certification of an NBCT candidate.
(2) Development and operation of candidate support
programs, which may include:
(i) Stipends for NBCT candidate mentors or
professional learning facilitators.
(ii) Program coordination and administrative costs.
(iii) Candidate support materials and meeting costs.
(c) Permissive uses.--If grant money remains following use
under subsection (b), grant funds may be used for:
(1) Payment of NBCT teacher maintenance of certification
fees.
(2) Stipends or salary supplements for NBCT teachers who
remain employed in a high-need school.

Section 1507-O. Reporting.
(a) Annual report.--Within 180 days of the conclusion of the
program, the department shall submit an evaluation and
recommendations regarding continuation, expansion or
modification to the program by considering the data collected
under subsection (b).
(b) Data collection.--The department shall collect the
following from school entities that participate in the program:
(1) The number of NBCT candidates supported by grants in
each year of the program.
(2) Certification completion rates of NBCT candidates.
(3) Data on student achievement in the school entities
where a grant was given.
(4) Data on teacher retention and vacancy rates in the
school entities where a grant was given.
