2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1470 PN 1943 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1943 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1470 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PENNYCUICK, VOGEL AND CULVER, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO ENVIRONMENTAL RESOURCES AND ENERGY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 27 (Environmental Resources) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in water resources planning, providing for water conservation requirements for data centers.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 27 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 3118.1. Water conservation requirements for data centers.(a) Requirement.--A data center shall utilize one or more of the following water conservation methods for cooling or other operational water needs:(1) a closed-loop cooling system;(2) reclaimed water;(3) recycled water; or(4) another water conservation technology or practice approved by the department based on best available market technology.(b) Department approval.--123456789101112131415161718 (1) The owner of a data center shall submit to the department a plan that establishes compliance with subsection (a). The department shall determine whether a plan satisfies the requirement under subsection (a).(2) The department shall have 30 days from the day of receipt of the plan to provide a determination. The department may extend the 30-day period once by 15 days.(c) Alternative compliance.--If the department determines that the use of a water conservation method under subsection (a)(1), (2) or (3) is not feasible, the data center shall submit an alternative compliance plan to the department and shall pay a large water user mitigation charge to the applicable water service provider. The alternative compliance plan shall include:(1) an explanation of why compliance with subsection (a)(1), (2) or (3) is not feasible;(2) projected daily, monthly and annual water withdrawals, purchases, discharges and consumptive use;(3) proposed water conservation measures;(4) proposed mitigation measures;(5) an analysis of impacts on the applicable water service provider and existing ratepayers or residential well systems; and(6) the proposed payment of a large water user mitigation charge under subsection (d).(d) Large water user mitigation charge.--The charge under subsection (c) shall be sufficient to recover all costs attributable to serving the data center, including water supply, treatment, distribution, wastewater, infrastructure construction and maintenance costs. A facility or group of facilities, including an ancillary use or associated structure located on the same tract or parcel of land, that:(1) is predominantly used to house working servers or similar data storage systems;(2) has an uninterruptible energy supply or generator backup power, or both, cooling systems, towers and other temperature control infrastructure; and(3) consumes or is projected to consume or otherwise use more than 100,000 gallons of water per year."Reclaimed water." Treated wastewater used in accordance with applicable guidelines for a beneficial purpose as a substitute for water withdrawn from a surface or groundwater source."Recycled water." Treated wastewater obtained from an application or process that is intended to be used again in the same application or process."Water service provider." A public water system, municipal authority, municipality, public utility, water company or other entity that supplies water or wastewater service to a data center.Section 2.
