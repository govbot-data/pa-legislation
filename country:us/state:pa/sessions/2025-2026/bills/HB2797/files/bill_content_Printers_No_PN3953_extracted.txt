Minimum reimbursement for 340B drugs.(a) Minimum reimbursement.--A pharmacy benefits manager, covered insurer or agent acting on behalf of a pharmacy benefits 20260HB2797PN3953 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 manager or covered insurer that reimburses a covered entity or contract pharmacy for a drug under a contract may not reimburse the covered entity or contract pharmacy in an amount less than:(1) The national average drug acquisition cost determined based on surveys conducted under 42 U.S.C. § 1396r-8(f) (relating to payment for covered outpatient drugs) for a 340B drug for which a national average drug acquisition cost is available.(2) The wholesale acquisition cost as defined in 42 U.S.C. § 1395w-3a(c)(6)(B) (relating to use of average sales price payment methodology) for a 340B drug for which a national average drug acquisition cost is not available.(b) Waiver.--A covered entity may, in the sole discretion of the covered entity, voluntarily waive the protections under subsection (a). Reimbursement claims methodology.The department, a covered insurer, a pharmacy benefits manager or another entity that administers or manages prescription drug benefits on behalf of the department or a Medicaid managed care organization shall, with respect to payment for drugs under Medicaid, rely exclusively on a claims-level methodology that uses a nationally recognized billing modifier to identify drugs purchased under 42 U.S.C. § 256b (relating to limitation on prices of drugs purchased by covered entities) to ensure that utilization data for the drugs are excluded from the information reported under 42 U.S.C. § 1396b(m)(2)(A)(xiii) (relating to payment to States) and from Medicaid drug rebate requests.Section 5. Safety net prescription drug distribution and access protections.(a) Distribution and access.--A manufacturer, an agent or affiliate of a manufacturer or a third-party logistics provider may not, directly or indirectly, take any of the following actions unless the acquisition or receipt of the 340B drug is prohibited by Federal law:(1) Deny, restrict, prohibit, delay or otherwise interfere with the acquisition of a 340B drug by, or delivery of a 340B drug to, a covered entity or contract pharmacy.(2) Place a condition on the acquisition or receipt of a 340B drug by a covered entity or contract pharmacy.(b) Claims and utilization data.--A manufacturer or an agent or affiliate of a manufacturer may not, directly or indirectly, require a covered entity to submit claims or utilization data as a condition for allowing the acquisition of a 340B drug by, or delivery of a 340B drug to, the covered entity unless submission of the claims or utilization data is required by Federal law.20260HB2797PN3953 - 7 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 Section 7. Enforcement, penalties and remedies.(a) Insurance Department.--The Insurance Department shall investigate complaints alleging violations of sections 3 and 5(a) and enforce those provisions. In administering this subsection, the Insurance Department may:(1) Examine or audit the books and records of a pharmacy benefits manager, covered insurer or related party to determine compliance with this act.(2) Issue subpoenas as necessary to carry out enforcement and oversight under this act.(b) Section 6 enforcement.--(1) A violation of section 6 shall constitute an unlawful practice within the meaning of the act of December 17, 1968 (P.L.1224, No.387), known as the Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law. The Office of Attorney General shall have jurisdiction, consistent with the Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law, to enforce section 6 and may promulgate regulations to implement section 6.(2) The department may promulgate regulations consistent with section 6. The department may investigate a complaint alleging a violation of section 6 by an individual or entity licensed by or registered with the department, impose discipline on the individual or entity, suspend or revoke the license or registration of the individual or entity and issue subpoenas as necessary to administer enforcement and oversight under this act.(c) Civil penalties.--(1) Upon written notice, the Insurance Department shall impose a civil penalty on a pharmacy benefits manager or 20260HB2797PN3953 - 8 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 covered insurer as follows:(i) For a violation of section 3 or section 5(a), other than section 5(a)(2), $1,000 for each wrongfully denied claim or improperly paid claim.(ii) For a violation of section 5(a)(2), $1,000 per day for each contract pharmacy excluded as a result of a limitation under section 5(a)(2).(2) Upon written notice, the Office of Attorney General shall impose a civil penalty of $50,000 for each violation of section 6 by a manufacturer or an agent or affiliate of a manufacturer. The aggregate amount of civil penalties imposed under this paragraph may not exceed $10,000,000 per year.
