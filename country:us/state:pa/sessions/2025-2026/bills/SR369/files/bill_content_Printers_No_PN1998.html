A RESOLUTION
Directing the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to
conduct a study on the future viability of data center
technology and facilities.
WHEREAS, Data centers are facilities that contain large
amounts of computer servers, storage and networking equipment
that store, process and distribute digital information; and
WHEREAS, Data centers use copious amounts of electricity as
well as large quantities of water for cooling; and
WHEREAS, The question of whether or not data centers will be
needed at the size or level of current proposed development has
been raised; and
WHEREAS, In order to protect land and unnecessary
infrastructure and utility utilization, a thorough review into
the current data storage technology advancements is warranted;
and
WHEREAS, As Pennsylvania data center development proposals
grow, these questions should be explored to determine smart and
responsible long-term decisions and necessity; and

WHEREAS, It is essential that the Commonwealth evaluate the
consequences that these data centers will have on local
communities and this Commonwealth; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate direct the Legislative Budget and
Finance Committee to conduct a study on current data storage
technology advancements and whether data centers will be needed
at the size or level of current proposed development; and be it
further
RESOLVED, That the study include:
(1) demand forecasting;
(2) technology trends;
(3) economic development;
(4) impact on energy and utilities, including land usage
and environmental impact;
(5) risk of over-development, technology becoming
obsolete and market consideration; and
(6) review of what other states are doing and how they
are dealing with data center growth;
and be it further
RESOLVED, That, during the study, the Legislative Budget and
Finance Committee consult with:
(1) the Department of Conservation and Natural
Resources;
(2) the Department of Environmental Protection;
(3) the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission;
(4) additional State agencies as determined to be
necessary by the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee;
and
(5) grid operators for energy demand forecasting and
grid capacity;
