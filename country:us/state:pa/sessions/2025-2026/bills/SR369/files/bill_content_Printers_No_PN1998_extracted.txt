2025-2026 Regular Session SR 369 PN 1998 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1998 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.369 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BROWN, FLYNN, VOGEL AND COMITTA, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMUNICATIONS AND TECHNOLOGY, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDirecting the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to conduct a study on the future viability of data center technology and facilities.WHEREAS, Data centers are facilities that contain large amounts of computer servers, storage and networking equipment that store, process and distribute digital information; andWHEREAS, Data centers use copious amounts of electricity as well as large quantities of water for cooling; andWHEREAS, The question of whether or not data centers will be needed at the size or level of current proposed development has been raised; andWHEREAS, In order to protect land and unnecessary infrastructure and utility utilization, a thorough review into the current data storage technology advancements is warranted; andWHEREAS, As Pennsylvania data center development proposals grow, these questions should be explored to determine smart and responsible long-term decisions and necessity; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, It is essential that the Commonwealth evaluate the consequences that these data centers will have on local communities and this Commonwealth; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate direct the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to conduct a study on current data storage technology advancements and whether data centers will be needed at the size or level of current proposed development; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the study include:(1) demand forecasting;(2) technology trends;(3) economic development;(4) impact on energy and utilities, including land usage and environmental impact;(5) risk of over-development, technology becoming obsolete and market consideration; and(6) review of what other states are doing and how they are dealing with data center growth;and be it furtherRESOLVED, That, during the study, the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee consult with:(1) the Department of Conservation and Natural Resources;(2) the Department of Environmental Protection;(3) the Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission;(4) additional State agencies as determined to be necessary by the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee;
