A RESOLUTION
Honoring the lives and expressing condolences for the losses of
Chief Jeffory L. Buck and Assistant Chief Robert R. Shick,
Jr.
WHEREAS, Chief Jeffory L. Buck and Assistant Chief Robert R.
Shick, Jr., were residents of Maidencreek Township, Berks
County, and members of the Walnuttown Fire Company in Richmond
Township, Berks County; and
WHEREAS, Chief Buck was born November 25, 1965, in West
Reading to Richard H. Buck and the late Sandra K. Schaeffer
Buck; and
WHEREAS, After graduating from Fleetwood Area High School,
Chief Buck worked at Boyertown Foundry; and
WHEREAS, Chief Buck would later join the East Penn
Manufacturing Company, where he was known for his strong work
ethic during his 35-year career as an assembly leadman; and
WHEREAS, Chief Buck was a member of the Walnuttown Fire
Company for 43 years, 20 of which he served as Deputy Chief; and

WHEREAS, In 2017, he was named chief of the company; and
WHEREAS, Chief Buck was an avid outdoorsman, a member of the
Alsace Manor Social Quarters and a regular blood donor; and
WHEREAS, Assistant Chief Shick was born August 22, 1969, in
Abington to Doris Mansley and the late Robert R. Shick, Sr.; and
WHEREAS, Assistant Chief Shick was a truck driver for Viwinco
Windows and Doors after having a 29-year career with the East
Penn Manufacturing Company; and
WHEREAS, Assistant Chief Shick originally joined Walnuttown
Fire Company as a teenager and had worked his way up to
Assistant Chief; and
WHEREAS, Assistant Chief Shick was a coach for the Fleetwood
Youth Soccer Club, a member of the Alsace Manor Fire Company
Social Quarters and a race enthusiast; and
WHEREAS, On April 4, 2026, Chief Buck and Assistant Chief
Shick were on duty and searching for a missing person when a
driver who was under the influence crashed into their vehicle;
and
WHEREAS, Chief Buck is survived by his wife, two children,
two grandchildren and his father; and
WHEREAS, Assistant Chief Shick is survived by his wife, 2
children, 8 grandchildren, his mother and stepfather; and
WHEREAS, These tragic and senseless losses are felt by their
fellow members of the Walnuttown Fire Company, their community
of Richmond Township, Berks County, and the surrounding area,
and their families and friends; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives honor the lives
and express condolences for the losses of Chief Jeffory L. Buck
and Assistant Chief Robert R. Shick, Jr.; and be it further
RESOLVED, That copies of this resolution be given to the
