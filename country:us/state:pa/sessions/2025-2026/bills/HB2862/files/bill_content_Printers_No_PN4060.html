Statutes, in fantasy contests, further providing for general
and specific powers of board; in interactive gaming, further
providing for regulatory authority; in sports wagering,
further providing for regulatory authority; and, in
administration and enforcement, further providing for
prohibited acts and penalties.

hereby enacts as follows:

Section 3. Section 1518(c) of Title 4 is amended by adding
paragraphs to read:
§ 1518. Prohibited acts; penalties.
* * *
(c) Board-imposed administrative sanctions.--
* * *
(4) In addition to any other fine or penalty under this
part or regulation promulgated under this part, if a licensed
operator violates section 311(b)(13) (relating to general and
specific powers of board), the board shall impose an
administrative penalty of no more than $100,000 and may
suspend or revoke the fantasy contest license of the licensed
operator.
(5) In addition to any other fine or penalty under this
part or regulation promulgated under this part, if an
interactive gaming certificate holder violates section
13B02(a)(15)(x) (relating to regulatory authority), the board
shall impose an administrative penalty of no more than
$100,000 and may suspend or revoke the license of the
interactive gaming certificate holder.
(6) In addition to any other fine or penalty under this
part or regulation promulgated under this part, if a sports
wagering certificate holder violates section 13C02(8)(viii)
(relating to regulatory authority), the board shall impose an
administrative penalty of no more than $100,000 and may
suspend or revoke the license of the sports wagering
certificate holder.
* * *
Section 4. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
