AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in special vehicles and pedestrians, further
providing for riding on roadways and pedalcycle paths.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3505(a) of Title 75 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended and the section is amended by
adding a subsection to read:
§ 3505. Riding on roadways and pedalcycle paths.
(a) General rule.--Except as provided in subsections (b)
[and], (c) and (c.1), every person operating a pedalcycle upon a
highway shall obey the applicable rules of the road as contained
in this title.
* * *

highway with a traffic-control signal or stop sign may abide by
the following:
(1) For an intersection controlled by a traffic-control

(i) Approaching an intersection that is controlled
by a traffic-control signal may proceed with caution
through the intersection on a steady red light only if
the pedalcycler:
(A) makes a complete stop at the steady red
traffic-control light before entering the
intersection; and
(B) yields the right-of-way to all pedestrians
within any marked or unmarked crosswalk and oncoming
traffic that constitutes an immediate hazard during
the time that the pedalcycler is moving across or
within the intersection.
(ii) May make a:
(A) right turn at a steady red traffic-control
light without stopping after slowing to a reasonable
speed and yielding the right-of-way to all
pedestrians within any marked or unmarked crosswalk
and oncoming traffic that constitutes an immediate
hazard; or
(B) left turn onto a one-way roadway at a steady
red traffic-control light after stopping and yielding
the right-of-way to all pedestrians within any marked
or unmarked crosswalk and oncoming traffic that
constitutes an immediate hazard.

and may cautiously make a right or left turn, or proceed
through the intersection without stopping at the stop sign,

pedalcycler must stop at the stop sign before entering
