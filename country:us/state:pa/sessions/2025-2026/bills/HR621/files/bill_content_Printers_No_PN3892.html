A RESOLUTION
Urging the Congress of the United States and the United States
Secretary of Defense to strengthen domestic content
requirements for defense systems.
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania's union workforce plays a critical role
in producing the aircraft, vehicles, ships, ammunition and other
defense systems that support the United States military; and
WHEREAS, Thousands of skilled union workers across
Pennsylvania contribute to our national defense, from
International Association of Machinists and Aerospace Workers
(IAM) members manufacturing components for advanced military
aircraft in Johnstown, to members of the Philadelphia Metal
Trades Council supporting United States Navy submarine
production and military shipbuilding in Philadelphia, to United
Auto Workers (UAW) members producing military vehicles in the
Lehigh Valley, to United Steelworkers (USW) members building
military vehicles in York, to IAM members manufacturing

WHEREAS, Current Federal law allows defense systems to be
labeled American-made with as little as 55% domestic content and
permits parts from 28 foreign nations to count as domestic; and
WHEREAS, Foreign-dependent defense supply chains create a
national security vulnerability while hurting Pennsylvania
workers; and
WHEREAS, Closing these loopholes in Federal law would protect
Pennsylvania jobs, strengthen national security and restore the
true meaning of "Made in America"; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania urge the Congress of the United
States and the United States Secretary of Defense to strengthen
domestic content requirements for defense systems by raising the
domestic content threshold for all defense systems to 75% by
2027, eliminating contractor exemption loopholes, restricting
foreign part waivers on all military platforms and defining
"USA-Made Defense System" clearly in Federal procurement law;
and be it further
RESOLVED, That copies of this resolution be transmitted to
the presiding officers of each house of the Congress of the
United States, to each member of the Congress of the United
States from Pennsylvania and to the United States Secretary of
Defense.
