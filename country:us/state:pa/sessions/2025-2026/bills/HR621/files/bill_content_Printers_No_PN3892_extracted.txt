WILLIAMS, PROBST, PASHINSKI, BOROWSKI, TIBURCIO AND MAYES, SEPTEMBER 21, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TOURISM, RECREATION AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT, SEPTEMBER 21, 2026 A RESOLUTIONUrging the Congress of the United States and the United States Secretary of Defense to strengthen domestic content requirements for defense systems.WHEREAS, Pennsylvania's union workforce plays a critical role in producing the aircraft, vehicles, ships, ammunition and other defense systems that support the United States military; andWHEREAS, Thousands of skilled union workers across Pennsylvania contribute to our national defense, from International Association of Machinists and Aerospace Workers (IAM) members manufacturing components for advanced military aircraft in Johnstown, to members of the Philadelphia Metal Trades Council supporting United States Navy submarine production and military shipbuilding in Philadelphia, to United Auto Workers (UAW) members producing military vehicles in the Lehigh Valley, to United Steelworkers (USW) members building military vehicles in York, to IAM members manufacturing 12345678910111213141516 artillery ammunition in Scranton, to IAM members supporting defense manufacturing in Erie and UAW members building CH-47 Chinook helicopters in Ridley Park; andWHEREAS, Current Federal law allows defense systems to be labeled American-made with as little as 55% domestic content and permits parts from 28 foreign nations to count as domestic; andWHEREAS, Foreign-dependent defense supply chains create a national security vulnerability while hurting Pennsylvania workers; andWHEREAS, Closing these loopholes in Federal law would protect Pennsylvania jobs, strengthen national security and restore the true meaning of "Made in America"; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania urge the Congress of the United States and the United States Secretary of Defense to strengthen domestic content requirements for defense systems by raising the domestic content threshold for all defense systems to 75% by 2027, eliminating contractor exemption loopholes, restricting foreign part waivers on all military platforms and defining "USA-Made Defense System" clearly in Federal procurement law;
