A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the month of September 2026 as "Chiari Malformation
Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Chiari malformations can occur when part of the
skull does not form properly, causing nerve tissues to become
crowded, pushing part of the cerebellum down and obstructing the
flow of cerebrospinal fluid into the spinal canal; and
WHEREAS, There are currently four recognized, distinct types
of Chiari malformations, each with varying symptoms; and
WHEREAS, Doctors and scientists are also investigating two
additional types of Chiari malformations; and
WHEREAS, Differentiation between each type occurs based on
the presence of developmental abnormalities and the type of
brain tissue that is compressed into the spinal canal; and
WHEREAS, Chiari malformations are typically congenital, but
may develop after birth or as the result of a mass in the brain
or due to a traumatic injury or infection; and
WHEREAS, These malformations may be diagnosed using magnetic
resonance imaging or computed tomography technology; and

WHEREAS, Conditions that are often associated with Chiari
malformation include spina bifida, hydrocephalus, idiopathic
intracranial hypertension and tethered cord syndrome; and
WHEREAS, Syringomyelia and Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome may also
co-occur with Chiari malformations; and
WHEREAS, With syringomyelia, a syrinx, or cavity, forms in
the spinal cord and can expand over time, eventually damaging
the middle of the spinal cord in severe cases; and
WHEREAS, Syringomyelia can develop without a Chiari
malformation, but many syringomyelia cases are related to
congenital Chiari malformations; and
WHEREAS, Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome affects the body's connective
tissue and can include symptoms such as hypermobility and tissue
fragility; and
WHEREAS, On average, symptoms related to co-occurring Chiari
malformations and Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome begin earlier than
symptoms affecting the wider population of individuals who have
a Chiari malformation; and
WHEREAS, Management of both a Chiari malformation and Ehlers-
Danlos Syndrome can be complex due to instability of the head
and neck region, further impacted by the increased risk of
cerebrospinal fluid leaks for these individuals; and
WHEREAS, Among individuals who have a Chiari malformation,
co-occurring Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome may negatively impact their
surgical outcomes, cause unusual symptoms and necessitate
adjusted treatment plans; and
WHEREAS, Early diagnosis of Chiari malformations and access
to treatment can help with symptom management and improve the
individual's quality of life; and
WHEREAS, While there is no cure for Chiari malformations,
