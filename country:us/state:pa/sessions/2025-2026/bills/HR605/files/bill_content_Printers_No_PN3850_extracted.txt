2025-2026 Regular Session HR 605 PN 3850 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3850 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.605 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BRENNAN, RIVERA, VENKAT AND DOUGHERTY, AUGUST 31, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, AUGUST 31, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of September 2026 as "Chiari Malformation Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Chiari malformations can occur when part of the skull does not form properly, causing nerve tissues to become crowded, pushing part of the cerebellum down and obstructing the flow of cerebrospinal fluid into the spinal canal; andWHEREAS, There are currently four recognized, distinct types of Chiari malformations, each with varying symptoms; andWHEREAS, Doctors and scientists are also investigating two additional types of Chiari malformations; andWHEREAS, Differentiation between each type occurs based on the presence of developmental abnormalities and the type of brain tissue that is compressed into the spinal canal; andWHEREAS, Chiari malformations are typically congenital, but may develop after birth or as the result of a mass in the brain or due to a traumatic injury or infection; andWHEREAS, These malformations may be diagnosed using magnetic resonance imaging or computed tomography technology; and123456789101112131415161718 WHEREAS, Conditions that are often associated with Chiari malformation include spina bifida, hydrocephalus, idiopathic intracranial hypertension and tethered cord syndrome; andWHEREAS, Syringomyelia and Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome may also co-occur with Chiari malformations; andWHEREAS, With syringomyelia, a syrinx, or cavity, forms in the spinal cord and can expand over time, eventually damaging the middle of the spinal cord in severe cases; andWHEREAS, Syringomyelia can develop without a Chiari malformation, but many syringomyelia cases are related to congenital Chiari malformations; andWHEREAS, Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome affects the body's connective tissue and can include symptoms such as hypermobility and tissue fragility; andWHEREAS, On average, symptoms related to co-occurring Chiari malformations and Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome begin earlier than symptoms affecting the wider population of individuals who have a Chiari malformation; andWHEREAS, Management of both a Chiari malformation and Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome can be complex due to instability of the head and neck region, further impacted by the increased risk of cerebrospinal fluid leaks for these individuals; andWHEREAS, Among individuals who have a Chiari malformation, co-occurring Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome may negatively impact their surgical outcomes, cause unusual symptoms and necessitate adjusted treatment plans;
