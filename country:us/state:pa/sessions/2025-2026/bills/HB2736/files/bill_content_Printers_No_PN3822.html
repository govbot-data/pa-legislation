Section 1. Section 1301.1 of the act of April 9, 1929
(P.L.343, No.176), known as The Fiscal Code, is amended by
adding definitions to read:
Section 1301.1. Definitions.--As used in this article,
unless the context otherwise requires:
* * *
"Digital asset" shall include any digital representation of
value that has established ownership and is recorded on a
cryptographically secured distributed ledger, distributed ledger
protocol or similar technology.
"Digital asset holder" shall:
(1) Include a person that, for compensation or profit,
engages in the business of:
(i) exchanging digital assets for monetary value;
(ii) exchanging digital assets for other digital assets;
(iii) transferring digital assets to a third party;
(iv) acting as a digital asset custodian; or
(v) participating in financial services relating to digital
asset issuance.
(2) Not include:
(i) a distributed ledger protocol;
(ii) developing, operating or engaging in the business of
developing distributed ledger protocols or self-custodial
software interfaces;
(iii) an immutable and self-custodial software interface;
(iv) developing, operating or engaging in the business of

(v) participating in a liquidity pool or other similar

"Digital asset owner" shall mean the owner of a digital

Section 2. The act is amended by adding sections to read:
Section 1301.10c. Digital Assets.--(a) Digital assets held
by a digital asset holder are presumed abandoned and unclaimed
if the digital asset owner has expressed no interest in the
digital asset for a period of three (3) uninterrupted years,
unless within the preceding three (3) years, the digital asset
owner has:
1. increased or decreased the asset balance or otherwise
transferred assets into or out of the account;
2. received payments or made deposits into the account;
3. communicated in writing, by first class mail or
electronic mail, or accessed the owner's account online;
4. communicated with the digital asset holder or otherwise
indicated an interest in the digital asset; or

unclaimed property pursuant to section 1301.10c within thirty

liquidation. If a digital asset holder has reason to believe it

digital assets cannot be liquidated pursuant to this section.

alternative means to liquidate the digital assets.
