andWHEREAS, Frank and Athena Sarris recognized the importance of a strong public library in building an educated, informed and connected community; andWHEREAS, Countless children and adults participate in programs, activities and educational opportunities at the Frank Sarris Public Library each year; andWHEREAS, The communities of Canonsburg, North Strabane Township and Cecil Township benefit from the numerous and varied year-round learning experiences, programs, resources and services sponsored by the library; andWHEREAS, The Frank Sarris Public Library has become a vital center of education, literacy, culture and community engagement, 123456789101112131415161718 made possible in large part through the generosity of the Sarris family and their enduring commitment to the library's mission;
