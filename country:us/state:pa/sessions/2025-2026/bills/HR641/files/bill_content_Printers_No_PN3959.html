INTRODUCED BY ORTITAY, SEPTEMBER 29, 2026
REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, SEPTEMBER 29, 2026
A RESOLUTION
Designating November 8, 2026, as "Frank and Athena Sarris Day"
in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Frank and Athena Sarris have been steadfast and
devoted supporters of the Frank Sarris Public Library and its
mission to promote literacy, education and lifelong learning;
and
WHEREAS, Frank and Athena Sarris recognized the importance of
a strong public library in building an educated, informed and
connected community; and
WHEREAS, Countless children and adults participate in
programs, activities and educational opportunities at the Frank
Sarris Public Library each year; and
WHEREAS, The communities of Canonsburg, North Strabane
Township and Cecil Township benefit from the numerous and varied
year-round learning experiences, programs, resources and
services sponsored by the library; and
WHEREAS, The Frank Sarris Public Library has become a vital
center of education, literacy, culture and community engagement,

made possible in large part through the generosity of the Sarris
family and their enduring commitment to the library's mission;
and
WHEREAS, The generosity and vision of Frank and Athena Sarris
have provided opportunities for generations of children,
families, students and lifelong learners throughout the region;
and
WHEREAS, Frank and Athena Sarris have demonstrated an
enduring commitment to education, literacy and lifelong
learning, recognizing that access to knowledge strengthens
individuals and communities; and
WHEREAS, Their generosity has created a lasting legacy that
will continue to benefit the residents of the communities served
by the Frank Sarris Public Library for generations to come; and
WHEREAS, The contributions of Frank and Athena Sarris
represent an example of how private philanthropy and community
partnership can strengthen public institutions and expand
opportunities for the people they serve; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives wishes to honor and
commemorate Frank and Athena Sarris for their devotion to
education, literacy, lifelong learning and the betterment of
their community; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate
November 8, 2026, as "Frank and Athena Sarris Day" in
Pennsylvania; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize their
extraordinary generosity, commitment to education and lasting
contribution to the communities served by the Frank Sarris
Public Library.
