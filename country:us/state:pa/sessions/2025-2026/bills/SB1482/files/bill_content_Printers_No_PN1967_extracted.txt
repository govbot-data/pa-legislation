2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1482 PN 1967 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1967 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1482 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PITTMAN, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 REFERRED TO TRANSPORTATION, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 AN ACTDesignating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 19239, carrying State Route 1019, also known as Valley Road, over Two Lick Creek in Green Township, Indiana County, as the Tec 5 John George Memorial Bridge.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Tec 5 John George Memorial Bridge.(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) John George was born December 2, 1912, in Everett Borough, Bedford County.(2) Mr. George enlisted in the United States Army on March 3, 1942, and served until October 2, 1945, when he was honorably discharged, as a Technician 5th Grade in the 45th Signal Company, 45th Division.(3) For his service, Mr. George was awarded a Good Conduct Medal and the European African Middle Eastern Service Medal with six Bronze Stars and one Bronze Arrowhead.(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key 12345678910111213141516171819 19239, carrying State Route 1019, also known as Valley Road, over Two Lick Creek in Green Township, Indiana County, is designated as the Tec 5 John George Memorial Bridge.(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to traffic in both directions on the bridge.Section 2. Effective date.This act shall take effect in 60 days.20260SB1482PN1967 - 2 - 12345678
