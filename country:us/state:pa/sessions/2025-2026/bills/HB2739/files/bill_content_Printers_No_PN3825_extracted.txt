NELSON, BOROWSKI, MALAGARI AND PARKER, AUGUST 17, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON CONSUMER PROTECTION, TECHNOLOGY AND UTILITIES, AUGUST 18, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in rates and distribution systems, further providing for voluntary changes in rates; and, in accounting and budgetary matters, further providing for depreciation accounts and reports.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Sections 1308 and 1703 of Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes are amended by adding subsections to read:§ 1308. Voluntary changes in rates.* * *(d.2) Depreciation procedure.--(1) All rate filings made under this section shall include depreciation expense and accumulated depreciation calculated in accordance with section 1703(a.1) (relating to depreciation accounts; reports). No public utility may propose any depreciation procedure inconsistent with section 1703(a.1).12345678910111213141516171819 (2) The commission may not:(i) substitute an alternative depreciation procedure; or(ii) adjust depreciation expense or accumulated depreciation in a manner that effectively results in the use of a different depreciation procedure.* * *§ 1703. Depreciation accounts; reports.* * *(a.1) Required depreciation procedure.--All depreciation for public utility property for ratemaking purposes shall be determined using the Average Life Group depreciation procedure.* * *(c.1) Review.--The commission's review of depreciation shall be limited to verification that the Average Life Group procedure under subsection (a.1) has been utilized for all plant accounts and a reasonableness determination for all inputs and assumptions.Section 2.
