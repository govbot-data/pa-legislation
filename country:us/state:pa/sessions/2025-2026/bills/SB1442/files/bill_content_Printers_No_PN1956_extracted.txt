2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1442 PN 1956 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1956 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1442 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PENNYCUICK, VOGEL AND CULVER, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 REFERRED TO JUDICIARY, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 AN ACTProhibiting data centers from being developed or operated by certain foreign entities; and providing for enforcement by Attorney General.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Secure Data Center Facilities Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Control." Having the power to direct or cause the direction of the management or policies of a business enterprise, whether through ownership of securities, by contract, or otherwise. A person or an entity that directly or indirectly has the right to vote at least 25% of the voting interests of a business enterprise or that is entitled to at least 25% of the profits of a business enterprise is presumed to control the business 12345678910111213141516171819 enterprise."Data center." A facility or group of facilities, including ancillary uses or associated structures located on the same tract or parcel of land, that:(1) are predominantly used to house working servers or similar data storage systems; and(2) have an uninterruptible energy supply or generator backup power, or both, cooling systems, towers and other temperature control infrastructure."Foreign country of concern." A country designated on the Countries of Particular Concern list or the Special Watch List Countries by the United States Department of State."Foreign entity." A business enterprise that is:(1) owned or controlled by the government of a foreign country of concern; or(2) a partnership, an association, a corporation, an organization or other combination of persons organized under the laws of or having its principal place of business in a foreign country of concern, or a subsidiary of such entity.Section 3. Data center controlled by foreign entity.(a) Prohibition.--A data center controlled by a foreign entity may not be operated or developed in this Commonwealth.(b) Existing data centers.--A data center operated or being developed in this Commonwealth by a foreign entity on the effective date of this subsection shall immediately cease operations or development of the data center.Section 4. Enforcement.The Attorney General may, on behalf of the Commonwealth, bring an action in a court of competent jurisdiction for injunctive relief to prevent a violation of this act.20260SB1442PN1956 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 Section 5.
