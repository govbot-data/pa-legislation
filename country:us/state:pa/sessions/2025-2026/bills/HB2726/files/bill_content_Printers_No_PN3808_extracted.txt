2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2726 PN 3808 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3808 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2726 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BONNER, GAYDOS AND ZIMMERMAN, AUGUST 4, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, AUGUST 4, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 42 (Judiciary and Judicial Procedure) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in general provisions relating to civil actions and proceedings, providing for indemnification.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 42 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 7104. Indemnification.A provision or a term in a contract in which a party shall be indemnified or held harmless from any claims, legal actions, damages, losses or expenses arising out of bodily injury to persons, damage to property or economic loss caused by or resulting from the party's negligence, strict liability or intentional or reckless wrongdoing, in whole or in part, shall be void as against public policy and unenforceable.Section 2. The addition of 42 Pa.C.S. § 7104 shall apply to contracts entered into on or after the effective date of this section.12345678910111213141516171819 Section 3.
