AN ACT
Amending Title 42 (Judiciary and Judicial Procedure) of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in general provisions
relating to civil actions and proceedings, providing for
indemnification.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 42 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 7104. Indemnification.
A provision or a term in a contract in which a party shall be
indemnified or held harmless from any claims, legal actions,
damages, losses or expenses arising out of bodily injury to
persons, damage to property or economic loss caused by or
resulting from the party's negligence, strict liability or
intentional or reckless wrongdoing, in whole or in part, shall
be void as against public policy and unenforceable.
Section 2. The addition of 42 Pa.C.S. § 7104 shall apply to
contracts entered into on or after the effective date of this
section.
