AN ACT
Amending Title 51 (Military Affairs) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in Educational Assistance Program,
further providing for definitions, for certification of
eligibility, for grants and for limitations, providing for
family member loan repayment alternative and further
providing for administration; and, in Military Family
Education Program, further providing for certification of
eligibility and for Military Family Education Program grants,
providing for family member loan repayment alternative and
further providing for administration.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3201 of Title 51 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a definition to read:
§ 3201. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this subchapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
* * *
"Family member." As defined in section 3210.1 (relating to
definitions).

Section 2. Sections 3203, 3204 heading and (a), 3206(b) and
3208 of Title 51 are amended to read:
§ 3203. Certification of eligibility.
The department shall:
(1) certify members of the Pennsylvania National Guard
as eligible to participate in the program who meet the
eligibility criteria set forth in section 3202 (relating to
eligibility) and shall provide the agency with a certificate
of eligibility for an eligible member[.]; or
(2) certify members of the Pennsylvania National Guard
who assign the benefit under this subchapter to a family
member to receive loan repayment under section 3208 (relating
to family member loan repayment alternative) and shall
provide the agency with a certificate of eligibility for an
eligible member's family member.
§ 3204. Grants and loan repayments.
(a) General rule.--From the funds appropriated for the
purposes of this subchapter[, grants]:
(1) Grants awarded under this subchapter shall be
provided to eligible members for the purpose of pursuing
approved programs of education. An educational assistance
grant shall be treated as a benefit earned by an eligible
member for honorable service to the country and this
Commonwealth.
(2) Loan repayments shall be provided under section 3208
(relating to family member loan repayment alternative). Loans
repaid under section 3208 shall be treated as a benefit
earned by the eligible member for honorable service to the
country and this Commonwealth.

§ 3206. Limitations.
* * *
(b) Active duty.--
(1) Except as otherwise provided in paragraph (2) or

§ 3208. [(Reserved).] Family member loan repayment alternative.
(a) Authorization.--A member of the Pennsylvania National
Guard who meets the eligibility criteria under section 3202(a)
(2) and (b)(2) and (4) (relating to eligibility) may assign the
benefit under this subchapter to the eligible member's family
member to receive loan repayment as an alternative to receiving
a grant under this subchapter if the family member of the
eligible member:
(1) is or was a full-time student in an approved program
of education at an approved institution of higher learning;
and
(2) has outstanding loans for the approved program of
education.
(b) Amount.--A loan repaid under this section may not exceed
the annual tuition rate.
(c) Application.--An eligible member may apply, on a form
designated by the agency, the eligible member's family member
for repayment of qualified loans.
(d) Service requirement.--A family member of an eligible
member may receive loan repayment under this section for up to
five academic years, or their equivalent as determined by the
agency, at an approved institution of higher learning if the
eligible member completes one year of service for each year of
benefit received, prior to the payment of the benefit.

Section 4. Sections 3210.3, 3210.4 heading and (a), 3210.8
and 3210.9(a) of Title 51 are amended to read:
§ 3210.3. Certification of eligibility.
The department shall provide the agency with a certificate of
eligibility for each eligible family member that applies for a
Military Family Education Grant[.] and each family member that
applies for family member loan repayment under section 3210.8
(relating to family member loan repayment alternative).
§ 3210.4. Military Family Education Program grants and family
member loan repayments.
(a) General rule.--From the funds appropriated for the
purposes of this subchapter[, Military]:
(1) Military Family Education Program grants shall be
provided to eligible family members for the purpose of
pursuing an approved program of education. A Military Family
Education Program grant shall be treated as a benefit earned
by an eligible member for honorable service to the country
and this Commonwealth.
(2) Loan repayments shall be provided under section
3210.8 (relating to family member loan repayment
alternative). Loans repaid under section 3210.8 shall be
treated as a benefit earned by the eligible member for
honorable service to the country and this Commonwealth.
