Maiale, former Member from the 183rd District of the House of Representatives, Philadelphia County.WHEREAS, The House of Representatives honors the memory and recognizes the legacy of Nicholas J. Maiale, who died July 5, 2025, at 73 years of age; andWHEREAS, A native of South Philadelphia, Mr. Maiale generously led, advised and inspired others throughout his life; andWHEREAS, In Harrisburg, Mr. Maiale enjoyed friendships on both sides of the aisle; andWHEREAS, Mr. Maiale held his legislative seat from 1980 through 1992, serving on the Judiciary, Finance, Insurance, Business and Commerce and Appropriations Committees; andWHEREAS, Mr. Maiale continued his public service as chair of the Pennsylvania State Employees' Retirement System through several administrations; andWHEREAS, Mr. Maiale built and managed a legal practice in 123456789101112131415161718 Philadelphia; andWHEREAS, Mr. Maiale was educated at the Abington and University Park campuses of The Pennsylvania State University, graduating Phi Beta Kappa in 1973; andWHEREAS, In 1976, Mr. Maiale received his law degree from Temple University; andWHEREAS, Mr. Maiale sponsored and joined Penn State Abington veteran students on an international trip in conjunction with the 100th anniversary of World War I; andWHEREAS, The Nick Maiale Student Government and Leadership Room in the Lares Building at Penn State Abington was dedicated in April 2024; andWHEREAS, At the same time, Mr. Maiale accepted the Donor Appreciation Award from Penn State Abington's Office of Development and Alumni Relations; andWHEREAS, The internship fund, Open Doors Scholarship and three separate endowments established by Mr. Maiale carry forward his generosity at Penn State Abington; andWHEREAS, Mr. Maiale was recognized as a Penn State Alumni Fellow and member of the Greater Delaware Valley Chapter of the National Multiple Sclerosis Society Volunteer Hall of Fame; andWHEREAS, Mr. Maiale was a benefactor of Thomas Jefferson University in Philadelphia; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives commends Mr. Maiale's dedicated public service and philanthropy; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives mourn the loss of the Honorable Nicholas J.
