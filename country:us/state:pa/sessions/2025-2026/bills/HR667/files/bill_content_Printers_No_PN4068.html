A RESOLUTION
Mourning the loss of the Honorable Nicholas J. Maiale, former
Member from the 183rd District of the House of
Representatives, Philadelphia County.
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives honors the memory and
recognizes the legacy of Nicholas J. Maiale, who died July 5,
2025, at 73 years of age; and
WHEREAS, A native of South Philadelphia, Mr. Maiale
generously led, advised and inspired others throughout his life;
and
WHEREAS, In Harrisburg, Mr. Maiale enjoyed friendships on
both sides of the aisle; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Maiale held his legislative seat from 1980
through 1992, serving on the Judiciary, Finance, Insurance,
Business and Commerce and Appropriations Committees; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Maiale continued his public service as chair of
the Pennsylvania State Employees' Retirement System through
several administrations; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Maiale built and managed a legal practice in

Philadelphia; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Maiale was educated at the Abington and
University Park campuses of The Pennsylvania State University,
graduating Phi Beta Kappa in 1973; and
WHEREAS, In 1976, Mr. Maiale received his law degree from
Temple University; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Maiale sponsored and joined Penn State Abington
veteran students on an international trip in conjunction with
the 100th anniversary of World War I; and
WHEREAS, The Nick Maiale Student Government and Leadership
Room in the Lares Building at Penn State Abington was dedicated
in April 2024; and
WHEREAS, At the same time, Mr. Maiale accepted the Donor
Appreciation Award from Penn State Abington's Office of
Development and Alumni Relations; and
WHEREAS, The internship fund, Open Doors Scholarship and
three separate endowments established by Mr. Maiale carry
forward his generosity at Penn State Abington; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Maiale was recognized as a Penn State Alumni
Fellow and member of the Greater Delaware Valley Chapter of the
National Multiple Sclerosis Society Volunteer Hall of Fame; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Maiale was a benefactor of Thomas Jefferson
University in Philadelphia; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives commends Mr. Maiale's
dedicated public service and philanthropy; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives mourn the loss of
the Honorable Nicholas J. Maiale, former Member from the 183rd
District of the House of Representatives, Philadelphia County.
