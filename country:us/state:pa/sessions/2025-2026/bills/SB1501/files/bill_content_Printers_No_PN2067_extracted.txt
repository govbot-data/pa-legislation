Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Pennsylvania Office of Freedmen Affairs Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Descendant group." Individuals who are the documented biological descendants of enslaved Africans in the United States, or descendants of black, negro or colored Americans or freedmen emancipated from slavery prior to or following the Civil War."Office." The Pennsylvania Office of Freedmen Affairs 12345678910111213141516171819 established under section 3."Reconstruction era obligations." The statutory commitments, land allocations and institutional protections originally established under the Federal Freedmen's Bureau Acts of 1865-1867 (13 Stat. 507) that went unfulfilled.Section 3. Establishment of office.The Pennsylvania Office of Freedmen Affairs is established within the executive branch of the Commonwealth. The office shall be headed by an executive director, who shall be appointed by the Governor and serve at the pleasure of the Governor.Section 4. Purpose and mandate.The office shall provide a formal, State-level institutional framework to engage with historical claims, evaluate the ongoing relevance of Reconstruction era obligations and craft systemic policy solutions. The primary objectives of the office shall include:(1) Lineage and verification services, such as standardizing mechanisms to assist residents in identifying and documenting their direct lineage to enslaved populations.(2) Economic equity and reparations, such as formulating economic initiatives, housing strategies, tax relief benefits and educational pathways to bridge systemic wealth gaps affecting the descendant group.(3) Coordinating across Commonwealth agencies to measure policy impacts and advance equity initiatives.Section 5.
