AN ACT
Establishing the Pennsylvania Office of Freedmen Affairs to
provide certain individuals with unfulfilled statutory
obligations from the Reconstruction era and craft systemic
policy solutions for the socioeconomic impacts of slavery.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Pennsylvania
Office of Freedmen Affairs Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Descendant group." Individuals who are the documented
biological descendants of enslaved Africans in the United
States, or descendants of black, negro or colored Americans or
freedmen emancipated from slavery prior to or following the
Civil War.
"Office." The Pennsylvania Office of Freedmen Affairs

"Reconstruction era obligations." The statutory commitments,
land allocations and institutional protections originally
established under the Federal Freedmen's Bureau Acts of 1865-
1867 (13 Stat. 507) that went unfulfilled.
Section 3. Establishment of office.
The Pennsylvania Office of Freedmen Affairs is established
within the executive branch of the Commonwealth. The office
shall be headed by an executive director, who shall be appointed
by the Governor and serve at the pleasure of the Governor.
Section 4. Purpose and mandate.
The office shall provide a formal, State-level institutional
framework to engage with historical claims, evaluate the ongoing
relevance of Reconstruction era obligations and craft systemic
policy solutions. The primary objectives of the office shall
include:
(1) Lineage and verification services, such as
standardizing mechanisms to assist residents in identifying
and documenting their direct lineage to enslaved populations.
(2) Economic equity and reparations, such as formulating
economic initiatives, housing strategies, tax relief benefits
and educational pathways to bridge systemic wealth gaps
affecting the descendant group.
(3) Coordinating across Commonwealth agencies to measure
policy impacts and advance equity initiatives.
Section 5. Powers and duties.
The office shall have the power and duty to:
(1) Create resource connection by acting as a central
hub connecting eligible residents to resources, grants and
programs designed to foster economic investment.

(2) Conduct research by reviewing historical State
archives, land records and the operations of early relief
entities, such as the historical Pennsylvania Freedmen's

(3) Propose legislation by submitting annual policy
recommendations to the General Assembly concerning
reparations frameworks, educational scholarships and
community targeted investments.
(4) Foster public engagement by hosting structured
community dialogues, educational forums and public hearings
to engage affected stakeholders.
Section 6. Funding and appropriations.
The office may accept money from cost-recovery mechanisms,
Federal grants or private donations authorized by law.
Section 7. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 60 days.
