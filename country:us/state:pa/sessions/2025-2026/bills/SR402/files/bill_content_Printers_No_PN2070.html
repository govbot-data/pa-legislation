A RESOLUTION
Recognizing and congratulating the Pennsylvania Chamber of
Business and Industry on being named the 2026 State Chamber
of the Year by the National Association of State Chambers.
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Chamber of Business and Industry
serves as the Statewide Voice of Business, representing more
than 13,000 employers and advocating for policies that
strengthen Pennsylvania's economy and competitiveness; and
WHEREAS, The National Association of State Chambers has named
the Pennsylvania Chamber of Business and Industry the 2026 State
Chamber of the Year, recognizing the organization's leadership
and effectiveness in advocating for Pennsylvania's business
community; and
WHEREAS, The award recognizes the Pennsylvania Chamber of
Business and Industry's efforts to advance bipartisan solutions
to the challenges facing Pennsylvania's economy and promote a
stronger, more competitive business climate; and
WHEREAS, Through its advocacy, the Pennsylvania Chamber of
Business and Industry works to advance sound public policies

concerning tax reform, permitting, workforce development,

WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Chamber of Business and Industry
works with policymakers from across the political spectrum to
identify areas of common ground and advance policies that

Pennsylvania's ability to compete for new and expanding

national recognition of its role in promoting policies that
support economic growth and opportunity throughout this
Commonwealth; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate recognize and congratulate the
Pennsylvania Chamber of Business and Industry on being named the
2026 State Chamber of the Year by the National Association of
State Chambers; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the Senate commend the Pennsylvania Chamber
for its advocacy on behalf of Pennsylvania businesses and for
advancing bipartisan solutions that promote economic growth,
competitiveness and opportunity throughout this Commonwealth.
