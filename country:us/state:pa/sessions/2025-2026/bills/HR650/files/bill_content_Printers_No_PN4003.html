A RESOLUTION
Designating the week of November 16 through 20, 2026, as
"Pennsylvania Education for Students Experiencing
Homelessness Awareness Week" and November 20, 2026, as "Red
Shirt Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, In the 2024-2025 school year, 52,347 children and
youths in Pennsylvania experienced homelessness; and
WHEREAS, In the 2023-2024 school year, 50,030 children and
youths in Pennsylvania experienced homelessness; and
WHEREAS, This represents a sustained and continued increase
from the 2022-2023 school year; and
WHEREAS, The purpose of this resolution is to educate the
public about the prevalence of students experiencing
homelessness, the barriers that may prevent youths from
attending school and the McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act
(Public Law 100-77, 101 Stat. 482), with which all schools must
comply to overcome those barriers; and
WHEREAS, There are limited resources available to youths
experiencing homelessness, including the shortage of affordable

WHEREAS, Homeless assistance service providers, as well as

WHEREAS, While there are many organizations committed to

WHEREAS, Some shelters will not accept a child under 18 years
of age without the company of a parent or guardian; and
WHEREAS, There is a dire need for additional resources to
assist students experiencing homelessness to attend school and
be successful, including the basic needs of food, clothing and
educational supplies; and
WHEREAS, To show solidarity with children experiencing
homelessness and their educators, the House of Representatives
encourages Pennsylvanians to wear red on November 20, 2026, for
"Red Shirt Day"; and
WHEREAS, The purpose of "Pennsylvania Education for Students
Experiencing Homelessness Awareness Week" is to encourage
schools, communities and individuals to plan for and support
assistance programs for youths experiencing homelessness as they
look to complete their education; therefore be it

week of November 16 through 20, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Education
