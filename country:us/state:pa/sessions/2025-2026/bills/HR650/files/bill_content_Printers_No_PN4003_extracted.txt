WILLIAMS AND BELLMON, OCTOBER 5, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, OCTOBER 5, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the week of November 16 through 20, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Education for Students Experiencing Homelessness Awareness Week" and November 20, 2026, as "Red Shirt Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, In the 2024-2025 school year, 52,347 children and youths in Pennsylvania experienced homelessness; andWHEREAS, In the 2023-2024 school year, 50,030 children and youths in Pennsylvania experienced homelessness; andWHEREAS, This represents a sustained and continued increase from the 2022-2023 school year; andWHEREAS, The purpose of this resolution is to educate the public about the prevalence of students experiencing homelessness, the barriers that may prevent youths from attending school and the McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act (Public Law 100-77, 101 Stat. 482), with which all schools must comply to overcome those barriers; andWHEREAS, There are limited resources available to youths experiencing homelessness, including the shortage of affordable 123456789101112131415161718 housing for displaced minors; andWHEREAS, Homeless assistance service providers, as well as community service opportunities for students and school service organizations, need public support; andWHEREAS, While there are many organizations committed to sheltering and providing support services, meals and food supplies to people experiencing homelessness, not all of these services are specific to the needs of unaccompanied minors; andWHEREAS, Some shelters will not accept a child under 18 years of age without the company of a parent or guardian; andWHEREAS, There is a dire need for additional resources to assist students experiencing homelessness to attend school and be successful, including the basic needs of food, clothing and educational supplies; andWHEREAS, To show solidarity with children experiencing homelessness and their educators, the House of Representatives encourages Pennsylvanians to wear red on November 20, 2026, for "Red Shirt Day"; andWHEREAS, The purpose of "Pennsylvania Education for Students Experiencing Homelessness Awareness Week" is to encourage schools, communities and individuals to plan for and support assistance programs for youths experiencing homelessness as they look to complete their education; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate the week of November 16 through 20, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Education for Students Experiencing Homelessness Awareness Week" and November 20, 2026, as "Red Shirt Day" in Pennsylvania;
