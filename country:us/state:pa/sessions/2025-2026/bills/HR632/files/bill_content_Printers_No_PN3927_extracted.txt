2025-2026 Regular Session HR 632 PN 3927 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3927 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.632 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BELLMON, VENKAT, WAXMAN, SANCHEZ, HOHENSTEIN, HILL-EVANS, GIRAL, BRENNAN, DEASY, GUZMAN, PARKER, COOPER, RIVERA, DOUGHERTY AND DUKES, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON STATE GOVERNMENT, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the week of October 5 through 9, 2026, as "National Voter Education Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, "National Voter Education Week" is a nonpartisan initiative that helps all voters overcome barriers to casting their ballots and encourages participation in our democracy; andWHEREAS, "National Voter Education Week" is observed annually during the first full week of October and helps voters, especially those who are newly eligible, find their polling place, understand their ballot and cast their vote with confidence and ease; andWHEREAS, Approximately 182,000 Pennsylvanians turn 18 years of age each year, and young voters are a growing part of our democracy and helping to shape the future of this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, Approximately 141,000 individuals 18 years of age in Pennsylvania who are eligible to vote are not registered to vote; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, By comparison, 82% of adults 45 years of age or older are registered to vote in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, These figures highlight the importance of "National Voter Education Week," which helps to ensure that eligible Pennsylvanians have the knowledge and resources necessary to cast their ballots; andWHEREAS, Beyond empowering individuals to register to vote, "National Voter Education Week" helps voters understand what is on the ballot, whether for a high-profile Federal election or a local election; andWHEREAS, "National Voter Education Week" provides an opportunity to promote accessible, accurate and nonpartisan information about voter registration, voting procedures and civic participation, thereby empowering voters to participate confidently in elections and supporting our nation's democratic process; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the week of October 5 through 9, 2026, as "National Voter Education Week" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0632PN3927 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819
