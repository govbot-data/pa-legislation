A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the week of October 5 through 9, 2026, as "National
Voter Education Week" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, "National Voter Education Week" is a nonpartisan
initiative that helps all voters overcome barriers to casting
their ballots and encourages participation in our democracy; and
WHEREAS, "National Voter Education Week" is observed annually
during the first full week of October and helps voters,
especially those who are newly eligible, find their polling
place, understand their ballot and cast their vote with
confidence and ease; and
WHEREAS, Approximately 182,000 Pennsylvanians turn 18 years
of age each year, and young voters are a growing part of our
democracy and helping to shape the future of this Commonwealth;
and
WHEREAS, Approximately 141,000 individuals 18 years of age in
Pennsylvania who are eligible to vote are not registered to
vote; and

WHEREAS, By comparison, 82% of adults 45 years of age or
older are registered to vote in this Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, These figures highlight the importance of "National
Voter Education Week," which helps to ensure that eligible
Pennsylvanians have the knowledge and resources necessary to
cast their ballots; and
WHEREAS, Beyond empowering individuals to register to vote,
"National Voter Education Week" helps voters understand what is
on the ballot, whether for a high-profile Federal election or a
local election; and
WHEREAS, "National Voter Education Week" provides an
opportunity to promote accessible, accurate and nonpartisan
information about voter registration, voting procedures and
civic participation, thereby empowering voters to participate
confidently in elections and supporting our nation's democratic
process; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the
week of October 5 through 9, 2026, as "National Voter Education
Week" in Pennsylvania.
