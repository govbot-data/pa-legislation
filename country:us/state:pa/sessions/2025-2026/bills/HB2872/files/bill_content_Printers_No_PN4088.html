AN ACT
Providing funding to the Department of Military and Veterans
Affairs for the purpose of providing current service members
and veterans with access to high-definition fiber tracking
brain scans and supporting ongoing clinical research in this
Commonwealth involving traumatic brain injuries; and making
an appropriation.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the High-
Definition Fiber Tracking for Veterans Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Brain scan equipment." Health care imaging equipment
capable of identifying brain damage by graphic depiction of
individual nerve tracts.

"Department." The Department of Military and Veterans
Affairs of the Commonwealth.
"Eligible center." A health care facility designated by the
department to provide access to HDFT brain scans.
"HDFT." A brain scan employing high-definition fiber
tracking technology.
Section 3. Military members and veterans.
(a) Diagnosis.--A military veteran or active duty or reserve
member of the United States Armed Forces or National Guard who
has symptoms of traumatic brain injury or posttraumatic stress
or evidence of combat-related concussion shall be eligible to
receive diagnostic treatment through the use of the new HDFT
technology using brain scan equipment located in this
Commonwealth.
(b) Eligible centers.--To begin the process for the use of
the diagnostic treatment and technology under this section, an
individual described in subsection (a) must visit an eligible
center.
(c) Brain scan equipment.--The department shall ensure that
brain scan equipment is purchased or leased and provided to
eligible centers to provide access to HDFT technology throughout
this Commonwealth.
Section 4. Regulations.
The department shall promulgate regulations for the
implementation and administration of this act. The regulations
must allow for the maximum number of individuals described in
section 3(a) to be able to use the HDFT technology.
Section 5. Funding.
(a) Appropriation.--The sum of $1,000,000, or as much
thereof as may be necessary, is appropriated to the department

(b) Distributions.--Of the $1,000,000 referenced in
subsection (a):
(1) The department shall distribute not more than the
sum of $500,000 to all eligible centers proportionally based
on the number of individuals described in section 3(a) who
are served at each eligible center. The money received by
each eligible center must be used to pay for hardware and
software costs of the diagnostic technology and
administrative expenses of the eligible center directly
related to providing brain scans under this act.
(2) The department shall distribute not more than the
sum of $500,000 to ensure availability of the brain scan
equipment at eligible centers throughout this Commonwealth.
Section 6. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 60 days.
