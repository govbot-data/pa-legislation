2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2872 PN 4088 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 4088 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2872 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY GUENST, GIRAL, HOHENSTEIN, McNEILL, PROBST, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, DOUGHERTY, SANCHEZ, PIELLI, KHAN, BOROWSKI, SCHLOSSBERG, HILL-EVANS, HOWARD, NEILSON, DELLOSO, FLEMING, OTTEN, WEBSTER, KENYATTA, DEASY, CERRATO, GREEN, GILLEN AND O'MARA, OCTOBER 21, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, OCTOBER 21, 2026 AN ACTProviding funding to the Department of Military and Veterans Affairs for the purpose of providing current service members and veterans with access to high-definition fiber tracking brain scans and supporting ongoing clinical research in this Commonwealth involving traumatic brain injuries; and making an appropriation.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the High-Definition Fiber Tracking for Veterans Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Brain scan equipment." Health care imaging equipment capable of identifying brain damage by graphic depiction of individual nerve tracts.123456789101112131415161718 "Department." The Department of Military and Veterans Affairs of the Commonwealth."Eligible center." A health care facility designated by the department to provide access to HDFT brain scans."HDFT." A brain scan employing high-definition fiber tracking technology.Section 3. Military members and veterans.(a) Diagnosis.--A military veteran or active duty or reserve member of the United States Armed Forces or National Guard who has symptoms of traumatic brain injury or posttraumatic stress or evidence of combat-related concussion shall be eligible to receive diagnostic treatment through the use of the new HDFT technology using brain scan equipment located in this Commonwealth.(b) Eligible centers.--To begin the process for the use of the diagnostic treatment and technology under this section, an individual described in subsection (a) must visit an eligible center.(c) Brain scan equipment.--The department shall ensure that brain scan equipment is purchased or leased and provided to eligible centers to provide access to HDFT technology throughout this Commonwealth.Section 4. Regulations.The department shall promulgate regulations for the implementation and administration of this act. The regulations must allow for the maximum number of individuals described in section 3(a) to be able to use the HDFT technology.Section 5. Funding.(a) Appropriation.--The sum of $1,000,000, or as much thereof as may be necessary, is appropriated to the department 20260HB2872PN4088 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 for the fiscal year July 1, 2025, through June 30, 2026, to carry out the provisions of this act.(b) Distributions.--Of the $1,000,000 referenced in subsection (a):(1) The department shall distribute not more than the sum of $500,000 to all eligible centers proportionally based on the number of individuals described in section 3(a) who are served at each eligible center. The money received by each eligible center must be used to pay for hardware and software costs of the diagnostic technology and administrative expenses of the eligible center directly related to providing brain scans under this act.(2) The department shall distribute not more than the sum of $500,000 to ensure availability of the brain scan equipment at eligible centers throughout this Commonwealth.Section 6.
