A RESOLUTION
Designating December 19, 2026, as "Wreaths Across America Day"
in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Having been inspired by a trip to Arlington National
Cemetery in his youth, Morrill Worcester donated 5,000 surplus
wreaths from his Worcester Wreath Company in Harrington, Maine,
to a sparsely visited section of Arlington National Cemetery in
December 1992; and
WHEREAS, In a community effort, volunteers from the American
Legion and Veterans of Foreign Wars posts and the local
community tied red bows onto each wreath, and the local trucking
company, Blue Bird Ranch, Inc., transported the wreaths to
Virginia; and
WHEREAS, This initiative became a tradition that continued
for more than 10 years when, in 2005, photos of the wreaths
circulated and gained national attention, leading to thousands

WHEREAS, Worcester began sending seven wreaths to each state,
one for each branch of the military and an additional wreath to
honor prisoners of war and those missing in action; and
WHEREAS, With the help of the Civil Air Patrol and other
civic organizations, simultaneous wreath-laying ceremonies were
held at more than 150 locations in December 2006; and
WHEREAS, The Patriot Guard Riders volunteered to escort the
convoy of wreaths south along the East Coast to Arlington
National Cemetery, now an annual tradition; and
WHEREAS, The Worcester family and countless volunteers formed
Wreaths Across America, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, in 2007 with the
mission to remember the fallen, honor those who serve and teach
the next generation the value of freedom; and
WHEREAS, During the December 2014 holiday season, Wreaths
Across America met its goal to place wreaths at all 226,525
graves at Arlington National Cemetery; and
WHEREAS, A veteran's wreath consists of 10 balsam bouquets,
representing 10 special qualities that veterans embody,
including:
(1) a belief in a greater good;
(2) love for one another;
(3) strength, work ethic and character;
(4) honesty and integrity;
(5) humility, selflessness and modesty;
(6) ambitions and aspirations;
(7) optimism for America;
(8) concern for the future;

WHEREAS, The evergreen branches of a veteran's wreath
symbolize longevity and endurance, and its forest scent
represents purity and simplicity; and
WHEREAS, The wreath's circular shape symbolizes eternity, and
the red bow symbolizes great sacrifice; and
WHEREAS, More than 2 million volunteers are expected to
participate at more than 5,200 locations across the United
States and abroad; and
WHEREAS, The theme for Wreaths Across America Day 2026 is
"Remember Me," honoring 250 years of American history by
focusing on individual service members whose stories are at risk
of being forgotten; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate
December 19, 2026, as "Wreaths Across America Day" in
Pennsylvania; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives encourage the
residents of this Commonwealth to remember, honor and teach the
stories of the brave individuals who wore the uniform to protect
and defend the United States of America.
