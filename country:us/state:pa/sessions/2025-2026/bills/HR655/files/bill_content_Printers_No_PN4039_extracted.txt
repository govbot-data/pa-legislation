andWHEREAS, In a community effort, volunteers from the American Legion and Veterans of Foreign Wars posts and the local community tied red bows onto each wreath, and the local trucking company, Blue Bird Ranch, Inc., transported the wreaths to Virginia; andWHEREAS, This initiative became a tradition that continued for more than 10 years when, in 2005, photos of the wreaths circulated and gained national attention, leading to thousands 123456789101112131415 of inquiries to purchase and lay wreaths at Arlington National Cemetery and thousands more hoping to emulate the wreath-laying at local sites; andWHEREAS, The Patriot Guard Riders volunteered to escort the convoy of wreaths south along the East Coast to Arlington National Cemetery, now an annual tradition; andWHEREAS, The Worcester family and countless volunteers formed Wreaths Across America, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, in 2007 with the mission to remember the fallen, honor those who serve and teach the next generation the value of freedom;
