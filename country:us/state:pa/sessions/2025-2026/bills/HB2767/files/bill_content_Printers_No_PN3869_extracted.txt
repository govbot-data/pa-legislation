Certification, form and submission.(a) Required certification.--An individual or entity selected for an audit by the department under section 3763 (relating to Equal Opportunity Audit Program) shall certify to the department that the individual or entity:(1) Maintains a nondiscrimination policy that covers 20260HB2767PN3869 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 protected classes in employment, contracting, subcontracting and service delivery.(2) Establishes and makes available to the public its merit criteria for covered opportunities determined by the department.(3) Collects and retains benchmark demographic data on applicant population and the outcome of an individual in the protected class, as permitted by Federal and State law.(b) Form and submission.--For the purpose specified in subsection (a), the department shall create and transmit the following to the Legislative Reference Bureau for publication in the next available issue of the Pennsylvania Bulletin:(1) The certification form.(2) Instructions to submit the certification form and any supporting documents requested by the department.(3) The criteria used to assess risk in the program.§ 3763. Equal Opportunity Audit Program.(a) Establishment.--The Equal Opportunity Audit Program is established in the department. The department, in consultation with the commission, shall administer the program for the purpose of conducting audits under this section.(b) Frequency of audit.--The department shall conduct an audit of entities at least once every three years. The department may conduct additional audits on an annual basis if an entity is selected at random or based on the most recent criteria used to assess risk published in the Pennsylvania Bulletin.(c) Scope of audit.--The audit shall include the following:(1) A review of data collection practices and merit criteria.20260HB2767PN3869 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (2) A statistical disparity analysis that compares the demographic data required for certification under section 3762 (relating to certification, form and submission) to the benchmark demographic data.(3) An assessment of procurement and subcontracting practices, including businesses operated or owned by the following groups:(i) Minorities.(ii) Women.(iii) Veterans.(iv) Individuals with disabilities.(4) Identification of issues that hinder or prevent a class protected from discrimination under Federal or State law from having an opportunity.(d) Execution of program.--The department shall:(1) Hire an independent auditor that complies with the following requirements:(i) Has a certification in auditing.(ii) Has demonstrated expertise in audits that require an individual or entity to comply with equity and inclusion, diversity and civil rights compliance as required by Federal and State law and subsection (c).(2) Gather the data compiled by the independent auditor and summarize the findings into a report in accordance with section 3764 (relating to reporting and corrective action).§ 3764. Reporting and corrective action.(a) Publication.--The department shall post a summary of the audit findings compiled under section 3763(d) (relating to Equal Opportunity Audit Program) available on the department's publicly accessible Internet website.20260HB2767PN3869 - 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (b) Confidentiality of information.--The department shall protect confidential business information and personally identifiable information in accordance with Federal and State law, including the act of February 14, 2008 (P.L.6, No.3), known as the Right-to-Know Law, and other applicable privacy protections.(c) Corrective action.--If the department determines that an individual or entity is in material noncompliance, the department shall send the individual or entity and the awarding agency a written notice of the individual's or entity's material noncompliance. The department, in its written notice, shall notify the individual or entity:(1) That the individual or entity has no more than 90 days after the date of the notice to submit a corrective action to the department and the awarding agency and to cure the material noncompliance.(2) If the material noncompliance is not cured, that the agency may take actions under section 3765 (relating to remedies).§ 3765. Remedies.(a) Remedies for continuing material noncompliance.--If material noncompliance, as determined under section 3764(c) (relating to reporting and corrective action) is not cured after the 90-day cure period, the awarding agency may take the following actions against the individual or entity:(1) Suspend or terminate a contract.(2) Withhold or recover grant or loan funds.(3) Deny or revoke a tax incentive, credit or abatement.(4) Request that the department commence debarment or suspension proceedings under section 531 (relating to 20260HB2767PN3869 - 6 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 debarment or suspension).(b) Proportionality.--The awarding agency and the department shall match the remedy to the severity and persistence of the violation and to any demonstrated corrective efforts.(c) Other laws.--The remedies under this section shall be in addition to remedies available under Federal or State law, regulation or contract.§ 3766. Regulations.The department, in consultation with the commission, shall promulgate regulations necessary to implement this subchapter no later than one year after the effective date of this section.§ 3767. Applicability and construction.(a) Applicability.--The provisions of 2 Pa.C.S. Ch. 5 Subch. A (relating to practice and procedure of Commonwealth agencies) shall apply to determinations or remedies under section 3764(c) (relating to reporting and corrective action) or 3765(a)(relating to remedies).(b) Construction.--Nothing in this subchapter shall be construed to modify the provisions of Chapter 5 (relating to source selection and contract formation).Section 2.
