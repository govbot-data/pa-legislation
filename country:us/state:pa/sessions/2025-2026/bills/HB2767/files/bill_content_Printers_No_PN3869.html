Statutes, in contract clauses and preference provisions,
providing for Equal Opportunity Audit Program; and imposing
powers and duties on the Department of General Services.

hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Chapter 37 of Title 62 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a subchapter to read:

The following words and phrases when used in this subchapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Applicant population." An individual or entity that applies
for an opportunity.
"Awarding agency." A Commonwealth agency or an
instrumentality of the Commonwealth that awards, issues,
approves or executes an opportunity.
"Benchmark demographic data." Data compiled from demographic
data on members of a class protected from discrimination under
Federal or State law.
"Commission." The Pennsylvania Human Relations Commission.
"Demographic data." Information on a protected class that an
entity collects under this subchapter and applicable law which
may be collected on a voluntary basis and stored in de-
identified or aggregated form when feasible.
"Department." The Department of General Services of the
Commonwealth.
"Entity." A contractor that applies for and contracts with
an awarding agency or business or nonprofit group that applies
for and receives an opportunity.
"Material noncompliance." A failure to comply with a duty
under this subchapter, regulation or order issued by the
department under this subchapter that is substantial in nature
or effect, including:
(1) A failure to submit a required certification or
submission of a false or misleading certification under
section 3762(a) (relating to certification, form and
submission).
(2) Failure to maintain a nondiscrimination policy that

§ 3763. Equal Opportunity Audit Program.
(a) Establishment.--The Equal Opportunity Audit Program is
established in the department. The department, in consultation
with the commission, shall administer the program for the
purpose of conducting audits under this section.
(b) Frequency of audit.--The department shall conduct an
audit of entities at least once every three years. The
department may conduct additional audits on an annual basis if
an entity is selected at random or based on the most recent
criteria used to assess risk published in the Pennsylvania
Bulletin.
(c) Scope of audit.--The audit shall include the following:
(1) A review of data collection practices and merit
criteria.

(a) Publication.--The department shall post a summary of the
audit findings compiled under section 3763(d) (relating to Equal
Opportunity Audit Program) available on the department's
publicly accessible Internet website.

(b) Confidentiality of information.--The department shall
protect confidential business information and personally

as the Right-to-Know Law, and other applicable privacy

(c) Corrective action.--If the department determines that an
individual or entity is in material noncompliance, the
department shall send the individual or entity and the awarding
agency a written notice of the individual's or entity's material
noncompliance. The department, in its written notice, shall
notify the individual or entity:
(1) That the individual or entity has no more than 90
days after the date of the notice to submit a corrective
action to the department and the awarding agency and to cure
the material noncompliance.
(2) If the material noncompliance is not cured, that the
agency may take actions under section 3765 (relating to
remedies).
§ 3765. Remedies.
(a) Remedies for continuing material noncompliance.--If
material noncompliance, as determined under section 3764(c)
(relating to reporting and corrective action) is not cured after
the 90-day cure period, the awarding agency may take the
following actions against the individual or entity:
(1) Suspend or terminate a contract.
(2) Withhold or recover grant or loan funds.
(3) Deny or revoke a tax incentive, credit or abatement.
(4) Request that the department commence debarment or
suspension proceedings under section 531 (relating to

§ 3766. Regulations.
The department, in consultation with the commission, shall
promulgate regulations necessary to implement this subchapter no
later than one year after the effective date of this section.
§ 3767. Applicability and construction.
(a) Applicability.--The provisions of 2 Pa.C.S. Ch. 5 Subch.
A (relating to practice and procedure of Commonwealth agencies)
shall apply to determinations or remedies under section 3764(c)
(relating to reporting and corrective action) or 3765(a)
(relating to remedies).
(b) Construction.--Nothing in this subchapter shall be
construed to modify the provisions of Chapter 5 (relating to
source selection and contract formation).
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
