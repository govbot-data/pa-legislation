A RESOLUTION
Honoring the distinguished life and service of the Honorable
Marie A. Lederer, seven-term former member of the House of
Representatives from the 175th District, Philadelphia County,
who died February 26, 2025, at 97 years of age.
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives remembers Marie A.
Lederer's vision, pragmatism and diligence as a legislator from
1993 through 2006; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives remembers Ms. Lederer's
faithful service as a steward of the 175th District's unique
resources and landmarks; and
WHEREAS, Ms. Lederer welcomed members of the General Assembly
to her district and worked for all Pennsylvanians; and
WHEREAS, Through her energies, special interests and efforts
on behalf of children and senior citizens, Ms. Lederer notably
improved the safety and welfare of her Fishtown neighborhood and
Philadelphia County; and
WHEREAS, For nearly two decades, the Marie Lederer Senior
Center at Frankford Avenue and Palmer Street in Fishtown has
provided safe, affordable senior housing at the former Saint

WHEREAS, The reuse and revitalization project which converted

leadership; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Lederer was elected to the House of
Representatives in 1965, 1967, 1971 and 1973 before being named
to the bench; and
WHEREAS, The Lederers, who met at the Starlight Ballroom in

Judge Lederer's death in 2008; and
