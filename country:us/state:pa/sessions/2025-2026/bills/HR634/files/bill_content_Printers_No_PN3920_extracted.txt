2025-2026 Regular Session HR 634 PN 3920 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3920 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.634 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, KHAN, PIELLI, SHUSTERMAN, MAYES, HOHENSTEIN, VENKAT, McNEILL, HILL-EVANS, SANCHEZ, RIVERA, PASHINSKI AND MADDEN, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON VETERANS AFFAIRS AND EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating the week of October 11 through 17, 2026, as "Military Sexual Trauma Survivor Week" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The United States Department of Defense's Annual Report on Sexual Assault in the Military highlights the continued impact of sexual assault on service members; andWHEREAS, The Department of Defense received 8,195 reports of sexual assault involving service members in fiscal year 2024, including 6,973 reports from service members for incidents that occurred during military service; andWHEREAS, The Department of Defense estimates that approximately 29,000 active-duty service members experienced unwanted sexual contact in fiscal year 2023, including approximately 15,200 women and 13,800 men, given that only about 25% of service members who experience unwanted sexual contact report the incidents; andWHEREAS, Barriers to reporting, such as fear of retaliation, concerns about career consequences and the belief that a report 1234567891011121314151617 will not be acted upon, may prevent survivors of sexual assault from seeking care and support or participating in the military justice process; andWHEREAS, The Department of Defense has taken steps to prevent sexual violence, including prosecution of such offenses and implementing recommendations from the Independent Review Commission on Sexual Assault in the Military to prevent sexual violence, hold offenders accountable and support survivors; andWHEREAS, Service members who experience sexual assault may face lasting effects on their military careers, such as higher rates of separation and demotion and increased rates of mental health-related diagnoses; andWHEREAS, The United States military's culture of duty, teamwork, strength and accomplishment may create unique challenges for survivors of military sexual assault in seeking support and reporting their experiences; andWHEREAS, Access to appropriate medical and mental health services is critical to supporting the recovery and well-being of military sexual trauma survivors; andWHEREAS, In this Commonwealth, the Department of Military and Veterans Affairs can connect veterans with available resources, while United States Department of Veterans Affairs Vet Centers and medical centers provide counseling, mental health care and other support for survivors of military sexual trauma; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives recognizes the strength, resilience and perseverance of military sexual trauma survivors and reaffirms its commitment to raising awareness and supporting those affected;
