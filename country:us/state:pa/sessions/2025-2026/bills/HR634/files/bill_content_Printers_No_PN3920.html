A RESOLUTION
Designating the week of October 11 through 17, 2026, as
"Military Sexual Trauma Survivor Week" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The United States Department of Defense's Annual
Report on Sexual Assault in the Military highlights the
continued impact of sexual assault on service members; and
WHEREAS, The Department of Defense received 8,195 reports of
sexual assault involving service members in fiscal year 2024,
including 6,973 reports from service members for incidents that
occurred during military service; and
WHEREAS, The Department of Defense estimates that
approximately 29,000 active-duty service members experienced
unwanted sexual contact in fiscal year 2023, including
approximately 15,200 women and 13,800 men, given that only about
25% of service members who experience unwanted sexual contact
report the incidents; and
WHEREAS, Barriers to reporting, such as fear of retaliation,
concerns about career consequences and the belief that a report

WHEREAS, The Department of Defense has taken steps to prevent

WHEREAS, Service members who experience sexual assault may
face lasting effects on their military careers, such as higher
rates of separation and demotion and increased rates of mental

WHEREAS, The United States military's culture of duty,
teamwork, strength and accomplishment may create unique
challenges for survivors of military sexual assault in seeking
support and reporting their experiences; and
WHEREAS, Access to appropriate medical and mental health
services is critical to supporting the recovery and well-being
of military sexual trauma survivors; and
WHEREAS, In this Commonwealth, the Department of Military and
Veterans Affairs can connect veterans with available resources,
while United States Department of Veterans Affairs Vet Centers
and medical centers provide counseling, mental health care and
other support for survivors of military sexual trauma; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives recognizes the
