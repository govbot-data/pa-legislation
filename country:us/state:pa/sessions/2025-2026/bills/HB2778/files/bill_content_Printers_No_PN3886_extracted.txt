Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Senior Security Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Department." The Department of Aging of the Commonwealth."Home security device." Includes:(1) A video doorbell camera with live monitoring capabilities.(2) Motion-activated exterior lighting.12345678910111213141516 (3) A sensor on a door or window.(4) An emergency alert or panic system connected to law enforcement or emergency services."Senior." A Pennsylvania resident who is 60 years of age or older.Section 3. Senior security program.(a) Establishment of program.--The department shall establish and administer a program in partnership with local area agencies on aging that distributes and installs home security devices to seniors.(b) Duties of department.--The department shall:(1) Develop a form and distribute the form to local area agencies on aging that allows a senior to apply for the program.(2) After receiving an application for the program, decide if the senior qualifies for the program.(3) If the senior qualifies for the program, decide what type of home security device to provide to the senior. When making a decision on the type and amount of home security devices to provide to a senior under the program, the department shall choose the most cost-effective option that meets the senior's needs and circumstances.(4) Install, or contract with a third party to install, home security devices for the senior.(c) Prioritization.--A senior who lives in the following conditions shall have priority for being provided a home security device:(1) Lives alone or with another senior.(2) Resides in neighborhoods with high rates of burglary or violent crime.20260HB2778PN3886 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (3) Has previously been a victim of a home invasion or burglary.(d) Qualification.--To qualify for the program established under this section, a senior shall sign documentation stating that the senior will share recorded footage with law enforcement officers when a crime occurs in the area of the home security device.(e) Installation and training.--The following shall be offered to the senior under the program at no cost to the senior:(1) Professional installation of the home security device.(2) User training of the home security device.Section 4. Funding.Funding for the program established under section 3 shall be provided by money appropriated by the General Assembly under the general appropriations act and any Federal grants that may be available.Section 5.
