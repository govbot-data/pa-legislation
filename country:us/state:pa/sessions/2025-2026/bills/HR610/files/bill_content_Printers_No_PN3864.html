A RESOLUTION
Recognizing September 26, 2026, as "National Hunting and Fishing
Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, "National Hunting and Fishing Day" is celebrated on
the fourth Saturday of September to acknowledge the numerous
contributions of hunters and anglers; and
WHEREAS, In May 1972, Congress enacted Senate Joint
Resolution 117, requesting that the fourth Saturday of September
1972 be declared "National Hunting and Fishing Day" to recognize
hunting and fishing and promote the conservation and respectful
use of wildlife and natural resources; and
WHEREAS, "National Hunting and Fishing Day" recognizes
sportsmen and women throughout the nation and celebrates
hunting, fishing and sport shooting through planned events and
activities; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania has a long history and culture of
hunting and angling, as the Pennsylvania Fish and Boat

WHEREAS, Pennsylvania lands and streams provide an abundance

WHEREAS, The economic impact of hunting and fishing in
Pennsylvania is significant, supporting an outdoor recreation
economy of more than $20 billion and ranking this Commonwealth
among the top 20 states for economic activity based on hunting
and fishing activities; and
WHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission and the
Pennsylvania Game Commission are funded in part by sportsmen and
women through the American System of Conservation Funding, a
"user pays-public benefits" approach that is widely recognized
as a successful model for funding fish and wildlife management;
and
WHEREAS, Through self-imposed fees and excise taxes on
fishing tackle, ammunition, firearms, motorboat fuel and other
equipment, sportsmen and women have contributed more than $3.5
billion nationally to conservation efforts; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania has more than 2 million paid hunting
and fishing license holders who help enhance conservation
efforts, promote outdoor recreation and improve the overall
economy; and
WHEREAS, Hunting and angling provide significant benefits to
the environment, economy and overall public good; and
