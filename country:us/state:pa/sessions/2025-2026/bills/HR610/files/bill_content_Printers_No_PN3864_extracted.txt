andWHEREAS, In May 1972, Congress enacted Senate Joint Resolution 117, requesting that the fourth Saturday of September 1972 be declared "National Hunting and Fishing Day" to recognize hunting and fishing and promote the conservation and respectful use of wildlife and natural resources; andWHEREAS, "National Hunting and Fishing Day" recognizes sportsmen and women throughout the nation and celebrates hunting, fishing and sport shooting through planned events and activities; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania has a long history and culture of hunting and angling, as the Pennsylvania Fish and Boat 12345678910111213141516 Commission was established in 1866 and the Pennsylvania Game Commission in 1895 to protect, conserve and enhance aquatic and wildlife resources and provide fishing and hunting opportunities; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania lands and streams provide an abundance of outdoor recreational opportunities for the public to enjoy, and hunting and fishing play a large role in conservation efforts and economic prosperity in this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, The economic impact of hunting and fishing in Pennsylvania is significant, supporting an outdoor recreation economy of more than $20 billion and ranking this Commonwealth among the top 20 states for economic activity based on hunting and fishing activities; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission and the Pennsylvania Game Commission are funded in part by sportsmen and women through the American System of Conservation Funding, a "user pays-public benefits" approach that is widely recognized as a successful model for funding fish and wildlife management; andWHEREAS, Through self-imposed fees and excise taxes on fishing tackle, ammunition, firearms, motorboat fuel and other equipment, sportsmen and women have contributed more than $3.5 billion nationally to conservation efforts; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania has more than 2 million paid hunting and fishing license holders who help enhance conservation efforts, promote outdoor recreation and improve the overall economy; andWHEREAS, Hunting and angling provide significant benefits to the environment, economy and overall public good;
