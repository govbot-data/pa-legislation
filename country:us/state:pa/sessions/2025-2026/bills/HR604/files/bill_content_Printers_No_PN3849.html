A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the month of September 2026 as "Suicide Prevention
Awareness Month" and September 10, 2026, as "World Suicide
Prevention Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, More than 720,000 people around the world die by
suicide each year; and
WHEREAS, The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
estimates that there were 2.2 million suicide attempts and 14.3
million individuals that seriously thought about suicide in the
United States in 2024; and
WHEREAS, After reaching its peak of 14.2 per 100,000
individuals in both 2018 and 2020, suicide rates are fortunately
on the decline; and
WHEREAS, According to the Centers for Disease Control and
Prevention, suicide accounted for nearly 49,000 deaths in 2024,
which is 1 death every 11 minutes; and
WHEREAS, Suicide is the tenth-leading cause of death in the
