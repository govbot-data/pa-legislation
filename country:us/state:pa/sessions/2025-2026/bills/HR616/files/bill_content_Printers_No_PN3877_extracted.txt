2025-2026 Regular Session HR 616 PN 3877 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3877 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.616 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY MARCELL, HOGAN, STAATS, PICKETT, RIVERA, ISAACSON, DEASY, BRENNAN, McNEILL, HOHENSTEIN, ROWE, COOPER AND KAUFFMAN, SEPTEMBER 9, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, SEPTEMBER 14, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of September 2026 as "Ovarian Cancer Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Ovarian cancer, which occurs when abnormal cells in a woman's ovaries, fallopian tubes or the related peritoneum grow out of control and spread, is the sixth leading cause of cancer deaths for women in the United States; andWHEREAS, There are more than 30 types of ovarian cancer, with epithelial ovarian cancers being the most common; andWHEREAS, A woman's risk of developing ovarian cancer during her lifetime is approximately 1 in 91; andWHEREAS, A woman's chance of dying from ovarian cancer is approximately 1 in 143; andWHEREAS, Increased risk factors for developing ovarian cancer include aging, obesity, a family history of ovarian or breast cancer, inherited gene mutations and a personal history of endometriosis or breast, uterine or colorectal cancer; andWHEREAS, About half of all women diagnosed with ovarian 1234567891011121314151617 cancer are 63 years of age or older; andWHEREAS, In 2026, the American Cancer Society estimates approximately 21,010 women will receive a new diagnosis of ovarian cancer, and approximately 12,450 women will die from ovarian cancer; andWHEREAS, The rate of ovarian cancer deaths has decreased by 45% since 1976, reflecting both decreased incidence and improved treatment; andWHEREAS, When detected early, 94% of ovarian cancer patients live longer than five years after diagnosis; andWHEREAS, An estimated 20% of ovarian cancers are detected at an early stage; andWHEREAS, Symptoms of ovarian cancer are often vague and include bloating, pelvic or abdominal pain, changes in appetite, frequent urination, gastrointestinal issues and unexplained changes in weight; andWHEREAS, The American Cancer Society recommends that women discuss potential ovarian cancer symptoms with their doctors; andWHEREAS, Raising awareness of ovarian cancer is critically important in achieving earlier diagnoses; andWHEREAS, September is the official "Ovarian Cancer Awareness Month," a time to spread awareness about ovarian cancer risk and prevention and to garner support for continued scientific research; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize September 2026 as "Ovarian Cancer Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0616PN3877 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122232425262728
