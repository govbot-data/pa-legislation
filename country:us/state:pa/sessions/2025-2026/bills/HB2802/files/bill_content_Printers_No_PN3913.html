AN ACT
Amending the act of June 27, 2006 (1st Sp.Sess., P.L.1873,
No.1), entitled "An act providing for taxation by school
districts, for the State funds formula, for tax relief in
first class cities, for school district choice and voter
participation, for other school district options and for a
task force on school cost reduction; making an appropriation;
prohibiting prior authorized taxation; providing for
installment payment of taxes; restricting the power of
certain school districts to levy, assess and collect taxes;
and making related repeals," in taxation by school districts,
further providing for general tax authorization, for
reimbursement and for disposition of income tax revenue and
property tax reduction allocations and repealing provisions
relating to school district options; in State funds formula,
further providing for certification and calculation of
minimum and maximum modifiers and repealing provisions
relating to State property tax reduction allocation;
providing for homeowner tax rebate; in tax relief in cities
of the first class, further providing for tax relief and for
supplemental senior citizen tax reduction; in school district
choice and voter participation, repealing provisions relating
to school district choice and further providing for voter
participation; and, in senior citizens property tax and rent
rebate assistance, further providing for property tax and
rent rebate.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:

Section 7. The act is amended by adding a chapter to read:
CHAPTER 6
ANNUAL GAMING DIVIDEND
Section 601. Scope of chapter.
This chapter relates to tax rebates for homeowners from
gaming revenue.
Section 602. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Annual gaming dividend." An amount equal to the amount
determined under section 604(e).
"Assessor." The chief assessor of a county, the equivalent
position in a home rule county or the equivalent position in a
city of the third class that performs its own assessments of
real property.
"Department." The Treasury Department of the Commonwealth.

Section 607. Erroneous annual gaming dividends.
(a) Department collection.--If the department determines or
finds an annual gaming dividend to have been incorrectly or
erroneously paid, the department shall determine the correct
amount of the annual gaming dividend and notify the homeowner of
the reason for the correction and the amount of the annual
gaming dividend .
(b) Department recovery.--If an annual gaming dividend has
been issued in error and the homeowner fails to refund the
annual gaming dividend upon the department's request, the annual
gaming dividend shall be recoverable by the Department of
Revenue in the same manner as assessments as provided for in
Chapter 13.
