AN ACT
Amending the act of June 13, 1967 (P.L.31, No.21), entitled "An
act to consolidate, editorially revise, and codify the public
welfare laws of the Commonwealth," in public assistance,
providing for fee schedule rates.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of June 13, 1967 (P.L.31, No.21), known
as the Human Services Code, is amended by adding a section to
read:
Section 443.16. 443.17. Fee Schedule Rates.--(a) Subject to
Federal approval and in addition to 55 Pa. Code § 6100.571
(relating to fee schedule rates), the following applies to the
fee schedule rates for home and community-based services
provided through intellectual disability and autism programs
administered by the Office of Developmental Programs for the
twelve-month period beginning October 1, 2026 2027 , and each
October 1 thereafter:

(1) To the extent funds are appropriated for the purposes
under this section, the fee schedule rates shall be increased by
the percentage change to the Consumer Price Index for ALL URBAN
CONSUMERS FOR the most recently reported twelve-month period.
(2) If the increase to the fee schedule rates under
paragraph (1) will result in the total estimated expenditures
exceeding the funds appropriated for the purpose, the THE
percentage increase to the fee schedule rates shall be limited
by the amount appropriated.
(3) (2) If no change or a decrease occurs in the Consumer
Price Index FOR ALL URBAN CONSUMERS under paragraph (1), the fee
schedule rates for home and community-based services under this
section shall remain unchanged for the year. For any year in
which the Consumer Price Index FOR ALL URBAN CONSUMERS increases
following a year of decrease, the percentage increase shall be
calculated by subtracting the cumulative percentage of any
consecutive preceding years of decrease.
(4) (3) The department shall transmit notice to the
Legislative Reference Bureau for publication in the next
available issue of the Pennsylvania Bulletin providing the
percentage increase to be applied to the fee schedule rates
under this section prior to implementing the adjustment to the
fee schedule rates.

a provider shall increase the total wages COMPENSATION of a

PARAGRAPH (3) no later than January 1 of the applicable fiscal
year of the rate increase.

(C) AS USED IN THIS SECTION, THE FOLLOWING WORDS AND PHRASES

THE CONTEXT CLEARLY INDICATES OTHERWISE:
"DIRECT SUPPORT PROFESSIONAL." A PERSON EMPLOYED OR
CONTRACTED BY A PROVIDER TO RENDER A SERVICE TO AN INDIVIDUAL
RECEIVING A SERVICE THROUGH AN INTELLECTUAL DISABILITY OR AUTISM

"LOWER BOUND WAGE RATE." THE MINIMUM HOURLY COMPENSATION
LEVEL THAT MUST BE PAID FOR A DIRECT SUPPORT PROFESSIONAL
RENDERING A HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICE PROVIDED THROUGH AN
INTELLECTUAL DISABILITY OR AUTISM PROGRAM ADMINISTERED BY THE
OFFICE OF DEVELOPMENTAL PROGRAMS, WITH THE RATE BEING:
(1) DERIVED FROM MARKET-BASED WAGE ASSUMPTIONS USED TO
DEVELOP FEE SCHEDULE RATES.
(2) THE LOWEST POINT IN THE WAGE RANGE ESTABLISHED BY THE
OFFICE OF DEVELOPMENTAL PROGRAMS FOR EACH SERVICE AREA USING THE
UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF LABOR, BUREAU OF LABOR STATISTICS
WAGE DATA FOR COMMENSURATE JOB CLASSIFICATIONS.
"PROVIDER." A PROVIDER OF HOME AND COMMUNITY-BASED SERVICES
PROVIDED THROUGH AN INTELLECTUAL DISABILITY OR AUTISM PROGRAM
ADMINISTERED BY THE OFFICE OF DEVELOPMENTAL PROGRAMS OF THE
DEPARTMENT.
SECTION 2. THIS ACT SHALL TAKE EFFECT IN 120 DAYS.
