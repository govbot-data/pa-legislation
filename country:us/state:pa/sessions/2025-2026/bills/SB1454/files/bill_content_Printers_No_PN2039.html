AN ACT
Amending the act of July 19, 1979 (P.L.130, No.48), entitled "An
act relating to health care; prescribing the powers and
duties of the Department of Health; establishing and
providing the powers and duties of the State Health
Coordinating Council, health systems agencies and Health Care
Policy Board in the Department of Health, and State Health
Facility Hearing Board in the Department of Justice;
providing for certification of need of health care providers
and prescribing penalties," in licensing of health care
facilities, further providing for definitions, for licensure,
for application for license and for reliance on national
accreditation organizations and Federal Government for health
care facilities other than hospitals; and, in general
provisions, repeals and effective date, providing for
Department of Human Services and participating rural
emergency hospitals.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The definition of "hospital" in section 802.1 of
the act of July 19, 1979 (P.L.130, No.48), known as the Health
Care Facilities Act, is amended and the section is amended by
adding definitions to read:
Section 802.1. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter

"Hospital." The term includes:
(1) An institution having an organized medical staff
established for the purpose of providing to inpatients, by or
under the supervision of physicians, diagnostic and therapeutic
services for the care of persons who are injured, disabled,
pregnant, diseased, sick or mentally ill or rehabilitation
services for the rehabilitation of persons who are injured,
disabled, pregnant, diseased, sick or mentally ill. The term
includes facilities for the diagnosis and treatment of disorders
within the scope of specific medical specialties, but not
facilities caring exclusively for the mentally ill.
(2) A rural emergency hospital.
* * *
"Rural area." As defined under 42 U.S.C. § 1395ww(d)(2)(D)
(relating to payments to hospitals for inpatient hospital
services).
"Rural emergency hospital." A facility that:
(1) provides emergency department services 24 hours a
day, seven days a week, in a rural area; and
(2) does not provide any inpatient services.
* * *
