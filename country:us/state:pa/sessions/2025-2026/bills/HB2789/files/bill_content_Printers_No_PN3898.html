AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," in institutions of higher education,
providing for miscellaneous provisions; and abrogating a
regulation.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Article XX-L of the act of March 10, 1949
(P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is
amended by adding a subarticle to read:
SUBARTICLE E
MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
Section 2071-L. Notice for closure of branch or campus.
(a) Notice.--No later than seven days after receipt of an
application from an institution of higher education seeking
approval to discontinue a branch or campus under 22 Pa. Code Ch.
34 (relating to branch campuses for State-supported
institutions) or, if the application was received prior to the

(e) Prohibition.--
(1) Notwithstanding 22 Pa. Code § 34.13, an application
submitted by an institution of higher education seeking
approval to discontinue a branch or campus may not be deemed
approved based on the secretary's failure to act within the
six-month time period.
(2) If the secretary fails to issue a decision within
six months, the institution of higher education may petition
Commonwealth Court for an order compelling the secretary to
issue a decision within 30 days.
(f) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Institution of higher education." An accredited entity
which includes:
(1) A university within the State System of Higher
Education under Article XX-A.
(2) The Pennsylvania State University, the University of
Pittsburgh, Temple University, Lincoln University or any
other institution designated as State-related by the
Commonwealth.
(3) The Thaddeus Stevens College of Technology.
(4) A college established under Article XIX-G.
(5) A community college operating under Article XIX-A.
"Secretary." The Secretary of Education of the Commonwealth.
