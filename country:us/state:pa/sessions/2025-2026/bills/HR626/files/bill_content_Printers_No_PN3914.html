A RESOLUTION
Designating October 23, 2026, as "Hungarian Revolution Memorial
Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, October 23 through November 4, 1956, marks the
period known as the Hungarian Uprising; and
WHEREAS, On October 23, 1956, the people of Hungary rose in a
courageous national uprising against the communist dictatorship
imposed under the influence of the Soviet Union; and
WHEREAS, This revolt represented one of the most significant
challenges to communist rule during the Cold War, demonstrating
the determination of the Hungarian people to live in freedom and
with self-governance, national sovereignty, freedom of speech
and respect for fundamental human rights; and
WHEREAS, The uprising lasted 15 days before Soviet military
forces brutally suppressed the revolution, resulting in
thousands of deaths and injuries, the imprisonment and execution
of political leaders and dissidents and the forced exile of
approximately 200,000 Hungarians; and

WHEREAS, The historical record demonstrates that communist
regimes across Central and Eastern Europe and the world were
responsible for widespread violations of human rights, political
repression, forced labor, imprisonment and deaths of millions of
people; and
WHEREAS, The Hungarian Revolution of 1956 and decades of
communist oppression compelled many Hungarians to flee their
homeland in search of freedom, opportunity and security; and
WHEREAS, Tens of thousands of Hungarian refugees were
welcomed by the United States, which became a beacon of liberty
for those escaping political persecution and authoritarian rule;
and
WHEREAS, Many Hungarian immigrants settled in this
Commonwealth, joining established Hungarian-American communities
in cities and towns across this Commonwealth, including
Pittsburgh, Bethlehem, McKeesport, Johnstown, Ligonier and the
greater Philadelphia region; and
WHEREAS, Hungarian immigrants and their descendants made
significant contributions to this Commonwealth's economic growth
through their work in manufacturing, steel production, coal
mining, skilled trades, small business ownership, engineering,
medicine, education, scientific research and entrepreneurship;
and
WHEREAS, These Hungarian Americans strengthened the civic,
cultural, religious and economic fabric of Pennsylvania while
preserving their heritage and passing on the values of hard
work, faith, family and freedom; and
WHEREAS, The success of Hungarian refugees in this
Commonwealth and throughout the United States stands as a
testament to the generations who transformed the hardships of

WHEREAS, In 1989, Hungary peacefully dismantled its communist
