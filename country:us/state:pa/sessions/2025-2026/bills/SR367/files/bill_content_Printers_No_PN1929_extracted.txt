2025-2026 Regular Session SR 367 PN 1929 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1929 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.367 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PENNYCUICK, ROBINSON, DUSH, MASTRIANO, ROTHMAN, YAW, PICOZZI, BARTOLOTTA, BROWN, STREET, PHILLIPS-HILL, BROOKS, HAYWOOD, J. WARD, MARTIN, CULVER, TARTAGLIONE, GEBHARD, CAPPELLETTI, VOGEL, KANE AND LAUGHLIN, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing September 18, 2026, as "POW/MIA Recognition Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, In accordance with the act of June 11, 1992 (P.L. 301, No.50), each year this Commonwealth observes "Pennsylvania POW/MIA Recognition Day" the third Friday in September; andWHEREAS, Many Americans across the United States pause to remember the sacrifices of individuals who were prisoners of war (POW) and those who are missing in action (MIA); andWHEREAS, More than 80,000 American service personnel are missing from previous conflicts; andWHEREAS, The loved ones of those soldiers, sailors, airmen and marines who did not return home are forced to live with unthinkable pain; andWHEREAS, Federal law requires the POW/MIA flag to be displayed whenever the American flag is displayed on prominent Federal buildings and national war memorials to honor those 12345678910111213141516 Americans who are listed as prisoners of war, missing in action or otherwise unaccounted for from our nation's past wars and conflicts; andWHEREAS, It is crucial that we continue to educate all Pennsylvanians, but especially children, on the sacrifice that these servicemembers made; andWHEREAS, The United States and our Commonwealth owe these individuals a debt that can never be repaid; andWHEREAS, We salute the men and women who, with courage, valor and selflessness, safeguard our country and our ideals; andWHEREAS, We pay solemn tribute and honor the individuals who are listed as POW or MIA, or are otherwise unaccounted for; andWHEREAS, This day of recognition of individuals who are listed as POW or MIA reminds us to support efforts to work to bring every servicemember home, and to uplift their families who are unable to be with their loved one; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize September 18, 2026, as "POW/MIA Recognition Day" in Pennsylvania.20260SR0367PN1929 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718
