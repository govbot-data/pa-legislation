A RESOLUTION
Recognizing September 18, 2026, as "POW/MIA Recognition Day" in
Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, In accordance with the act of June 11, 1992 (P.L.
301, No.50), each year this Commonwealth observes "Pennsylvania
POW/MIA Recognition Day" the third Friday in September; and
WHEREAS, Many Americans across the United States pause to
remember the sacrifices of individuals who were prisoners of war
(POW) and those who are missing in action (MIA); and
WHEREAS, More than 80,000 American service personnel are
missing from previous conflicts; and
WHEREAS, The loved ones of those soldiers, sailors, airmen
and marines who did not return home are forced to live with
unthinkable pain; and
WHEREAS, Federal law requires the POW/MIA flag to be
displayed whenever the American flag is displayed on prominent
Federal buildings and national war memorials to honor those

Americans who are listed as prisoners of war, missing in action
or otherwise unaccounted for from our nation's past wars and
conflicts; and
WHEREAS, It is crucial that we continue to educate all
Pennsylvanians, but especially children, on the sacrifice that
these servicemembers made; and
WHEREAS, The United States and our Commonwealth owe these
individuals a debt that can never be repaid; and
WHEREAS, We salute the men and women who, with courage, valor
and selflessness, safeguard our country and our ideals; and
WHEREAS, We pay solemn tribute and honor the individuals who
are listed as POW or MIA, or are otherwise unaccounted for; and
WHEREAS, This day of recognition of individuals who are
listed as POW or MIA reminds us to support efforts to work to
bring every servicemember home, and to uplift their families who
are unable to be with their loved one; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate recognize September 18, 2026, as
"POW/MIA Recognition Day" in Pennsylvania.
