Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Universal Child Care Task Force Act.Section 2. Legislative findings and intent.The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) It is within the purview of a responsible government to provide for and maintain the conditions necessary to public stability, out of which are born opportunity and prosperity.(2) Opportunity is fair and just access to one forum or another. Opportunity enables the free exercise of choice and facilitates economic competition.(3) Prosperity is an abundance of resources and freedom from want and requires:(i) An exchange of energy, time and talent for fair and just compensation.(ii) Multiple systems working in coordination to protect and preserve the economy of the home.(4) A concentration of wealth that confines resources to a small number of individuals does not strengthen society. Resources create the most value when they circulate, support innovation and contribute to shared prosperity. When resources remain inactive, held only to accumulate more value, they cannot fulfill their broader purpose of advancing the public good.(5) Opportunity and prosperity benefit this Commonwealth as a whole and further contribute to the conditions necessary 20260SB1474PN1954 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 to flourish as a society.(6) If this government fails to act in service to its obligations, the signs and symptoms will manifest in disruptions to even the most crucial services offered in this Commonwealth.(7) For working families across this Commonwealth, child care is a crucial service that allows those with children to pursue opportunity and prosperity.(8) For working families across this Commonwealth, child care services may be difficult to access or entirely unavailable.(9) For working families across this Commonwealth, available child care services are too often too expensive to justify the continued maintenance of multiple income sources within the household. For single-parent homes, this difficulty may be even more pronounced.(10) The resulting effect of this breakdown is deleterious to the economy of the home and to each economy beyond the home, including the community, this Commonwealth and the country.(11) It is the responsibility of the Commonwealth to provide for and maintain the conditions necessary to public stability. Those conditions include a robust, publicly funded child care system that is available, conveniently accessible and free to all Pennsylvanians.(12) The current landscape of child care funding, affordability, availability and demand within this Commonwealth constitutes a market failure. Private markets alone have not adequately resolved these deficiencies, resulting in a persistent gap between the child care needs of 20260SB1474PN1954 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 families and the services accessible to them. This failure threatens the stability and prosperity of households, undermines workforce participation and impedes the economic vitality and competitiveness of this Commonwealth. It is therefore necessary for the government to act, both to support the people of this Commonwealth and to ensure the strength, resilience and long-term growth of its economy.Section 3.
