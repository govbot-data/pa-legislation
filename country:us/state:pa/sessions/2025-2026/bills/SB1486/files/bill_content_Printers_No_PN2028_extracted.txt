Prohibition of certain discriminatory actions by a manufacturer or distributor.(a) Prohibition.--A manufacturer, an agent or affiliate of a manufacturer, or distributor or third-party logistics provider of a manufacturer's drugs, may not, directly or indirectly, deny, restrict, prohibit or otherwise interfere with the acquisition of a 340B drug by, or delivery of a 340B drug to, a 20260SB1486PN2028 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 location authorized by a covered entity to receive the 340B drug, unless receipt of the 340B drug is prohibited by Federal law.(b) Claims and utilization data.--A manufacturer, an agent or affiliate of a manufacturer, or distributor or third-party logistics provider may not, either directly or indirectly, require a covered entity to submit claims or utilization data as a condition for allowing the acquisition of a 340B drug by, or delivery of a 340B drug to, a covered entity unless the claims or utilization data sharing is required by Federal law.Section 4. Reimbursement claims methodology.The department, covered insurer, pharmacy benefit manager or any other entity that administers or manages prescription drug benefits on behalf of the department or a managed care organization, shall rely exclusively on a claims-level methodology involving the use of a nationally recognized billing modifier for identifying drugs purchased under section 340B of the Public Health Service Act when paying for drugs on behalf of Medicaid to ensure that claims for the drugs are removed from the encounter data required under section 1903(m)(2)(A)(xiii) of the Social Security Act (Public Law 74-271, 42 U.S.C. § 1396b(m)(2)(A)(xiii)) and excluded from Medicaid drug rebate requests.Section 5. Sexually transmitted disease clinics.The department shall ensure that:(1) no later than 90 days after the effective date of this paragraph, each sexually transmitted disease clinic shall receive a grant of at least $500 from the department's Federal funding under section 318 of the Public Health Service Act to perform sexually transmitted disease testing and treatment; and20260SB1486PN2028 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (2) all new awards or renewals provided to sexually transmitted disease clinics shall provide for a grant of at least $500 from the department's Federal funding under section 318 of the Public Health Service Act to perform sexually transmitted disease testing and treatment.Section 6. Penalties.(a) Enforcement.--The commission of any act that violates section 3 shall constitute an unlawful practice within the meaning of the act of December 17, 1968 (P.L.1224, No.387), known as the Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law. The Office of Attorney General shall have jurisdiction, consistent with the provisions of the Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law, to enforce the provisions of section 3 and to promulgate regulations implementing the provisions of section 3.(b) Fines.--The Office of Attorney General shall impose, upon written notice, a fine on a manufacturer, wholesale distributor of prescription drugs, third-party logistics provider, or an agent or affiliate of the manufacturer, wholesale distributor of prescription drugs or third party logistics provider, that violates section 3, in an amount of $50,000 per each violation. Each package of 340B drugs determined to be subject to a prohibited act under section 3 shall constitute a separate violation under section 3.(c) Suspension.--The Office of Attorney General shall suspend the ability of a manufacturer, wholesale distributor of prescription drugs, third-party logistics provider, or an agent or affiliate of a manufacturer, wholesale distributor of prescription drugs or third-party logistics provider, to conduct business in this Commonwealth if a violation under section 3 is 20260SB1486PN2028 - 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 not rectified within 30 days of the date when the written notice is sent to the manufacturer, wholesale distributor of prescription drugs, third-party logistics provider, or an agent or affiliate of the manufacturer, wholesale distributor of prescription drugs or third-party logistics provider under section 3(b).(d) Injunctive relief.--A covered entity or contract pharmacy may immediately seek injunctive relief in Commonwealth Court to enforce the provisions of this act.Section 7. Federal preemption.Nothing in this act shall be construed or applied to be in conflict with Federal law and related regulations, including 21 U.S.C. § 355-1 (relating to risk evaluation and mitigation strategies), or other laws of this State, if the State law is compatible with applicable Federal law.Section 8.
