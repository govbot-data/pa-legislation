AN ACT
Providing for prohibitions on discriminatory actions by
manufacturers or distributors of certain drugs and for
reimbursement claims methodology; providing for grants for
sexually transmitted disease clinics; imposing duties on the
Department of Human Services and Office of Attorney General;
and imposing penalties.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the 340B Contract
Pharmacy Protection Act.

prices of drugs purchased by covered entities).

Section 3. Prohibition of certain discriminatory actions by a
manufacturer or distributor.
(a) Prohibition.--A manufacturer, an agent or affiliate of a
manufacturer, or distributor or third-party logistics provider
of a manufacturer's drugs, may not, directly or indirectly,
deny, restrict, prohibit or otherwise interfere with the
acquisition of a 340B drug by, or delivery of a 340B drug to, a

(b) Claims and utilization data.--A manufacturer, an agent
or affiliate of a manufacturer, or distributor or third-party
logistics provider may not, either directly or indirectly,
require a covered entity to submit claims or utilization data as
a condition for allowing the acquisition of a 340B drug by, or
delivery of a 340B drug to, a covered entity unless the claims
or utilization data sharing is required by Federal law.
Section 4. Reimbursement claims methodology.
The department, covered insurer, pharmacy benefit manager or
any other entity that administers or manages prescription drug
benefits on behalf of the department or a managed care
organization, shall rely exclusively on a claims-level
methodology involving the use of a nationally recognized billing
modifier for identifying drugs purchased under section 340B of
the Public Health Service Act when paying for drugs on behalf of
Medicaid to ensure that claims for the drugs are removed from
the encounter data required under section 1903(m)(2)(A)(xiii) of
the Social Security Act (Public Law 74-271, 42 U.S.C. § 1396b(m)
(2)(A)(xiii)) and excluded from Medicaid drug rebate requests.

this paragraph, each sexually transmitted disease clinic
shall receive a grant of at least $500 from the department's
Federal funding under section 318 of the Public Health
Service Act to perform sexually transmitted disease testing

Section 6. Penalties.
(a) Enforcement.--The commission of any act that violates
section 3 shall constitute an unlawful practice within the
meaning of the act of December 17, 1968 (P.L.1224, No.387),
known as the Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law.
The Office of Attorney General shall have jurisdiction,
consistent with the provisions of the Unfair Trade Practices and
Consumer Protection Law, to enforce the provisions of section 3
and to promulgate regulations implementing the provisions of
section 3.
(b) Fines.--The Office of Attorney General shall impose,
upon written notice, a fine on a manufacturer, wholesale
distributor of prescription drugs, third-party logistics
provider, or an agent or affiliate of the manufacturer,
wholesale distributor of prescription drugs or third party
logistics provider, that violates section 3, in an amount of
$50,000 per each violation. Each package of 340B drugs
determined to be subject to a prohibited act under section 3
shall constitute a separate violation under section 3.
(c) Suspension.--The Office of Attorney General shall
suspend the ability of a manufacturer, wholesale distributor of
prescription drugs, third-party logistics provider, or an agent
or affiliate of a manufacturer, wholesale distributor of
prescription drugs or third-party logistics provider, to conduct
business in this Commonwealth if a violation under section 3 is

Section 7. Federal preemption.
Nothing in this act shall be construed or applied to be in
conflict with Federal law and related regulations, including 21
U.S.C. § 355-1 (relating to risk evaluation and mitigation
strategies), or other laws of this State, if the State law is
compatible with applicable Federal law.
Section 8. Effective date.
This act shall take effect immediately.
