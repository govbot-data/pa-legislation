AN ACT
Designating rye whiskey as the official State spirit of the
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Findings and declarations.
The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:
(1) Rye whiskey has a long history in Pennsylvania and
traces back to the late 1700s when European settlers
introduced rye as a hardy grain suitable for the region's
climate.
(2) Farmers converted surplus rye into whiskey, which
became both an agricultural necessity and an economic
stabilizer as whiskey was more valuable, easier to transport
and served as a form of currency on the frontier.
(3) This Commonwealth's rye industry played a central
role in the Whiskey Rebellion, a major test of Federal
authority, demonstrating the spirit's significance in the

(4) Today distilleries of this Commonwealth continue
this long craft history by utilizing heritage rye varieties
and traditional production methods to create quality rye
whiskey distinct to this Commonwealth.
(5) Distilling rye whiskey has meaningful agricultural
benefits as it strengthens family farms through encouraging
greater rye cultivation.
(6) Rye whiskey and the spirit industry bring economic
growth through manufacturing jobs, distribution and tourism.
(7) The historical and economic significance of rye
whiskey over the history of this Commonwealth align well with
the deep agricultural roots and unique past of this
Commonwealth.
Section 2. Official State spirit.
Rye whiskey is designated as the official State spirit of the
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
Section 3. Effective date.
This act shall take effect immediately.
