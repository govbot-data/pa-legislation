Findings and declarations.The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) Rye whiskey has a long history in Pennsylvania and traces back to the late 1700s when European settlers introduced rye as a hardy grain suitable for the region's climate.(2) Farmers converted surplus rye into whiskey, which became both an agricultural necessity and an economic stabilizer as whiskey was more valuable, easier to transport and served as a form of currency on the frontier.(3) This Commonwealth's rye industry played a central role in the Whiskey Rebellion, a major test of Federal authority, demonstrating the spirit's significance in the 1234567891011121314151617 establishment of early American identity.(4) Today distilleries of this Commonwealth continue this long craft history by utilizing heritage rye varieties and traditional production methods to create quality rye whiskey distinct to this Commonwealth.(5) Distilling rye whiskey has meaningful agricultural benefits as it strengthens family farms through encouraging greater rye cultivation.(6) Rye whiskey and the spirit industry bring economic growth through manufacturing jobs, distribution and tourism.(7) The historical and economic significance of rye whiskey over the history of this Commonwealth align well with the deep agricultural roots and unique past of this Commonwealth.Section 2. Official State spirit.Rye whiskey is designated as the official State spirit of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.Section 3. Effective date.This act shall take effect immediately.20260HB2757PN3852 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819
