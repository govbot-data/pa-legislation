AN ACT
Amending Title 23 (Domestic Relations) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in child protective services, further
providing for exclusions from child abuse and for persons
required to report suspected child abuse.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:

Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:

apply:

(e) Additional reporting.--A mandated reporter enumerated in
subsection (a) shall make a report via the Statewide toll-free
telephone number under section 6332 (relating to establishment
of Statewide toll-free telephone number) or by electronic
submission under section 6305 (relating to electronic reporting)
if the mandated reporter has reasonable cause to suspect the
existence of child sexual abuse material or artificially
generated child sexual abuse material as those terms are defined
in 18 Pa.C.S. § 6312(g) (relating to sexual abuse of children).
This subsection shall apply whether or not the material was
created, displayed or exhibited to others by a minor. Upon
receipt of a report under this subsection, the department shall
transmit a copy of the report to the appropriate law enforcement
agency and may transmit a copy of the report to the appropriate
county agency.

Section 3. This act shall take effect in one year.
(E) ADDITIONAL REPORTING.--
(1) A MANDATED REPORTER ENUMERATED IN SUBSECTION (A)
SHALL MAKE A REPORT VIA THE STATEWIDE TOLL-FREE TELEPHONE
NUMBER UNDER SECTION 6332 (RELATING TO ESTABLISHMENT OF
STATEWIDE TOLL-FREE TELEPHONE NUMBER) OR BY ELECTRONIC
SUBMISSION UNDER SECTION 6305 (RELATING TO ELECTRONIC
REPORTING) IF THE MANDATED REPORTER HAS REASONABLE CAUSE TO
SUSPECT:
(I) THE EXISTENCE OF CHILD SEXUAL ABUSE MATERIAL OR
ARTIFICIALLY GENERATED CHILD SEXUAL ABUSE MATERIAL,
REGARDLESS OF WHETHER THE MATERIAL WAS CREATED, DISPLAYED
OR EXHIBITED TO OTHERS BY A MINOR; OR
(II) THAT HAZING OR AGGRAVATED HAZING HAS OCCURRED.
(2) UPON RECEIPT OF A REPORT UNDER THIS SUBSECTION, THE
DEPARTMENT SHALL TRANSMIT A COPY OF THE REPORT TO THE
APPROPRIATE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCY AND MAY TRANSMIT A COPY OF
THE REPORT TO THE APPROPRIATE COUNTY AGENCY.
(3) THE FOLLOWING WORDS AND PHRASES WHEN USED IN THIS
SUBSECTION SHALL HAVE THE MEANINGS GIVEN TO THEM IN THIS
PARAGRAPH UNLESS THE CONTEXT CLEARLY INDICATES OTHERWISE:
"AGGRAVATED HAZING." AS DEFINED IN 18 PA.C.S. § 2803(A)
(RELATING TO AGGRAVATED HAZING).
"ARTIFICIALLY GENERATED CHILD SEXUAL ABUSE MATERIAL." AS
DEFINED IN 18 PA.C.S. § 6312(G) (RELATING TO SEXUAL ABUSE OF
CHILDREN).
"CHILD SEXUAL ABUSE MATERIAL." AS DEFINED IN 18 PA.C.S.
§ 6312(G).
"HAZING." AS DEFINED IN 18 PA.C.S. § 2802(A) (RELATING
TO HAZING).
