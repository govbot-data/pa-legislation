Program establishment and department duties.(a) Program.--The National Board Certified Teacher High-Need District Pilot Grant Program is established within the department to provide grants in accordance with this article.(b) Goal.--The goal of the program is to increase the number of NBCT teachers employed in high-need schools.(c) Duties of department.--The department shall:(1) Identify high-need schools in this Commonwealth.(2) Identify the number of NBCT teachers currently teaching in this Commonwealth disaggregated by school entity.(3) Identify the retention rate of teachers in high-need schools.(4) Award grants in accordance with this article and in a manner to ensure geographic diversity across this Commonwealth.(d) Priority.--The department shall give priority to an application from an eligible applicant that:(1) Demonstrates existing local supports for NBCT teachers.(2) Provides matching funds to supplement a grant from the program.(3) Provides a plan to maintain candidate support beyond the grant period.(4) Represents a consortium of small or rural districts.Section 1504-O. Eligibility.(a) Eligibility.--(1) To be eligible for a grant under the program, the school entity must be identified as a high-need school.(2) To maintain eligibility in the program, the school entity must increase the number of NBCT teachers in each year of the program.(b) Use of consortia.--Two or more eligible applicants may apply jointly and shall designate a fiscal agent for purposes of the grant.Section 1506-O. Allowable uses of funds.(a) Term.--The term of a grant shall be for five years.(b) Required uses.--A grant shall be used for:(1) Payment of fees associated with obtaining initial certification of an NBCT candidate.(2) Development and operation of candidate support programs, which may include:(i) Stipends for NBCT candidate mentors or professional learning facilitators.(ii) Program coordination and administrative costs.(iii) Candidate support materials and meeting costs.(c) Permissive uses.--If grant money remains following use under subsection (b), grant funds may be used for:(1) Payment of NBCT teacher maintenance of certification fees.(2) Stipends or salary supplements for NBCT teachers who remain employed in a high-need school.20260HB2805PN3936 - 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (3) Promotion and outreach activities to recruit NBCT candidates, which may include:(i) Development and distribution of informational materials.(ii) Hosting informational sessions or workshops.(iii) Costs of marketing campaigns or communications to encourage teacher participation.(4) Contracting with an institution of higher education, nonprofit organization or other qualified partner to design, coordinate or deliver candidate support programming on behalf of the grantee.(d) Limitation.--No more than 10% of the grant may be used for the uses under subsection (c).Section 1507-O. Reporting.(a) Annual report.--Within 180 days of the conclusion of the program, the department shall submit an evaluation and recommendations regarding continuation, expansion or modification to the program by considering the data collected under subsection (b).(b) Data collection.--The department shall collect the following from school entities that participate in the program:(1) The number of NBCT candidates supported by grants in each year of the program.(2) Certification completion rates of NBCT candidates.(3) Data on student achievement in the school entities where a grant was given.(4) Data on teacher retention and vacancy rates in the school entities where a grant was given.Section 1508-O.
