AN ACT
Amending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in Public Utility Commission, further
providing for consumer protection and information.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 308.1(b) of Title 66 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:
§ 308.1. Consumer protection and information.
* * *
(b) Rate comparison report.--
(1) Annually, by April 15, the commission shall submit a
report to the Governor and to the General Assembly. The
report shall compare all categories of ratepayers for all
electric [and], gas, water and waste water public utilities
so that reasonably accurate comparisons of rates can be made
between similar individuals or groups of ratepayers receiving
services in different service areas.
