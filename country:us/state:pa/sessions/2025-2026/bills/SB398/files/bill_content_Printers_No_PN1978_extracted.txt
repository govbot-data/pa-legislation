The act of December 19, 1988 (P.L.1262, No.156), known as the Local Option Small Games of Chance Act, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 309. Online raffles by fire companies.(a) Online raffles AUTHORIZATION .--A fire company that has a valid small games of chance license issued under section 307 may conduct raffles over the Internet subject to the following: (1) An individual may purchase a ticket for the raffles only by means of a debit or credit card.<--<--1234567891011121314151617181920 (2) The fire company may not conduct more than two raffles per year under this section. INTERNET. AN INDIVIDUAL MAY PURCHASE A TICKET FOR THE RAFFLES ONLY BY MEANS OF A DEBIT OR CREDIT CARD.(b) Expiration.--This section shall expire December 31, 2026.(B) METHODS.--(1) NOTWITHSTANDING ANY OTHER PROVISIONS OF LAW, A FIRE COMPANY MAY ACCEPT PAYMENT VIA CREDIT CARD OR DEBIT CARD FOR THE PURCHASE OF A RAFFLE TICKET, PROVIDED THAT THE ORGANIZATION:(I) EMPLOYS A METHOD TO VERIFY THAT THE INDIVIDUAL WHO BUYS A RAFFLE TICKET IS LOCATED WITHIN THIS COMMONWEALTH AT THE TIME OF SALE. THE ORGANIZATION MAY NOT ACCEPT A PAYMENT FROM AN INDIVIDUAL WHO IS LOCATED OUTSIDE THIS COMMONWEALTH AT THE TIME OF SALE.(II) EMPLOYS A METHOD TO VERIFY THAT THE INDIVIDUAL WHO BUYS A RAFFLE TICKET IS AT LEAST 18 YEARS OF AGE. THE ORGANIZATION MAY NOT ACCEPT A PAYMENT FROM AN INDIVIDUAL UNDER 18 YEARS OF AGE.(III) DOES NOT VIOLATE THE PROVISIONS OF 31 U.S.C. CH. 53 SUBCH. IV (RELATING TO PROHIBITION ON FUNDING OF UNLAWFUL INTERNET GAMBLING).(C) EXPIRATION.--THIS SECTION SHALL EXPIRE DECEMBER 31, 2027.Section 2.
