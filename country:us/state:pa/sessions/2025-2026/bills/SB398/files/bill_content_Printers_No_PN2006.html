AN ACT
Amending the act of December 19, 1988 (P.L.1262, No.156),
entitled "An act providing for the licensing of eligible
organizations to conduct games of chance, for the licensing
of persons to distribute games of chance, for the
registration of manufacturers of games of chance, and for
suspensions and revocations of licenses and permits;
requiring records; providing for local referendum by
electorate; and prescribing penalties," in games of chance,
FURTHER PROVIDING FOR PRIZE LIMITS AND providing for online
raffles by fire companies.; AND, IN CLUB LICENSEES, FURTHER
PROVIDING FOR DISTRIBUTION OF PROCEEDS.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of December 19, 1988 (P.L.1262, No.156),
known as the Local Option Small Games of Chance Act, is amended
by adding a section to read:
SECTION 1. SECTION 302(B) OF THE ACT OF DECEMBER 19, 1988
(P.L.1262, NO.156), KNOWN AS THE LOCAL OPTION SMALL GAMES OF
CHANCE ACT, IS AMENDED TO READ:
SECTION 302. PRIZE LIMITS.

(B) AGGREGATE PRIZE LIMIT.--NO MORE THAN [$35,000] $60,000
IN PRIZES SHALL BE AWARDED FROM GAMES OF CHANCE BY A LICENSED
ELIGIBLE ORGANIZATION IN ANY SEVEN-DAY PERIOD.
* * *
SECTION 2. THE ACT IS AMENDED BY ADDING A SECTION TO READ:
Section 309. Online raffles by fire companies.
(a) Authorization .--A fire company that has a valid small
games of chance license issued under section 307 may conduct
raffles over the Internet. An individual may purchase a ticket
for the raffles only by means of a debit or credit card.
(b) Methods.--
(1) Notwithstanding any other provisions of law, a fire
company may accept payment via credit card or debit card for
the purchase of a raffle ticket, provided that the
organization:
(i) Employs a method to verify that the individual
who buys a raffle ticket is located within this
Commonwealth at the time of sale. The organization may
not accept a payment from an individual who is located
outside this Commonwealth at the time of sale.
(ii) Employs a method to verify that the individual
who buys a raffle ticket is at least 18 years of age. The
organization may not accept a payment from an individual
under 18 years of age.
(iii) Does not violate the provisions of 31 U.S.C.
Ch. 53 Subch. IV (relating to prohibition on funding of
unlawful Internet gambling).
(c) Expiration.--This section shall expire December 31,
2027.
Section 2 3. This act shall take effect immediately.

SECTION 3. SECTION 502(A) OF THE ACT IS AMENDED TO READ:
SECTION 502. DISTRIBUTION OF PROCEEDS.
(A) DISTRIBUTION.--THE PROCEEDS FROM GAMES OF CHANCE
RECEIVED BY A CLUB LICENSEE SHALL BE DISTRIBUTED AS FOLLOWS:
(1) NO LESS THAN [60%] 50% OF THE PROCEEDS SHALL BE PAID
FOR PUBLIC INTEREST PURPOSES WITHIN ONE YEAR OF THE END OF
THE CALENDAR YEAR IN WHICH THE PROCEEDS WERE OBTAINED.
(2) NO MORE THAN [40%] 50% OF THE PROCEEDS OBTAINED IN A
CALENDAR YEAR MAY BE RETAINED BY A CLUB LICENSEE.
* * *
SECTION 4. THE AMENDMENT OF SECTION 502(A) OF THE ACT SHALL
APPLY TO CALENDAR YEARS COMMENCING AFTER DECEMBER 31, 2025.
SECTION 5. THIS ACT SHALL TAKE EFFECT AS FOLLOWS:
(1) THE AMENDMENT OF SECTIONS 302(B) AND 502(A) OF THE
ACT SHALL TAKE EFFECT IN 60 DAYS.
(2) THE REMAINDER OF THIS ACT SHALL TAKE EFFECT
IMMEDIATELY.
