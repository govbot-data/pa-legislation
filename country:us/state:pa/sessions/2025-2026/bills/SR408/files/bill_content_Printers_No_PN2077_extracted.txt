WARD, OCTOBER 27, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, OCTOBER 27, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing November 17, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Veterans Day of Caring" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Veterans of the United States Armed Forces served with honor, courage and selflessness; andWHEREAS, Veterans Day originated as Armistice Day in 1919 to commemorate the end of World War I and has since evolved to honor all who served in the United States Armed Forces; andWHEREAS, Every year, the United States and this Commonwealth recognize November 11 as Veterans Day, honoring the courage and sacrifice of the men and women who served in the United States Armed Forces; andWHEREAS, Many residents of this Commonwealth, regardless of their veteran status, participate in parades, wreath-laying ceremonies and community events to commemorate Veterans Day; andWHEREAS, There are many opportunities for a resident of this Commonwealth to continue to support veterans all year, not just on Veterans Day; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, This Commonwealth is home to more than 700,000 veterans, including veterans who have served in times of war and peace, across all branches of the armed forces; andWHEREAS, Veterans across this Commonwealth continue to strengthen communities in civilian life by contributing leadership, service and expertise through their roles as small business owners, employees and volunteers; andWHEREAS, Residents of this Commonwealth are encouraged to support veterans through volunteering, helping with household chores or spending the day talking to a veteran about his or her service to the United States; andWHEREAS, Acts of service and community engagement strengthen bonds between veterans and civilians, fostering greater appreciation, understanding and unity throughout this Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, Encouraging residents of this Commonwealth to patronize veteran-owned businesses and engage with community initiatives led by veterans is important; andWHEREAS, Recognizing November 17, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Veterans Day of Caring" encourages a day dedicated to performing helpful and kind acts for this Commonwealth's veterans; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize November 17, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Veterans Day of Caring" in Pennsylvania;
