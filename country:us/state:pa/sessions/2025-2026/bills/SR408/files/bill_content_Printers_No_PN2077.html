A RESOLUTION
Recognizing November 17, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Veterans Day of
Caring" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Veterans of the United States Armed Forces served
with honor, courage and selflessness; and
WHEREAS, Veterans Day originated as Armistice Day in 1919 to
commemorate the end of World War I and has since evolved to
honor all who served in the United States Armed Forces; and
WHEREAS, Every year, the United States and this Commonwealth
recognize November 11 as Veterans Day, honoring the courage and
sacrifice of the men and women who served in the United States
Armed Forces; and
WHEREAS, Many residents of this Commonwealth, regardless of
their veteran status, participate in parades, wreath-laying
ceremonies and community events to commemorate Veterans Day; and
WHEREAS, There are many opportunities for a resident of this
Commonwealth to continue to support veterans all year, not just
on Veterans Day; and

WHEREAS, This Commonwealth is home to more than 700,000
veterans, including veterans who have served in times of war and
peace, across all branches of the armed forces; and
WHEREAS, Veterans across this Commonwealth continue to
strengthen communities in civilian life by contributing
leadership, service and expertise through their roles as small
business owners, employees and volunteers; and
WHEREAS, Residents of this Commonwealth are encouraged to
support veterans through volunteering, helping with household
chores or spending the day talking to a veteran about his or her
service to the United States; and
WHEREAS, Acts of service and community engagement strengthen
bonds between veterans and civilians, fostering greater
appreciation, understanding and unity throughout this
Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, Encouraging residents of this Commonwealth to
patronize veteran-owned businesses and engage with community
initiatives led by veterans is important; and
WHEREAS, Recognizing November 17, 2026, as "Pennsylvania
Veterans Day of Caring" encourages a day dedicated to performing
helpful and kind acts for this Commonwealth's veterans;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate recognize November 17, 2026, as
"Pennsylvania Veterans Day of Caring" in Pennsylvania; and be it
further
RESOLVED, That the Senate encourage residents of this
Commonwealth to continue to support this Commonwealth's veterans
through meaningful acts of kindness, community involvement and
sustained engagement.
