§ 7001. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Automated license plate reader system."
(1) A system of one or more mobile or fixed high-speed
cameras combined with computer algorithms to convert images
of registration plates into computer-readable data.
(2) The term does not include a system used in
electronic toll collection.
(3) The term does not include an automated red light
enforcement system as defined in section 102 (relating to
definitions) and used under sections 3116 (relating to
automated red light enforcement systems in first class
cities) and 3117 (relating to automated red light enforcement
systems in certain municipalities).
(4) The term does not include any other automated
enforcement system authorized by this title.
"Captured data." The global positioning system coordinates,
dates and times, photographs, registration plate numbers and any
other data collected by or derived from an automated license
plate reader system.
"Government entity." An office, agency, board, bureau,
department, commission, political subdivision or authority
established by statute, or a private entity under contract with
an office, agency, board, bureau, department, commission,
political subdivision or authority established by statute, to
carry out a government function.
"Private entity." A person, entity, group or organization
that is not the Federal Government, the Commonwealth or a

§ 7002. Use of automated license plate reader systems.
(a) Authorized purposes.--
(1) A State, county or local law enforcement agency may
only use an automated license plate reader system for
legitimate law enforcement purposes, conducting criminal
investigations or ensuring compliance with Federal, State and
local laws.
(2) In addition to the purposes under paragraph (1), a
government entity may use an automated license plate reader
system for the purpose of:
(i) Enforcing State and local parking laws.
(ii) Controlling access to a secured area.
(b) Prohibition.--Except as authorized under subsection (a),
the use of an automated license plate reader system is
prohibited.
(c) Manual entries.--Any State, county or local law
enforcement agency or government entity that manually enters
license plate numbers into an automated license plate reader
system must document the reason for the entry to ensure use for
a legitimate law enforcement purpose , conducting criminal
investigations or ensuring compliance with Federal, State and
local laws.
(d) Training required.--
(1) Prior to using an automated license plate reader
system for a purpose identified in subsection (a)(1), an
individual shall complete a training course approved by the

systems.--An individual who knowingly or intentionally violates

(b) Other violations.--An individual who knowingly or
intentionally violates section 7002(c), (d), (e) or (f) commits
a misdemeanor of the third degree.

IT IS UNLAWFUL FOR ANY GOVERNMENT ENTITY OR PRIVATE ENTITY TO

RECOGNIZED AS A FELONY UNDER THE LAWS OF THIS
COMMONWEALTH.

§ 7003. APPLICABILITY OF RIGHT-TO-KNOW.
(A) GENERAL RULE.--EXCEPT AS PROVIDED IN SUBSECTION (B),
CAPTURED DATA COLLECTED OR RETAINED THROUGH THE USE OF AN
AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEM SHALL NOT BE ACCESSIBLE
UNDER THE ACT OF FEBRUARY 14, 2008 (P.L.6, NO.3), KNOWN AS THE
RIGHT-TO-KNOW LAW.
(B) PUBLIC RECORD.--NOTWITHSTANDING SUBSECTION (A), AN
AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEM ' S AUDIT, USE AND ACCESS
LOGS, INCLUDING RECORDS OF THE REASON FOR THE QUERY IN TERMS OF
THE USES AUTHORIZED UNDER THIS CHAPTER, TOGETHER WITH
INFORMATION IDENTIFYING THE USER AND THE RELEVANT CASE,
INVESTIGATION OR OTHER ENFORCEMENT MATTER, IN CONNECTION WITH
WHICH THE QUERY WAS MADE, SHALL BE SUBJECT TO ACCESS UNDER THE
RIGHT-TO-KNOW LAW. IN RESPONDING TO A VALID REQUEST FOR ACCESS
UNDER CHAPTER 7 OF THE RIGHT-TO-KNOW LAW FOR INFORMATION
ACCESSIBLE UNDER THIS SUBSECTION, PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE
INFORMATION OF ANY INDIVIDUAL ASSOCIATED WITH CAPTURED DATA,
INDIVIDUAL LICENSE PLATE INFORMATION OR OTHER VEHICLE-SPECIFIC
IDENTIFYING INFORMATION, SUCH AS VEHICLE PHOTOGRAPHS AND
INFORMATION REGARDING A VEHICLE ' S MAKE, MODEL, OR COLOR, SHALL
BE DEEMED INACCESSIBLE AND SHALL BE REDACTED FROM THE RESPONSIVE
DOCUMENTS AND DATA PURSUANT TO SECTION 706 OF THE RIGHT-TO-KNOW
LAW.

§ 7005. AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEM POLICY.
THE PENNSYLVANIA STATE POLICE SHALL DEVELOP A POLICY RELATED
TO THE USE OF AN AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEM WHICH
INCLUDES THE BASIC PROTOCOL, GUIDELINES OR WRITTEN POLICIES
GOVERNING THE IMPLEMENTATION, USE, MAINTENANCE AND STORAGE OF
AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEMS AND CAPTURED DATA WHICH A
GOVERNMENT ENTITY OR PRIVATE ENTITY MUST ADOPT PRIOR TO USING
AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEMS OR CAPTURED DATA. THE
BASIC PROTOCOL, GUIDELINES OR WRITTEN POLICIES MUST INCLUDE, AT
A MINIMUM, THE FOLLOWING:
(1) AN AUDIT PROCESS TO ENSURE THAT INFORMATION OBTAINED
THROUGH THE USE OF AN AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEM
IS USED ONLY AS AUTHORIZED IN SECTION 7002(B) (RELATING TO
RESTRICTIONS ON THE USE OF AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER
SYSTEMS), INCLUDING AUDITS OF REQUESTS MADE BY GOVERNMENT
ENTITIES OR PRIVATE ENTITIES.
(2) PROCEDURES AND SAFEGUARDS TO ENSURE THAT PERSONS
WITH ACCESS TO THE AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEM AND
CAPTURED DATA ARE ADEQUATELY SCREENED AND TRAINED.
(3) A COPY OF THE TRAINING COURSE USED UNDER SECTION
7002(E)(2), IF APPLICABLE.
(4) HUMAN VERIFICATION OF THE ACCURACY OF CAPTURED DATA.
(5) MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS FOR THE SECURE STORAGE OF
CAPTURED DATA INCLUDING END-TO-END ENCRYPTION.

§ 7008. PENALTIES.
(A) PROHIBITED USE OF AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER
SYSTEMS.--AN INDIVIDUAL WHO KNOWINGLY OR INTENTIONALLY VIOLATES
SECTION 7002(A) (RELATING TO RESTRICTIONS ON THE USE OF
AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEMS) COMMITS A MISDEMEANOR OF
THE SECOND DEGREE.
(B) OTHER VIOLATIONS.--AN INDIVIDUAL WHO KNOWINGLY OR
INTENTIONALLY VIOLATES SECTION 7002(D), (E), (F) OR (G) COMMITS
A MISDEMEANOR OF THE THIRD DEGREE.
(C) LIABILITY.--ANY GOVERNMENT ENTITY, PRIVATE ENTITY OR
OTHER PERSON WHO VIOLATES THE PROVISIONS OF THIS CHAPTER SHALL
BE LIABLE FOR CIVIL DAMAGES TO A PERSON CLAIMING THAT A
VIOLATION OF THIS CHAPTER HAS INJURED THE BUSINESS, PERSON OR
REPUTATION OF THAT PERSON. A PERSON SO INJURED SHALL BE ENTITLED
TO ACTUAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING MENTAL PAIN AND SUFFERING ENDURED
ON ACCOUNT OF VIOLATION OF THE PROVISIONS OF THIS ACT, OR
LIQUIDATED DAMAGES OF $1,000, AND A REASONABLE ATTORNEY FEE AND
OTHER COSTS OF LITIGATION, WHICHEVER IS LESS.
