THE BASIC PROTOCOL, GUIDELINES OR WRITTEN POLICIES MUST INCLUDE, AT A MINIMUM, THE FOLLOWING:(1) AN AUDIT PROCESS TO ENSURE THAT INFORMATION OBTAINED THROUGH THE USE OF AN AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEM IS USED ONLY AS AUTHORIZED IN SECTION 7002(B) (RELATING TO RESTRICTIONS ON THE USE OF AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEMS), INCLUDING AUDITS OF REQUESTS MADE BY GOVERNMENT ENTITIES OR PRIVATE ENTITIES.(2) PROCEDURES AND SAFEGUARDS TO ENSURE THAT PERSONS WITH ACCESS TO THE AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEM AND CAPTURED DATA ARE ADEQUATELY SCREENED AND TRAINED.(3) A COPY OF THE TRAINING COURSE USED UNDER SECTION 7002(E)(2), IF APPLICABLE.(4) HUMAN VERIFICATION OF THE ACCURACY OF CAPTURED DATA.(5) MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS FOR THE SECURE STORAGE OF CAPTURED DATA INCLUDING END-TO-END ENCRYPTION.§ 7006. PENALTIES.(A) PROHIBITED USE OF AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEMS.--AN INDIVIDUAL WHO KNOWINGLY OR INTENTIONALLY VIOLATES SECTION 7002(A) (RELATING TO RESTRICTIONS ON THE USE OF AUTOMATED LICENSE PLATE READER SYSTEMS) COMMITS A MISDEMEANOR OF THE SECOND DEGREE.(B) OTHER VIOLATIONS.--AN INDIVIDUAL WHO KNOWINGLY OR INTENTIONALLY VIOLATES SECTION 7002(D), (E), (F) OR (G) COMMITS A MISDEMEANOR OF THE THIRD DEGREE.(C) LIABILITY.--ANY GOVERNMENT ENTITY, PRIVATE ENTITY OR OTHER PERSON WHO VIOLATES THE PROVISIONS OF THIS CHAPTER SHALL BE LIABLE FOR CIVIL DAMAGES TO A PERSON CLAIMING THAT A VIOLATION OF THIS CHAPTER HAS INJURED THE BUSINESS, PERSON OR REPUTATION OF THAT PERSON. A PERSON SO INJURED SHALL BE ENTITLED TO ACTUAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING MENTAL PAIN AND SUFFERING ENDURED ON ACCOUNT OF VIOLATION OF THE PROVISIONS OF THIS ACT, OR LIQUIDATED DAMAGES OF $1,000, AND A REASONABLE ATTORNEY FEE AND OTHER COSTS OF LITIGATION, WHICHEVER IS LESS.§ 7009.
