A RESOLUTION
Recognizing October 20, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Career
Development Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, This Commonwealth's economic future depends on a
workforce that is informed, prepared and connected to career
opportunities before graduation, yet too many students lack
awareness of the careers available to them across this
Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, Employers across this Commonwealth continue to face
persistent workforce shortages, while students report
uncertainty and lack of direction about their postsecondary
pathways, creating a growing disconnect between education and
workforce needs; and
WHEREAS, The Department of Education has established Career
Education and Work Standards requiring career awareness and
preparation, yet schools often lack the capacity to deliver
consistent, high-quality, real-world, career-connected learning
experiences at scale; and

WHEREAS, Workforce development begins with awareness, and
students cannot dream about careers they do not know about,
making early and ongoing career awareness essential to informed
decision making and long-term success; and
WHEREAS, Data from the Partnership for Career Development
demonstrates the measurable impact of structured, real-world,
career-connected learning, including that:
(1) 95% of students reported reduced anxiety through a
better understanding of "what comes next";
(2) 89% of students reported increased confidence in
their future; and
(3) 82% of students gained awareness of work-based
learning and apprenticeship opportunities;
and
WHEREAS, These outcomes demonstrate that, when students are
given access to authentic, multi-industry career experiences,
they are better equipped to pursue in-demand careers, avoid
costly postsecondary misalignment and contribute to this
Commonwealth's workforce; and
WHEREAS, Intermediary organizations, such as the Partnership
for Career Development, play a critical role in bridging
education and industry by delivering structured, high-quality
career awareness and preparation experiences that extend the
capacity of schools and align with workforce needs; and
WHEREAS, The work of intermediary organizations has received
bipartisan recognition and support from members of the General
Assembly, including Representatives Justin Fleming, Thomas Kutz
and Nate Davidson and Senators Greg Rothman and Patty Kim; and
WHEREAS, The demand for connections continues to grow across
urban, suburban and rural communities, underscoring the urgency

WHEREAS, Failure to act will result in continued workforce
gaps, lost economic opportunity and generations of students
entering adulthood without clear direction or connection to

RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize October
20, 2026, as "Pennsylvania Career Development Day" in
Pennsylvania; and be it further
RESOLVED, That this day serve as a call to action for
policymakers, educators, employers and community leaders to
prioritize and strengthen career awareness and preparation as
foundational to this Commonwealth's workforce strategy; and be
it further
RESOLVED, That the General Assembly encourage the expansion
of partnerships that connect students to real-world career
opportunities and support systems that prepare students for
success in this Commonwealth's evolving economy; and be it
further
RESOLVED, That the General Assembly reaffirm its commitment
to ensuring that every student in this Commonwealth has access
to meaningful career awareness and preparation experiences
because students cannot dream about careers they do not know
about.
