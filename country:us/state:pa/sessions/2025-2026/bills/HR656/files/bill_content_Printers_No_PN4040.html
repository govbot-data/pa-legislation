A RESOLUTION
Designating October 27, 2026, as "Fraternal Benefit Societies
Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The fraternal benefit societies of Pennsylvania are
longstanding mutual aid organizations created to serve the needs
of communities and provide for the payment of life, health,
accident and other benefits to their members; and
WHEREAS, Fraternal benefit societies represent a successful,
modern-day model under which individuals come together with a
common purpose to collectively provide charitable and other
beneficial activities for society; and
WHEREAS, Fraternal benefit societies operate under a chapter
system, creating a Statewide infrastructure, combined with local
energy and knowledge, which positions fraternal benefit
societies to most efficiently address unmet needs in
communities, many of which the government cannot address; and
WHEREAS, The fraternal benefit society model represents a
large member-volunteer network in Pennsylvania, with

approximately 500,000 people belonging to local chapters across

WHEREAS, The value of volunteer hours to Pennsylvania is
estimated at $64,951,538 through charitable giving, educational

capital that strengthens the fabric, safety and quality of life
in thousands of local communities; and
WHEREAS, In Pennsylvania, fraternal benefit societies provide
more than $17 billion in life insurance, protecting Pennsylvania
families from the unexpected and providing financial peace of
mind; and
WHEREAS, The efforts of fraternal benefit societies to help
Pennsylvanians save money and be financially secure relieves
pressure on government safety net programs; and
WHEREAS, Fraternal benefit societies are private sector
economic and social support systems that help meet needs that
would otherwise go unmet; and
WHEREAS, We recognize that fraternal benefit societies have
served their original purpose for more than a century, helping
countless individuals, families and communities through
fraternal member activities; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate October
27, 2026, as "Fraternal Benefit Societies Day" in Pennsylvania.
