2025-2026 Regular Session HR 656 PN 4040 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 4040 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.656 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY HADDOCK, BRENNAN, HARKINS, RIVERA, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, GALLAGHER, KHAN, NEILSON AND SANCHEZ, OCTOBER 13, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON INTERGOVERNMENTAL AFFAIRS AND OPERATIONS, OCTOBER 14, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDesignating October 27, 2026, as "Fraternal Benefit Societies Day" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, The fraternal benefit societies of Pennsylvania are longstanding mutual aid organizations created to serve the needs of communities and provide for the payment of life, health, accident and other benefits to their members; andWHEREAS, Fraternal benefit societies represent a successful, modern-day model under which individuals come together with a common purpose to collectively provide charitable and other beneficial activities for society; andWHEREAS, Fraternal benefit societies operate under a chapter system, creating a Statewide infrastructure, combined with local energy and knowledge, which positions fraternal benefit societies to most efficiently address unmet needs in communities, many of which the government cannot address; andWHEREAS, The fraternal benefit society model represents a large member-volunteer network in Pennsylvania, with 1234567891011121314151617 approximately 500,000 people belonging to local chapters across the State; andWHEREAS, The value of volunteer hours to Pennsylvania is estimated at $64,951,538 through charitable giving, educational programs and volunteer activities, as well as important social capital that strengthens the fabric, safety and quality of life in thousands of local communities; andWHEREAS, In Pennsylvania, fraternal benefit societies provide more than $17 billion in life insurance, protecting Pennsylvania families from the unexpected and providing financial peace of mind; andWHEREAS, The efforts of fraternal benefit societies to help Pennsylvanians save money and be financially secure relieves pressure on government safety net programs; andWHEREAS, Fraternal benefit societies are private sector economic and social support systems that help meet needs that would otherwise go unmet; andWHEREAS, We recognize that fraternal benefit societies have served their original purpose for more than a century, helping countless individuals, families and communities through fraternal member activities;
