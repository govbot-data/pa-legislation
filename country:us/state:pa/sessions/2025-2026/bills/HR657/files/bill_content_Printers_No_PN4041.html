INTRODUCED BY CARROLL, WAXMAN AND SANCHEZ, OCTOBER 14, 2026
REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON APPROPRIATIONS, OCTOBER 14, 2026
A RESOLUTION
Designating November 18, 2026, as "Kada Scott Day" in
Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Born in Philadelphia on November 18, 2001, Kada
Yanda Scott was the daughter of Kevin Scott and Kim Matthews;
and
WHEREAS, Ms. Scott attended Eastern University Academy
Charter School and The Pennsylvania State University; and
WHEREAS, Ms. Scott's creativity and passion for design led
her to establish her own clothing brand, K-Kouture; and
WHEREAS, A competitor in the Miss Pennsylvania Pageant, Ms.
Scott showcased her confidence and creativity through her
YouTube videos and other social media outlets; and
WHEREAS, A seeker of peace, balance and growth, Ms. Scott
enjoyed journaling, traveling and spending time in nature; and
WHEREAS, Ms. Scott was known for her contagious laughter,
bright smile, talent and ability to make everyone feel seen and
valued; and
WHEREAS, Ms. Scott was a beloved daughter, sister,

WHEREAS, Ms. Scott's inspiring presence and genuine love for

WHEREAS, Kada Yanda Scott passed away at the age of 23 in

WHEREAS, Ms. Scott's spirit lives on through the work of the
Kada Scott Foundation, which empowers the young women of
Philadelphia through scholarships, creative business micro
grants, mentorship and community programs; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate
November 18, 2026, which would have been her 25th birthday, as
"Kada Scott Day" in Pennsylvania; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives extend heartfelt
condolences to Ms. Scott's father, Kevin Scott, mother, Kim
Matthews, sister, Kinsey Scott, grandparents, May Matthews,
Cornelius Thomas and Joseph Pearson, and many other family
members and friends; and be it further
RESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to
the family of Kada Yanda Scott.
