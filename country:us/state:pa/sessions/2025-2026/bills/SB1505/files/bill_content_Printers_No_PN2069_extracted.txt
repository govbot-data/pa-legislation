The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 1323-B. P rohibition of artificial intelligence for school-based mental health and counseling.(a) Prohibition.--Except as otherwise provided in this section, a school entity may not utilize artificial intelligence, artificial intelligence chatbots or generative artificial intelligence, including synthetic content, to provide a service or experience to a student that would constitute the practice of a school-based mental health professional.1234567891011121314151617181920 (b) Certain limitations on contract.-- An artificial intelligence supplier shall not enter into a contract with a school entity to provide an artificial intelligence system, artificial intelligence technology or any generative artificial intelligence, including synthetic content, that is programmed to provide a service or experience to a student that would constitute the practice of a school-based mental health professional.(c) Prohibited uses.--A school entity may not use or provide the use of an artificial intelligence mental health system to:(1) Diagnose the mental health condition of a student.(2) Provide psychological or behavioral therapy or counseling to a student.(3) Provide individualized mental health treatment or therapeutic intervention to a student.(4) Determine or recommend an individualized mental health diagnosis or treatment plan to a student.(5) Represent, expressly or by implication, that the artificial intelligence system is a school psychologist, school counselor or school social worker.(6) Represent, expressly or by implication, that the artificial intelligence system can substitute for a school psychologist, school counselor or school social worker.(7) Provide a service to a student that would constitute mental health therapy, psychological therapy or individualized mental health treatment.(d) Construction.--Nothing in this section shall be construed to:(1) prohibit a school-based mental health professional employed by a school entity from using artificial 20260SB1505PN2069 -
