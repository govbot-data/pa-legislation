AN ACT
Amending Titles 30 (Fish) and 34 (Game) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in Pennsylvania Fish and Boat
Commission, further providing for organization of commission;
and, in Pennsylvania Game Commission, further providing for
meetings of commission.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 301(e) of Title 30 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:
§ 301. Organization of commission.
* * *
(e) Meetings, officers and quorum.--
(1) The commission shall have an office in the
Harrisburg area and shall hold meetings in the Harrisburg
area in January and July and at such other times and places
within this Commonwealth as the commission shall designate
for the transaction of business.
(2) At the meeting held in July of each year, the
commission shall elect one of its members as president and

(3) Six members shall constitute a quorum.

Section 2. Section 321(a) of Title 34 is amended to read:
§ 321. Meetings of commission.
(a) [General rule] Frequency, location and manner.--
(1) The commission shall have an office in or near
Harrisburg and shall annually hold meetings at such office in
January and June or July and at such other times and places
within this Commonwealth as the commission shall designate
for the transaction of business.
(2) All meetings of the commission shall comply with
[the act of July 19, 1974 (P.L.486, No.175), referred to as
the Public Agency Open Meeting Law.] 65 Pa.C.S. Ch. 7
(relating to open meetings) and shall be live-streamed and
video recorded in accordance with 65 Pa.C.S. Ch. 7. Video
recordings of the meetings must be made available to the
public free of charge through:
(i) the publicly accessible Internet website of the
commission; or
