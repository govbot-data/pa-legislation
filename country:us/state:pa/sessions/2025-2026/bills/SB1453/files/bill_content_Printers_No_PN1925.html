AN ACT
Amending the act of July 19, 1979 (P.L.130, No.48), entitled "An
act relating to health care; prescribing the powers and
duties of the Department of Health; establishing and
providing the powers and duties of the State Health
Coordinating Council, health systems agencies and Health Care
Policy Board in the Department of Health, and State Health
Facility Hearing Board in the Department of Justice;
providing for certification of need of health care providers
and prescribing penalties," in licensing of health care
facilities, further providing for ambulatory surgical
facility permitted surgical procedures and providing for
ambulatory surgical facility attestation for new services;
and abrogating a regulation.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 822(c) and (d) of the act of July 19,
1979 (P.L.130, No.48), known as the Health Care Facilities Act,
are amended to read:

procedures.

Section 2. The act is amended by adding a section to read:
Section 823. Ambulatory surgical facility attestation for new
services.
(a) Submission of attestation.--
(1) An ambulatory surgical facility that seeks to
implement a new service shall submit a written attestation to
the director of the division in the department responsible
for licensure of ambulatory surgical facilities at least 60
days before the intended commencement date of the new
service.
(2) An ambulatory surgical facility may submit a single

(b) Contents of attestation.--An ambulatory surgical
facility submitting an attestation under subsection (a) shall
attest that, for each new service, all of the following
conditions have been satisfied:
(1) The ambulatory surgical facility has provided to the
department a narrative description of the new service that
includes the scope of care, purpose and function of the new
service, staffing requirements and qualifications and
equipment required for the new service.
(2) The ambulatory surgical facility has developed
policies and procedures necessary for the new service.
(3) The ambulatory surgical facility has appropriately
credentialed physicians and staff who will provide the new
service, oriented the physicians and staff to the policies
and procedures for the new service and placed documentation
of credentialing in the applicable credential file.
(4) The ambulatory surgical facility has updated staff
job descriptions, if necessary, and placed documentation of
the updated job descriptions in the applicable employee
files.
(5) If approval is required by the ambulatory surgical
facility's policies or governing documents, the governing
body of the ambulatory surgical facility has approved the new
service and the ambulatory surgical facility has documented
the approval in the governing body's minutes.
(6) If the new service involves radiologic equipment,
the ambulatory surgical facility has obtained all applicable
certifications and physicist's reports.

(8) The attestation identifies the intended commencement

(c) Documentation.--
(1) An ambulatory surgical facility shall maintain
documentation sufficient to demonstrate compliance with each
condition attested to under subsection (b).
(2) An ambulatory surgical facility shall make
documentation maintained under paragraph (1) available to the
department upon request or during a survey or inspection of
the ambulatory surgical facility.
(d) Effect of attestation.--
(1) Submission of an attestation under this section
shall satisfy the notification requirements of 28 Pa. Code §
51.3(a) and (h) (relating to notification) for the new
service identified in the attestation.
(2) Notwithstanding 28 Pa. Code § 51.3(l), an ambulatory
surgical facility may implement the new service on or after
the intended commencement date stated in the attestation if
the ambulatory surgical facility has satisfied the
requirements of this section.
(3) Nothing in this section shall be construed to
satisfy or otherwise alter a requirement under 28 Pa. Code §
51.3(d) or (h) that applies to proposed new construction,
alteration or renovation of the ambulatory surgical facility.

construed to limit the authority of the department under section

Section 3. The provisions of 28 Pa. Code § 51.3(l) (relating
to notification) are abrogated insofar as they are inconsistent
with the addition of section 823 of the act.
Section 4. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
