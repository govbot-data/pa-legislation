The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 1205.9. Use of Artificial Intelligence in Education.--(a) A school entity may provide a school employe with training on the use of artificial intelligence in education. The following apply:(1) Training shall address, but shall not be limited to:(i) The uses and limitations of artificial intelligence for instructional programming.(ii) The identification, evaluation and verification of artificial intelligence-generated information and content.1234567891011121314151617181920 (iii) Academic integrity, assessment and appropriate student use of artificial intelligence.(iv) Student privacy, personally identifiable information, data security and cybersecurity risks associated with artificial intelligence.(v) Algorithmic bias, discrimination, accessibility, transparency and ethical considerations.(vi) The school entity's policies regarding the use of artificial intelligence in education.(2) A school employe required to undergo continuing professional education under section 1205.2 or 1205.5 shall receive credit toward the school employe's continuing professional education requirements if the training program has been approved by the department.(3) The school entity shall make a reasonable effort to facilitate a time and location for school employes to participate in the training during paid working hours or in-service training.(b) To the extent funding is made available, the department shall provide grants to school entities to aid with the costs associated with providing training on the use of artificial intelligence in education in accordance with this section. All of the following:(1) A machine-based system that can, for a given set of human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations or decisions influencing real or virtual environments, including the ability to:(i) perceive real and virtual environments;(ii) abstract perceptions made under this paragraph into AI models through analysis in an automated manner; and(iii) use model inference to formulate options for information or action based on outcomes under subparagraphs (i) and (ii).(2) The term includes generative artificial intelligence."Artificial intelligence-generated information and content."
