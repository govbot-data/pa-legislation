AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," in certification of teachers,
providing for use of artificial intelligence in education.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known
as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a
section to read:
Section 1205.9. Use of Artificial Intelligence in
Education.--(a) A school entity may provide a school employe
with training on the use of artificial intelligence in
education. The following apply:
(1) Training shall address, but shall not be limited to:
(i) The uses and limitations of artificial intelligence for
instructional programming.
(ii) The identification, evaluation and verification of
artificial intelligence-generated information and content.

(iii) Academic integrity, assessment and appropriate student
use of artificial intelligence.
(iv) Student privacy, personally identifiable information,
data security and cybersecurity risks associated with artificial
intelligence.
(v) Algorithmic bias, discrimination, accessibility,
transparency and ethical considerations.
(vi) The school entity's policies regarding the use of
artificial intelligence in education.
(2) A school employe required to undergo continuing
professional education under section 1205.2 or 1205.5 shall
receive credit toward the school employe's continuing
professional education requirements if the training program has
been approved by the department.
(3) The school entity shall make a reasonable effort to
facilitate a time and location for school employes to
participate in the training during paid working hours or in-
service training.
(b) To the extent funding is made available, the department
shall provide grants to school entities to aid with the costs
associated with providing training on the use of artificial
intelligence in education in accordance with this section. The
following apply:
(1) The department shall establish an application process.
(2) The department shall issue guidelines, including, but
not limited to, eligible uses of grant funds and minimum and
maximum grant awards.
(3) A school entity may use funds awarded to contract with
institutions of higher education, intermediate units, nonprofit
organizations or other providers of professional development

(4) For each fiscal year grant funding is provided, the
department shall publish a report on its publicly accessible
Internet website that includes the following information:
(i) Award amounts by recipient.
(ii) The use of grant awards by recipient, including any

(c) As used in this section, the following words and phrases
shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless
the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Artificial intelligence." All of the following:
(1) A machine-based system that can, for a given set of
human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations or
decisions influencing real or virtual environments, including
the ability to:
(i) perceive real and virtual environments;
(ii) abstract perceptions made under this paragraph into AI
models through analysis in an automated manner; and
(iii) use model inference to formulate options for
information or action based on outcomes under subparagraphs (i)
and (ii).
(2) The term includes generative artificial intelligence.
"Artificial intelligence-generated information and content."
Text, code, images, audio or video data that has been created or
modified from its original source data through the use of
artificial intelligence technology, programs or platforms.

Commonwealth.
"Generative artificial intelligence." The class of
artificial intelligence models that emulate the structure and
characteristics of input data in order to generate synthetic

"Institution of higher education." As defined under section
2001-H.
"School employe." The term shall have the meaning given to
the term "professional employe" under section 1101(1).
"School entity." A school district, charter school, cyber
charter school, regional charter school, intermediate unit or
area career and technical school.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
