ALPR contracts.An ALPR contract between a law enforcement agency and a vendor for the purchase, lease, operation, maintenance or support of an ALPR system must comply with this chapter and must include the following provisions:(1) The vendor shall comply with this chapter.(2) ALPR data and audit trail data are the exclusive property of the law enforcement agency.(3) The vendor shall provide written notice to the law enforcement agency at least 60 days before any merger, acquisition, transfer of ownership or other transaction that would result in a foreign adversary obtaining a direct or indirect ownership or controlling interest in the vendor.(4) The law enforcement agency may terminate the contract without penalty if the vendor becomes owned or controlled by a foreign adversary.(5) The vendor shall purge ALPR data collected or generated in this Commonwealth upon expiration of the retention period.(6) The vendor shall cooperate with an audit conducted under section 7505 (relating to compliance audits) and shall provide the law enforcement agency and the Pennsylvania State Police with the records, systems and other information reasonably necessary to complete the audit.§ 7504. Vendor maintenance access.(a) Express authorization required.--A vendor may access an ALPR system or ALPR data for maintenance, repair, software updates or technical support only upon the express authorization of the law enforcement agency that entered into the ALPR contract with the vendor.(b) Limitation on access.--(1) Access by a vendor, as described in subsection (a), shall be limited to the period reasonably necessary to complete the authorized work and shall terminate immediately upon completion of the work.(2) A vendor may not have permanent, unrestricted or continuous remote access to an ALPR system or ALPR data.(c) Audit trail.--Access by a vendor to ALPR data shall be recorded in an audit trail.§ 7507. Data security.(a) Data storage and encryption.--ALPR data shall be:(1) Hosted, processed and stored only on physical hardware and server infrastructure located within the geographical boundaries of the United States.(2) If hosted, processed or stored in a cloud-computing environment, maintained in an environment that complies with the Criminal Justice Information Services Security Policy of the Federal Bureau of Investigation.(3) Maintained in a system that complies with applicable requirements of the Commonwealth Law Enforcement Assistance Network.(4) Encrypted at rest and in transit.(b) Vendor security safeguards.--A vendor shall establish, implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect ALPR data against unauthorized access, acquisition, use, modification, disclosure, destruction and loss.(c) Commonwealth safeguards.--The Pennsylvania State Police shall promulgate rules and regulations governing ALPR data collected or maintained by or on behalf of a law enforcement agency.
