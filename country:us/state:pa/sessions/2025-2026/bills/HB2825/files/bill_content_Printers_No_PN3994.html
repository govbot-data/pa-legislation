§ 7501. Scope of chapter.
This chapter relates to automated license plate reader
systems.
§ 7502. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"ALPR contract." A contract made in accordance with section
7503 (relating to ALPR contracts).
"ALPR data." Information that is captured, generated,
produced or derived through the operation of an ALPR system,
including:
(1) An image of a license plate or motor vehicle.
(2) The characters appearing on a license plate.
(3) The date, time or geographic location at which an
image was captured, including global positioning system
coordinates.
(4) The make, model, color or other identifying
characteristics of a motor vehicle.
"ALPR system." As follows:
(1) A system of one or more ALPRs.
(2) The term includes equipment used to collect, access,
store, process or analyze ALPR data.
(3) The term does not include the following:
(i) Camera systems authorized under 75 Pa.C.S. §
3116 (relating to automated red light enforcement systems
in first class cities).

"Automated license plate reader" or "ALPR." A high-speed
camera used in combination with computer algorithms to convert
images of license plates or vehicles into computer-readable
data.
"Foreign adversary." Either of the following:
(1) A foreign government or foreign nongovernment person
that is designated as a foreign adversary under 15 CFR 791.4
(relating to determination of foreign adversaries).
(2) A covered nation under 10 U.S.C. § 4872(f)(2)
(relating to acquisition of sensitive materials from non-
allied foreign nations: prohibition).
"Law enforcement agency." Any of the following:
(1) A police department of a city, borough, incorporated
town or township.
(2) A regional police department.
(3) The Pennsylvania State Police.
(4) Campus police, as defined in section 302 of the act
of November 29, 2004 (P.L.1383, No.180), known as the Uniform
Crime Reporting Act.
"Motor vehicle." As defined in 75 Pa.C.S. § 102 (relating to
definitions).
"Retention period." The period, not exceeding six months
after the date on which ALPR data is collected, during which a
vendor may possess ALPR data. The term does not include a period
during which ALPR data is preserved in accordance with section
7504(d)(2) (relating to data).

enforcement agency:
(1) manufactures, installs, operates or maintains an

§ 7503. ALPR contracts.
An ALPR contract between a law enforcement agency and a
vendor for the purchase, lease, operation, maintenance or
support of an ALPR system must comply with this chapter and must
include the following provisions:
(1) The vendor shall comply with this chapter.
(2) ALPR data and audit trail data are the exclusive
property of the law enforcement agency.
(3) The vendor shall provide written notice to the law
enforcement agency at least 60 days before any merger,
acquisition, transfer of ownership or other transaction that
would result in a foreign adversary obtaining a direct or
indirect ownership or controlling interest in the vendor.
(4) The law enforcement agency may terminate the
contract without penalty if the vendor becomes owned or
controlled by a foreign adversary.
(5) The vendor shall purge ALPR data collected or
generated in this Commonwealth upon expiration of the
retention period.
(6) The vendor shall cooperate with an audit conducted
under section 7505 (relating to compliance audits) and shall
provide the law enforcement agency and the Pennsylvania State
Police with the records, systems and other information
reasonably necessary to complete the audit.

§ 7505. Compliance audits.
(a) Authorization.--A law enforcement agency that operates,
maintains, accesses or uses an ALPR system or ALPR data shall
annually conduct, or engage an independent entity to conduct, an
audit to verify the law enforcement agency's compliance with
this chapter.

§ 7506. Vendor maintenance access.
(a) Express authorization required.--A vendor may access an
ALPR system or ALPR data for maintenance, repair, software
updates or technical support only upon the express authorization
of the law enforcement agency that entered into the ALPR
contract with the vendor.
(b) Limitation on access.--
(1) Access by a vendor, as described in subsection (a),
shall be limited to the period reasonably necessary to
complete the authorized work and shall terminate immediately
upon completion of the work.
(2) A vendor may not have permanent, unrestricted or
continuous remote access to an ALPR system or ALPR data.
(c) Audit trail.--Access by a vendor to ALPR data shall be
recorded in an audit trail.

(ii) is lawfully sought by a law enforcement agency
in connection with a criminal investigation; or
(iii) escaped from custody.

§ 7508. Data security.
(a) Data storage and encryption.--ALPR data shall be:
(1) Hosted, processed and stored only on physical
hardware and server infrastructure located within the
geographical boundaries of the United States.
(2) If hosted, processed or stored in a cloud-computing
environment, maintained in an environment that complies with
the Criminal Justice Information Services Security Policy of
the Federal Bureau of Investigation.
(3) Maintained in a system that complies with applicable
requirements of the Commonwealth Law Enforcement Assistance
Network.
(4) Encrypted at rest and in transit.
(b) Vendor security safeguards.--A vendor shall establish,
implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical and
physical safeguards to protect ALPR data against unauthorized
access, acquisition, use, modification, disclosure, destruction
and loss.
(c) Commonwealth safeguards.--The Pennsylvania State Police
shall promulgate rules and regulations governing ALPR data
collected or maintained by or on behalf of a law enforcement
agency. The rules and regulations shall prescribe the manner and

(d) Security incidents.--A vendor shall immediately notify

§ 7509. Public record and access.
ALPR data and audit trail records do not constitute a public
record and are exempt from access under the act of February 14,
2008 (P.L.6, No.3), known as the Right-to-Know Law.
§ 7510. Enforcement and penalties.
(a) Enforcement.--The Attorney General may bring an action
in the name of the Commonwealth against a vendor or authorized
user that willfully violates this chapter.
(b) Relief.--In an action under this section, a court may:
(1) Temporarily or permanently enjoin a violation of
this chapter.
(2) Require the return, deletion or secure destruction
of ALPR data.
(3) Assess a civil penalty not exceeding $25,000 for
each violation.
(4) Award the Commonwealth reasonable investigation and
litigation costs.
