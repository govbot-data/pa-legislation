AN ACT
Providing for licensure requirements for certain licensed
professionals.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as Marnie's Law.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Certified registered nurse practitioner." An individual
licensed as a certified registered nurse practitioner as defined
under the act of May 22, 1951 (P.L.317, No.69), known as The
Professional Nursing Law.
"Licensing board." The State Board of Medicine, State Board
of Nursing or State Board of Osteopathic Medicine, as

"Medical professional." A certified registered nurse
practitioner, midwife, physician or physician assistant.
"Midwife." An individual licensed as a nurse-midwife or
certified midwife under the act of December 20, 1985 (P.L.457,
No.112), known as the Medical Practice Act of 1985.
"Physician." An individual licensed as a physician under the
Medical Practice Act of 1985 or the act of October 5, 1978
(P.L.1109, No.261), known as the Osteopathic Medical Practice
Act.
"Physician assistant." An individual licensed as a physician
assistant under the Medical Practice Act of 1985 or the
Osteopathic Medical Practice Act.
Section 3. Requirement for licensure.
(a) Requirements generally.--Each licensing board with
jurisdiction over professional licensees identified as medical
professionals under this act shall:
(1) Require all individuals applying for a license or
certification as a medical professional to submit
documentation acceptable to the licensing board of the
completion of a training course on inflammatory breast
cancer.
(2) Require all individuals applying for the renewal of
a license or certification as a medical professional to
submit documentation acceptable to the licensing board of
completion of a training course on inflammatory breast
cancer.
(b) One-time only.--A licensing board shall only require
completion of a training course under subsection (a) one time.

(c) No cost.--The training requirement under this section

Section 4. Approval of course.
A licensing board shall approve any course that:
(1) Includes information about the risk factors for
inflammatory breast cancer.
(2) Includes information about the symptoms of
inflammatory breast cancer, including those specific to
inflammatory breast cancer.
(3) Includes information about why inflammatory breast
cancer is often misdiagnosed and the risks of a delayed
diagnosis.
(4) Includes information about the treatments for
inflammatory breast cancer.
(5) Includes an electronic certificate issued to an
individual who completes the course.
(6) Is offered at no cost to participants.
(7) Satisfies other requirements as determined by a
licensing board.
Section 5. Powers of licensing boards.
A licensing board shall have the power to adopt regulations
necessary to carry out the purpose of this act.
Section 6. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 180 days.
