2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1469 PN 1940 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1940 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1469 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PENNYCUICK, VOGEL AND CULVER, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO CONSUMER PROTECTION AND PROFESSIONAL LICENSURE, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in rates and distribution systems, providing for large load hyperscale data center tariffs.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1302.1. Large load hyperscale data center tariffs.(a) Development of tariffs.--The commission shall promulgate regulations to develop a large load tariff for hyperscale data centers and require each electric distribution company subject to the commission's jurisdiction to institute the large load tariff for hyperscale data centers. The large load tariff for hyperscale data centers shall adhere to cost-causation and at a minimum shall:(1) Establish a separate rate class for hyperscale data centers.(2) Account for full costs of service and ensure that 123456789101112131415161718 the cost is not shifted to the general body of ratepayers, including connection, transmission and any other infrastructure costs.(3) Account for operations and maintenance expenses.(4) Account for other costs required to serve a hyperscale data center customer.(b) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Data center." A facility or group of facilities, including an ancillary use or associated structure located on the same tract or parcel of land, that:(1) is predominantly used to house working servers or similar data storage systems; and(2) has an uninterruptible energy supply or generator backup power, or both, cooling systems, towers and other temperature control infrastructure."Hyperscale data center." A data center that has a peak load equal to or above 25 megawatts individually or 50 megawatts in the aggregate.Section 2. This act shall take effect immediately.20260SB1469PN1940 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021
