AN ACT
Amending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in rates and distribution systems,
providing for large load hyperscale data center tariffs.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1302.1. Large load hyperscale data center tariffs.
(a) Development of tariffs.--The commission shall promulgate
regulations to develop a large load tariff for hyperscale data
centers and require each electric distribution company subject
to the commission's jurisdiction to institute the large load
tariff for hyperscale data centers. The large load tariff for
hyperscale data centers shall adhere to cost-causation and at a
minimum shall:
(1) Establish a separate rate class for hyperscale data
centers.
(2) Account for full costs of service and ensure that

the cost is not shifted to the general body of ratepayers,

(3) Account for operations and maintenance expenses.
(4) Account for other costs required to serve a
hyperscale data center customer.
(b) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Data center." A facility or group of facilities, including
an ancillary use or associated structure located on the same
tract or parcel of land, that:
(1) is predominantly used to house working servers or
similar data storage systems; and
(2) has an uninterruptible energy supply or generator
backup power, or both, cooling systems, towers and other
temperature control infrastructure.
"Hyperscale data center." A data center that has a peak load
equal to or above 25 megawatts individually or 50 megawatts in
the aggregate.
Section 2. This act shall take effect immediately.
