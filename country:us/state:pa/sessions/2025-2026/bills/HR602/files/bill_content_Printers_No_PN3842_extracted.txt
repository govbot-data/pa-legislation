andWHEREAS, Advances in medical research expanded the understanding of sleep-related infant deaths beyond SIDS to include other sleep-related causes, such as accidental suffocation and strangulation in bed (ASSB), leading to broader evidence-based safe sleep recommendations aimed at reducing Sudden Unexpected Infant Death (SUID); andWHEREAS, Organizations such as the Pennsylvania-based Cribs for Kids have played a nationally recognized role in advancing evidence-based infant safe sleep education and promoting October as "Infant Safe Sleep and SIDS Awareness Month," helping shift public awareness toward preventing sleep-related infant deaths; 1234567891011121314151617 andWHEREAS, Sleep-related infant deaths remain among the leading causes of death for infants; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Department of Health's 2025 Child Death Review Annual Report found that unsafe sleep factors were present in 86.8% of reviewed SUID cases, underscoring the continued need for public education and awareness; andWHEREAS, The same report found that more than half of reviewed SUID deaths occurred in an adult bed rather than in a crib, bassinet or other recommended infant sleep space; andWHEREAS, The Commonwealth has taken important steps to protect infants through Act 73 of 2010, which requires hospitals, birth centers and birth providers to provide parents and caregivers with evidence-based infant safe sleep education before newborns are discharged; andWHEREAS, The Pennsylvania Department of Health recommends that infants be placed alone, on their backs, in a crib, bassinet or play yard with a firm mattress and fitted sheet, free of blankets, pillows, toys and other soft objects, for every sleep; andWHEREAS, Increasing awareness of safe sleep recommendations through education and outreach can help reduce preventable infant deaths and provide parents and caregivers with the information needed to create safe sleep environments for infants;
