Sweeney, Jr.WHEREAS, The Honorable John James Sweeney, Jr., former Pennsylvania State Senator, age 99, of Haverford, peacefully passed into eternal life on October 2, 2026; andWHEREAS, Senator Sweeney's father passed when he was nine years of age, and he honored his father by caring for his mother and sisters; andWHEREAS, The challenges Senator Sweeney faced as he grew up laid the foundation for his future success; andWHEREAS, As a young man, Senator Sweeney worked local jobs and attended St. Gregory's Grade School and St. Thomas More High School, graduated early to serve in the United States Army and Reserves, was stationed in occupied Japan and achieved the rank of Staff Sergeant; andWHEREAS, Throughout his career, Senator Sweeney served in many roles, most notably as a Pennsylvania State Senator representing the 26th District from 1974 to 1978, being the 123456789101112131415161718 first Democrat to be elected to this position; andWHEREAS, Senator Sweeney worked as a Federal Government administrator and analyst, retiring after 30 years; andWHEREAS, Senator Sweeney was also a vice president of a mortgage company; andWHEREAS, Senator Sweeney was a member of the Pennsylvania Public Television Network Commission and the Irish-American representative to the Pennsylvania Heritage Affairs Commission; andWHEREAS, In his community, Senator Sweeney served as a communion minister and taught CCD at St. Andrew the Apostle Catholic Church in Drexel Hill; andWHEREAS, Senator Sweeney served as the chairman of the Upper Darby Youth Panel and volunteered as an Ombudsman for the Delaware County Office of Services for the Aging; andWHEREAS, Senator Sweeney was very proud of achieving the status of a Fourth Degree Knight of Columbus, as well as receiving the honorary title of Kentucky Colonel; andWHEREAS, Senator Sweeney was always ready to lend a hand, give a hug, wipe a tear or just listen; andWHEREAS, Senator Sweeney's seat at the table may now be empty, but it will always be filled with the important lessons he taught, the love he gave and wonderful memories left behind; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate honor the life and extend condolences to the family of the Honorable John J.
