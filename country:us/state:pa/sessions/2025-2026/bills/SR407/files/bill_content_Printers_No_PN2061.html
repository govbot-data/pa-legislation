A RESOLUTION
Honoring the life and extending condolences to the family of the
Honorable John J. Sweeney, Jr.
WHEREAS, The Honorable John James Sweeney, Jr., former
Pennsylvania State Senator, age 99, of Haverford, peacefully
passed into eternal life on October 2, 2026; and
WHEREAS, Senator Sweeney's father passed when he was nine
years of age, and he honored his father by caring for his mother
and sisters; and
WHEREAS, The challenges Senator Sweeney faced as he grew up
laid the foundation for his future success; and
WHEREAS, As a young man, Senator Sweeney worked local jobs
and attended St. Gregory's Grade School and St. Thomas More High
School, graduated early to serve in the United States Army and
Reserves, was stationed in occupied Japan and achieved the rank
of Staff Sergeant; and
WHEREAS, Throughout his career, Senator Sweeney served in
many roles, most notably as a Pennsylvania State Senator
representing the 26th District from 1974 to 1978, being the

first Democrat to be elected to this position; and
WHEREAS, Senator Sweeney worked as a Federal Government
administrator and analyst, retiring after 30 years; and
WHEREAS, Senator Sweeney was also a vice president of a
mortgage company; and
WHEREAS, Senator Sweeney was a member of the Pennsylvania
Public Television Network Commission and the Irish-American
representative to the Pennsylvania Heritage Affairs Commission;
and
WHEREAS, In his community, Senator Sweeney served as a
communion minister and taught CCD at St. Andrew the Apostle
Catholic Church in Drexel Hill; and
WHEREAS, Senator Sweeney served as the chairman of the Upper
Darby Youth Panel and volunteered as an Ombudsman for the
Delaware County Office of Services for the Aging; and
WHEREAS, Senator Sweeney was very proud of achieving the
status of a Fourth Degree Knight of Columbus, as well as
receiving the honorary title of Kentucky Colonel; and
WHEREAS, Senator Sweeney was always ready to lend a hand,
give a hug, wipe a tear or just listen; and
WHEREAS, Senator Sweeney's seat at the table may now be
empty, but it will always be filled with the important lessons
he taught, the love he gave and wonderful memories left behind;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate honor the life and extend
condolences to the family of the Honorable John J. Sweeney, Jr.
