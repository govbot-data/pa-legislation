2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1493 PN 2038 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2038 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1493 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BAKER, TARTAGLIONE AND PENNYCUICK, OCTOBER 13, 2026 REFERRED TO CONSUMER PROTECTION AND PROFESSIONAL LICENSURE, OCTOBER 13, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in services and facilities, providing for electricity outage responses.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1512.1. Electricity outage responses.(a) Plans.--Following an electrical outage, each public utility company supplying electric service shall prioritize restoration of service to any facility or entity providing for the health and safety of individuals in a 24-hour care setting.(b) Penalties.--A public utility that fails to comply with subsection (a) may be subject to an enforcement action by the commission.Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.12345678910111213141516
