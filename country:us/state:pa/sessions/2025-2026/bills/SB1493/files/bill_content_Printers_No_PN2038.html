AN ACT
Amending Title 66 (Public Utilities) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in services and facilities, providing
for electricity outage responses.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 66 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1512.1. Electricity outage responses.
(a) Plans.--Following an electrical outage, each public
utility company supplying electric service shall prioritize
restoration of service to any facility or entity providing for
the health and safety of individuals in a 24-hour care setting.
(b) Penalties.--A public utility that fails to comply with
subsection (a) may be subject to an enforcement action by the
commission.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
