Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Safety Net Nondiscrimination Act.Section 2. Discriminatory pharmacy reimbursement prohibited.(a) Payment.--Neither a pharmacy benefit manager nor a covered insurer that reimburses a covered entity or a contract pharmacy for drugs under a contract, or any agent acting on behalf of a pharmacy benefit manager or covered insurer, shall pay the covered entity or contract pharmacy less than:(1) the national average drug acquisition cost as described in 42 U.S.C. § 1396r-8(f) (relating to payment for covered outpatient drugs) for 340B drugs; or(2) the wholesale acquisition cost as specified in 42 U.S.C. § 1395w-3a(c)(6)(B) (relating to use of average sales price payment methodology) for 340B drugs that do not have a defined national average drug acquisition cost.(b) Waiver.--Notwithstanding subsection (a), a covered entity shall have the sole discretion to voluntarily agree to waive the protections in this section, provided that a person may not obtain a waiver through means of any direct or indirect coercion. A covered entity may withdraw a waiver upon reasonable 20260SB1481PN1965 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 notice.Section 4. Reimbursement claims methodology.The department, a covered insurer, a pharmacy benefit manager or any other entity that administers or manages prescription drug benefits on behalf of the department or a Medicaid managed care organization, shall rely exclusively on a claims-level methodology involving the use of a nationally recognized billing modifier for identifying drugs purchased under section 340B of the Public Health Service Act when paying for drugs on behalf of Medicaid to ensure that claims for the drugs are removed from the encounter data required under section 42 U.S.C. § 1396b(m)(2)(A)(xiii) (relating to payment to States) and excluded from Medicaid drug rebate requests.Section 5. Safety net reimbursement protection.(a) Prohibitions.--A Medicaid managed care organization, a pharmacy benefit manager or other entity administering or managing prescription drug benefits on behalf of a Medicaid managed care organization may not:(1) prevent a covered entity or contract pharmacy from administering or dispensing 340B drugs by any legal means, including mail delivery, or submitting a claim for reimbursement for 340B drugs or decline to cover a claim for reimbursement for administered or dispensed 340B drugs because the drug was purchased under section 340B of the Public Health Service Act;(2) impose a limitation on the distance between the covered entity and contract pharmacy or between the covered entity or contract pharmacy and the patient; or(3) except as necessary to comply with Federal or State law or to prevent a duplicate discount prohibited under 20260SB1481PN1965 - 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 Federal law, request, require or compel the transmission of any ingredient cost, pricing data or other data pertaining to the program established under section 340B of the Public Health Service Act as a condition of reimbursement or participation.(b) Effect of violation.--Any rule, regulation or statement of policy in violation of this section shall be null and void.(c) Outpatient prescription drugs.--The department shall maintain coverage for all outpatient prescription drugs through Medicaid managed care organizations for as long as any medical assistance services described in 42 U.S.C. § 1396d(a) (relating to definitions) are delivered by any Medicaid managed care organization.(d) Grants for sexually transmitted disease clinics.--The department shall ensure that:(1) no later than 90 days after the effective date of this paragraph each sexually transmitted disease clinic shall receive a grant of at least $500 from the department's Federal funding under section 318 of the Public Health Service Act to perform sexually transmitted disease testing and treatment; and(2) all new awards or renewals provided to sexually transmitted disease clinics shall provide for a grant of at least $500 from the department's Federal funding under section 318 of the Public Health Service Act to perform sexually transmitted disease testing and treatment.Section 6. Safety net prescription drug distribution and access protections.(a) Acquisitions.--A manufacturer, or an agent or affiliate of a manufacturer, or third-party logistics provider may not, 20260SB1481PN1965 - 6 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 either directly or indirectly, deny, restrict, prohibit, delay or otherwise interfere with the acquisition of a 340B drug by, or delivery of a 340B drug to, a covered entity or contract pharmacy, or place any conditions on the acquisition or receipt, unless the acquisition or receipt is prohibited by Federal law.(b) Conditions.--A manufacturer, or an agent or affiliate of a manufacturer, may not, either directly or indirectly, require a covered entity to submit any claims or utilization data as a condition for allowing the acquisition of a 340B drug by, or delivery of a 340B drug to, a covered entity or contract pharmacy unless the claims or utilization data submission is required by Federal law.Section 7.
