AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in general provisions, further providing for
definitions; in licensing of drivers, further providing for
examination of applicant for driver's license; in special
vehicles and pedestrians, providing for electric scooters and
for shared electric scooters; and imposing penalties.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:

§ 3597.1. Scope of subchapter.
This subchapter relates to the operation of electric scooters
on certain highways or roadways in this Commonwealth.
§ 3597.2. Title and registration exemption.
Notwithstanding Subchapter A of Chapter 11 (relating to
certificate of title) or Subchapter A of Chapter 13 (relating to
general provisions), an electric scooter authorized under this
subchapter shall not be required to satisfy certificate of title
or vehicle registration requirements under this title.

scooter at a speed greater than 25 miles per hour on a
pedalcycle lane on a highway, roadway or pedalcycle path.

§ 3597.6. Local enforcement.
A city, borough, incorporated town, township or home rule
municipality that has its own police department may pass an
ordinance to allow for enhanced penalties for a violation of
section 3597.3(b) (relating to operation of electric scooters).
The ordinance may create fines for a first offense, second
offense and subsequent offenses but a single fine may not exceed
$100.
§ 3597.7. Police powers.
For the purposes of this subchapter, it shall be presumed to
be a reasonable exercise of police power to regulate and enforce
the use of electric scooters consistent with this subchapter and
the regulation of pedalcycles, as applicable.
§ 3597.8. Purchase of electric scooters.
A person must be at least 18 years of age to purchase an
electric scooter from a seller.
§ 3597.9. Assumption of risk.

scooter can be hazardous to the rider, regardless of all
feasible safety measures. Therefore, each rider expressly
assumes the risk of, and all legal responsibility for, any
injury to person or property that results from or is caused by
the following:
(1) Variations in the pavement or terrain.
(2) Surface or subsurface defects.
(3) Wet or slippery conditions.

§ 3598.1 . Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this subchapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Abandoned shared electric scooter." A shared electric
scooter that meets all of the following:
(1) The user of the shared electric scooter is not
visible to an individual making an initial report on a
hotline that the shared electric scooter is abandoned.
(2) The shared electric scooter is illegally parked on a
highway or is on property on which the shared electric
scooter is prohibited to be used or kept as provided under
this subchapter or an applicable municipal ordinance or
executive order.
"Authorized municipality." Any of the following:
(1) A city of the second class.
(2) A city of the second class A.
(3) A city of the third class.

§ 3598.5 . Safety issues.
If a program includes the use of a highway or roadway owned
or under the jurisdiction of the department, the department may
restrict access, permanently or temporarily, to a segment of the
highway or roadway if the department identifies a pattern of
safety issues that cannot be reasonably corrected. The
department shall establish a process to:
(1) Examine the safety issue, limited to the evaluation
of accidents and damage to property on the highway.
(2) Communicate the safety issue and consult with an
authorized municipality and commercial electric scooter-
sharing enterprise prior to restricting access.

(b) Availability.--Labor peace agreements must be made
available upon request.
