§ 3597.1. SCOPE OF SUBCHAPTER.
THIS SUBCHAPTER RELATES TO THE OPERATION OF ELECTRIC SCOOTERS
ON CERTAIN HIGHWAYS OR ROADWAYS IN THIS COMMONWEALTH.
§ 3597.2. TITLE AND REGISTRATION EXEMPTION.
NOTWITHSTANDING SUBCHAPTER A OF CHAPTER 11 (RELATING TO
CERTIFICATE OF TITLE) OR SUBCHAPTER A OF CHAPTER 13 (RELATING TO
GENERAL PROVISIONS), AN ELECTRIC SCOOTER AUTHORIZED UNDER THIS
SUBCHAPTER SHALL NOT BE REQUIRED TO SATISFY CERTIFICATE OF TITLE
OR VEHICLE REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS UNDER THIS TITLE.
§ 3597.3. OPERATION OF ELECTRIC SCOOTERS.
(A) SPEED LIMITATION.--A PERSON MAY NOT OPERATE AN ELECTRIC
SCOOTER AT A SPEED GREATER THAN 25 MILES PER HOUR ON A
PEDALCYCLE LANE ON A HIGHWAY, ROADWAY OR PEDALCYCLE PATH.
(B) AGE LIMITATION.--A PERSON UNDER 16 YEARS OF AGE MAY NOT
OPERATE AN ELECTRIC SCOOTER.
(C) HELMET REQUIREMENT.--A PERSON UNDER 18 YEARS OF AGE MAY
NOT OPERATE AN ELECTRIC SCOOTER UNLESS THE PERSON IS WEARING A
PEDALCYCLE HELMET THAT MEETS THE REQUIREMENTS OF SECTION 3510
(RELATING TO PEDALCYCLE HELMETS FOR CERTAIN PERSONS).
(D) NUMBER OF RIDERS.--A N ELECTRIC SCOOTER MAY NOT BE
OPERATED TO CARRY MORE PERSONS AT ONE TIME THAN THE NUMBER FOR
WHICH THE DEVICE IS DESIGNED AND EQUIPPED.
§ 3597.4. APPLICABILITY OF PEDALCYCLE TRAFFIC LAWS.
(A) GENERAL RULE.--A PERSON RIDING AN ELECTRIC SCOOTER ON A
ROADWAY HAS ALL RIGHTS AND IS SUBJECT TO ALL DUTIES APPLICABLE
TO A RIDER OF A PEDALCYCLE UNDER THIS TITLE, INCLUDING SECTIONS
3501 (RELATING TO APPLICABILITY OF TRAFFIC LAWS TO PEDALCYCLES),
3502 (RELATING TO PENALTY FOR VIOLATION OF SUBCHAPTER), 3503

SECTION 3597.3(B) (RELATING TO OPERATION OF ELECTRIC SCOOTERS).

OFFENSE AND SUBSEQUENT OFFENSES BUT A SINGLE FINE MAY NOT EXCEED
$100.

§ 3597.9. ASSUMPTION OF RISK.
(A) GENERALLY.--IT IS RECOGNIZED THAT RIDING AN ELECTRIC
SCOOTER CAN BE HAZARDOUS TO THE RIDER, REGARDLESS OF ALL
FEASIBLE SAFETY MEASURES. THEREFORE, EACH RIDER EXPRESSLY
ASSUMES THE RISK OF, AND ALL LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR, ANY
INJURY TO PERSON OR PROPERTY THAT RESULTS FROM OR IS CAUSED BY
THE FOLLOWING:
(1) VARIATIONS IN THE PAVEMENT OR TERRAIN.
(2) SURFACE OR SUBSURFACE DEFECTS.
(3) WET OR SLIPPERY CONDITIONS.
(4) DEBRIS.
(5) CURBS.
(6) POTHOLES.
(7) UTILITY POLES.
(8) TRAFFIC CONTROL DEVICES.
(9) EQUIPMENT OR STRUCTURES THAT ARE PLAINLY VISIBLE OR
PLAINLY MARKED IN ACCORDANCE WITH APPLICABLE SAFETY
REQUIREMENTS.
(B) RIDER RESPONSIBILITIES.--EACH RIDER SHALL HAVE THE SOLE
RESPONSIBILITY FOR:
(1) KNOWING THE LIMITS OF THEIR OWN ABILITY TO OPERATE
AN ELECTRIC SCOOTER.
(2) RIDING WITHIN THE LIMITS OF THAT ABILITY.
(3) MAINTAINING REASONABLE CONTROL OF SPEED AND
DIRECTION AT ALL TIMES.
(4) OBEYING ALL POSTED WARNING AND TRAFFIC REGULATIONS.
(5) RIDING ONLY IN AREAS DESIGNATED FOR ELECTRIC SCOOTER
USE.

§ 3535 3598.5 . Safety issues.
If a program includes the use of a highway OR ROADWAY owned
or under the jurisdiction of the department, the department may
restrict access, permanently or temporarily, to a segment of the
highway under the program OR ROADWAY if the department
identifies a pattern of safety issues that cannot be reasonably
corrected. The department shall establish a process to:
(1) Examine the safety issue, limited to the evaluation
of accidents and damage to property on the highway.
(2) Communicate the safety issue and consult with an
authorized municipality and commercial electric low-speed-
scooter-sharing enterprise prior to restricting access.
§ 3536 3598.6 . Labor peace agreements.
(a) Agreement required.-- To ensure uninterrupted service and
minimize labor disputes, all operators and service providers of
electric low-speed scooters shall present a fully executed labor
peace agreement with any labor organization representing their
employees as part of their license application or within 90 days
of being informed of the representation if their employees were
not previously represented by the labor organization.
(b) Availability.--Labor peace agreements must be made
available upon request.

§ 3537 3598.7 . Enforcement.
In addition to any fines or penalties relating to a
pedalcycle under Subchapter A (relating to operation of
pedalcycles) AND SECTION 3597.6 (RELATING TO LOCAL ENFORCEMENT) ,
an authorized municipality may impose one or more of the
following:
(1) A fine for the failure to operate a shared electric
low-speed scooter consistent with this subchapter, not to
exceed $50 $100 .
(2) A fine for the failure to park a shared electric
low-speed scooter consistent with this subchapter, not to
exceed $150.
(3) A fine on a commercial electric low-speed- scooter-
sharing enterprise for each abandoned shared electric low-
speed scooter that is not collected and returned to a corral
or storage within three hours of the abandonment of the
shared electric low-speed scooter, not to exceed $150 per
day.
(4) A civil penalty on a commercial electric low-speed-
scooter-sharing enterprise that does not provide the
insurance required under section 3534 3598.4 (relating to
insurance), not to exceed $1,000 for each day that the
commercial electric low-speed- scooter-sharing enterprise is
operated without the required insurance. The civil penalty
collected under this paragraph by the authorized municipality
shall be used for the safety, operation and management of
shared electric low-speed scooters or pedalcycles.

§ 3538 3598.8 . Police powers.
(a) Presumption.--For the purposes of this subchapter, it
shall be presumed to be a reasonable exercise of police power to
regulate and enforce the use of shared electric low-speed
scooters consistent with this subchapter and the regulation of
pedalcycles, as applicable. SCOOTERS IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION
3597.7 (RELATING TO POLICE POWERS).
(b) Construction.--Nothing in this subchapter shall be
construed to prevent the department on a State-designated
highway or a local authority on a local-designated highway from
the reasonable exercise of the police powers of the department
or local authority in accordance with section 6109 (relating to
specific powers of department and local authorities).

§ 3540. Applicability and construction.
(a) Title and registration.--Notwithstanding Subchapter A of
Chapter 11 (relating to certificate of title) or Subchapter A of
Chapter 13 (relating to general provisions), a shared electric
low-speed scooter authorized under this subchapter shall not be
required to comply with certificate of title or vehicle
registration requirements under this title.
(b) Motor vehicles.--An electric low-speed scooter
authorized under this subchapter shall not be construed as a
motor vehicle.
(c) Private ownership.--Nothing in this subchapter shall be
construed to prohibit a person that is not a commercial electric
low-speed-scooter-sharing enterprise from owning or operating an
electric low-speed scooter. The person shall not be subject to
the requirements under this subchapter that apply specifically
to a commercial electric low-speed-scooter-sharing enterprise.
§ 3598.10. ASSUMPTION OF RISK.
(A) GENERALLY.--IT IS RECOGNIZED THAT RIDING AN ELECTRIC
SCOOTER CAN BE HAZARDOUS TO THE RIDER, REGARDLESS OF ALL
FEASIBLE SAFETY MEASURES. THEREFORE, EACH RIDER EXPRESSLY
ASSUMES THE RISK OF, AND ALL LEGAL RESPONSIBILITY FOR, ANY
INJURY TO PERSON OR PROPERTY THAT RESULTS FROM OR IS CAUSED BY
THE FOLLOWING:
(1) VARIATIONS IN THE PAVEMENT OR TERRAIN.
(2) SURFACE OR SUBSURFACE DEFECTS.
(3) WET OR SLIPPERY CONDITIONS.
