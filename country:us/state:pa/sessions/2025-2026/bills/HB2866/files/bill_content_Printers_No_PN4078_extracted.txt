Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Energy Ratepayer Protection Act.Section 2. Imposition of fee.(a) Payment and amount.--An energy consumption taxpayer shall pay a fee to the Commonwealth equal to 10¢ per kilowatt hour of energy consumed by the energy consumption taxpayer during the taxable year.20260HB2866PN4078 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (b) Deposit.--The fee paid under subsection (a) shall be deposited into the fund.Section 4. Energy Ratepayer Protection Fund.(a) Establishment.--The Energy Ratepayer Protection Fund is established in the State Treasury. The money in the fund is appropriated on a continuing basis to the commission for the purpose of paying energy ratepayer rebates.(b) Payment of energy ratepayer rebates.--(1) The commission, in consultation with the Department of Revenue, shall determine the amount payable to each energy ratepayer based on the amount of money in the fund subject to the following:(i) An energy ratepayer rebate may not be paid to an individual who files a tax return under Article III of the act of March 4, 1971 (P.L.6, No.2), known as the Tax Reform Code of 1971, as a single individual and has a household income of more than $150,000 in a year.(ii) An energy ratepayer rebate may not be paid to an individual who files a tax return under Article III of the Tax Reform Code of 1971 jointly with another individual and has a household income of more than $300,000 in a year.(2) The commission shall issue energy ratepayer rebates to energy ratepayers not later than 120 days after the end of each fiscal year.(3) The commission shall include with the energy ratepayer rebate a notice that is conspicuous and takes the following form:ENERGY RATEPAYER RELIEFEnclosed you will find an energy ratepayer relief rebate of 20260HB2866PN4078 - 5 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (amount). This relief measure is mandated by law through an act of the Pennsylvania General Assembly that uses revenue collected through a fee on major energy-consuming companies. The rebate received by each energy ratepayer is determined by dividing the amount of money in the Energy Ratepayer Protection Fund by the number of eligible energy ratepayers.(4) An energy ratepayer rebate shall apply only to a homestead or farmstead.Section 5. Reports.(a) Publication and contents.--By March 30, 2027, and each March 30 thereafter, the commission shall report the energy usage in megawatt hours by energy consumption taxpayers during the calendar year ending on the preceding December 31.(b) Transmittal.--The commission shall transmit the report to:(1) The Governor.(2) The President pro tempore of the Senate.(3) The Speaker of the House of Representatives.(4) The Minority Leader of the Senate.(5) The Minority Leader of the House of Representatives.(6) The chairperson and minority chairperson of the Finance Committee of the Senate.(7) The chairperson and minority chairperson of the Finance Committee of the House of Representatives.Section 6. Guidelines.The commission shall establish guidelines necessary to implement this act.
