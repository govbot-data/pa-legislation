AN ACT
Imposing a fee on energy consumption taxpayers; providing for an
energy ratepayer rebate; imposing duties on the Pennsylvania
Public Utility Commission; and establishing the Energy
Ratepayer Protection Fund.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Energy
Ratepayer Protection Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Commission." The Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission.

is written, displayed or presented in a manner that a reasonable
individual against whom the notice is to operate should have
noticed. A conspicuous notice includes the following:

(i) is a resident of this Commonwealth; and

energy ratepayer under this act from money in the fund at the

"Farmstead." Buildings and structures on a farm not less
than 10 contiguous acres in area, which are not otherwise exempt
from real property taxation or qualified for any other abatement

shall only apply to farms used as the domicile of an owner.

under section 4.

Section 3. Imposition of fee.
(a) Payment and amount.--An energy consumption taxpayer
shall pay a fee to the Commonwealth equal to 10¢ per kilowatt
hour of energy consumed by the energy consumption taxpayer
during the taxable year.

Section 4. Energy Ratepayer Protection Fund.
(a) Establishment.--The Energy Ratepayer Protection Fund is
established in the State Treasury. The money in the fund is
appropriated on a continuing basis to the commission for the
purpose of paying energy ratepayer rebates.
(b) Payment of energy ratepayer rebates.--
(1) The commission, in consultation with the Department
of Revenue, shall determine the amount payable to each energy
ratepayer based on the amount of money in the fund subject to
the following:
(i) An energy ratepayer rebate may not be paid to an
individual who files a tax return under Article III of
the act of March 4, 1971 (P.L.6, No.2), known as the Tax
Reform Code of 1971, as a single individual and has a
household income of more than $150,000 in a year.
(ii) An energy ratepayer rebate may not be paid to
an individual who files a tax return under Article III of
the Tax Reform Code of 1971 jointly with another
individual and has a household income of more than
$300,000 in a year.
(2) The commission shall issue energy ratepayer rebates
to energy ratepayers not later than 120 days after the end of
each fiscal year.
(3) The commission shall include with the energy
ratepayer rebate a notice that is conspicuous and takes the
following form:
ENERGY RATEPAYER RELIEF
Enclosed you will find an energy ratepayer relief rebate of

Section 5. Reports.
(a) Publication and contents.--By March 30, 2027, and each
March 30 thereafter, the commission shall report the energy
usage in megawatt hours by energy consumption taxpayers during
the calendar year ending on the preceding December 31.
(b) Transmittal.--The commission shall transmit the report
to:
(1) The Governor.
(2) The President pro tempore of the Senate.
(3) The Speaker of the House of Representatives.
(4) The Minority Leader of the Senate.
(5) The Minority Leader of the House of Representatives.
(6) The chairperson and minority chairperson of the
Finance Committee of the Senate.
(7) The chairperson and minority chairperson of the
Finance Committee of the House of Representatives.
Section 6. Guidelines.
The commission shall establish guidelines necessary to
implement this act. The guidelines shall include calculation of
energy usage in megawatt hours and the fee imposed under section
3.
