A RESOLUTION
Recognizing October 21 NOVEMBER 10, 2026, as "Bill White Day" in
Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, The National Baseball Hall of Fame's Buck O'Neil
Lifetime Achievement Award is named after its first recipient,
whose seven-decade-long career started prior to baseball's
integration and included time as a player, scout, manager and
ambassador; and
WHEREAS, The National Baseball Hall of Fame awards the Buck
O'Neil Lifetime Achievement Award no more than once every three
years to an individual whose extraordinary qualities and
contributions to the sport are in keeping with those of Buck
O'Neil; and
WHEREAS, Bill White was born January 28, 1934, in Lakewood,
Florida, was raised in Warren, Ohio, and is a longtime resident
of Bucks County, Pennsylvania; and
WHEREAS, Bill White's trailblazing baseball career started in
1953 in the Carolina League, where he significantly contributed

WHEREAS, He went on to play in the major league in 1956 with

WHEREAS, Bill White helped lead the St. Louis Cardinals to
the World Series title in 1964; and
WHEREAS, Bill White was selected for Major League Baseball's
(MLB) All-Star Game eight times and won seven Gold Glove awards
from 1960 through 1966 for his position as first baseman in the
National League; and
WHEREAS, His successful career in sports broadcasting and
reporting started during his time as a player and included a
stint at WFIL-TV, now WPVI-TV, in Philadelphia during his time
with the Phillies; and
WHEREAS, Postretirement, Bill White served as WFIL-TV's
sports director for a year before being hired as a broadcaster
for the New York Yankees, where he worked for nearly two
decades; and
WHEREAS, His radio position with the New York Yankees made
Bill White the first Black play-by-play broadcaster for any MLB
team; and
WHEREAS, From 1989 to 1994, Bill White served not only as the
first Black National League President, but also as the first
former player to hold that office in 70 years; and
WHEREAS, During his time as National League President, Bill
White was instrumental in the National League expansion that
added the Colorado Rockies and the Miami Marlins; and
