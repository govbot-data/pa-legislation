2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2816 PN 3982 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3982 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2816 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY YOUNG, HILL-EVANS, DOUGHERTY, DEASY, GUZMAN, SANCHEZ, MERSKI, PARKER AND CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, OCTOBER 2, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An act relating to the public school system, including certain provisions applicable as well to private and parochial schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," in student supports, further providing for policy relating to bullying and cyberbullying.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Section 1303.1-A of the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, amended July 12, 2026 (P.L.220, No.20), is amended to read:Section 1303.1-A. Policy Relating to Bullying and Cyberbullying.--(a) Each school entity shall adopt a policy or amend its existing policy relating to bullying and cyberbullying and incorporate the policy into the school entity's code of student conduct required under 22 Pa. Code § 12.3(c) (relating to school rules). No later than January 1, 2027, each nonpublic school shall adopt a policy or amend its existing policy relating to bullying and cyberbullying and incorporate the 12345678910111213141516171819 policy into the nonpublic school's code of student conduct. The policy shall delineate disciplinary consequences for bullying and cyberbullying and may provide for prevention, intervention and education programs, provided that no school entity or nonpublic school shall be required to establish a new policy under this section if one currently exists and reasonably fulfills the requirements of this section. The policy shall identify the appropriate school staff person to receive reports of incidents of alleged bullying and cyberbullying. The policy shall require all incidents of alleged bullying and cyberbullying to be investigated promptly by the school entity or nonpublic school and include a procedure for investigating a report of an incident of alleged bullying or cyberbullying received by the school entity[.] or nonpublic school. The policy shall require a process to protect the safety, well-being and educational environment of all students involved in a bullying or cyberbullying allegation both during the investigation and after a determination has been made. After determining that an incident of bullying or cyberbullying has occurred, the school entity or nonpublic school shall immediately notify, as soon as practicable, any student involved in the incident and the parents or legal guardians of any student involved in the incident of the determination.(b) Each school entity and nonpublic school shall make the policy available on its publicly accessible Internet website, if available, and in every classroom. Each school entity and nonpublic school shall post the policy at a prominent location within each school building where such notices are usually posted.
