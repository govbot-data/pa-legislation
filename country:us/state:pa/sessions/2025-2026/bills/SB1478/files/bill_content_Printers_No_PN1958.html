AN ACT
Designating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 70470, carrying
State Route 2012, also known as River Road, over Blacklick
Creek between Buffington and East Wheatfield Townships in
Indiana County, as the Sgt. SOF Frank J. McCloskey Memorial
Bridge.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Sgt. SOF Frank J. McCloskey Memorial Bridge.
(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as
follows:
(1) Francis J. McCloskey, Jr., was born August 1, 1948.
(2) A graduate of United High School, he enlisted in the
United States Army on June 3, 1966. Mr. McCloskey trained at
Fort Bragg and Fort Benning.
(3) Upon completion of his training, Mr. McCloskey
deployed with the 502nd Battalion of the 101st Airborne
Division in Vietnam for his first tour.
(4) Mr. McCloskey was wounded in action during his first
tour and was awarded the Purple Heart, Combat Infantryman
Badge and Bronze Star with Valor.

(5) Mr. McCloskey volunteered for Special Forces duty
after his first tour, earned his Green Beret and served with
the Military Assistance Command, Vietnam - Studies and
Observations Group, commanding his own recon team in 1968.
Mr. McCloskey was awarded his second Bronze Star Medal with
Valor after undertaking heroic measures moving his team to
safety during an overwhelming enemy attack.
(6) Mr. McCloskey was transferred to the 11th Special
Forces Group (Reserves) in Ft. Mead, Maryland, in 1969 and
was sworn in as a police officer with the Metropolitan Police
District of Columbia (MPDC).
(7) Mr. McCloskey was later promoted to detective at
MPDC, where he was part of the response to the attempted
assassination of President Ronald Reagan in March 1981.
(8) Mr. McCloskey was recruited by the Central
Intelligence Agency (CIA) where he performed clandestine
operations in the Latin American Division and was trained as
a helicopter pilot in 1981.
(9) CIA Director William Casey awarded Mr. McCloskey the
Intelligence Star for Valor for actions taken to save another
during a violent guerrilla assault. Mr. McCloskey was also
the only non-Guatemalan military member to receive a Battle
Cross for Valor.
(10) Mr. McCloskey retired from government service in
1995.
(11) Mr. McCloskey was selected to be Chief of Police
for the Chitimacha Tribal Police Department in Louisiana,
where he assisted with prosecutions navigating Tribal Law and
Napoleonic Code in 1996 and later was hired as a detective in
the St. Mary Parish Sheriff's Office. During this time, Mr.

McCloskey was solicited by the Department of Justice to
return to Guatemala on a fact-finding mission concerning the
previous war.
(12) Mr. McCloskey was hired as a member of the United
Nations Civilian Police Force in Kosovo in 1999 in the
aftermath of the Bosnian War, during which time he served as
an investigator-trainer for the police in Pristina.
(13) Mr. McCloskey returned to flying and served as a
MedStar pilot in the Washington, DC, and Maryland area,
saving countless lives from traumatic scenes. Mr. McCloskey
relocated with his wife and family to Johnstown, Colorado,
where he worked as a flight dispatcher and scheduler at AIMS
Community College in Greely until the time of his passing.
(14) Mr. McCloskey passed away on November 18, 2025, at
77 years of age.
(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key
70470, carrying State Route 2012, also known as River Road, over
Blacklick Creek between Buffington and East Wheatfield Townships
in Indiana County, is designated as the Sgt. SOF Frank J.
McCloskey Memorial Bridge.
(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and
maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to
traffic in both directions on the bridge.
Section 2. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 60 days.
