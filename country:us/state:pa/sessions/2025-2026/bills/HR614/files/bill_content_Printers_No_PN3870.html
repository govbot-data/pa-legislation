A RESOLUTION
Recognizing the month of August 2026 as "Appendix Cancer
Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Appendix cancer, also known as appendiceal cancer,
is a rare but serious disease, affecting approximately two to
nine people per million each year in the United States; and
WHEREAS, Although appendix cancer is genetically distinct
from colorectal cancer, its incidence has been rising
significantly in recent years, particularly among individuals
under 50 years of age; and
WHEREAS, Due in part to its rarity, appendix cancer is often
misunderstood and can be difficult to diagnose, resulting in
frequent delays or misdiagnoses that may negatively impact
patient outcomes and quality of life; and
WHEREAS, Symptoms of appendix cancer may be vague or absent
in early stages and many individuals are not diagnosed until the
disease has advanced, making treatment more complex and
challenging; and

WHEREAS, Treatment for appendix cancer varies based on type
and stage, and may include surgery, chemotherapy and other
therapies, underscoring the importance of early detection and
increased awareness; and
WHEREAS, The Appendix Cancer PMP Research Foundation, founded
in 2008, works to fund research and education to improve the
diagnosis and treatment of appendix cancer and pseudomyxoma
peritonei; and
WHEREAS, Patients, survivors, caregivers, health care
professionals, advocates and organizations across the country
are working together to raise awareness, support research and
improve outcomes for those affected by appendix cancer;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the
month of August 2026 as "Appendix Cancer Awareness Month" in
Pennsylvania; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives urge all
Pennsylvanians to be aware of this serious disease, take steps
to promote early detection and work toward a cure.
