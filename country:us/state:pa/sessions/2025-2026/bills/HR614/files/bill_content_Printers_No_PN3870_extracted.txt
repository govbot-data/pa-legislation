2025-2026 Regular Session HR 614 PN 3870 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3870 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.614 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BOROWSKI, GUZMAN, DEASY, FREEMAN, TIBURCIO, VENKAT, DOUGHERTY, HILL-EVANS, RIVERA, HOWARD, SANCHEZ, CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, HOHENSTEIN, GALLAGHER AND MALAGARI, SEPTEMBER 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON HEALTH, SEPTEMBER 9, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of August 2026 as "Appendix Cancer Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Appendix cancer, also known as appendiceal cancer, is a rare but serious disease, affecting approximately two to nine people per million each year in the United States; andWHEREAS, Although appendix cancer is genetically distinct from colorectal cancer, its incidence has been rising significantly in recent years, particularly among individuals under 50 years of age; andWHEREAS, Due in part to its rarity, appendix cancer is often misunderstood and can be difficult to diagnose, resulting in frequent delays or misdiagnoses that may negatively impact patient outcomes and quality of life; andWHEREAS, Symptoms of appendix cancer may be vague or absent in early stages and many individuals are not diagnosed until the disease has advanced, making treatment more complex and challenging; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, Treatment for appendix cancer varies based on type and stage, and may include surgery, chemotherapy and other therapies, underscoring the importance of early detection and increased awareness; andWHEREAS, The Appendix Cancer PMP Research Foundation, founded in 2008, works to fund research and education to improve the diagnosis and treatment of appendix cancer and pseudomyxoma peritonei; andWHEREAS, Patients, survivors, caregivers, health care professionals, advocates and organizations across the country are working together to raise awareness, support research and improve outcomes for those affected by appendix cancer; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize the month of August 2026 as "Appendix Cancer Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives urge all Pennsylvanians to be aware of this serious disease, take steps to promote early detection and work toward a cure.20260HR0614PN3870 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819
