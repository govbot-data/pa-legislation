andWHEREAS, Hospital licensure regulations have not been updated in nearly 40 years and do not reflect the current operating environment; andWHEREAS, Hospital policies, as written, create a greater need to use an exception or waiver process, lead to inconsistent interpretations by the Department of Health surveyors and make it difficult for facilities to plan for new technologies or strategies to ensure health care access;
