A RESOLUTION
Directing the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to
conduct a study on EXAMINING the financial impact of outdated
hospital regulations on hospitals in this Commonwealth., THE
EROSION OF THE SCOPE OF PRACTICE OF PHYSICIANS IN RURAL AREAS
AND MEASURES TO PRESERVE PHYSICIAN-LED MEDICAL PRACTICE AND
IMPROVE PHYSICIAN RECRUITMENT AND RETENTION IN RURAL AREAS OF
THIS COMMONWEALTH.
WHEREAS, The Senate recognizes the paramount importance of
hospital regulations to ensure that patients receive safe, high-
quality care; and
WHEREAS, Hospital licensure regulations have not been updated
in nearly 40 years and do not reflect the current operating
environment; and
WHEREAS, Hospital policies, as written, create a greater need
to use an exception or waiver process, lead to inconsistent
interpretations by the Department of Health surveyors and make
it difficult for facilities to plan for new technologies or
strategies to ensure health care access; and
WHEREAS, Misalignment with Federal regulations imposes undue
administrative burden on hospitals in this Commonwealth with no
