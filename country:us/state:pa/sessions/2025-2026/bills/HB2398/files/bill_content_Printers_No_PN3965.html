AN ACT
Amending Title 35 (Health and Safety) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in local organizations and services,
providing for flood evacuation plans for organized camps.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 35 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 7505. Flood evacuation plans for organized camps.
(a) Evacuation plans.--
(1) The governing body of each political subdivision
shall require the operator of an organized camp located
within 500 feet of a special flood hazard area to establish
and maintain a written flood evacuation plan for the camp
premises.
(2) An executive agency operating an organized camp
located within 500 feet of a special flood hazard area shall
establish and maintain, or shall cause a lessee to establish

(b) Filing and review.--
(1) The operator of an organized camp, including an
executive agency, shall file a copy of the flood evacuation
plan with the agency and each county emergency management
agency and local emergency management organizations of
jurisdiction where the organized camp is located.
(2) THE AGENCY SHALL HAVE 30 DAYS TO REVIEW THE PLAN AND
NOTIFY THE ORGANIZER OF ANY DEFICIENCIES REQUIRING
CORRECTION.
(2) (3) The flood evacuation plan shall be reviewed
annually and modified as necessary by the operator, including
an executive agency, in consultation with each county
emergency management agency and local emergency management
organization of jurisdiction where the organized camp is
located, before the camp opens for the camp season EACH YEAR .
(c) Posting and distribution.--The operator of an organized
camp shall have the following duties:
(1) Post a diagram or summary of evacuation routes and
safe locations in each sleeping and dining facility within
the camp premises.
(2) Provide a copy or summary of the flood evacuation
plan to adult campers or parents or guardians of children and
youth campers before each camp season THE CAMP OPENS EACH
YEAR .

issuance or renewal of any license, permit or zoning approval
for an organized camp on compliance with this section. A
political subdivision, in consultation with the agency, shall

(e) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Operator." A person who owns or manages an organized camp.
"Organized camp." A s defined in section 1 of the act of
November 10, 1959 (P.L.1400, No.497), entitled "An act providing
for the annual registration of organized camps for children,
youth and adults; defining the duties of the Department of
Health of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania; and prescribing
penalties."
"Special flood hazard area." Land in a floodplain having a
1% or greater annual probability of flooding as identified by
the Federal Emergency Management Agency on a Flood Insurance
Rate Map or Digital Flood Insurance Rate Map.
Section 2. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
