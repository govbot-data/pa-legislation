Title 35 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 7505. Flood evacuation plans for organized camps.(a) Evacuation plans.--(1) The governing body of each political subdivision shall require the operator of an organized camp located within 500 feet of a special flood hazard area to establish and maintain a written flood evacuation plan for the camp premises.(2) An executive agency operating an organized camp located within 500 feet of a special flood hazard area shall establish and maintain, or shall cause a lessee to establish 1234567891011121314151617 and maintain, a written flood evacuation plan for the camp premises.(b) Filing and review.--(1) The operator of an organized camp, including an executive agency, shall file a copy of the flood evacuation plan with the agency and each county emergency management agency and local emergency management organizations of jurisdiction where the organized camp is located.(2) THE AGENCY SHALL HAVE 30 DAYS TO REVIEW THE PLAN AND NOTIFY THE ORGANIZER OF ANY DEFICIENCIES REQUIRING CORRECTION.(2) (3) The flood evacuation plan shall be reviewed annually and modified as necessary by the operator, including an executive agency, in consultation with each county emergency management agency and local emergency management organization of jurisdiction where the organized camp is located, before the camp opens for the camp season EACH YEAR . (c) Posting and distribution.--The operator of an organized camp shall have the following duties:(1) Post a diagram or summary of evacuation routes and safe locations in each sleeping and dining facility within the camp premises.(2) Provide a copy or summary of the flood evacuation plan to adult campers or parents or guardians of children and youth campers before each camp season THE CAMP OPENS EACH YEAR . (d) Enforcement.--A political subdivision may condition the issuance or renewal of any license, permit or zoning approval for an organized camp on compliance with this section.
