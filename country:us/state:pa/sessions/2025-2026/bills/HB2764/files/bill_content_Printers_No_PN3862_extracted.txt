2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2764 PN 3862 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 3862 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2764 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KEPHART, VITALI, BANTA, SMITH, VEROBISH, STENDER, MARCELL, GUENST, GILLEN, ANDERSON, STAATS, ROWE, KAUFFMAN AND BERNSTINE, SEPTEMBER 8, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON TRANSPORTATION, SEPTEMBER 8, 2026 AN ACTDesignating a bridge, identified as Bridge Key 47686, carrying U.S. Route 219 over the West Branch Susquehanna River in Susquehanna Township, Cambria County, as the Seaman First Class Paul Kephart Memorial Bridge.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Seaman First Class Paul Kephart Memorial Bridge.(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as follows:(1) Paul Kephart was born in 1924 in Germantown, Susquehanna Township, Cambria County.(2) Seaman First Class Kephart attended Harnesboro High School where he graduated in 1943.(3) Outside of work, Seaman First Class Kephart enjoyed spending time with family. He was the youngest of 10 children to Mr. and Mrs. George Kephart of Germantown.(4) He entered the United States Navy in 1943 and served as a Seaman First Class.123456789101112131415161718 (5) Seaman First Class Kephart was serving aboard the USS Indianapolis (CA-35) off the Caroline Islands on July 30, 1945, when it was sunk off the coast of Leyte after being struck by torpedoes from a Japanese submarine. Seaman First Class Kephart was lost in the incident and has not been associated with any remains recovered after the war.(6) Seaman First Class Kephart was awarded the Purple Heart Medal and Certificate on March 27, 1946, and is memorialized on the Walls of the Missing at the Manila American Cemetery in the Philippines.(b) Designation.--The bridge, identified as Bridge Key 47686, carrying U.S. Route 219 over the West Branch Susquehanna River in Susquehanna Township, Cambria County, is designated the Seaman First Class Paul Kephart Memorial Bridge.(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the bridge to traffic in both directions on the bridge.Section 2.
