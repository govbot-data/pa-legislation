AN ACT
Amending Title 1 (General Provisions) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in rules of construction, providing
for definition of antisemitism.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 1 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes
is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1992. Definition of antisemitism.
(a) Definition.--When the term "antisemitism" is used but
not defined in a statute or regulation:
(1) The term shall mean, based on the definition and
description of the term "antisemitism" adopted May 26, 2016,
by the International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance, a
certain perception of Jews, which may b e expressed as hatred
toward Jews.
(2) The term includes a rhetorical or physical
manifestation that meets any of the following conditions:
(i) Is directed toward any of the following:

(A) Jewish or non-Jewish individuals.
(B) The property of Jewish or non-Jewish
individuals.
(C) Jewish community institutions.
(D) Jewish religious facilities.
(ii) Does any of the following:
(A) Targets the state of Israel, conceived as a
Jewish collectivity.
(B) Charges Jews with conspiring to harm
humanity.
(C) Blames Jews for "why things go wrong."
(iii) Is expressed in speech, writing, visual form
or action.
(iv) Employs sinister stereotypes or negative
character traits.
(3) The term includes any of the following in public
life, the media, schools or workplaces, or in the religious
sphere, taking into account the overall context:
(i) Calling for, aiding or justifying the killing or
harming of Jews in the name of a radical ideology or an
extremist view of religion.
(ii) Making mendacious, dehumanizing, demonizing or
stereotypical allegations about Jews or the power of Jews
as a collective, including the myth about a world Jewish
conspiracy or of Jews controlling the media, economy,
government or other societal institutions.
(iii) Accusing Jews as a people of being responsible
for real or imagined wrongdoing committed by a single
Jewish person or group, or even for acts committed by
non-Jews.

(iv) Denying the fact, scope, mechanisms, including

and its supporters and accomplices during the Holocaust
during World War II.
(v) Accusing the Jews as a people, or Israel as a
state, of inventing or exaggerating the Holocaust.
(vi) Accusing Jewish citizens of being more loyal to
Israel, or to the alleged priorities of Jews worldwide,
than to the interests of their own nations.
(vii) Denying the Jewish people their right to self-
determination, including by claiming that the existence

(viii) Applying a double standard by requiring of
the state of Israel a behavior not expected or demanded

classic antisemitism, including a claim of Jews killing
Jesus or blood libel, to characterize Israel or Israelis.
(x) Drawing comparisons of contemporary Israeli
policy to that of the Nazis.
(xi) Holding Jews collectively responsible for
actions of the state of Israel.
(4) The term does not include criticism of Israel
similar to that leveled against any other country.

(a):
(1) A criminal act is antisemitic if a target of the
criminal act is:
(i) an individual or property, including a building,

(2) Antisemitic discrimination is the denial to Jews of

(c) Construction.--T his section shall not be construed to:
(1) Diminish or infringe upon any right protected under

or the Constitution of Pennsylvania.

discrimination.
