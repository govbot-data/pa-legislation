Title 1 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1992. Definition of antisemitism.(a) Definition.--When the term "antisemitism" is used but not defined in a statute or regulation:(1) The term shall mean, based on the definition and description of the term "antisemitism" adopted May 26, 2016, by the International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance, a certain perception of Jews, which may b e expressed as hatred toward Jews.(2) The term includes a rhetorical or physical manifestation that meets any of the following conditions:(i) Is directed toward any of the following:123456789101112131415161718 (A) Jewish or non-Jewish individuals.(B) The property of Jewish or non-Jewish individuals.(C) Jewish community institutions.(D) Jewish religious facilities.(ii) Does any of the following: (A) Targets the state of Israel, conceived as a Jewish collectivity.(B) Charges Jews with conspiring to harm humanity.(C) Blames Jews for "why things go wrong."(iii) Is expressed in speech, writing, visual form or action.(iv) Employs sinister stereotypes or negative character traits.(3) The term includes any of the following in public life, the media, schools or workplaces, or in the religious sphere, taking into account the overall context:(i) Calling for, aiding or justifying the killing or harming of Jews in the name of a radical ideology or an extremist view of religion.(ii) Making mendacious, dehumanizing, demonizing or stereotypical allegations about Jews or the power of Jews as a collective, including the myth about a world Jewish conspiracy or of Jews controlling the media, economy, government or other societal institutions.(iii) Accusing Jews as a people of being responsible for real or imagined wrongdoing committed by a single Jewish person or group, or even for acts committed by non-Jews.20260SB1489PN2029 -
