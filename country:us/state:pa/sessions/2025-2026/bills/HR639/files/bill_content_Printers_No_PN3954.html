A RESOLUTION
Designating August 15, 2026, as "One Million Degrees Day" in
Pennsylvania in recognition of a significant milestone in
higher education and its impact on the Commonwealth's
workforce, economy and communities.
WHEREAS, Since its founding in 1855, The Pennsylvania State
University's (Penn State) enduring promise to provide
accessible, world-class education has prepared students to reach
their full potential and contribute to Pennsylvania's workforce
and economy for more than 170 years; and
WHEREAS, Penn State, originally named the Farmers' High
School of Pennsylvania, awarded bachelor of scientific
agriculture degrees to its first 11 graduates in 1861, with the
goal of encouraging the application of science to farming and
creating well-rounded citizens; and
WHEREAS, Under the 1862 Morrill Land-Grant Act, Penn State
was designated as Pennsylvania's sole land-grant university,
establishing a long-standing partnership with the Commonwealth

WHEREAS, Penn State's contributions go beyond the classroom

the largest Statewide footprint of any university in the Big Ten

WHEREAS, Students travel from all over the Commonwealth,
nation and world to attend Penn State campuses, or pursue a
degree online through World Campus, and after they graduate,
more than 50% of them choose to stay in Pennsylvania; and
WHEREAS, More than 430,000 Penn State alumni work in the
Commonwealth, and more than 1 in 10 jobs in the State are
supported by Penn State and its alumni; and
WHEREAS, On August 15, 2026, Penn State conferred its one
millionth degree, marking a historic moment in Pennsylvania
higher education and reflecting the university's longstanding
partnership with the Commonwealth and its role in strengthening
communities and industries across this Commonwealth, the nation
and the world; and
WHEREAS, One million Penn State degrees represent generations
of educational opportunity and lasting contributions to
communities throughout Pennsylvania, the nation and the world;
therefore be it
