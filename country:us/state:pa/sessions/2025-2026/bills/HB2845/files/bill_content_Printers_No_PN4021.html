AN ACT
Amending Title 4 (Amusements) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in fantasy contests, further providing for
definitions, for conditions of licensure and for
prohibitions.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The definition of "fantasy contest" in section
302 of Title 4 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is
amended and the section is amended by adding a definition to
read:
§ 302. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Accumulated statistical results." The collective or
compiled athletic performance data of two or more individual
athletes. This includes:

(2) separate individual statistical categories for each

(3) the determination of whether each individual

events meets, exceeds or falls short of a preestablished
statistical threshold, provided that the overall winning
outcome is determined by the aggregate of the determinations
across two or more individual athletes.

"Fantasy contest." As follows:
(1) An online fantasy or simulated game or contest with
an entry fee and a prize or award in which:
(i) The value of all prizes or awards offered to
winning participants is established and made known to
participants in advance of the contest and the value is
not determined by the number of participants or the
amount of [any] total fees paid collectively by those
participants. Multiple winning participants in the same
contest may share a single established prize or award.
Participants may pay different entry fee amounts to
participate in the same contest. Prizes or awards may be
determined in relation to the fixed entry fee paid by a
participant.
(ii) All winning outcomes reflect the relative
knowledge and skill of participants and are determined by
accumulated statistical results of the performance of
individuals, including athletes in the case of sports
events. The exercise of skill and knowledge among
