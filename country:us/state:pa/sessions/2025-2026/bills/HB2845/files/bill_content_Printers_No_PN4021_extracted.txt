The definition of "fantasy contest" in section 302 of Title 4 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended and the section is amended by adding a definition to read:§ 302. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this chapter shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Accumulated statistical results." The collective or compiled athletic performance data of two or more individual athletes. This includes:1234567891011121314151617 (1) composite statistical categories such as a 'fantasy score';(2) separate individual statistical categories for each individual athlete; and(3) the determination of whether each individual athlete's statistical performance in one or more sports events meets, exceeds or falls short of a preestablished statistical threshold, provided that the overall winning outcome is determined by the aggregate of the determinations across two or more individual athletes.* * *"Fantasy contest." As follows:(1) An online fantasy or simulated game or contest with an entry fee and a prize or award in which:(i) The value of all prizes or awards offered to winning participants is established and made known to participants in advance of the contest and the value is not determined by the number of participants or the amount of [any] total fees paid collectively by those participants. Multiple winning participants in the same contest may share a single established prize or award. Participants may pay different entry fee amounts to participate in the same contest. Prizes or awards may be determined in relation to the fixed entry fee paid by a participant.(ii) All winning outcomes reflect the relative knowledge and skill of participants and are determined by accumulated statistical results of the performance of individuals, including athletes in the case of sports events.
