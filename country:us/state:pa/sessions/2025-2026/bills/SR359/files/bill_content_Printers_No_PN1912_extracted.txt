2025-2026 Regular Session SR 359 PN 1912 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1912 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.359 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY PENNYCUICK, ROBINSON, YAW, ROTHMAN, MASTRIANO, DUSH, LANGERHOLC, BROOKS, PICOZZI, BROWN, BAKER, HAYWOOD AND KANE, AUGUST 24, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, AUGUST 24, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing the month of September 2026 as "Veteran Suicide Prevention and Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Studies confirm a high suicide rate among United States military veterans; andWHEREAS, Suicide is a national public health issue among veterans and nonveteran adults; andWHEREAS, In this Commonwealth, nearly 250 veterans per year are lost to suicide; andWHEREAS, Veterans are 1.5 times more likely to die by suicide, with the risk factor for female veterans being 2.2 times higher; andWHEREAS, Veterans often struggle with mental health conditions such as post-traumatic stress disorder, anxiety, depression and traumatic brain injuries, all factors that contribute to the elevated suicide rate; andWHEREAS, Societal factors such as unemployment, homelessness, loneliness and difficulties reintegrating into civilian life 1234567891011121314151617 also contribute to the elevated suicide rate; andWHEREAS, The Department of Military and Veterans Affairs has responded to the veteran suicide crisis by leading the Pennsylvania Governor's and Mayor's Challenges to Prevent Suicide Among Service Members, Veterans and their Families and by launching Statewide efforts to host Lethal Means Safety events to educate the public about suicide prevention; andWHEREAS, Major General John R. Pippy, the Adjutant General of Pennsylvania, originated the Department of Military and Veterans Affairs motto, "One Team, One Mission," which reflects the values of strength, honor and commitment in serving our warfighters, veterans and their families and recognizes the importance of providing veterans with the support, resources and respect they have earned through their sacrifice; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the month of September 2026 as "Veteran Suicide Prevention and Awareness Month" in Pennsylvania; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the Senate fully support the actions of the Department of Military and Veterans Affairs as the department continues to work on the needed support for our veterans and their families.20260SR0359PN1912 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122
