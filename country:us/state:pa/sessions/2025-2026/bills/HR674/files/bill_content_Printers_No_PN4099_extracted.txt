2025-2026 Regular Session HR 674 PN 4099 Bill Text (HTM) PRINTER'S NO. 4099 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE RESOLUTION No.674 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY RIVERA, PIELLI, McNEILL, KINKEAD, ISAACSON, SANCHEZ, SHUSTERMAN AND CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, OCTOBER 23, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON JUDICIARY, OCTOBER 23, 2026 A RESOLUTIONRecognizing November 20, 2026, as "Transgender Day of Remembrance" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, Transgender Day of Remembrance was first observed in 1999, beginning an important tradition of honoring and remembering the lives of transgender people lost to violence and hatred; andWHEREAS, Twenty-two years after the first Transgender Day of Remembrance, research from the University of California, Los Angeles, found that transgender people are still more likely to experience violent crime than cisgender people; andWHEREAS, Specifically, transgender individuals experience violent crime at a rate over four times higher than cisgender individuals; andWHEREAS, In 2024, at least 32 transgender and gender-expansive people had their lives taken through violent means, including here in Pennsylvania; andWHEREAS, Too often, the deaths of transgender people go unreported or are misreported, resulting in an incomplete record 123456789101112131415161718 of the number of lives lost to violence and obscuring the full extent of such violence against transgender people; andWHEREAS, In addition to this underreporting, Pennsylvania law lags behind in ensuring that transgender people can seek justice when they are the victim of a hate crime; andWHEREAS, Pennsylvania still maintains the "LGBTQ+ panic" defense, which allows assailants to receive a lesser sentence or to completely avoid conviction or punishment by blaming their actions on the victim's actual or perceived sexual orientation or gender expression; andWHEREAS, By recognizing Transgender Day of Remembrance, we honor the loved ones whose lives were taken too soon while also acknowledging the work that remains to prevent future acts of violence and protect transgender people from further loss of life; andWHEREAS, It is incumbent upon all of us to help create a Commonwealth in which transgender Pennsylvanians can live their lives safely and authentically, free from the threat of violence; andWHEREAS; Transgender Day of Remembrance is an opportunity to mourn the loss of loved ones while striving to eradicate violence against LGBTQ+ Pennsylvanians through education; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize November 20, 2026, as "Transgender Day of Remembrance" in Pennsylvania.20260HR0674PN4099 - 2 - 1234567891011121314151617181920212223242526
