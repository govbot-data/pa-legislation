A RESOLUTION
Recognizing November 20, 2026, as "Transgender Day of
Remembrance" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Transgender Day of Remembrance was first observed in
1999, beginning an important tradition of honoring and
remembering the lives of transgender people lost to violence and
hatred; and
WHEREAS, Twenty-two years after the first Transgender Day of
Remembrance, research from the University of California, Los
Angeles, found that transgender people are still more likely to
experience violent crime than cisgender people; and
WHEREAS, Specifically, transgender individuals experience
violent crime at a rate over four times higher than cisgender
individuals; and
WHEREAS, In 2024, at least 32 transgender and gender-
expansive people had their lives taken through violent means,
including here in Pennsylvania; and
WHEREAS, Too often, the deaths of transgender people go
unreported or are misreported, resulting in an incomplete record

of the number of lives lost to violence and obscuring the full
extent of such violence against transgender people; and
WHEREAS, In addition to this underreporting, Pennsylvania law
lags behind in ensuring that transgender people can seek justice
when they are the victim of a hate crime; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvania still maintains the "LGBTQ+ panic"
defense, which allows assailants to receive a lesser sentence or
to completely avoid conviction or punishment by blaming their
actions on the victim's actual or perceived sexual orientation
or gender expression; and
WHEREAS, By recognizing Transgender Day of Remembrance, we
honor the loved ones whose lives were taken too soon while also
acknowledging the work that remains to prevent future acts of
violence and protect transgender people from further loss of
life; and
WHEREAS, It is incumbent upon all of us to help create a
Commonwealth in which transgender Pennsylvanians can live their
lives safely and authentically, free from the threat of
violence; and
WHEREAS; Transgender Day of Remembrance is an opportunity to
mourn the loss of loved ones while striving to eradicate
violence against LGBTQ+ Pennsylvanians through education;
therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize
November 20, 2026, as "Transgender Day of Remembrance" in
Pennsylvania.
