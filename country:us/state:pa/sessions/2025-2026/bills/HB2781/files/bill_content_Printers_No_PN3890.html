AN ACT
Providing for limitations on the purchase of single-family
residences; and imposing powers and duties on the
Pennsylvania State Real Estate Commission and the Office of
the Attorney General.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Single-Family
Residence Access Act.
Section 2. Legislative findings and declarations.
(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds and declares as
follows:
(1) Covered entities buying up properties, often at
costs affordable for these entities but costs that are too
high for individuals, does not make housing affordable.
(2) This increases the costs of these homes on the
market or rent, inhibiting the ability for hardworking

"Single-family residence." A residential structure designed

detached single-family residences, condominiums, duplexes and

Section 4. Limitations on the purchase of single-family
residences.
A covered entity shall not purchase, acquire or offer to
purchase or acquire a fee simple title to a single-family
residence unless the single-family residence:
(1) Will be used as the principal residence of a person
with an ownership interest in the covered entity.
(2) Was constructed or will be purchased, acquired or
operated with money from the Federal, State or local
government.
(3) Is occupied by a tenant as defined in section 102 of
the act of April 6, 1951 (P.L.69, No.20), known as The
Landlord and Tenant Act of 1951.
(4) Was built for occupancy by a tenant as defined in
section 102 of The Landlord and Tenant Act of 1951.
(5) Is not publicly listed for sale at the time the
covered entity makes an offer on the single-family residence.
(6) Is acquired through a judicial or nonjudicial
foreclose sale, forfeiture, a deed in lieu of foreclosure or
the enforcement of a judgment, lien, debt or property
interest.
(7) Has been publicly listed for sale for at least 90
consecutive days immediately preceding the covered entity
first offering or accepting an offer to purchase or acquire
any interest in the single-family residence.

Section 5. Disclosure of status of covered entity.
(a) Creation of disclosure form.--The commission shall
develop and publish a form to disclose the status of a covered
entity.
(b) Disclosure to agents.--Upon making or prior to accepting
an offer to purchase or acquire a single-family residence under
section 3, a covered entity shall complete a form to disclose to
the seller or the seller's agent and the Office of the Attorney
General that the buyer of the single-family residence is a
covered entity before an agreement of transfer is signed.
(c) Publication.--Completed forms submitted to the Office of
the Attorney General shall be posted on the Office of the
Attorney General's publicly accessible Internet website.
Section 6. Penalties.
The Office of the Attorney General may prosecute a civil
action against a covered entity that fails to comply with the
requirements to comply with the 90-day waiting period or
complete the disclosure form published by the commission.
Section 7. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 180 days.
