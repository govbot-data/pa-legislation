AN ACT
Amending the act of June 13, 1967 (P.L.31, No.21), entitled "An
act to consolidate, editorially revise, and codify the public
welfare laws of the Commonwealth," in departmental powers and
duties as to supervision, providing for notification of child
care closure; and, in departmental powers as to licensing,
providing for notification of child care closure.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The act of June 13, 1967 (P.L.31, No.21), known
as the Human Services Code, is amended by adding a section to
read:
Section 912. Notification of Child Care Closure.--(a) A
children's institution that provides child care that intends to
PERMANENTLY close or cease its operations shall provide
notification in writing to the department, employes and parents
and guardians of children in the care of the institution no less
than thirty days prior to the anticipated closing. The notice
shall include a plan for the storage and preservation of all

(b) T his section does not apply if the reason for the
closure is a disaster emergency, as declared by the Governor
under section 20 of Article IV of the Constitution of

(B) THIS SECTION SHALL NOT APPLY IF THE REASON FOR THE
CLOSURE IS ANY OF THE FOLLOWING:
(1) A DISASTER EMERGENCY AS DECLARED BY THE GOVERNOR UNDER
SECTION 20 OF ARTICLE IV OF THE CONSTITUTION OF PENNSYLVANIA.
(2) AN EMERGENCY REMOVAL ACTION ISSUED BY THE DEPARTMENT.
(3) A FIRE, FLOOD, NATURAL DISASTER OR OTHER UNFORESEEN
EVENT.
(4) A CONDITION THAT COMPROMISES THE HEALTH OR SAFETY OF
CHILDREN OR STAFF.
(5) THE LOSS, CANCELLATION OR INABILITY TO OBTAIN REQUIRED
LIABILITY INSURANCE COVERAGE.
( 6) THE DEATH, PERMANENT DISABILITY OR SERIOUS ILLNESS OF
THE OWNER, OPERATOR OR ANOTHER INDIVIDUAL WHOSE PRESENCE IS
NECESSARY FOR THE LAWFUL OPERATION OF THE CHILDREN'S
INSTITUTION.
(7) THE LOSS OF LEGAL AUTHORITY TO OCCUPY THE PREMISES,
INCLUDING EVICTION, CONDEMNATION OR TERMINATION OF A LEASE
THROUGH NO FAULT OF THE CHILDREN'S INSTITUTION.
(8) ANOTHER CIRCUMSTANCE BEYOND THE REASONABLE CONTROL OF
THE CHILDREN'S INSTITUTION THAT MAKES CONTINUED OPERATION
IMPOSSIBLE.
(C) NOTICE UNDER THIS SECTION SHALL NOT CONSTITUTE AN
INVOLUNTARY CLOSURE.

(e) Miscellaneous Provisions
Section 1091. Notification of Child Care Closure.--(a) A
child care center or family child care home that intends to
PERMANENTLY close or cease its operations shall provide
notification in writing to the department, employees and parents
and guardians of children in the care of the child care center
or family child care home no less than thirty days prior to the

storage and preservation of all required records.

department .

SECTION 20 OF ARTICLE IV OF THE CONSTITUTION OF PENNSYLVANIA.

(3) A FIRE, FLOOD, NATURAL DISASTER OR OTHER UNFORESEEN
EVENT.
(4) A CONDITION THAT COMPROMISES THE HEALTH OR SAFETY OF

(5) THE LOSS, CANCELLATION OR INABILITY TO OBTAIN REQUIRED
