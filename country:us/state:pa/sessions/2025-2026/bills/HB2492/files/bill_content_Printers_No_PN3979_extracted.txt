The act of June 13, 1967 (P.L.31, No.21), known as the Human Services Code, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 912. Notification of Child Care Closure.--(a) A children's institution that provides child care that intends to PERMANENTLY close or cease its operations shall provide notification in writing to the department, employes and parents and guardians of children in the care of the institution no less than thirty days prior to the anticipated closing.
