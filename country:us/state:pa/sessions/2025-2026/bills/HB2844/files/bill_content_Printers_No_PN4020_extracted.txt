Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 6323. Grooming of a minor.(a) Offense defined.--(1) An individual 18 years of age or older commits an offense if the individual intentionally engages in a course of conduct directed at a minor who is under 14 years of age with the intent to entice, coerce, solicit or induce the minor to engage in prohibited sexual conduct with the individual or another person.(2) An individual commits an offense if the individual is a person responsible for the child's welfare and intentionally engages in a course of conduct directed at a 123456789101112131415161718 minor who is under 18 years of age with the intent to: (i) entice, coerce, solicit or induce the minor to engage in prohibited sexual conduct with the individual or another person; or(ii) develop an intimate or secretive relationship with the minor. (b) Grading.--(1) Except as provided under paragraph (2), an offense under subsection (a)(1) constitutes a misdemeanor of the first degree.(2) An offense under subsection (a)(2), or an offense under subsection (a)(1) where the victim is under 12 years of age, constitutes a felony of the third degree.(c) Rules of construction.--(1) An offense under subsection (a)(2)(ii) shall not apply to ordinary, lawful communication or contact between a person responsible for a child's welfare and a minor that is incidental to a professional, educational, athletic, religious or familial relationship.(2) Conduct that is open, transparent and known to the minor's parent, guardian or person responsible for the minor's welfare shall create a rebuttable presumption that the conduct does not constitute an intimate or secretive relationship under this section.(d) Definitions.--As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Course of conduct." The term shall mean the same as defined under section 2709.1(f) (relating to stalking)."Intimate or secretive relationship."
