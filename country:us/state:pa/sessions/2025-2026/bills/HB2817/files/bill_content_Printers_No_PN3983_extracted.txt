BROWN AND KUZMA, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON EDUCATION, OCTOBER 2, 2026 AN ACTAmending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An act relating to the public school system, including certain provisions applicable as well to private and parochial schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the laws relating thereto," in preliminary provisions, providing for career and technical education students occupational competency testing; and abrogating a regulation.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. The act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended by adding a section to read:Section 121.2. Career and Technical Education Students Occupational Competency Testing.-- (a) A school entity shall use one of the following methods to assess the occupational competency of a student of the school entity who has completed an approved career and technical education program:(1) completion of the appropriate assessment under the Pennsylvania Skills Certificate Program;(2) completion of another occupational competency assessment 1234567891011121314151617181920 approved by the Department of Education; or(3) attainment of an industry-based competency certification related to the CTE concentrator's program of study.(b) As used in this section, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"CTE concentrator." A student under Article XVIII who, by the end of a reporting year, will be reported as successfully completing at least fifty percent (50%) of the minimum technical instructional hours required under 22 Pa. Code Ch. 339 (relating to vocational education)."Industry-based competency certification." A credential that:(1) demonstrates a student's mastery of specific skills and knowledge required for a particular career or field; and(2) verifies that the student has met industry standards and is ready to enter the workforce."School entity." A school entity or an area career and technical school.Section 2. The regulations at 22 Pa. Code § 4.31(a) are abrogated to the extent of any inconsistency with this act.Section 3. This act shall take effect immediately.20260HB2817PN3983 - 2 - 12345678910111213141516171819202122
