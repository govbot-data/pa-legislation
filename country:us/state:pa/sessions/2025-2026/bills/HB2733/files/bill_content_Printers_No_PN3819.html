AN ACT
Providing for prenatal care benefits and for prenatal care leave
account; conferring powers and imposing duties on the
Department of Labor and Industry; and imposing penalties.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Banked Use
for Maternity Protection Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Account." A prenatal care leave account established under
section 5.
"Department." The Department of Labor and Industry of the
Commonwealth.
"Eligible employee." An employee who has worked at least 30
days for an employer.

"Employee." An individual who is employed by an employer
doing business in this Commonwealth.
"Employer." An entity employing one or more individuals in
this Commonwealth. The term does not include a Federal agency.
"Leave." The allotted amount of time approved by an employer
for the receipt of prenatal care leave.
"Pregnancy disclosure." A written or verbal notification
provided by an employee to an employer indicating pregnancy
status.
Section 3. Requirement to provide prenatal care leave.
An employer shall provide leave in accordance with this act
to eligible employees.
Section 4. Eligibility for prenatal care leave.
(a) Eligibility.--An employee who has worked at least 30
days in this Commonwealth for the same employer shall be
eligible to receive benefits under this act.
(b) Pregnancy disclosure permitted.--An employer may require
that an eligible employee provide a pregnancy disclosure.
Section 5. Prenatal care leave account.
(a) Establishment of account.--An employer shall establish a
prenatal care leave account for leave within two days of
notification from an employee of the employee's eligibility for
leave. The account shall be established separate from other
nonprenatal care leave provided by the employer to the eligible
employee.
(b) Amount of paid leave.--An employer shall place 24 hours
of paid leave into the account within two days of the
establishment of an account under subsection (a).

Section 7. Pay requirements.
(a) Compensation.--An employer shall compensate an eligible
employee at the same rate as the eligible employee is
compensated when not using leave.
(b) Accounting of leave time used.--Time used for leave
shall count as hours worked, including for determining
eligibility for:
(1) Benefits.
(2) Seniority accrual.
(3) Workplace protections.

(b) Exemption for certain employers.--An employer with 15 or

manner as determined by the department, for a six-month hardship
exemption.
Section 9. Retaliation.
An employer may not:
(1) Terminate an eligible employee for using leave in
accordance with this act.
(2) Discipline an eligible employee for using leave in
accordance with this act, including reducing the hours, pay
or assignments of an eligible employee.
Section 10. Regulations.
The department may promulgate regulations as necessary to
carry out the provisions of this act.
Section 11. Penalties.
An employer that violates this act shall be subject to a fine
of not more than $50,000 for each violation.
Section 12. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in one year.
