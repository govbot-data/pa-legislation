WILLIAMS, AUGUST 12, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LABOR AND INDUSTRY, AUGUST 18, 2026 AN ACTProviding for prenatal care benefits and for prenatal care leave account; conferring powers and imposing duties on the Department of Labor and Industry; and imposing penalties.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Banked Use for Maternity Protection Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Account." A prenatal care leave account established under section 5."Department." The Department of Labor and Industry of the Commonwealth."Eligible employee." An employee who has worked at least 30 days for an employer.123456789101112131415161718 "Employee." An individual who is employed by an employer doing business in this Commonwealth."Employer." An entity employing one or more individuals in this Commonwealth. The term does not include a Federal agency."Leave." The allotted amount of time approved by an employer for the receipt of prenatal care leave."Pregnancy disclosure." A written or verbal notification provided by an employee to an employer indicating pregnancy status.Section 3. Requirement to provide prenatal care leave.An employer shall provide leave in accordance with this act to eligible employees.Section 4. Eligibility for prenatal care leave.(a) Eligibility.--An employee who has worked at least 30 days in this Commonwealth for the same employer shall be eligible to receive benefits under this act.(b) Pregnancy disclosure permitted.--An employer may require that an eligible employee provide a pregnancy disclosure.Section 5. Prenatal care leave account.(a) Establishment of account.--An employer shall establish a prenatal care leave account for leave within two days of notification from an employee of the employee's eligibility for leave. The account shall be established separate from other nonprenatal care leave provided by the employer to the eligible employee.(b) Amount of paid leave.--An employer shall place 24 hours of paid leave into the account within two days of the establishment of an account under subsection (a).Section 6. Use of prenatal care leave.(a) Use.--An employer shall provide leave to an eligible 20260HB2733PN3819 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 employee for:(1) Routine prenatal medical appointments.(2) Diagnostic testing and screenings.(3) Pregnancy-related medical consultations.(4) Travel time to the appointments under paragraphs (1), (2) and (3).(b) Employer permitted to request documentation.--An employer may request documentation from an eligible employee for an appointment under subsection (a)(1), (2) and (3). An employer is not authorized to request medical information about the eligible employee.(c) Required use of nonprenatal leave not permitted.--An employer may not require that an eligible employee use other nonprenatal care leave provided by an employer in place of using prenatal care leave.Section 7. Pay requirements.(a) Compensation.--An employer shall compensate an eligible employee at the same rate as the eligible employee is compensated when not using leave.(b) Accounting of leave time used.--Time used for leave shall count as hours worked, including for determining eligibility for:(1) Benefits.(2) Seniority accrual.(3) Workplace protections.Section 8. Employer responsibilities.(a) Duties.--An employer shall:(1) Maintain a separate account for leave for an eligible employee.(2) Provide written notice to employees of the rights 20260HB2733PN3819 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 afforded under this act.(3) Follow Federal guidance on pregnancy accommodations, including guidance under 42 U.S.C. Ch. 21G (relating to pregnant worker fairness).(b) Exemption for certain employers.--An employer with 15 or fewer employees may apply to the department, on a form and in a manner as determined by the department, for a six-month hardship exemption.Section 9. Retaliation.An employer may not:(1) Terminate an eligible employee for using leave in accordance with this act.(2) Discipline an eligible employee for using leave in accordance with this act, including reducing the hours, pay or assignments of an eligible employee.Section 10. Regulations.The department may promulgate regulations as necessary to carry out the provisions of this act.Section 11. Penalties.An employer that violates this act shall be subject to a fine of not more than $50,000 for each violation.Section 12.
