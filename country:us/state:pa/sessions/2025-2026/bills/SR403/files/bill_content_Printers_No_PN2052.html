A RESOLUTION
Noting with great sadness the passing of the Honorable Timothy
Francis Murphy, whose life greatly benefited all those who
lived, served and worked with him; and extending heartfelt
condolences to Dr. Murphy's wife, Nanette Missig Murphy,
daughter, Bevin DeSantis, grandchildren, Thomasina, Luca and
Nico and many other family members and friends.
WHEREAS, The Senate of Pennsylvania mourns the loss of the
Honorable Timothy Francis Murphy, a former member of the Senate
of Pennsylvania, who passed away July 18, 2026, at 73 years of
age; and
WHEREAS, Born in Cleveland, Ohio, on September 11, 1952,
Timothy Francis Murphy was the son of John and Florence Rybicki
Murphy; and
WHEREAS, Dr. Murphy graduated from Walsh Jesuit High School,
Wheeling Jesuit College and Cleveland State University before
earning his doctoral degree from the University of Pittsburgh;
and
WHEREAS, In his early career, Dr. Murphy was a child
psychologist, appearing as a guest on local television and

WHEREAS, Dr. Murphy was an assistant professor of pediatrics
at the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and an

School of Public Health; and
WHEREAS, To his great credit, Dr. Murphy served this country
with honor and distinction as a Commander and psychologist with
the United States Navy Reserve Medical Service Corps from 2009
to 2017; and
WHEREAS, Dr. Murphy began his tenure with the Senate in 1996,
serving the 37th Senatorial District until 2003, and was the
author of the Pennsylvania Patient Bill of Rights; and
WHEREAS, Dr. Murphy went on to represent the 18th
Congressional District in the United States House of
Representatives until 2017; and
WHEREAS, During this time, Dr. Murphy was a leading voice for
mental health reform and was instrumental in authoring and
passing the 21st Century Cures Act; and
WHEREAS, Dr. Murphy also supported the continued operation of
the 911th Air Force Reserve Base in Moon Township and trade
protections for local manufacturing; and
WHEREAS, As a man of great faith, Dr. Murphy was a longtime
member of Saint Thomas More Church and served in leadership
positions with a variety of organizations in his community; and
WHEREAS, Dr. Murphy enjoyed playing the guitar, gardening,
fly fishing, hiking, camping and traveling; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate note with great sadness the passing
of the Honorable Timothy Francis Murphy, whose life greatly
benefited all those who lived, served and worked with him; and
