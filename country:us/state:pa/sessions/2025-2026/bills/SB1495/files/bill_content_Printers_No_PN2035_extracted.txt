Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Veteran Suicide Prevention Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Department." The Department of Military and Veterans Affairs of the Commonwealth."Task force." The Veteran Suicide Prevention Task Force established under section 3.Section 3. Veteran Suicide Prevention Task Force.(a) Establishment.--The Veteran Suicide Prevention Task 123456789101112131415161718 Force is established within the department to study veteran suicides in this Commonwealth and to provide recommendations to the Governor and General Assembly on actions to slow the rate of veteran suicide in this Commonwealth.(b) Powers and duties.--The task force shall study the monthly data provided by county coroners under section 4(a). The task force shall examine veteran suicide in this Commonwealth, including the total number of veteran suicides, proximity to veteran support efforts and proximity to mental health resources. The task force shall submit a report to the Governor and General Assembly as outlined under subsection (d).(c) Membership.--The task force shall be chaired by the Adjutant General or a designee. The Adjutant General shall be responsible for appointing the following members and the task force shall be composed of:(1) The chairman of the State Veterans Commission or a designee.(2) Two veterans affiliated with an advocacy group for veteran mental health and who reside in this Commonwealth.(3) A mental health care provider in this Commonwealth who has experience in providing mental health care treatment to returning veterans.(4) An individual from a Commonwealth advocacy group that represents the interests of veterans.(5) A health care provider in this Commonwealth that has experience serving as a medical officer and is a veteran of the armed services.(6) Two coroners in this Commonwealth.(7) A forensic pathologist who practices and resides in this Commonwealth.20260SB1495PN2035 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (8) The President of the Pennsylvania State Association of County Directors of Veterans Affairs or a designee.(9) Two members from the department.(10) A substance abuse and addiction treatment provider in this Commonwealth with experience in providing substance abuse and addiction treatment to veterans.(11) A judge that serves on a county veteran's court.(12) Two commanders of veteran service organizations from the State Veterans Commission.(13) Any individual the Adjutant General identifies as having relevant, salient and timely experience within the veteran's affairs health care system for veterans in this Commonwealth.(d) Report and recommendation to Governor and General Assembly.--No later than 30 months after the effective date of this subsection, the task force shall submit to the Governor and General Assembly a report outlining veteran suicide data along with a proposal to slow the rate of veteran suicide in this Commonwealth.Section 4. Required task force information.(a) Data sharing.--Each county coroner shall provide data to the task force once a month regarding veteran suicides that occurred that month in a manner determined by the department under subsection (c).
