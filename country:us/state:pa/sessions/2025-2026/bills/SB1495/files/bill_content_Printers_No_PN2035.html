AN ACT
Establishing the Veteran Suicide Prevention Task Force and
providing for its powers and duties; and imposing duties on
the Department of Military and Veterans Affairs.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Veteran
Suicide Prevention Act.
Section 2. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this act shall
have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Department." The Department of Military and Veterans
Affairs of the Commonwealth.
"Task force." The Veteran Suicide Prevention Task Force
established under section 3.
Section 3. Veteran Suicide Prevention Task Force.
(a) Establishment.--The Veteran Suicide Prevention Task

Force is established within the department to study veteran
suicides in this Commonwealth and to provide recommendations to
the Governor and General Assembly on actions to slow the rate of
veteran suicide in this Commonwealth.
(b) Powers and duties.--The task force shall study the
monthly data provided by county coroners under section 4(a). The
task force shall examine veteran suicide in this Commonwealth,
including the total number of veteran suicides, proximity to
veteran support efforts and proximity to mental health
resources. The task force shall submit a report to the Governor
and General Assembly as outlined under subsection (d).
(c) Membership.--The task force shall be chaired by the
Adjutant General or a designee. The Adjutant General shall be
responsible for appointing the following members and the task
force shall be composed of:
(1) The chairman of the State Veterans Commission or a
designee.
(2) Two veterans affiliated with an advocacy group for
veteran mental health and who reside in this Commonwealth.
(3) A mental health care provider in this Commonwealth
who has experience in providing mental health care treatment
to returning veterans.
(4) An individual from a Commonwealth advocacy group
that represents the interests of veterans.
(5) A health care provider in this Commonwealth that has
experience serving as a medical officer and is a veteran of
the armed services.
(6) Two coroners in this Commonwealth.
(7) A forensic pathologist who practices and resides in
this Commonwealth.

this subsection, the task force shall submit to the Governor and
General Assembly a report outlining veteran suicide data along
with a proposal to slow the rate of veteran suicide in this
Commonwealth.
Section 4. Required task force information.
(a) Data sharing.--Each county coroner shall provide data to
the task force once a month regarding veteran suicides that
occurred that month in a manner determined by the department
under subsection (c). This subsection shall expire two years
after the effective date of this subsection.
(b) Data information.--The data required to be shared under
subsection (a) must contain the following information about the
veteran:
(1) Sex.
(2) Age.
