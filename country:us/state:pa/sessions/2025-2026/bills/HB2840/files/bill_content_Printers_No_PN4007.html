AN ACT
Amending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes, in theft and related offenses, further
providing for the offense of theft by extortion; and, in
criminal history record information, providing for request
and removal of the dissemination of booking photographs.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 3923 of Title 18 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding subsections to read:
§ 3923. Theft by extortion.
* * *
(a.1) Prohibition on consideration for the destruction and
removal of booking photographs.--A person that is engaged in
publishing or otherwise disseminating a booking photograph
commits an offense if the person solicits or accepts the payment
of a fee or other consideration to remove and destroy or refrain
from publishing or otherwise disseminating the booking
photograph. Each payment or other consideration solicited or
accepted in violation of this subsection constitutes a separate

(c) Definition.--As used in this section, the term "booking
photograph" means a photograph or image of an individual
generated by law enforcement for identification purposes in
accordance with 61 Pa.C.S. § 1103 (relating to recording system
for identification of criminal offenders).
Section 2. Title 18 is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 9126. Request and removal of the dissemination of booking
photographs.
(a) Requirements.--A noncriminal justice agency that is
engaged in publishing or otherwise disseminating a booking
photograph shall:
(1) Clearly and conspicuously post on the noncriminal
justice agency's publicly accessible Internet website an
active and monitored email address, telephone number and
mailing address by which an individual may request the
removal and destruction of the individual's booking
photograph.
(2) Remove and destroy an individual's booking
photograph within seven calendar days of receiving a request
for removal and destruction from the individual if:
(i) The booking photograph relates to criminal
charges that:
(A) resulted in a final disposition other than a
conviction;
(B) are subject to a court order for limited
access as provided in section 9122.1 (relating to
petition for limited access);
(C) were granted clean slate limited access

(D) were expunged or vacated pursuant to a court

(ii) The individual requesting removal and

(b) Sanction.--In addition to any relief provided under
section 9183 (relating to civil actions), a noncriminal justice
agency that fails to comply with subsection (a)(2) shall be
liable for a civil penalty of $100 per day for each day
following the expiration of the seven-calendar-day period during
which the booking photograph remains published or otherwise
disseminated.
(c) Definition.--As used in this section, the term "booking
photograph" means a photograph or image of an individual
generated by law enforcement for identification purposes in
accordance with 61 Pa.C.S. § 1103 (relating to recording system
for identification of criminal offenders).
Section 3. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
