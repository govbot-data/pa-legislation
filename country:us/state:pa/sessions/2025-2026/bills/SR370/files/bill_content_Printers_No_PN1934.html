A RESOLUTION
Observing September 15 through October 15, 2026, as "Hispanic
Heritage Month" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, "National Hispanic Heritage Month" is celebrated
annually from September 15 through October 15 to honor the
histories, cultures and contributions of Americans whose
ancestors came from Mexico, the Caribbean, Central and South
America and Spain; and
WHEREAS, This observance began as "Hispanic Heritage Week" in
1968 and expanded to a full-month observance in 1988; and
WHEREAS, September was chosen to mark the beginning of this
observance because it is the anniversary of independence for
Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras and Nicaragua while
also encompassing the independence anniversaries of Mexico and
Chile; and
WHEREAS, The Hispanic and Latino community is one of the
fastest-growing populations in this Commonwealth, making up 9%
of the State's population; and

WHEREAS, Pennsylvanians of Puerto Rican descent represent the
largest share of the Hispanic population in this Commonwealth,
with other significant communities tracing their roots to
Mexico, the Caribbean, Central and South America and Spain; and
WHEREAS, Cities such as Philadelphia, Reading, Allentown and
Lancaster are home to some of the largest established Hispanic
communities in this Commonwealth that have shaped local
economies, cultures and civic life; and
WHEREAS, Hispanic and Latino Pennsylvanians perform
outstanding work in business, education, health care, public
service and the armed forces, which strengthens the social and
economic structure of our Commonwealth; and
WHEREAS, The Hispanic and Latino community continues to grow
participation on the Commonwealth's electorate and workforce,
underscoring the importance of civil participation and
representation in shaping Pennsylvania's future; and
WHEREAS, It is fitting that the Senate of Pennsylvania
recognize the great contributions of Hispanic and Latino
individuals and families to the history, culture and prosperity
of this Commonwealth; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate observe September 15 through
October 15, 2026, as "Hispanic Heritage Month" in Pennsylvania;
and be it further
RESOLVED, That the Senate recognize the achievements of
Hispanic Americans in our Commonwealth.
