2025-2026 Regular Session SR 370 PN 1934 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1934 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.370 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY TARTAGLIONE, PICOZZI, STREET, MALONE, HAYWOOD, KANE, VOGEL, SCHWANK, COSTA, COMITTA, CAPPELLETTI, ARGALL AND SANTARSIERO, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO RULES AND EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 A RESOLUTIONObserving September 15 through October 15, 2026, as "Hispanic Heritage Month" in Pennsylvania.WHEREAS, "National Hispanic Heritage Month" is celebrated annually from September 15 through October 15 to honor the histories, cultures and contributions of Americans whose ancestors came from Mexico, the Caribbean, Central and South America and Spain; andWHEREAS, This observance began as "Hispanic Heritage Week" in 1968 and expanded to a full-month observance in 1988; andWHEREAS, September was chosen to mark the beginning of this observance because it is the anniversary of independence for Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras and Nicaragua while also encompassing the independence anniversaries of Mexico and Chile; andWHEREAS, The Hispanic and Latino community is one of the fastest-growing populations in this Commonwealth, making up 9% of the State's population; and1234567891011121314151617 WHEREAS, Pennsylvanians of Puerto Rican descent represent the largest share of the Hispanic population in this Commonwealth, with other significant communities tracing their roots to Mexico, the Caribbean, Central and South America and Spain; andWHEREAS, Cities such as Philadelphia, Reading, Allentown and Lancaster are home to some of the largest established Hispanic communities in this Commonwealth that have shaped local economies, cultures and civic life; andWHEREAS, Hispanic and Latino Pennsylvanians perform outstanding work in business, education, health care, public service and the armed forces, which strengthens the social and economic structure of our Commonwealth; andWHEREAS, The Hispanic and Latino community continues to grow participation on the Commonwealth's electorate and workforce, underscoring the importance of civil participation and representation in shaping Pennsylvania's future; andWHEREAS, It is fitting that the Senate of Pennsylvania recognize the great contributions of Hispanic and Latino individuals and families to the history, culture and prosperity of this Commonwealth;
