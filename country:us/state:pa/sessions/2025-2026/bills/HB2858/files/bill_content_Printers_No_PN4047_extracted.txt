2025-2026 Regular Session HB 2858 PN 4047 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 4047 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIAHOUSE BILL No.2858 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BOROWSKI, CERRATO, PROKOPIAK, KHAN, HILL-EVANS, BRENNAN, SANCHEZ, DEASY AND CEPEDA-FREYTIZ, OCTOBER 15, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LABOR AND INDUSTRY, OCTOBER 16, 2026 AN ACTProviding for construction worker and site supervisor training requirements; and conferring powers and imposing duties on the Department of Labor and Industry.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Construction Worker Safety Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Contractor." A person that, in any capacity for compensation, engages in construction. The term includes a subcontractor of any tier."Department." The Department of Labor and Industry of the Commonwealth. "Major building." A building that is any of the following:123456789101112131415161718 (1) more than three stories in height;(2) more than 40 feet in height; or(3) 10,000 square feet or more of lot space, regardless of height."Secretary." The Secretary of Labor and Industry of the Commonwealth or an authorized representative."Subcontractor." A person that has been contracted to furnish labor or materials to, or has performed labor for, a contractor or another subcontractor in connection with a contract. The term does not include a person that only supplies material for a construction project.Section 3. Training required.(a) Construction worker required course.--A construction worker must successfully complete a course in construction safety and health approved by the United States Occupational Safety and Health Administration that is at least 10 hours in duration prior to performing any work within this Commonwealth.(b) Proof of completion.--A construction worker shall carry proof of completion of the approved training course on the construction worker's person while on a construction site at all times.(c) Exception.--The requirements of this section shall not apply to a delivery person, design professional and the design professional's staff, special inspector or similar persons performing duties at a construction site whose work is not considered construction regulated by a permit.Section 4. Site supervisor.(a) Supervisor required onsite.--A site supervisor shall be present at a site performing work upon a major building in this Commonwealth.20260HB2858PN4047 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (b) A site supervisor must successfully complete a course in construction safety and health approved by the United States Occupational Safety and Health Administration that is at least 30 hours in duration prior to a subordinate performing any construction work at the site.(c) Proof of completion.--A site supervisor shall carry proof of completion of the approved training course on the site supervisor's person while onsite at all times.Section 5. Duty of licensed contractor.A licensed contractor who employs a construction worker or site supervisor subject to the requirements of this act shall maintain a copy of the written proof issued by the training provider upon completion of the training course required under this act and make the written proof available to the department upon request.Section 6. Powers and duties of department.(a) Enforcement.--The department shall enforce this act.(b) Regulations.--The department may promulgate regulations as necessary to implement and administer this act.(c) Acceptance of identification.--The department shall accept the following as proof that a construction worker or site supervisor has successfully completed the required training under this act:(1) a wallet-sized identification card, including, at a minimum, the name of the applicant, an issue date and the level of training completed; or(2) other written proof issued by the training provider, including, at a minimum, the name of the applicant, an issue date and the level of training completed.Section 7.
