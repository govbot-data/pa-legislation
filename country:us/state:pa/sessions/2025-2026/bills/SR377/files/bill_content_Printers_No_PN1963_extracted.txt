2025-2026 Regular Session SR 377 PN 1963 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 1963 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE RESOLUTION No.377 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY BAKER AND CAPPELLETTI, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 REFERRED TO JUDICIARY, SEPTEMBER 25, 2026 A RESOLUTIONDirecting the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to conduct a study on costs to crime victims regarding the impoundment and holding of their vehicles and other property as part of crime investigations and to issue a report of its findings to the Senate.WHEREAS, In the course of an investigation of a crime, a law enforcement agency may need to take possession of a vehicle or other property in order to solve the crime; andWHEREAS, If a law enforcement agency temporarily takes a crime victim's vehicle or other property as part of a crime investigation, the crime victim, in some instances, must pay in order for the impounded vehicle or other property to be returned; andWHEREAS, A crime victim is not responsible for the action of another and should not be liable for paying unnecessary costs for the return of a vehicle or other property that the crime victim owns; andWHEREAS, A crime victim should not have to pay for the return of the crime victim's vehicle or other property when the crime victim is not at fault; and1234567891011121314151617181920 WHEREAS, It is incumbent on the Commonwealth to ensure that crime victims are able to get their property back in a timely manner with minimal to no cost to them; andWHEREAS, Due to no fault of their own, many crime victims are forced to pay high fees in order to have their vehicle or other property returned to them after their vehicle or other property is released from evidence; andWHEREAS, Crime victims should not have to pay the price for actions done by criminal actors; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the Senate direct the Legislative Budget and Finance Committee to conduct a study on costs to crime victims regarding the impoundment and holding of their vehicles and other property as part of crime investigations and to issue a report of its findings to the Senate; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the study include findings regarding:(1) the cost to a law enforcement agency to impound and hold a vehicle or other property as part of a crime investigation;(2) the cost to a municipality to impound and hold a vehicle or other property as part of a crime investigation;(3) feasible alternatives to the impoundment and holding of a victim's vehicle or other property, including a review of current practices for the impoundment and holding by a municipality;(4) best practices of a county to eliminate out-of-pocket costs to a crime victim when the crime victim's vehicle or other property is taken as part of a crime investigation;(5) the cost that a crime victim may incur in order for the crime victim's vehicle or other property to be returned;
