A RESOLUTION
Urging the Congress of the United States to pass Federal
legislation that would regulate frontier artificial
intelligence models.
WHEREAS, Artificial intelligence (AI) technologies have
transformative potential for our economy and many industries,
including education, energy, entertainment, finance, health
care, information technology, legal services, manufacturing,
retail, telecommunications and transportation; and
WHEREAS, Frontier AI models are at the forefront of
innovation in AI and the most advanced AI systems available,
requiring high-intensity training using large datasets and being
designed for generality of output; and
WHEREAS, Researchers have raised concerns about several areas
of potential harm to society that frontier AI models could
cause, including the propagation of unreliable information,
labor market disruption, cybersecurity risks and malicious use
to create weapons of mass destruction; and

WHEREAS, There have been multiple recent incidents of AI
models being misaligned with the intentions and goals of the AI
developers, to the extent that they violate the security of
other entities and disobey protocols set in place for them to
follow; and
WHEREAS, Notable companies that are leading in the
development of frontier AI models have supported risk-prevention
measures, including endorsements of laws enacted by other
states; and
WHEREAS, World leaders from many countries allied with the
United States have issued statements urging stronger regulation
of frontier AI model development; and
WHEREAS, President Donald J. Trump signed Executive Order
14409 on June 2, 2026, which acknowledged new national security
considerations stemming from advanced AI capabilities; and
WHEREAS, Federal legislation, formally titled as the Frontier
Risk Oversight, National Transparency, Independent Evaluation,
and Reporting Act, has been introduced, which would regulate
frontier AI models; and
WHEREAS, Several states, including Pennsylvania, have enacted
or introduced bipartisan legislation to require safety measures
for frontier AI models and the AI models' continued development;
and
WHEREAS, States must maintain their right to create
legislation and regulations on artificial intelligence and its
use so that the states can protect the interests of local
residents; and
WHEREAS, The Federal Government should collaborate with other
countries and seek to create or contribute to international
standards regarding the safe development and deployment of

frontier AI models; and
WHEREAS, Establishing reasonable safety standards for
frontier AI models through a means of cooperative federalism and
international engagement is necessary for a future where AI is a
benefit to the public; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania urge the Congress of the United
States to pass Federal legislation that would regulate frontier
artificial intelligence models; and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania:
(1) Affirm the sovereign right and constitutional
responsibility of the Pennsylvania General Assembly to
legislate in the interest of Pennsylvania residents,
including in matters related to the use and effects of
artificial intelligence technology.
(2) Urge United States President Donald J. Trump to
promote reasonable safety standards for frontier AI models,
both nationally and internationally.
(3) Call upon Pennsylvania's Congressional delegation to
advocate for a regulatory framework for frontier AI models
that respects state authority, promotes innovation and
safeguards public welfare;
and be it further
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania direct the Chief Clerk of the House
of Representatives to transmit a copy of this resolution to the
Office of the White House, the President of the United States
Senate, the Speaker of the United States House of
Representatives and each member of Congress from Pennsylvania.
