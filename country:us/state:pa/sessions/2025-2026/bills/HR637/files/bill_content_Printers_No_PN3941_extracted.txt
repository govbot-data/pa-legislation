Trump signed Executive Order 14409 on June 2, 2026, which acknowledged new national security considerations stemming from advanced AI capabilities; andWHEREAS, Federal legislation, formally titled as the Frontier Risk Oversight, National Transparency, Independent Evaluation, and Reporting Act, has been introduced, which would regulate frontier AI models; andWHEREAS, Several states, including Pennsylvania, have enacted or introduced bipartisan legislation to require safety measures for frontier AI models and the AI models' continued development; andWHEREAS, States must maintain their right to create legislation and regulations on artificial intelligence and its use so that the states can protect the interests of local residents; andWHEREAS, The Federal Government should collaborate with other countries and seek to create or contribute to international standards regarding the safe development and deployment of 20260HR0637PN3941 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 frontier AI models; andWHEREAS, Establishing reasonable safety standards for frontier AI models through a means of cooperative federalism and international engagement is necessary for a future where AI is a benefit to the public; therefore be itRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania urge the Congress of the United States to pass Federal legislation that would regulate frontier artificial intelligence models; and be it furtherRESOLVED, That the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania:(1) Affirm the sovereign right and constitutional responsibility of the Pennsylvania General Assembly to legislate in the interest of Pennsylvania residents, including in matters related to the use and effects of artificial intelligence technology.(2) Urge United States President Donald J.
