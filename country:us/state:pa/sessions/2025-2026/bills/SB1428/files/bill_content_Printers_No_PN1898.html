AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in registration of vehicles, further providing for
veteran plates and placard, for special plates for recipients
of Purple Heart, repealing provisions relating to special
plates for Pearl Harbor survivors, further providing for
special plates for veterans of Korean War, for special plates
for veterans of Persian Gulf War, for Pennsylvania monument
registration plate, for special plates for recipients of
Expeditionary Forces Medal, for special plates for recipients
of Global War on Terrorism Expeditionary Medal, repealing
provisions relating to special plates for World War II
veterans and further providing for special plates for
individuals in the service of the United States Merchant
Marine, for special plates for veterans of Vietnam Conflict,
for special motorcycle plates related to veterans, for
Operation Iraqi Freedom veterans plate, for Operation
Inherent Resolve veterans plate, for Operation Enduring
Freedom veterans plate, for Afghanistan and Iraq veterans
plate, for special plates for veterans, for special plates
for current members of the armed forces of the United States,
for Gold Star Family plate, for special plates for recipients
of Silver Star, for special plates for United States military
airborne units, for Blue Star Family plate, for special
plates for recipients of Air Medal, for special plates for
recipients of Bronze Star, for special plates for recipients
of Bronze Star for Valor, for special plates for recipients
of Soldier's Medal, for special plates for recipients of
Presidential Service Badge, for special plates for recipients
of Legion of Merit, for special plates for recipients of
Borinqueneers Congressional Gold Medal, for special plates
for recipients of Distinguished Service Cross, Distinguished
Flying Cross, Navy Cross or Air Force Cross, for special
plates for recipients of Combat Action Badge, Combat
Infantryman Badge, Combat Action Ribbon, Combat Action Medal

§ 1342. Veteran plates and placard.

motorcycle, motor home, trailer or truck with a registered gross
weight of not more than 14,000 pounds. In the case of a
motorcycle, the department shall issue a decal containing the
international symbol for access for persons with disabilities
and the words "disabled veteran" for display on the registration
plate. Five dollars of the fee paid by an applicant under this

§ 1346. Special plates for recipients of Purple Heart.
Upon application of any person who is a recipient of the
Purple Heart, accompanied by a fee of $5 which shall be in
addition to the fee imposed by the department for the plate, the
department shall issue to such person a special registration
plate designating the vehicle so licensed as belonging to a
person who is a recipient of the Purple Heart. A severely
disabled veteran, as described in section 1342(a) (relating to
veteran plates and placard), who is qualified to receive a plate
under this section may also elect to receive a placard under
section 1342(b). The special registration plate may be used only
on a passenger car, motor home, trailer, motorcycle or truck
with a registered gross weight of not more than 14,000 pounds.
Five dollars of the fee paid by an applicant under this section
shall be deposited into the Veterans' Trust Fund.

§ 1368.4. Special plates for recipients of Borinqueneers
Congressional Gold Medal.
(a) Issuance.--Upon application of a person who is a
recipient of the Borinqueneers Congressional Gold Medal,
accompanied by a fee of [$26] $31 which shall be in addition to
the registration fee and any documentation the department
requires, the department shall issue to the person a special
registration plate designating the vehicle so licensed as
belonging to a person who is a recipient of the Borinqueneers
Congressional Gold Medal. Five dollars of the fee paid by an
applicant under this section shall be deposited into the
Veterans' Trust Fund.
(b) Limitation.--A special registration plate under this
section may be used only on a passenger car, motorcycle, motor
home, trailer or truck with a registered gross weight of not
more than 14,000 pounds.

§ 1369.3. Special plates for veterans of an ally foreign
country.
Upon application of a person who was a citizen of a foreign
country that allied with the United States in a military
conflict and served in a military branch of that foreign country
during the military conflict, accompanied by a fee of [$20] $25
which shall be in addition to the registration fee and by such
documentation as the department shall require, the department
shall issue to the person a special registration plate
designating the vehicle so licensed as belonging to a person who
is a veteran of a military branch of a foreign country that
allied with the United States in a military conflict. The
special registration plate may be used only on a passenger car,
motorcycle, motor home, trailer or truck with a registered gross
weight of not more than 14,000 pounds. Five dollars of the fee
paid by an applicant under this section shall be deposited into
the Veterans' Trust Fund.
