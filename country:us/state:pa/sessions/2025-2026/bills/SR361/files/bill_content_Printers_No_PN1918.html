A RESOLUTION
Recognizing September 10, 2026, as "World Suicide Prevention
Day" in Pennsylvania.
WHEREAS, Suicidal behavior is a major public health concern;
and
WHEREAS, Cultures of silence and post-suicide stigmas still
exist both in the United States and in many societies worldwide;
and
WHEREAS, Around the globe, suicide ranks as a leading cause
of death and remains a major preventable cause of premature
death; and
WHEREAS, More than 720,000 people are estimated to die from
suicide each year, with one death from suicide occurring every
40 seconds; and
WHEREAS, In the United States, suicide is the 11th leading
cause of death; and
WHEREAS, In Pennsylvania, 1,955 people died from suicide in
2022; and

WHEREAS, Survivors of suicide are 25 times more likely to
commit a suicidal act in the future, underscoring the importance
of continued prevention; and
WHEREAS, "World Suicide Prevention Day" is part of a
worldwide suicide prevention initiative by the International
Association for Suicide Prevention and the World Health
Organization; and
WHEREAS, "World Suicide Prevention Day" encourages
individuals, communities, organizations and governments to
engage in open, honest discussions about mental health and
suicide; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the Senate recognize September 10, 2026, as
"World Suicide Prevention Day" in Pennsylvania.
