AN ACT
Amending Title 34 (Game) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in Pennsylvania Game Commission, further providing
for Game Commission officers and employees; and, in
enforcement, further providing for powers and duties of
enforcement officers.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 303(c) of Title 34 of the Pennsylvania
Consolidated Statutes is amended to read:
§ 303. Game Commission officers and employees.
* * *
(c) Power and authority.--Every officer, employee or
representative of the commission in the exercise of their powers
and duties shall have the right and authority, with a valid
warrant or under a judicially recognized exception to the
warrant requirement , to go upon or enter any property, posted or
otherwise, outside of buildings.
Section 2. Section 901 of Title 34 is amended by adding a
subsection to read:
