REPORTED FROM COMMITTEE ON ENERGY, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, AS AMENDED, SEPTEMBER 30, 2026A RESOLUTIONDirecting the Joint State Government Commission to conduct a study and issue a report on waste heat recovery in this Commonwealth.WHEREAS, Data centers require significant inputs of energy and water to cool heat produced by daily operations; andWHEREAS, The cooling operations of data centers present a challenge to consumers by competing for energy and water resources; andWHEREAS, Waste heat recovery serves as a potential solution to alleviate the negative effects of data centers by repurposing waste heat produced by data centers to meet heating demands, reducing the energy and water input needed for daily operations of data centers; andWHEREAS, Waste heat recovery has been tried and tested successfully in cases domestically and in other countries; andWHEREAS, The House of Representatives shall promote potential solutions to alleviate the concerns of Pennsylvanians regarding
