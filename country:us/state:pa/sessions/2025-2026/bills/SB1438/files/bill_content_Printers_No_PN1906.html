AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in registration of vehicles, providing for Green
Star Family plate.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1365.1. Green Star Family plate.
(a) Issuance.--Upon application of a family member of a
person who died by suicide related to the person's military
service and who was discharged or released from service under
conditions other than dishonorable, accompanied by a fee of $28 ,
which shall be in addition to the annual or biennial
registration fee and by such documentation as the department
shall require, the department shall issue to the family member a
special registration plate designating the vehicle so licensed
as belonging to a family member of a person who died by suicide
related to the person's military service. The department shall

design and produce the special registration plate carrying the
Green Star. The words "Green Star Family" shall be clearly
displayed along the bottom of the plate. The special
registration plate may be used only on a passenger car or truck
with a registered gross weight of not more than 14,000 pounds.
(b) Documentation required.--An applicant for a Green Star
Family plate shall certify on a form approved by the department
that the applicant is a family member of a person who died by
suicide related to the person's military service.
(c) Definition.--As used in this section, the term "family
member" includes the following:
(1) Widow.
(2) Widower.
(3) Mother.
(4) Father.
(5) Stepmother.
(6) Stepfather.
(7) Mother through adoption.
(8) Father through adoption.
(9) Foster mother in loco parentis.
(10) Foster father in loco parentis.
(11) Son.
(12) Daughter.
(13) Stepson.
(14) Stepdaughter.
(15) Son by adoption.
(16) Daughter by adoption.
(17) Brother.
(18) Sister.
(19) Half brother.
