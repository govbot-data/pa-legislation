AN ACT
Amending Titles 25 (Elections) and 75 (Vehicles) of the
Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in changes in records,
further providing for death of registrant; in enforcement,
providing for referral for prosecution; in provisions
contingent on Federal law, further providing for removal of
electors and providing for certification of list maintenance
and for registration records; in licensing of drivers,
further providing for notice of change of name or address;
imposing duties on the Secretary of the Commonwealth to
prepare a report; and imposing penalties.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:

Section 2. Title 25 is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1805. Referral for prosecution.
(a) Authorization.--If the secretary determines that a
person has intentionally or knowingly failed in the performance
of official duties under section 1505 (relating to death of
registrant), 1901 (relating to removal of electors), 1901.1
(relating to certification of list maintenance) or 1901.2
(relating to registration records), the secretary shall refer
the matter to the appropriate prosecutorial officer, whose duty
shall be to institute criminal proceedings as the prosecutorial
officer deems necessary.
(b) Definitions.-- As used in this section, the following
words and phrases shall have the meanings given to them in this
subsection unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Prosecutorial officer." The Attorney General of the
Commonwealth or a district attorney in this Commonwealth.

§ 1901.2. Registration records.
If the registration of a registered elector is canceled for
any cause, the commission shall mark on the registration record
of the elector the word "canceled" and the date and cause of
cancellation and shall remove the record from the general and
district registers. The record shall be kept for five years
after which the commission may destroy it.

Section 6. Not later than four years after the effective
date of this section, the Secretary of the Commonwealth shall
prepare a report regarding the implementation and effectiveness
of this act and shall submit the report to the chair and
minority chair of the State Government Committee of the Senate
and the chair and minority chair of the State Government
Committee of the House of Representatives. The report must
include:
(1) The number of registered electors erroneously or
illegally removed from the registration rolls, including, to
the extent known, the number of affected individuals whose
ability to vote in an election was affected as a result of
the removal.
(2) The number of individuals determined to have been
registered to vote despite not meeting the eligibility
requirements for registration, categorized by the basis for
ineligibility.
(3) The number of registrations removed under this act,
categorized by the basis for removal.
(4) Recommendations for improving the accuracy and
administration of voter list maintenance and reducing
erroneous removals or registrations.
Section 7. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
