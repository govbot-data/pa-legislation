§ 5302. DEFINITIONS.
THE FOLLOWING WORDS AND PHRASES WHEN USED IN THIS CHAPTER
SHALL HAVE THE MEANINGS GIVEN TO THEM IN THIS SECTION UNLESS THE
CONTEXT CLEARLY INDICATES OTHERWISE:
"911 AUTHORITY." AS FOLLOWS:
(1) THE GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY THAT OPERATES OR HAS
ADMINISTRATIVE AUTHORITY OVER ANY ASPECT OF A COMMUNICATIONS
NETWORK FOR THE RECEIPT OF 911 TRAFFIC AT NG911 DELIVERY
POINTS AND FOR THE TRANSMISSION OF TRAFFIC FROM THAT POINT TO
PSAPS.
(2) THE PENNSYLVANIA EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY.
* * *
"911 TRAFFIC." TRANSMISSIONS CONSISTING OF 911 CALLS, AS
DEFINED IN 47 CFR CH. 1 SUBCH. A PT. 9 (RELATING TO 911
REQUIREMENTS) OR SUCCESSOR REGULATIONS, AND INFORMATION ABOUT
CALLING PARTIES' LOCATIONS, ORIGINATING TELEPHONE NUMBERS AND
ROUTING INFORMATION TRANSMITTED WITH THE CALLS AND TEXT
MESSAGES.
"988 CRISIS CALL CENTER." A CALL CENTER DESIGNATED BY THE
COMMONWEALTH TO RESPOND TO STATEWIDE OR REGIONAL CALLS FROM THE
988 SUICIDE AND CRISIS LIFELINE.
"988 SUICIDE AND CRISIS LIFELINE." THE UNIVERSAL TELEPHONE
NUMBER WITHIN THE UNITED STATES DESIGNATED FOR THE PURPOSE OF
THE NATIONAL SUICIDE PREVENTION AND MENTAL HEALTH CRISIS HOTLINE

"CALL PROCESSING RECORD." A RECORD STORED IN A NEXT
GENERATION 911 TECHNOLOGY DATABASE THAT DERIVED FROM THE PSAP
CALL HANDLING EQUIPMENT AND NEXT GENERATION 911 TECHNOLOGY,
WHICH MAY INCLUDE TRANSACTIONAL CALL DETAILS, AUDIO, MEDIA,

"COMMONLY ACCEPTED INDUSTRY STANDARDS." THE TECHNICAL
STANDARDS FOLLOWED BY THE COMMUNICATIONS INDUSTRY FOR NETWORK,
DEVICE AND INTERNET PROTOCOL CONNECTIVITY THAT:
(1) ENABLE INTEROPERABILITY;
(2) ARE DEVELOPED AND APPROVED BY A STANDARDS
DEVELOPMENT ORGANIZATION THAT IS ACCREDITED BY A UNITED
STATES STANDARDS BODY SUCH AS THE AMERICAN NATIONAL STANDARDS
INSTITUTE, THE NATIONAL EMERGENCY NUMBER ASSOCIATION, THE
INTERNET ENGINEERING TASK FORCE, THE ASSOCIATION OF PUBLIC-
SAFETY COMMUNICATIONS OFFICIALS OR AN EQUIVALENT
INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS BODY IN A PROCESS THAT:
(I) IS OPEN TO THE PUBLIC, INCLUDING OPEN FOR
PARTICIPATION BY ANY PERSON; AND
(II) PROVIDES FOR A CONFLICT RESOLUTION PROCESS;
(3) ARE SUBJECT TO AN OPEN COMMENT AND INPUT PROCESS
BEFORE BEING FINALIZED BY THE STANDARDS DEVELOPMENT
ORGANIZATION;
(4) ARE CONSENSUS-BASED; AND
(5) ARE MADE PUBLICLY AVAILABLE ONCE APPROVED.
