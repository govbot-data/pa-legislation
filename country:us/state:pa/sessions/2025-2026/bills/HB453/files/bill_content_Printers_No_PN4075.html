Section 3. The definition of "emergency responder" in

§ 75B01. DEFINITIONS.
THE FOLLOWING WORDS AND PHRASES WHEN USED IN THIS CHAPTER
SHALL HAVE THE MEANINGS GIVEN TO THEM IN THIS SECTION UNLESS THE
CONTEXT CLEARLY INDICATES OTHERWISE:
"FULL HEALTH BENEFITS." THE SAME LEVEL OF HEALTH INSURANCE
COVERAGE FROM THE CARRIER UTILIZED BY A LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER
CURRENTLY EMPLOYED BY A MUNICIPALITY OR REGIONAL POLICE
DEPARTMENT.
"HEALTH CARE SERVICES." ANY TREATMENT, ADMISSION, PROCEDURE,
MEDICAL SUPPLIES AND EQUIPMENT OR OTHER SERVICES, INCLUDING
BEHAVIORAL HEALTH, PRESCRIBED OR OTHERWISE PROVIDED OR PROPOSED
TO BE PROVIDED BY A HEALTH CARE PROVIDER TO A PATIENT FOR THE
DIAGNOSIS, PREVENTION, TREATMENT, CURE OR RELIEF OF A HEALTH
CONDITION, ILLNESS, INJURY OR DISEASE.
"LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER." A FULL-TIME EMPLOYEE ASSIGNED TO
CRIMINAL OR TRAFFIC LAW ENFORCEMENT DUTIES OF:
(1) A POLICE DEPARTMENT OF A MUNICIPALITY; OR
(2) A REGIONAL POLICE DEPARTMENT.
"MEDICARE." HEALTH BENEFITS PROVIDED UNDER 42 U.S.C. CH. 7
SUBCH. XVIII (RELATING TO HEALTH INSURANCE FOR AGED AND
DISABLED).
"MUNICIPALITY." A COUNTY, CITY, BOROUGH, INCORPORATED TOWN
OR TOWNSHIP.
"POLICE DEPARTMENT." AS DEFINED IN 53 PA.C.S. § 2162
(RELATING TO DEFINITIONS).
"REGIONAL POLICE DEPARTMENT." A POLICE DEPARTMENT FORMED BY
TWO OR MORE MUNICIPALITIES UNDER 53 PA.C.S. CH. 23 SUBCH. A
(RELATING TO INTERGOVERNMENTAL COOPERATION).

(A) DEATH IN LINE OF DUTY.--EXCEPT AS OTHERWISE PROVIDED IN
THIS CHAPTER, IF A LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER DIES IN THE LINE OF
DUTY:
(1) THE SURVIVING SPOUSE OF THE LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER
SHALL BE ENTITLED TO RECEIVE FULL HEALTH BENEFITS FOR THE
REMAINDER OF THE LIFE OF THE SPOUSE, UNLESS SUBSECTION (D) OR
(E) APPLIES.
(2) EACH ELIGIBLE DEPENDENT OF THE LAW ENFORCEMENT
OFFICER SHALL BE ENTITLED TO RECEIVE FULL HEALTH BENEFITS
UNTIL REACHING 26 YEARS OF AGE IF THE DEPENDENT IS NOT
ELIGIBLE FOR COVERAGE UNDER ANOTHER ELIGIBLE EMPLOYER-
SPONSORED HEALTH PLAN.
(B) LINE-OF-DUTY DEATH INVESTIGATION AND DETERMINATION.--
(1) IF AN INVESTIGATION IS NEEDED PRIOR TO MAKING A
LINE-OF-DUTY DEATH DETERMINATION REGARDING A LAW ENFORCEMENT
OFFICER FOR THE PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION, EXISTING HEALTH
BENEFITS OF THE LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER SHALL BE
UNINTERRUPTED FOR THE DURATION OF THE INVESTIGATION.
(2) THE FOLLOWING MAY MAKE A LINE-OF-DUTY DEATH
DETERMINATION REGARDING A LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER FOR THE
PURPOSES OF THIS SECTION:
(I) THE POLICE DEPARTMENT OR REGIONAL POLICE
DEPARTMENT THAT EMPLOYED THE LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER.
(II) THE DEPARTMENT OF GENERAL SERVICES IN
ACCORDANCE WITH THE ACT OF JUNE 24, 1976 (P.L.424,
NO.101), REFERRED TO AS THE EMERGENCY AND LAW ENFORCEMENT
PERSONNEL DEATH BENEFITS ACT.
(III) THE BUREAU OF JUSTICE ASSISTANCE WITHIN THE
UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE IN ACCORDANCE WITH 34
U.S.C. CH. 101 SUBCH. XI PT. A (RELATING TO DEATH

(C) ASSISTANCE.--A MUNICIPALITY OR REGIONAL POLICE
DEPARTMENT SHALL ASSIST A SURVIVING SPOUSE WITH NAVIGATING THE
PROCESS OF CLAIMS AND PAYMENTS UNDER THIS SECTION.
(D) TERMINATION OF BENEFITS.--A MUNICIPALITY MAY TERMINATE
HEALTH BENEFITS UNDER THIS CHAPTER IF:
(1) A SURVIVING SPOUSE OF A DECEASED LAW ENFORCEMENT
OFFICER REMARRIES; OR
(2) A SURVIVING SPOUSE RECEIVES EQUIVALENT HEALTH
BENEFITS FROM THE SURVIVING SPOUSE'S EMPLOYER.
(E) MEDICARE ELIGIBILITY.--WHEN A RECIPIENT OF HEALTH
BENEFITS UNDER THIS CHAPTER IS ENTITLED TO OR ENROLLED IN
MEDICARE:
(1) THE RECIPIENT SHALL UTILIZE THE FEDERAL MEDICARE
PROGRAM AS THE RECIPIENT'S PRIMARY HEALTH BENEFIT.
(2) IN THE CASE OF A SURVIVING SPOUSE OF A DECEASED LAW
ENFORCEMENT OFFICER WHO SERVED A MUNICIPALITY, THE
MUNICIPALITY SHALL PROVIDE OR REIMBURSE THE SURVIVING SPOUSE
FOR SUPPLEMENTAL MEDICARE PROGRAM COVERAGE.
(3) IN THE CASE OF A SURVIVING SPOUSE OF A DECEASED LAW
ENFORCEMENT OFFICER WHO SERVED A REGIONAL POLICE DEPARTMENT,
THE REGIONAL POLICE DEPARTMENT SHALL PROVIDE OR REIMBURSE THE
SURVIVING SPOUSE FOR SUPPLEMENTAL MEDICARE PROGRAM COVERAGE.

THIS CHAPTER SHALL APPLY TO A SURVIVING SPOUSE OR DEPENDENT
OF A LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER WHO DIED IN THE LINE OF DUTY
BEFORE, ON OR AFTER THE EFFECTIVE DATE OF THIS SECTION.
