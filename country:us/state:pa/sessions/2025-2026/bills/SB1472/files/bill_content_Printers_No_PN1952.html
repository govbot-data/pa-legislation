AN ACT
Designating a portion of State Route 3062 from the intersection
with Lieds Road in West Bradford Township, Chester County, to
the intersection with Ryan Boulevard in West Bradford
Township, Chester County, as the Corporal Timothy J.
O'Connor, Jr. Memorial Highway.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Corporal Timothy J. O'Connor, Jr. Memorial Highway.
(a) Findings.--The General Assembly finds that:
(1) Corporal Timothy J. O'Connor, Jr., dedicated 15
years of his life to serving the people of this Commonwealth
as a member of the Pennsylvania State Police.
(2) Corporal O'Connor enlisted with the Pennsylvania
State Police in September 2010 as a member of the 131st Cadet
Class.
(3) Corporal O'Connor was assigned to the patrol unit at
Troop J in Avondale, Chester County, upon his graduation from
the State Police Academy.
(4) Corporal O'Connor later served with the Troop J Vice

Unit before transferring to the patrol unit at Troop J,
Embreeville, Chester County, in March 2022.
(5) Corporal O'Connor was promoted to corporal in July
2022 and remained stationed at Embreeville.
(6) Corporal O'Connor was more than a dedicated law
enforcement officer. He was a husband, father, son,
colleague, friend and valued member of the Chester County
community.
(7) Corporal O'Connor also served as a member of the
First West Chester Fire Company.
(8) On March 8, 2026, Corporal O'Connor responded to a
report of an erratic driver in Chester County. During the
traffic stop, he was fatally shot in the line of duty. He was
40 years old.
(9) Corporal O'Connor became the 105th member of the
Pennsylvania State Police to make the ultimate sacrifice in
the line of duty.
(b) Designation.--The portion of State Route 3062 from the
intersection with Lieds Road in West Bradford Township, Chester
County, to the intersection with Ryan Boulevard in West Bradford
Township, Chester County, as the Corporal Timothy J. O'Connor,
Jr. Memorial Highway.
(c) Signs.--The Department of Transportation shall erect and
maintain appropriate signs displaying the name of the highway to
traffic in both directions on the highway.
Section 2. Effective date.
This act shall take effect in 60 days.
