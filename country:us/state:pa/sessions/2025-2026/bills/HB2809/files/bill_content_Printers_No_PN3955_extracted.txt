Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 7333. Unlicensed sale of liquor.(a) Penalties.--A person who is convicted of violating section 491(1) of the Liquor Code shall be sentenced as follows:(1) For a first offense, to pay a fine of not less than $1,000 nor more than $5,000.(2) For a second offense committed within five years after the date of conviction for a prior violation, to:(i) pay a fine of not less than $5,000 nor more than $10,000; and(ii) a term of imprisonment that shall be fixed by the court at not more than one year.12345678910111213141516171819 (3) For a third or subsequent offense committed within five years after the date of conviction for a prior violation, to:(i) pay a fine of not less than $10,000 nor more than $25,000; and(ii) a term of imprisonment that shall be fixed by the court at not more than two years.(b) Organized unlawful alcohol sales operation.--A violation of section 491(1) of the Liquor Code constitutes a misdemeanor of the first degree if the Commonwealth proves beyond a reasonable doubt that the defendant knowingly and intentionally operated an organized unlawful liquor sales operation for commercial profit.(c) Aggravating circumstances.--In determining penalties or enforcement priorities, aggravating circumstances may include, in order of seriousness:(1) Occurrence of unlawful alcohol activity in connection with violent criminal conduct or other serious offenses.(2) Repeated police responses to the same location indicating ongoing unlawful operations.(3) Violations involving occupancy limits or fire and safety code violations.(d) Limited concurrent municipal enforcement relating to unlawful liquor sales activity.--(1) A law enforcement officer authorized by law to exercise municipal police powers and acting within the scope of otherwise lawful duties may enforce section 491(1) of the Liquor Code and applicable provisions of this title relating to unlawful liquor sales activity.20260HB2809PN3955 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 (2) Municipal enforcement under this subsection shall not require prior approval from, or coordination with, the Bureau of Liquor Control Enforcement as a condition of initiating enforcement activity.(3) Training of municipal law enforcement officers under this subsection, where applicable, shall be consistent with standards established or recognized by the Bureau of Liquor Control Enforcement.
