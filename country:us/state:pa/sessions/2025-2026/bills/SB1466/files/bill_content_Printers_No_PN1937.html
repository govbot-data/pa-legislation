AN ACT
Amending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in registration of vehicles, providing for special
plates for recipients of Meritorious Service Medal.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:
§ 1368.5. Special plates for recipients of Meritorious Service
Medal.
Upon application of any person who is a recipient of the
Meritorious Service Medal, accompanied by a fee of $28 which
shall be in addition to the annual or biennial registration fee
and by any documentation that the department shall require, the
department shall issue to the person a special registration
plate designating the vehicle licensed as belonging to a person
who is a recipient of the Meritorious Service Medal. The special
registration plate may be used only on a passenger car or truck
with a registered gross weight of not more than 14,000 pounds.
