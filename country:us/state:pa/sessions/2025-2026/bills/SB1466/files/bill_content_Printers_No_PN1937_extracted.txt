WARD, PHILLIPS-HILL, PICOZZI, PENNYCUICK, MARTIN, YAW, BROWN, BARTOLOTTA, VOGEL, DUSH AND STEFANO, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 REFERRED TO TRANSPORTATION, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 75 (Vehicles) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in registration of vehicles, providing for special plates for recipients of Meritorious Service Medal.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 75 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 1368.5. Special plates for recipients of Meritorious Service Medal.Upon application of any person who is a recipient of the Meritorious Service Medal, accompanied by a fee of $28 which shall be in addition to the annual or biennial registration fee and by any documentation that the department shall require, the department shall issue to the person a special registration plate designating the vehicle licensed as belonging to a person who is a recipient of the Meritorious Service Medal. The special registration plate may be used only on a passenger car or truck with a registered gross weight of not more than 14,000 pounds.123456789101112131415161718 Section 2.
