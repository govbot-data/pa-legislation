AN ACT
Providing for the regulation and sale of kratom products;
imposing a tobacco products tax on natural kratom products;
TAX ON NATURAL KRATOM PRODUCTS; ESTABLISHING THE KRATOM
REGULATORY FUND; imposing duties on the Department of Health
AND THE DEPARTMENT OF REVENUE; and imposing penalties.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Short title.
This act shall be known and may be cited as the Regulation
and Oversight of Kratom Act.

have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Dealer." A wholesaler or retailer.
"Department." The Department of Health of the Commonwealth.

The tobacco products tax under section 1202-A(a) of the act
of March 4, 1971 (P.L.6, No.2), known as the Tax Reform Code of
1971, is imposed on a dealer or manufacturer at the time the
natural kratom product is first sold to a retailer in this
Commonwealth as provided under Article XII-A of the Tax Reform
Code of 1971.

SECTION 7. KRATOM REGULATORY FUND.
(A) ESTABLISHMENT.--THE KRATOM REGULATORY FUND IS
ESTABLISHED IN THE STATE TREASURY. THE FOLLOWING ACCOUNTS ARE
ESTABLISHED WITHIN THE FUND:
(1) KRATOM ADMINISTRATION ACCOUNT. THE MONEY IN THE
ACCOUNT IS APPROPRIATED ON A CONTINUING BASIS TO THE
DEPARTMENT. THE ACCOUNT SHALL CONSIST OF THE MONEY DEPOSITED
UNDER SUBSECTION (B)(1) AND THE INTEREST EARNED ON THE MONEY.
(2) KRATOM ENFORCEMENT ACCOUNT. THE MONEY IN THE ACCOUNT
IS APPROPRIATED ON A CONTINUING BASIS TO THE OFFICE OF
ATTORNEY GENERAL. THE ACCOUNT SHALL CONSIST OF THE MONEY
DEPOSITED UNDER SUBSECTION (B)(2) AND THE INTEREST EARNED ON
THE MONEY.
(B) SOURCES OF FUNDING.--FEES AND TAXES COLLECTED UNDER THIS
ACT SHALL BE DEPOSITED AS FOLLOWS:
(1) SEVENTY-FIVE PERCENT OF THE FEES AND TAXES SHALL BE
DEPOSITED INTO THE KRATOM ADMINISTRATION ACCOUNT.
(2) TWENTY-FIVE PERCENT OF THE FEES AND TAXES SHALL BE
DEPOSITED INTO THE KRATOM ENFORCEMENT ACCOUNT.
