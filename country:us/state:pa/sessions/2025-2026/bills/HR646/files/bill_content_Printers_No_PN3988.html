A RESOLUTION
Designating November 5, 2026, as "Frankford Yellow Jackets
Championship Day" in Pennsylvania in honor of the team's 1926
National Football League Championship.
WHEREAS, The Frankford Yellow Jackets were a professional
football team based in the Frankford neighborhood of
Philadelphia; and
WHEREAS, The Frankford Yellow Jackets won the 1926 National
Football League (NFL) championship with a league-best record of
14 wins, 1 loss and 2 ties; and
WHEREAS, The Frankford Yellow Jackets led the NFL that season
in average points scored per game, touchdowns scored,
interception-return touchdowns, touchdowns scored on returns of
blocked kicks or missed field goals, average margin of victory
and point differential and tied for the league lead in rushing
touchdowns and fumble-recovery touchdowns; and
WHEREAS, In 1926, Coach Guy "The Champ" Chamberlin led the
Frankford Yellow Jackets to their first NFL championship and his

WHEREAS, The 1926 Frankford Yellow Jackets are being inducted
into the Philadelphia Sports Hall of Fame as part of the 2026
class for their athletic contributions to the City of
Philadelphia, this Commonwealth and the NFL; and
WHEREAS, The year 2026 marks the 100th anniversary of the
team's championship victory; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives designate
November 5, 2026, as "Frankford Yellow Jackets Championship Day"
in Pennsylvania in honor of the team's 1926 National Football
League Championship; and be it further
RESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to
the Philadelphia Sports Hall of Fame in celebration of the
Frankford Yellow Jackets' contributions to Pennsylvania sports
culture; and be it further
RESOLVED, That a copy of this resolution be transmitted to
The Historical Society of Frankford in honor of the Frankford
Yellow Jackets' contributions to the history of Frankford.
