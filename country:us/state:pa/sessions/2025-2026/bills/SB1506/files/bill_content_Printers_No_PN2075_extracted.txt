2025-2026 Regular Session SB 1506 PN 2075 Bill Text (HTM) See other bills under thesame topic PRINTER'S NO. 2075 THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF PENNSYLVANIASENATE BILL No.1506 Session of 2026 INTRODUCED BY KANE, COSTA AND KEARNEY, OCTOBER 27, 2026 REFERRED TO CONSUMER PROTECTION AND PROFESSIONAL LICENSURE, OCTOBER 27, 2026 AN ACTProviding for consumer rights in digital games; imposing a penalty; and providing for severability.The General Assembly finds and declares that:(1) Consumers increasingly purchase digital games rather than physical copies and often pay full retail prices comparable to or exceeding those of physical media.(2) Many digital games depend on services controlled by a publisher or developer, including authentication systems, server access, digital rights management and required software updates, without which the game cannot be used.(3) When an operator ceases to provide those services, consumers who paid to acquire a game may lose all ability to use it, notwithstanding a reasonable expectation of continued access established at the time of purchase.(4) Consumers are frequently given little or no advance notice before losing access and are left without a functional product, a means of continued use or a refund.(5) It is the intent of the General Assembly to protect 123456789101112131415161718 consumers by requiring reasonable notice before the loss of services necessary for the ordinary use of a digital game and by requiring operators to provide a means of continued use or a refund when those services cease.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Short title.This act shall be known and may be cited as the Protect Our Games Act.Section 2. Definitions.The following words and phrases when used in this act shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:"Digital game." A game that a person accesses and manipulates using a specialized electronic gaming device, computer, mobile device, tablet or other device with a display screen, including any add-on or additional content for the game."Digital game operator." A publisher, developer or other person that controls whether a purchaser of a digital game can make ordinary use of the digital game, including, but not limited to, controlling authentication systems, server access, digital rights management or required software updates. The term does not include a person that provides general-purpose hosting, cloud computing, storage, network services or an online platform or marketplace for the distribution of a digital game but does not control whether a purchaser may make ordinary use of the digital game."Ordinary use." A purchaser's ability to use the core features of a digital game consistent with the reasonable expectations of a purchaser based on how the digital game was 20260SB1506PN2075 - 2 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 advertised, marketed or otherwise described by the digital game operator at the time of purchase.Section 3. Notice, continued use and refund requirements.(a) Notice.--The following shall apply to a digital game first available for purchase or rereleased for purchase on or after January 1, 2028:(1) Sixty days before a digital game operator ceases to provide services necessary for the ordinary use of the digital game, the operator shall communicate the following to each purchaser or prospective purchaser of the digital game:(i) The date on which services necessary for the ordinary use of the digital game will cease.(ii) Any services that will no longer be provided by the operator.(iii) Any game features that will no longer be available to the purchaser.(iv) Any known security risks that may result from the cessation of services.(v) How the purchaser can continue to use the digital game or obtain a refund in accordance with subsection (b)(3).(2) A digital game operator shall communicate the information required under paragraph (1) by:(i) notifying purchasers directly through the operator's digital game; or(ii) posting the information on the operator's publicly accessible Internet website.(b) Requirement for continued use.--Beginning on the date that a digital game operator ceases to provide services necessary for the ordinary use of a digital game, the operator 20260SB1506PN2075 - 3 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 shall provide a purchaser with one or more of the following:(1) A version of the digital game that can be used by a purchaser independent of services controlled by the operator.(2) A patch or update to the purchaser's version of the digital game that enables continued use of the digital game independent of services controlled by the operator.(3) A refund in an amount equal to the highest price of the digital game offered by the digital game operator within the 12 months before the digital game operator has ceased providing services necessary for the ordinary use of the digital game. The refund amount must be equal to or more than 25% of the initial sale price on the game's release date.(4) All necessary documentation to allow the purchaser to host a private or community server with which the purchaser could make ordinary use of the game independent of services controlled by the operator.(5) A version of server software that the game may connect to in order to make ordinary use of the game independent of other ongoing services if:(i) the operator has communicated to a purchaser that the server software contains additional hardware or software requirements beyond what was necessary for the original game; and(ii) the additional hardware or software requirements are reasonably attainable at the time services by the digital game operator cease.(c) Limitation on cessation of service.--Beginning on the date a digital game operator has ceased to provide services necessary for the ordinary use of the digital game, the operator may not sell, lease or otherwise distribute a version of the 20260SB1506PN2075 - 4 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 game that cannot be used by a purchaser independent of services controlled by the operator.(d) Nonapplicability.--This section does not apply to the following:(1) Any subscription-based service that advertises or offers for sale access to any digital game solely for the duration of the subscription.(2) Any digital game that is advertised or offered to a person for no monetary consideration, unless the person has made purchases or invested money to upgrade characters or unlock features within the game. If a consumer has spent $50 or more on upgrades or features for a game offered for no monetary consideration, the gaming operator shall follow the conditions of subsection (b).(3) Any digital game that is advertised or offered to a person to whom the seller cannot revoke access after the transaction, including a digital game that has been made available at the time of purchase for permanent offline download to an external storage source to be used without a connection to the Internet.Section 4. Penalty.A violation of this act shall be subject to a civil penalty of $1,000 for a first violation, $5,000 for a second violation and $10,000 for a third or subsequent violation. The violator shall pay attorney fees and investigative costs of the Attorney General or district attorney that brings an action under this act.Section 5.
