AN ACT
Amending Title 40 (Insurance) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in preliminary provisions relating to health
insurance markets oversight, further providing for
definitions; and, in State-based exchange, further providing
for powers and duties of exchange authority and for reports.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The definition of "enrollee" in section 9103 of
Title 40 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to
read:
§ 9103. Definitions.
Subject to additional definitions contained in subsequent
provisions of this part which are applicable to specific
provisions of this part, the following words and phrases when
used in this part shall have the meanings given to them in this
section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
* * *
"Enrollee." A policyholder, certificate holder, subscriber,
covered person or other individual who:

Section 2. Sections 9305 and 9311 of Title 40 are amended by
adding subsections to read:
§ 9305. Powers and duties of exchange authority.
* * *
(d) Insurer rescission requests.--Upon receiving a request
for rescission from an insurer, the exchange authority shall:
(1) respond to the request within one business day of
receiving the request; and
(2) make a final determination on the request within
five business days.
(e) Office of Fraud Prevention.-- No later than 180 days
after the effective date of this subsection, the board shall
establish within the exchange authority the Office of Fraud
Prevention, which shall:
(1) Receive and investigate reports and complaints of
suspected fraud.
(2) Create a standardized form by which an insurer shall
report suspected fraud to the exchange authority.
(3) Develop policies and procedures by which the
exchange authority shall:
(i) report and resolve allegations of fraud,

(ii) certify that each employee of the exchange

§ 9311. Reports.
* * *
(d) Fraud report.--By November 30, 2026, and by November 30
of each subsequent year, the exchange authority shall submit an
annual report to the President pro tempore of the Senate and the
Speaker of the House of Representatives, which shall include the
following:
(1) The following data relating to suspected fraud:
(i) For the prior calendar year, the number of
suspected fraud reports received and the total dollar
amount of the suspected fraud.
(ii) For the prior calendar year, the number of
confirmed fraud reports and the total dollar amount of
the confirmed fraud.
(iii) The total dollar amount of advance premium tax
credits provided to rescinded policies during the prior
calendar year.
(2) A copy of the standardized form created under
section 9305(e)(2) (relating to powers and duties of exchange
authority).
(3) A copy of any policy or procedure adopted to
implement the requirements related to rescission requests
under section 9305(d).
