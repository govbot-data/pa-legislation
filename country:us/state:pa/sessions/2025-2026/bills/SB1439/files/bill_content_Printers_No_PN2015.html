AN ACT
Amending Title 40 (Insurance) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated
Statutes, in preliminary provisions relating to health
insurance markets oversight, further providing for
definitions; and, in State-based exchange, further providing
for powers and duties of exchange authority and for reports.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. The definition of "enrollee" in section 9103 of
Title 40 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended to
read:
§ 9103. Definitions.
Subject to additional definitions contained in subsequent
provisions of this part which are applicable to specific
provisions of this part, the following words and phrases when
used in this part shall have the meanings given to them in this
section unless the context clearly indicates otherwise:
* * *
"Enrollee." A policyholder, certificate holder, subscriber,
covered person or other individual who:

Section 2. Sections 9305 and 9311 of Title 40 are amended by
adding subsections to read:
§ 9305. Powers and duties of exchange authority.
* * *
(d) Insurer rescission requests.--Upon receiving a request
for rescission from an insurer, the exchange authority shall:
(1) respond to the request within one business day of
receiving the request; and
(2) make a final determination on the request within
five business days.

(D) INSURER RESCISSION REQUESTS.--
(1) AFTER RECEIVING A NEW REQUEST FOR RESCISSION FROM AN
INSURER, THE EXCHANGE AUTHORITY SHALL DO ALL OF THE
FOLLOWING:
(I) ACKNOWLEDGE RECEIPT OF THE REQUEST WITHIN FIVE
BUSINESS DAYS OF RECEIVING THE REQUEST.
(II) ISSUE TO THE INSURER A FINAL DETERMINATION ON
THE REQUEST WITHIN 30 BUSINESS DAYS.
(2) IF A REQUEST FOR RESCISSION FROM AN INSURER IS
DENIED BY THE EXCHANGE AUTHORITY DUE TO INSUFFICIENT
EVIDENCE, THE INSURER MAY SUBMIT A NEW REQUEST.
(3) IF THE EXCHANGE AUTHORITY REQUESTS ADDITIONAL
INFORMATION, AN INSURER SHALL RESPOND WITHIN FIVE BUSINESS
DAYS TO THE REQUEST AND THE TIMELINE UNDER PARAGRAPH (1)(II)
SHALL BE TOLLED.

suspected fraud.
(2) Create a standardized form by which an insurer shall
report suspected fraud to the exchange authority.
