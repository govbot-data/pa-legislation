AN ACT

Consolidated Statutes, in jurisdiction and parties, further
providing for representation; in proceedings prior to
petition to adopt, further providing for home study and
preplacement report; providing for adoption intermediary
services and advertising; establishing the Adoption
Intermediary Services Fund; IMPOSING DUTIES ON THE OFFICE OF
ATTORNEY GENERAL; and imposing penalties.

hereby enacts as follows:

Section 2. Title 23 is amended by adding a chapter to read:
CHAPTER 28
ADOPTION INTERMEDIARY SERVICES AND ADVERTISING
Sec.
2801. Definitions.
2802. Authority of department OFFICE OF ATTORNEY GENERAL .

§ 2801. Definitions.
The following words and phrases when used in this chapter
shall have the meanings given to them in this section unless the
context clearly indicates otherwise:
"Adoption advertising." An advertisement, article, notice or
other paid communication published in a newspaper or magazine,
on the Internet, on a billboard, over radio or television or
through any public media that does any of the following:
(1) Solicits placing parents or prospective adoptive
parents for the purpose of acting as a link between a placing
parent and prospective adoptive parent for the placement of a
child for adoption.
(2) Offers to pay, reimburse or otherwise provide
anything of value to a placing parent of a child in
connection with the birth or adoption of the child, including
living expenses, medical or hospital care and other expenses.

§ 2802. Authority of department OFFICE OF ATTORNEY GENERAL .
The department OFFICE OF ATTORNEY GENERAL shall administer
and enforce the provisions of this chapter.
§ 2803. Adoption intermediary services.
(a) Offense.--A person may not provide adoption intermediary
services unless the person is an agency, the department or an
attorney licensed to practice law in this Commonwealth.
(b) Penalties.--A person that violates this section shall be
subject to the following:
(1) For a first violation, a fine of $15,000. The
department OFFICE OF ATTORNEY GENERAL shall waive the fine
under this paragraph unless the department OFFICE OF ATTORNEY
GENERAL determines that the person demonstrated willful
disregard of the requirements of this section.
(2) For a second violation, a fine of $20,000.
(3) For a third or subsequent violation, a fine of
$30,000.
(c) Deposit in fund GENERAL FUND .--Each fine imposed and
collected by the department OFFICE OF ATTORNEY GENERAL under
this section shall be deposited into the fund GENERAL FUND .

§ 2805. Adoption Intermediary Services Fund.
(a) Establishment.--The Adoption Intermediary Services Fund
is established in the State Treasury.
(b) Use of money.--The money in the fund shall be
appropriated on a continuing basis to the department to be used
to administer and enforce sections 2803 (relating to adoption
intermediary services) and 2804 (relating to adoption
advertising).
Section 3. This act shall apply to proceedings and actions
commenced on or after the effective date of this section.
Section 4. This act shall take effect in 60 days.
