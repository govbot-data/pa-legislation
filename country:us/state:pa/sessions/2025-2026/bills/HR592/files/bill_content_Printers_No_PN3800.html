A RESOLUTION
Commending the United States Senators representing this
Commonwealth, businesses, trade associations, labor
organizations, agricultural leaders and community partners
for ensuring Commonwealth representation at the American
State Fair; and urging the Office of the Governor to
participate in future similar national celebrations.
WHEREAS, The residents of this Commonwealth deserve to have
this Commonwealth's agriculture, manufacturing, energy, tourism,
innovation and cultural heritage showcased on the national stage
whenever the opportunity arises; and
WHEREAS, Participation in the American State Fair provides an
opportunity to attract investment, promote tourism, celebrate
this Commonwealth's agriculture and highlight the industries and
workers that make this Commonwealth exceptional; and
WHEREAS, Pennsylvanians expect their elected officials to
seize opportunities to promote this Commonwealth rather than
allow partisan disagreements or political grievances to stand in
the way; and
WHEREAS, When the Office of the Governor failed to ensure
