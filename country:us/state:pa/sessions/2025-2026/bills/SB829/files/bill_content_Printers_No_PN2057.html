AN ACT
Designating the Hynerpeton bassetti, a stem tetrapod, as the
official State fossil of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania;
DESIGNATING THE EASTERN BOX TURTLE (TERRAPENE CAROLINA
CAROLINA) AS THE OFFICIAL STATE REPTILE OF THE COMMONWEALTH
OF PENNSYLVANIA; DESIGNATING HERSHEY'S KISSES AS THE OFFICIAL
STATE CANDY OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF PENNSYLVANIA; and making a
repeal.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Findings and declarations. OFFICIAL STATE FOSSIL.
(A) FINDINGS AND DECLARATIONS.--The General Assembly finds
and declares as follows:
(1) Hynerpeton bassetti is a specific type of extinct
tetrapod and an early four-limbed vertebrate that resembled a
modern day amphibian.
(2) Hynerpeton, meaning "crawling animal from Hyner," is
unique to this Commonwealth and was discovered in 1993 at the
Red Hill fossil site near Hyner, Pennsylvania.
(3) Due to the robust structure of the shoulder girdle,
the Hynerpeton was thought to have very powerful muscles,

(4) The fossilized remains of Hynerpeton bassetti were

(5) Naming the Hynerpeton bassetti as the official State

Section 2. Official State fossil.
(B) HYNERPETON BASSETTI.--The Hynerpeton bassetti is
selected, designated and adopted as the official State fossil of
the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
SECTION 2. OFFICIAL STATE REPTILE.
(A) FINDINGS AND DECLARATIONS.--THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY FINDS
AND DECLARES AS FOLLOWS:
(1) THE EASTERN BOX TURTLE CAN BE FOUND ACROSS THIS
COMMONWEALTH IN RURAL, SUBURBAN AND EVEN URBAN HABITATS IN
SHRUBBY GRASSLANDS, MARSHY MEADOWS, OPEN WOODLANDS AND FIELD
FOREST EDGES.
(2) THE EASTERN BOX TURTLE IS OFTEN FOUND NEAR STREAMS
OR PONDS OR AREAS THAT HAVE EXPERIENCED HEAVY RAINFALL.
(3) THE EASTERN BOX TURTLE GENERALLY LIVES FOR 25 TO 35
YEARS BUT HAS BEEN KNOWN TO SURVIVE FOR MORE THAN 100 YEARS.
(4) THE EASTERN BOX TURTLE HAS MANY UNIQUE IDENTIFYING
CHARACTERISTICS, INCLUDING:
(I) WHEN IN DANGER, THE EASTERN BOX TURTLE CAN CLOSE
THE HINGED SECTIONS OF ITS SHELL, EFFECTIVELY SEALING ITS
SOFT BODY IN BONE, FORMING A "BOX."
(II) WHEN INJURED OR DAMAGED, THE EASTERN BOX
TURTLE'S SHELL HAS THE CAPACITY TO REGENERATE AND REFORM.

(5) UNFORTUNATELY, THE EASTERN BOX TURTLE IS BECOMING AN

MOST OF ITS RANGE DUE TO HABITAT FRAGMENTATION, ROADWAY

OTHER TURTLE, SNAKE AND LIZARD SPECIES.

CAROLINA CAROLINA) IS DESIGNATED AS THE OFFICIAL STATE REPTILE
