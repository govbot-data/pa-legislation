A RESOLUTION
Recognizing December 7, 2026, as "Pearl Harbor Remembrance Day"
in Pennsylvania in honor of the courage, sacrifice and memory
of the American service members, civilians and citizens of
Hawaii who were killed, wounded or forever impacted by the
attack on Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941.
WHEREAS, On the morning of December 7, 1941, the United
States naval base at Pearl Harbor, Hawaii, was attacked by
Imperial Japan, forever changing the course of American history;
and
WHEREAS, The attack on Pearl Harbor claimed the lives of more
than 2,400 Americans and wounded more than 1,100 others,
including members of the United States Armed Forces and
civilians; and
WHEREAS, The citizens of Hawaii experienced the terror,
destruction and uncertainty of that day firsthand, as families,
workers, children and communities across the island of Oahu were
suddenly thrust into war; and

WHEREAS, Many Hawaiian citizens assisted in the aftermath of
the attack, helping the wounded, supporting military personnel,
protecting their communities and demonstrating extraordinary
courage and resilience during one of the darkest moments in
American history; and
WHEREAS, The USS Arizona, USS Oklahoma, USS Utah and many
other ships and aircraft suffered devastating losses and damage
during the attack; and
WHEREAS, The USS Pennsylvania, proudly bearing the name of
this Commonwealth, was present at Pearl Harbor and sustained
casualties during the attack, creating a lasting connection
between Pennsylvania and that solemn day in American history;
and
WHEREAS, The courage and sacrifice displayed by the service
members, civilians and citizens of Hawaii at Pearl Harbor
awakened a nation, strengthened American resolve and led the
United States into World War II; and
WHEREAS, The service members who fought at Pearl Harbor, the
citizens of Hawaii who endured the attack and the millions of
Americans who later served during World War II became part of
the enduring legacy of the Greatest Generation; and
WHEREAS, This Commonwealth has a solemn duty to remember the
sacrifices of those who defended our Nation, honor the lives
lost at Pearl Harbor, recognize the citizens of Hawaii who
endured the attack and teach future generations the meaning of
service, courage, resilience and patriotism; therefore be it
RESOLVED, That the House of Representatives recognize
December 7, 2026, as "Pearl Harbor Remembrance Day" in
Pennsylvania in honor of the courage, sacrifice and memory of
the American service members, civilians and citizens of Hawaii
