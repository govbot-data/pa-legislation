The following shall be excluded from the tax:(i) Social Security.(ii) Supplemental Security Income.(iii) Social Security Disability Income.(iv) Unemployment benefits.(v) Workers' compensation benefits.(vi) Sick pay.(vii) Paid family and medical leave.(viii) Defined contribution and defined benefit pension payments.(c) Payment of tax obligations.--An employer, whether private or public, may agree to pay all or part of an employee's payroll tax obligation under subsection (b). A payment under this subsection shall not be considered income to the employee for Pennsylvania income tax purposes.(d) Remedies.--Except as provided under this act, default, underpayment or late payment of a tax or other obligation imposed by the office shall be subject to the remedies and penalties as provided by the laws of this Commonwealth.(e) Eligibility.--Eligibility for covered services shall not be impaired by a default, underpayment or late payment of a tax or other obligation imposed by the office.(f) Consolidation of spending.--The office shall ensure that 20260SB1465PN1936 - 27 - 123456789101112131415161718192021222324252627282930 public spending on health insurance is consolidated into the office to the greatest extent possible. Implementation of office.(a) Initial appointments.--No later than 60 days after the effective date of this subsection, the Governor and Attorney General shall make the initial appointments to the board.(b) Hiring and budgeting process.--(1) The board shall immediately begin the process of hiring an executive director, reviewing enabling legislation and educating itself regarding general purposes, economics and authority of the office.(2) The board shall develop a budget for the first year of transition to the system and initiate the process of obtaining Federal waivers and agreements concerning payments from Medicare, Medicaid and other public programs.(3) The board shall set a general time period for establishing the office no earlier than one year and no later than 18 months after the first meeting of the board.(c) Transition phases.--(1) In the first phase of transition to the system, the executive director shall begin hiring staff, establishing the administrative and information technology infrastructure for the office and negotiating reimbursement rates for health care services, pharmaceuticals and medical equipment.
