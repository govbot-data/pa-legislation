A RESOLUTION
Honoring the memory of the late Honorable Andrew James Carn,
former member of the House of Representatives from the 197th
Legislative District, Philadelphia County.
WHEREAS, Mr. Carn was born in Philadelphia on August 25,
1950, to Reggie and Evonia Carn; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Carn was a 1968 graduate of Thomas Edison High
School and earned a bachelor's degree in electrical engineering
from Howard University in 1973; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Carn was employed as an engineer for United
Engineers, Inc., and as a producer and moderator of The
Breakfast Show on WDAS radio, where he engaged in urban issues;
and
WHEREAS, Mr. Carn went on to serve as director of the Negro
Trade Union Leadership Council and Philadelphia Housing
Neighborhood Services, Inc., as well as executive director of
Philadelphia Committee for Service to Youth, Inc.; and

WHEREAS, Mr. Carn was elected to the House of Representatives
in 1982 and served the 197th Legislative District through 2000;
and
WHEREAS, Mr. Carn's legislative record included championing
causes such as neighborhood revitalization, youth development,
African-American entrepreneurship and expanded economic
opportunities; and
WHEREAS, During his tenure, Mr. Carn served on the House
Appropriations Committee and the Select Committee to Investigate
the Commonwealth's Response to Medicaid Fraud; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Carn then served as President of the Berean
Institute prior to becoming a videographer, capturing political
discourse, cultural vibrancy, musical heritage and the living
history of the Philadelphia community; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Carn lived a life defined by courage, public
purpose and an unyielding love for the people; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Carn's work strengthened the foundations of this
Commonwealth, and his legacy will continue to inspire public
servants and community leaders for generations to come; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives joins in expressing
sorrow for the loss of Mr. Carn, who died July 20, 2026, at 75
years of age; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives offers condolences to
Mr. Carn's family, including his wife, daughter, stepson,
grandchildren, siblings and extended family and friends; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives commends Mr. Carn's
contributions as a legislator and public servant; and
WHEREAS, The House of Representatives celebrates the
meaningful ways in which Mr. Carn contributed to the residents,
businesses and organizations of Philadelphia County and this
