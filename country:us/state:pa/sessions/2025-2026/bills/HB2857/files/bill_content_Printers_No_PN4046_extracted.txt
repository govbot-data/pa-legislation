MACKENZIE, McNEILL, PROBST AND MAKO, OCTOBER 15, 2026 REFERRED TO COMMITTEE ON LABOR AND INDUSTRY, OCTOBER 16, 2026 AN ACTAmending Title 18 (Crimes and Offenses) of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes, in burglary and other criminal intrusion, providing for the offense of unlawful entry into animal enclosure.The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania hereby enacts as follows:Section 1. Title 18 of the Pennsylvania Consolidated Statutes is amended by adding a section to read:§ 3503.1. Unlawful entry into animal enclosure.(a) Offense defined.--A person commits the offense of unlawful entry into an animal enclosure if, knowing that the person is not licensed or privileged to do so, the person:(1) enters a locked or restricted area in an animal enclosure; or(2) climbs over, under, through or otherwise bypasses a fence, wall, moat, gate or other protective structure designed to separate the public from an animal enclosure.(b) Grading.--(1) Except as provided under paragraphs (2) and (3), a 12345678910111213141516171819 violation of this section constitutes a misdemeanor of the third degree.(2) If the violation causes the evacuation of visitors or emergency response by law enforcement, fire, emergency medical services, animal control personnel or staff, the offense constitutes a misdemeanor of the second degree.(3) If the violation results in injury, death or substantial risk of injury or death to an animal, employee, emergency responder or member of the public, the offense constitutes a felony of the third degree.(c) Restitution.--In addition to any other penalty imposed by law, the court shall order restitution for costs incurred by the facility, including animal recovery, veterinary treatment, emergency response, enclosure repair and operational disruptions.(d) Construction.--Nothing in this section shall be construed to prohibit prosecution under any other provision of law, including criminal trespass, criminal mischief, recklessly endangering another person, cruelty to animals or related offenses.(e) Exceptions.--Subsection (a) does not apply to:(1) employees, contractors, veterinarians or volunteers acting within the scope of authorized duties;(2) law enforcement officers, emergency responders or animal control personnel acting within the scope of official duties;
