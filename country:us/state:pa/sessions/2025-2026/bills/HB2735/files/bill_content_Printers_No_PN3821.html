AN ACT
Amending the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30, No.14), entitled "An
act relating to the public school system, including certain
provisions applicable as well to private and parochial
schools; amending, revising, consolidating and changing the
laws relating thereto," in school finances, further providing
for limitations on certain unreserved fund balances.
The General Assembly of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania
hereby enacts as follows:
Section 1. Section 688 of the act of March 10, 1949 (P.L.30,
No.14), known as the Public School Code of 1949, is amended to
read:
Section 688. Limitations on Certain Unreserved Fund
Balances.--(a) For the [2005-2006] 2027-2028 school year and
each school year thereafter, no school district shall approve an
increase in real property taxes unless it has adopted a budget
that includes an estimated ending [unreserved, undesignated]
committed, assigned and unassigned fund balance less than the
percentages set forth as follows:

budgeted expenditures for that school year.
